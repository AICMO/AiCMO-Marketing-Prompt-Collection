- Create a custom `Expansion_Readiness_Score__c` field on the Account object
- Build a Salesforce Flow that creates a CSM Task when ERS crosses 75 threshold
- Use Expansion Opportunity record type (separate from new-logo pipeline) to track expansion ARR separately from new ARR

- Map ERS components to CTA (Call to Action) rules — ERS ≥ 75 triggers "Expansion Conversation" CTA
- Configure Cockpit playbooks from the upsell sequences above (one playbook per expansion product)
- Use Timeline to log all expansion touches; report on touch rate vs. conversion
- Power Reviews (EBR) with auto-generated slide data from product analytics connector

**Amplitude / Mixpanel:**
- Create a saved Cohort for each ERS signal threshold (e.g., "Accounts: Seat Util ≥ 85%")

- Configure weekly automated email digest of top expansion signal movers to CS Slack channel

- Join product telemetry + CRM data to compute ERS at account level
- Push ERS scores back to Salesforce via Fivetran or Census (reverse ETL)
- Build dbt model: `expansion_tier_assignment` with full scoring logic as SQL

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Product analytics data isn't available at account/company level — only user level.**
Fix: Map user email domains to Salesforce Account IDs using a lookup table. Build a Snowflake dbt model that aggregates user-level Amplitude events to account_id using the domain-to-account mapping. This is a 2-3 day data engineering task but unlocks the entire signal framework. Short-term workaround: use seat count and login frequency from your billing system as proxy signals.

**Problem: CSMs say the ERS scores don't match their intuition — they distrust the model.**
Fix: Run a 30-day calibration exercise. Have CSMs rate their top 20 accounts by expansion readiness (1–5 scale) before seeing the ERS. Compare rankings. Find signal weights that increase correlation. CSM intuition often captures relationship quality and executive sponsor strength — add a "CSM Intuition Override" field (1–5) that contributes 15% to final ERS. This builds trust in the model while preserving human judgment.

**Problem: Tech-touch expansion emails are getting low open rates (<15%) and no conversions.**
Fix: The most common cause is sending from a no-reply address or a generic marketing email. Tech-touch expansion emails must appear to come from the assigned CSM's actual email address, even when automated. Use Outreach/Salesloft's "send as CSM" feature with the CSM's Salesforce user as the sender. Also A/B test subject lines — data-driven subjects ("Your DataFlow usage is up 67%") outperform generic ones ("Unlock more value") by 2–3x open rate.
