## Overview
Designs a complete AI-powered Mutual Success Plan (MSP) system that converts customer-defined success milestones into automated expansion revenue triggers — giving CSMs a structured, co-authored roadmap that aligns on value delivery, surfaces expansion signals in real time, and transforms every QBR into a data-backed expansion conversation. Use this when customers churn before realizing full value, expansion conversations feel unnatural or premature, CSMs lack a consistent framework for post-onboarding engagement, or your NRR is below 110% despite strong product usage.

## Troubleshooting

**"Our customers agree to milestones at kickoff but never reference them again — the MSP becomes shelf-ware"**
The root cause is milestones that customers didn't truly co-author. If a CSM proposes milestones and customers simply agree, they have no ownership. Fix: In the first 30-day kickoff call, ask four specific questions before proposing anything: (1) "What would you need to achieve in the next 6 months to consider this investment successful when you present it to your CEO?" (2) "How are you currently measuring the problem this product solves?" (3) "What's the business cost if we don't solve this by year-end?" (4) "Who else on your team has a stake in these outcomes?" Build milestones exclusively from their answers. If their answers are vague, push back: "Help me make that specific enough that we could both look at a dashboard and agree it's been achieved."

**"CSMs don't have time to maintain MSPs — they become outdated within 60 days"**
The MSP should be 95% automated, with CSMs providing judgment only on the 5% that requires human relationship context. If CSMs are manually updating milestone status, the system design is wrong. Configure your CS platform (Gainsight/ChurnZero) to auto-update milestone status from product data signals you define. Milestone status emails to customers should be auto-drafted and require only a 30-second CSM review before sending. Reserve CSM time for the conversation layer — interpreting what milestone progress means and connecting it to expansion — not for administrative tracking.

**"We achieved all the milestones but customers still didn't expand — they just said 'great, see you at renewal'"**
Milestone achievement is necessary but not sufficient for expansion. The missing piece is proactively connecting achievement to the expansion conversation while the milestone is emotionally fresh — ideally within 48 hours of achievement, not at the next scheduled QBR 2 months later. Build a milestone achievement notification workflow: when a milestone is marked Achieved, the CSM receives a Slack alert with the pre-scripted expansion conversation opener and a calendar link to schedule a 20-minute "milestone celebration and next chapter" call with the customer champion. The conversation should happen while the positive energy of achievement is at its peak.
