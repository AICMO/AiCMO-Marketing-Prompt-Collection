## Overview

Builds a CMO-level strategic architecture for treating AI language models (ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini, Copilot) as a distinct, measurable marketing distribution channel — auditing your current brand presence in AI-generated answers, engineering a content and PR strategy that maximizes citation and recommendation frequency, and establishing Share of AI Voice (SoAV) as a board-level revenue intelligence metric. Use this when your ICP reports using AI tools for vendor research, when you suspect competitors are being recommended over you in AI-generated answers, or when planning Q1/Q2 GTM strategy in an AI-first buyer environment.

## Troubleshooting

**"Our SoAV isn't improving even after 90 days of content production."**

The most common cause is content distribution failure — you're creating citation-worthy content but it's not being indexed by AI models or picked up by the authority sources they reference. Fix: Prioritize earned media placement over owned publication. A single article in TechCrunch, VentureBeat, or a category-specific trade publication creates more AI citation probability than 10 blog posts on your own domain. Brief your PR team specifically on AI indexability as a distribution objective and track media placements by outlet tier.

**"AI models are mentioning us but saying inaccurate things (wrong pricing, wrong use cases, outdated features)."**

AI models base factual claims on their training data and retrieval sources — inaccurate information in Wikipedia, outdated Crunchbase profiles, or old press releases is the typical culprit. Fix: (1) Update your Wikipedia page with accurate, cited information; (2) Refresh your Crunchbase, G2, and Capterra profiles with current information; (3) Publish a current "About [Company]" page on your site with clear structured data markup; (4) Issue a press release when significant facts change (funding, major customer wins, product pivots) to create fresh indexed sources that override stale training data.

**"We can't get original research published — we don't have enough customers or data for a credible report."**

Early-stage companies can still produce AI-citation-worthy research. Fix: Run a third-party survey through SurveyMonkey Audience, Pollfish, or UserInterviews targeting your ICP (100-200 respondents is sufficient for credibility). The research doesn't have to be about your product — it should be about the problem your product solves. "2025 B2B Revenue Forecasting Accuracy Survey: 200 CFOs Weigh In" is credible and citable regardless of your ARR. Partner with a complementary vendor (non-competitor) to split survey costs and double your distribution reach.
