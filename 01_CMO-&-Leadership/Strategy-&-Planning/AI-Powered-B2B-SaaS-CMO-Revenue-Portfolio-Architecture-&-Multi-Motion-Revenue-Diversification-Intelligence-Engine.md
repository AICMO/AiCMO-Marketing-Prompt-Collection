## Overview
Designs a diversified portfolio of revenue-generating GTM motions (self-serve PLG, sales-assisted PLG, community-led, partner-led, expansion marketing) that compound on each other, reduce single-channel dependence, and allow a CMO to own measurable ARR contribution across new logo, expansion, and retention. Use this when your pipeline is too dependent on one motion, when you need to build the board case for expanded CMO revenue ownership, or when transitioning from a purely outbound-sourced revenue model to a multi-motion compound growth architecture.

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: CRO pushes back on CMO claiming revenue ownership for motions that AEs close**
Solution: Reframe ownership as "origination credit" not "full credit." Present a tiered model: CMO owns origination data (how the opportunity entered the system), CRO owns conversion data (how it closed). Propose a shared Revenue Attribution Committee meeting monthly to review disputed attributions. Show peer benchmarks where CMO + CRO co-own pipeline reporting — this is standard practice at companies like Gong, Drift, and Outreach.

**Problem: Self-serve / PLG motion underperforms because product isn't built for self-serve**
Solution: Do not force PLG if product requires configuration, implementation, or security review. Instead, activate a PQL-assisted motion: free trial or limited sandbox that feeds high-intent signals to AEs rather than converting without sales. This captures intent signal value without requiring true self-serve product infrastructure. Redirect PLG budget to partner and expansion motions that have higher readiness scores.

**Problem: Board asks "why are we splitting focus instead of doubling down on what works?"**
Solution: Reframe multi-motion as risk reduction, not distraction. Build the concentration risk case: show that 91% of ARR from a single motion means a 30% SDR team disruption causes an existential pipeline crisis. Present competitor analysis showing how well-funded competitors are already running 3-4 motions simultaneously. Use historical SaaS data: companies with ≥3 revenue motions at Series C have 40% higher median ARR multiples at exit. Offer a milestone-based approach: unlock new motion budget only when it hits initial ROI targets.
