Deploys a complete AI-powered marketing content architecture for your Annual (or H1/H2) Sales Kickoff — covering the updated company narrative, competitive intelligence refresh, product messaging deck, campaign roadmap presentation, and go-to-market playbook that arms every rep to win in the new period. ## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Reps say "the SKO content was great but I've forgotten it by week 3."**
Solution: Don't treat SKO as a one-time event.
