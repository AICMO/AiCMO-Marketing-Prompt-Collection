## Integration Tips

- **HubSpot/Salesforce:** Create deal-stage-based enrollment workflows that auto-trigger the correct marketing sequence when CRM stage updates. Use lifecycle stage + deal age fields to fire "stalled deal" interventions at Day 14.
- **Gong/Chorus:** Extract top stall reasons automatically from call recordings — pipe this into monthly prompt refresh so your compression interventions stay current with actual buyer objections.
- **6sense/Demandbase:** Identify buying committee members not yet in CRM — serve them persona-specific pre-sell content during the anonymous research phase.
- **Highspot/Seismic:** House the Champion Kit and security pack in your sales enablement platform with usage analytics — track which assets reps actually share and correlate to deal velocity.
- **Outreach/Salesloft:** Build marketing-led nurture sequences that complement SDR cadences during evaluation — coordinate timing to avoid buyer fatigue while maintaining presence.
- **Slack/CRM alerts:** When deal moves to "Approval" stage, auto-notify marketing ops to manually deploy the CFO pre-sell package if not already engaged — close the automation gap.

## Troubleshooting

**Problem:** Sales team isn't flagging deals for marketing intervention at the right stages.
**Solution:** Create a simple "Marketing Assist" button in Salesforce/HubSpot that reps click when a deal stalls — this triggers the appropriate intervention package automatically. Remove the judgment call from reps by making it one click. Also review pipeline in weekly marketing-sales sync to manually catch stalled deals marketing hasn't yet touched.

**Problem:** Champion Kit is built but champion isn't using it — they forward something generic to their CFO instead.
**Solution:** Redesign the kit to be a single email template with the CFO one-pager pre-attached — reduce friction to zero. Follow up with champion directly (marketing can send a personalized email via Outreach sequence) 48 hours after sharing: "Here's a version specifically for [CFO Name] if your company focuses on [their industry]." Personalization at point of use drives adoption.

**Problem:** Sales cycle compression metrics show no improvement after 60 days.
**Solution:** Run a "stall audit" — pull every deal that moved backward or stalled in the last quarter and manually review what marketing interventions were (or weren't) used. Most common finding: the right assets exist but aren't being triggered because the CRM workflow was configured incorrectly. Recheck enrollment criteria, and shadow 3-5 live deals as a control group to verify the sequence is actually executing as designed.
