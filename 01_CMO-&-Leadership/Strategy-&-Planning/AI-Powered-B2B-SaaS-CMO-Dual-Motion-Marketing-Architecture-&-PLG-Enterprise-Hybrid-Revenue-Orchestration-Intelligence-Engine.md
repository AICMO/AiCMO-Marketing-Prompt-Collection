## Overview
This prompt engineers a complete CMO-level architecture for companies running both a product-led growth (PLG) self-serve motion and an enterprise sales-led motion simultaneously — the most complex and high-stakes GTM design challenge in B2B SaaS. Use it when your company has both free/freemium users and a direct enterprise sales team, when your two motions are cannibalizing each other or creating internal conflict, or when your board is asking for unified revenue accountability across self-serve and sales-led channels.

## Troubleshooting

**Problem:** PQL thresholds are triggering too early — AEs are contacting users who aren't actually enterprise-ready, damaging the self-serve experience and frustrating the sales team.
**Solution:** Run a PQL Calibration Audit: pull all PQL-sourced opportunities from the last 6 months and calculate the close rate and ACV by PQL tier. If Tier 1 PQL close rate is below 15%, your threshold is too low — increase the required point total or add a mandatory Enterprise Feature Signal requirement. Also add a "PQL Age Rule": a contact must sustain Tier 1 status for ≥ 3 consecutive days before triggering an AE alert (eliminates one-day spikes from users exploring features without intent).

**Problem:** Enterprise AEs resist the dual-motion architecture because they feel they're being restricted from accessing free user accounts they could close quickly.
**Solution:** Show them the data. Pull the close rate and ACV of deals where an AE contacted a free user via cold outreach vs. deals where the user was a confirmed PQL before AE contact. In virtually every company, PQL-sourced deals close at 2–4x higher rates and at 20–40% higher ACV. Present this in the next sales team meeting — not as a policy lecture, but as a "here's how to hit quota faster" data story. Also give AEs visibility into the PQL queue so they see accounts moving toward readiness — this creates patience because they can see the pipeline building.

**Problem:** The board doesn't accept the attribution model because self-serve revenue and enterprise revenue are reported in separate systems and don't add up cleanly.
**Solution:** Implement a "Revenue Origin" field on every closed-won opportunity that is set at the time of close — not retroactively and not by algorithm. The values are: PLG-Self-Serve-Only / PLG-to-Enterprise-Conversion / Enterprise-Direct / Enterprise-Partner. This manually-assigned field becomes the board's reporting standard. Yes, it requires human judgment at deal close, but it is auditable, contestable, and comprehensible to a non-technical board. Layer on an AI-suggested origin classification that the AE confirms or overrides in Salesforce — this gives you both automation efficiency and human accountability.
