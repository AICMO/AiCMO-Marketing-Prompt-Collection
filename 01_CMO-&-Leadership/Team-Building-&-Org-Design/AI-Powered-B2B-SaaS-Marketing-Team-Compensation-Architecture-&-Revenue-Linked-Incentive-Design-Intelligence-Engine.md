## Integration Tips

- **HRIS (BambooHR, Rippling, Workday):** Export the final pay bands table directly into your HRIS compensation module to automate range flagging on offers and annual reviews. Map band midpoints as your "compa-ratio" anchors.
- **CRM + Marketing Attribution (Salesforce, HubSpot):** For variable pay tied to pipeline-sourced metrics, connect your MAP attribution model to a Salesforce report filtered by marketing source. Schedule this report to auto-generate on the first business day of each month so pipeline measurement is automated, not manually compiled.
- **Spreadsheet/Notion:** Build a compensation calculator in Google Sheets or Notion database where managers can input a candidate's expected performance level and instantly see projected OTE — useful during offer negotiations to explain total comp value transparently.
- **Equity Management (Carta, Pulley):** When designing equity refresh grants as part of this architecture, use Carta's scenario modeling to show employees what their equity is worth at 3x, 5x, and 10x ARR multiples — this dramatically increases perceived equity value and aids retention.
- **Finance/Budget (Adaptive Insights, Pigment):** Input final pay bands into your headcount planning model so Finance can auto-flag when approved headcount requests fall outside band, requiring exception approval before offer stage.
- **Slack/Teams Workflow:** Automate the exception approval workflow using a Slack bot (e.g., via Zapier or native Workflow Builder) — compensation exceptions above band require sequential approvals from CMO → CFO → CEO with auto-reminders and a 48-hour SLA.

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Finance rejects the variable pay design because pipeline attribution between marketing and sales is disputed.**
Solution: This is the #1 blocker to marketing variable pay. Before presenting to finance, pre-align with your CRO on a formal attribution framework — define what counts as "marketing-sourced" in your CRM with written rules, and commit to a monthly Joint Revenue Committee (CMO + CRO + Ops) that owns attribution disputes with a 5-business-day resolution SLA. Present this governance model alongside the comp plan so finance sees the measurement system, not just the targets.

**Problem: The AI produces pay bands that are too high for your company's actual budget constraints.**
Solution: Add explicit budget constraints in the prompt (total marketing comp budget, headcount count, and company ARR). Also specify your market positioning intent ("50th percentile, not 75th") and tell the AI which roles are non-negotiable for competitive pay vs. which have more flexibility. Demand gen roles typically require market-competitive OTE; brand and event roles have more flexible band positioning.

**Problem: Some marketing team members push back that variable pay creates unhealthy competition between channels.**
Solution: This is a legitimate concern — the prompt is designed to address it via the Team Pool mechanic (10-20% of variable assessed by manager on collaboration). Emphasize this component during rollout. Also consider a company-wide performance gate (company must hit 80%+ of ARR target before any individual variable pays) — this creates shared fate and dramatically reduces channel-level sandbagging because everyone's payout is linked to overall company performance.
