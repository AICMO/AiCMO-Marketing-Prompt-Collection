## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Team pushback on AI upskilling — "this will replace our jobs"**
Fix: Reframe immediately with data. Show that AI-fluent marketers at peer companies earn 20-30% more and are more promotion-eligible. Publish an internal policy: "We use AI to eliminate repetitive work so humans focus on strategy and judgment. No role is being eliminated — all roles are being upgraded." Tie upskilling completion to Q-end bonuses.

**Problem: Upskilling plan loses momentum after Week 3**
Fix: This is a structural failure, not a motivation failure. The common cause: learning is treated as optional/additive to full workloads. Fix by formally blocking 3-4 hours/week per person as "protected learning time" in calendars — non-negotiable during sprints. Make the VP/CMO model the behavior by sharing their own AI learning wins in team standups.

**Problem: Skills assessment scores are too subjective / self-reported scores are inflated**
Fix: Replace self-assessment with demonstrated proof. For prompt engineering: ask each team member to produce a specific output (e.g., a 5-email nurture sequence using Claude) and evaluate output quality against defined rubric. For SQL: give a standardized query challenge against a test dataset. Calibrate scores against output quality, not self-perception.
