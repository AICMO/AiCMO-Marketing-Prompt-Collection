## Integration Tips

- **Notion/Confluence:** Build your contractor network roster and scorecards in Notion with a database per specialist category. Link to each contractor's portfolio, rate card, and performance history.
- **HubSpot/Salesforce:** Create a "Contractor" contact type in your CRM to track engagement history, deliverable volume, and performance ratings — the same way you track customer health.
- **Zapier/Make:** Automate contractor onboarding workflows — trigger NDA signature request via DocuSign when a new contractor record is created, then auto-provision access to Notion brand assets, kick off Loom brand orientation video sequence.
- **Slack:** Create a private Slack workspace channel structure for your active contractor network: `#briefs-active`, `#reviews-pending`, `#brand-assets`, `#contractor-questions`. Keep it lightweight — contractors shouldn't need to navigate your internal Slack.
- **Linear/Asana/Monday:** Use your project management tool's "External" workspace (most enterprise plans support this) to give contractors visibility into just their active projects and deliverable deadlines without seeing your entire roadmap.
- **Airtable:** Best tool for a Contractor Network Roster — build a relational database linking contractors to: role categories, active engagements, past deliverables with ratings, rate history, and availability status. Run this as your master network intelligence layer.
- **Google Sheets (Quick Start):** If you're just getting started, a simple Google Sheet with tabs per specialist category and a shared "Status" tab showing all active contractors + their current deliverables is sufficient for teams with <10 active contractors.

## Troubleshooting

**Problem:** Contractors keep missing the brand voice, requiring extensive revision rounds.
**Solution:** The brief is almost always the root cause, not the contractor. Audit your brief quality first — add three concrete "tone examples" from approved content directly in the brief body. Second, use the 48-hour onboarding calibration exercise to identify voice fit before assigning high-stakes work. If a contractor has done 3+ engagements and still misses voice, they're not the right fit regardless of their other skills.

**Problem:** You can't activate a specialist fast enough when a campaign launches and you realize you need help.
**Solution:** This is a cold-bench problem. Build a pre-vetted "warm bench" across your top 5 specialist categories — at least 2 contractors per category who have completed a paid test project and are on a pre-negotiated rate card. The test project ($300-800 investment per contractor) eliminates the onboarding risk. When demand spikes, you ping warm bench first; you should have someone activated within 24 hours.

**Problem:** A contractor is producing good work but they're slowly getting more expensive (scope creep, hourly bills expanding).
**Solution:** This is a misaligned engagement structure problem. Switch from hourly to milestone/deliverable-based pricing. Define: "You'll produce X deliverables per month at $Y total. Additional deliverables are billed at $Z each." Scope creep disappears when the incentive is aligned — the contractor benefits from being efficient, not billing more hours. Revisit this structure quarterly.
