## Troubleshooting

**Problem: A top-10 customer account received the rebrand announcement from a journalist before your CS team could reach them.**

Resolution: Activate the executive escalation protocol immediately. The CEO or CCO should call the account within 2 hours with a personal apology and a clear explanation of the sequence failure. Offer a concession (e.g., dedicated executive business review, roadmap preview, contract credit for the inconvenience). Document the failure and update the embargo checklist to add an explicit "no VIP customer should see this before personal contact" gate with named accountability.

**Problem: Google organic traffic dropped 35% in week 2 post-launch, significantly above the 20% tolerance threshold.**

Resolution: First, audit all 301 redirects with Screaming Frog — confirm every priority URL is redirecting correctly with a 301 (not 302 or meta refresh). Submit the new sitemap to Search Console immediately if not already done. Check for any crawl errors in Search Console. Temporarily increase brand PPC budget by 30% to compensate for organic loss during recovery. If the domain migration was done correctly, expect recovery within 8–12 weeks; if errors are found, correct them within 48 hours and submit for re-crawl.

**Problem: A competitor published a blog post titled "DataSync is now Veloxa — what it means for customers" framing the rebrand as a sign of struggling growth.**

Resolution: Do not respond publicly in a way that amplifies the competitor's post. Instead: (1) publish your own authoritative "Why We Rebranded" content on your blog within 24 hours of the competitor post, optimized for "Company Name rebrand" queries so it outranks the competitor piece; (2) brief your top 3 analysts with the rebuttal narrative within 48 hours; (3) alert your sales team with a specific competitive talk track for if prospects bring up the competitor's framing; (4) do NOT engage the competitor directly on social media.
