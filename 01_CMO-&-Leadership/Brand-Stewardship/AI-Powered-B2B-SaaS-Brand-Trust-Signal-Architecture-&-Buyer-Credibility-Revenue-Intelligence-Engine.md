## Troubleshooting

**Problem: "Buyers still express skepticism even after we've shared case studies and reviews"**

Solution: Shift from *volume* to *specificity*. Generic case studies ("Company X saved time and reduced costs") have near-zero trust impact in 2026. The fix: rewrite every case study to include exact metrics (e.g., "$340K in contract risk identified in first 90 days, preventing one material breach scenario"), named customer contacts with visible LinkedIn profiles, and a direct customer quote that addresses the specific concern your buyer raised. Specific, verifiable, human-attributed claims bypass AI-content skepticism where generic claims fail.

**Problem: "Customers are willing to be references but we can't get them to record videos or write case studies — the process is too slow"**

Solution: Remove all friction from customer participation. Deploy a three-step rapid capture process: (1) Send a Typeform with 5 pre-written questions that take 3 minutes to answer — auto-format into a draft case study; (2) Schedule a 15-minute Zoom with an AI transcription tool (Otter.ai, Fireflies) — convert transcript into draft with customer's own language; (3) Offer customers a branded "Customer Results Report" they can share internally as a perk for participating. The goal is that participating takes under 20 minutes of customer time and produces content that makes them look good internally.

**Problem: "Our trust signals aren't showing up when buyers do independent research (AI search, Reddit, peer networks)"**

Solution: Trust signals need distribution beyond your own website. A three-channel strategy: (1) **AI Search presence** — ensure your case study metrics and customer quotes are indexed and cited by training AI search systems via structured data markup, authoritative third-party mentions (analyst reports, media), and consistent terminology across platforms; (2) **Dark Social distribution** — ask customer champions to share their results on LinkedIn with specific metrics (offer to ghostwrite their post); (3) **Peer community seeding** — have your team (transparently disclosed) answer questions in relevant Slack communities, Reddit threads, and LinkedIn groups with links to your authentic proof — not promotional content, but genuine answers that reference real customer outcomes.
