## Overview
This prompt builds a complete AI-powered system for B2B SaaS CMOs to select, onboard, manage, and evaluate external marketing resources — agencies, freelancers, and contractors — with the rigor of an in-house performance management system. Use it when you're launching an agency RFP, inheriting a cluttered agency roster, evaluating whether to bring a function in-house, or when your current agency relationships are producing outputs that don't move revenue.

## Integration Tips

- **HubSpot / Salesforce**: Create a custom "Agency Attribution" field on campaign objects. Tag every campaign created or influenced by an external agency. Run a monthly report: `Campaign Source = [Agency Name] → Contacts Created → MQLs → Pipeline → Revenue`. This is your agency ROI proof.
- **Asana / Monday.com / Notion**: Build an agency project template that auto-creates the onboarding checklist, monthly scorecard task, and QBR prep task when a new agency is added to the workspace.
- **Slack**: Create a dedicated `#agency-[name]` channel for every agency relationship. Pipe automated weekly performance summaries (from your analytics stack) into the channel so both sides see numbers without a meeting.
- **Google Looker Studio / Tableau**: Build an Agency Performance Dashboard that pulls from your CRM, paid media platforms, and GA4. Auto-email to CMO and agency lead on the 1st of every month. Agencies who see their own scorecards in real time self-correct faster than agencies who wait for a monthly review meeting.
- **Zapier / Make.com**: Automate brief distribution — when a new campaign brief is created in Notion and marked "Ready for Agency," auto-send the brief to the agency's Asana project and notify their account lead via Slack. Eliminate email chains.

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Agency produces great work in month 1, quality degrades by month 3.**
Cause: Account lead switches. The senior person who pitched you is managing 12 other accounts. You got the A-team for the RFP, you get the B-team for execution.
Fix: In every agency contract, specify by name the account lead and senior strategist assigned to your account. Include a clause that any staff change requires 30 days notice and CMO approval. If they swap without notice, it's a contract breach and triggers a fee reduction.

**Problem: Agency blames internal stakeholders for poor results ("we can't do good work because approvals take 3 weeks").**
Cause: Approval workflow wasn't defined upfront. Both true and used as cover.
Fix: Implement the tiered approval workflow in the brief template above. Define that round 1 feedback is due within 48 business hours. If your team misses the SLA, the agency's deadline extends by the same number of days — and so does their contract delivery date, not yours.

**Problem: Two agencies both claiming credit for the same pipeline — PR agency says "we got the coverage that drove the demo," paid media agency says "we drove the click that converted."**
Cause: No unified attribution model and agencies optimize for self-preservation metrics.
Fix: Define in each agency contract exactly one primary metric they own and are accountable for — paid media owns cost-per-MQL, PR owns media placements + share of voice. Pipeline attribution lives in your CRM and is owned by RevOps, not any agency. Agencies can reference it as context but cannot claim it as their primary proof of value.
