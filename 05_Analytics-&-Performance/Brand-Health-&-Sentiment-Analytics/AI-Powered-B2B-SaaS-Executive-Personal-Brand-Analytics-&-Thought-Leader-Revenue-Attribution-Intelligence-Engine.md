## Overview

This engine builds a continuous, AI-automated measurement system that tracks the business impact of individual executive and founder personal brands — translating LinkedIn follower growth, content engagement, speaking reach, podcast appearances, and media mentions into pipeline influenced, revenue attributed, and recruiting lift. Use it when your CMO, CEO, or VP-level executives are investing in thought leadership and you need to prove (or disprove) the revenue ROI of that investment.

## MODULE 1: AUDIENCE INTELLIGENCE & ICP QUALITY SCORING

Design an AI-powered audience analysis system:

b) FOLLOWER ACQUISITION QUALITY ANALYSIS: For each content piece that drove >200 new followers, analyze the new followers' ICP match. Which content formats, topics, and hooks attract the highest-ICP followers vs. vanity followers (wrong audience)?

c) ENGAGEMENT QUALITY RATIO: Track not just engagement rate but ICP Engagement Rate = (comments + saves from ICP-matched accounts) / total reach. A post with 50 ICP comments beats one with 1,000 likes from non-buyers.

d) AUDIENCE GROWTH VELOCITY vs. QUALITY CURVE: Plot follower growth rate against ICP match rate over time. Identify inflection points where quality degraded while quantity grew (common when content goes viral with wrong audience).

## Integration Tips

- **LinkedIn Analytics:** Export monthly demographic data (follower job titles, industries, company sizes) to build ICP match score. LinkedIn Analytics API provides programmatic access for enterprise accounts.
- **Salesforce:** Create a custom "Executive Brand Touch" field on the Lead/Contact object. Train SDRs and AEs to update this field during discovery calls with values like "LinkedIn Follower," "Event Attendee," "Podcast Listener," or "None Known."
- **HubSpot:** Use contact properties and lifecycle stage triggers to tag contacts who converted via executive-content UTM links. Build a dedicated "Executive Brand" pipeline source in your attribution reporting.
- **Mention.com / Talkwalker:** Set up keyword alerts for executive name, company name, and core topic keywords. Automatically log media mentions into a shared Notion or Google Sheet that the AR team reviews weekly.
- **Typeform / Qualtrics:** Build the self-reported attribution survey at demo booking (post-form submission redirect) and in the first customer success call. Sync responses to Salesforce via Zapier.
- **6sense / Bombora:** Cross-reference your executive's LinkedIn follower export (available via LinkedIn recruiter or third-party tools like PhantomBuster) against your intent data account list. Identify accounts showing buying intent where employees follow your executive.
- **Google Analytics 4:** Set up UTM parameters at the executive-level (utm_source=linkedin, utm_medium=executive_brand, utm_campaign=ceo_sarah_chen, utm_content=[post-id]) for all content with CTAs. Track goal completions and revenue attribution in GA4's attribution reports.
- **Notion / Airtable:** Build a centralized content performance database where each post is logged with ICP follower growth attributed, ICP engagement count, and any pipeline self-reports. Review this in weekly executive brand check-ins.

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Self-reported attribution surveys get very low completion rates (< 20%).**
*Solution:* Trigger the survey immediately after demo booking confirmation (while motivation is high) rather than sending it as a separate email. Keep it to 2 questions max. Use Calendly or ChiliPiper post-booking redirect pages to embed the survey inline. Offer a small incentive ($10 Starbucks gift card) for completion if legal allows.

**Problem: Sales team isn't capturing executive brand touchpoints in CRM — field is always empty.**
*Solution:* Add the executive brand touchpoint question to the required discovery call script with a specific phrase: "Quick question — in your research before this call, did you come across any of [Executive Name]'s content on LinkedIn or elsewhere?" Make the CRM field required for deal stage advancement. Share a monthly "Executive Brand Pipeline" report with the team to make attribution visible and meaningful to reps.

**Problem: LinkedIn follower demographics show a growing mismatch — wrong audience attracted by viral content.**
*Solution:* This is the classic "vanity vs. value" trap. When non-ICP content goes viral (e.g., a motivational post), it inflates follower count but degrades ICP match rate. Create a "content guardrails" policy: at least 70% of posts must be tied to a core topic pillar aligned with your buyer's professional interests. Viral experiments are acceptable for 30% of content but must be evaluated against ICP follower quality impact, not just reach.
