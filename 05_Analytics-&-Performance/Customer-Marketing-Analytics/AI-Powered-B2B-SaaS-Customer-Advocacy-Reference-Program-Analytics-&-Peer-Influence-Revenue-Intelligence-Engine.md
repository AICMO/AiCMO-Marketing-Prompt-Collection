## Integration Tips

**Salesforce / HubSpot CRM:**
- Tag opportunities with `Reference_Call_Used = TRUE/FALSE` and `Case_Study_Sent = TRUE/FALSE` fields; run win rate and cycle time reports by these fields quarterly
- Use opportunity custom fields to capture "peer review cited by buyer" during discovery/qualification to build advocacy influence data over time

**Reference Management (ReferenceEdge, Influitive, Orca):**
- Export reference call logs with outcome data; join to CRM opportunity outcome for win rate calculation
- Set automated burnout alerts when any advocate exceeds 3 reference calls in a 60-day window

**G2 / Gartner Peer Insights:**
- Connect G2 Buyer Intent data to your ABM platform (6sense, Demandbase) so accounts actively researching your category on G2 automatically enter intent-triggered outbound sequences
- Use G2's API or export to track review velocity weekly and alert when competitors gain >5 reviews in 7 days

**Content Analytics (Highspot, Seismic, Notion, Google Analytics):**
- Instrument case study pages with UTM parameters to track sales-sent links separately from organic web traffic
- Build a deal room engagement report: case studies opened in Highspot/Seismic → linked to opportunity → joined with won/lost outcome

**Notion / Google Sheets Advocacy Dashboard:**
- Build a live advocate health tracker: advocate name, company, ICP segment, calls YTD, last activity date, burnout risk flag, and priority segments they cover

**Zapier / Make Automation:**
- Trigger: customer hits 90-day product adoption milestone → Zap sends personalized G2 review request email with individual tracking link → log response in CRM contact record
- Trigger: deal marked Closed Won → Zap starts case study recruitment sequence (3-email, 30-day drip) to champion contact

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: "We don't track whether a reference call influenced a deal in our CRM."**
*Fix:* Add a retroactive survey to recently closed-won and closed-lost deals (10-question form, 5 minutes): "Did you speak with a current customer during your evaluation?" This creates 90 days of validated influence data within one quarter. Meanwhile, create a Salesforce checkbox field `Reference_Call_Completed` and train AEs to check it — operationally, this is a 2-second step that unlocks 12 months of ROI data going forward.

**Problem: "Our win rate with reference calls is high, but we can't tell if it's causation vs. selection bias (already-warm deals get references)."**
*Fix:* Segment reference-supported deals by pipeline stage at time of reference request. If references are only used at Proposal/Negotiation (Stage 4–5), selection bias is likely strong. To test incrementality, run a 90-day experiment: randomly assign one cohort of Stage 3 deals (Validation) to receive proactive reference outreach vs. waiting for the prospect to ask. Compare outcomes. This is the closest B2B analog to an A/B test without withholding service.

**Problem: "Our advocates keep saying yes to reference calls but sound unenthusiastic, and sales reps say call quality is declining."**
*Fix:* Institute mandatory advocate "refresh" conversations (15-min CS check-in before any reference call) to re-brief the advocate on the prospect's use case and what the buyer most needs to hear. Also cap reference calls at 2 per advocate per 45 days unless the advocate proactively opts in for more. Quality drops when advocates feel like on-call unpaid sales reps rather than valued community members.
