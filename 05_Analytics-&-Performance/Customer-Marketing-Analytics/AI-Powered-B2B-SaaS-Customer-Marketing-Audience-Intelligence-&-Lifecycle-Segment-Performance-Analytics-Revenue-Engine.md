## Overview
Transforms your customer base from a single undifferentiated audience into precisely segmented, marketing-actionable cohorts — revealing which customers are expansion-ready, which need health rescue, which will make the best advocates, and which are invisible to your marketing programs entirely. Use this monthly to sharpen your customer marketing audience strategy, quarterly to reallocate program investment toward the highest-ROI segments, and annually to demonstrate how audience intelligence drives NRR improvement and churn reduction.

### Analysis Framework

**Step 1: Segment Taxonomy Construction**

Build a 7-segment taxonomy using a combination of:
- **Behavioral axis:** Product engagement depth (high/medium/low/none) × Marketing engagement (active/passive/dark)
- **Revenue axis:** Expansion trajectory (growing/stable/declining) × ACV tier
- **Advocacy axis:** NPS + reference program participation + champion presence

Name each segment with a practitioner-friendly label (e.g., "Champions," "Sleepers," "Expansion-Ready," "At-Risk Actives," "Dark Core," etc.) and specify:
- Quantified size (% of base, total ARR in segment)
- Defining characteristics (3-4 data signals that place a customer in this segment)
- Primary risk or opportunity
- Marketing motion priority (1=immediate action, 5=maintenance mode)

**Step 2: ROI Potential Scoring by Segment**

For each segment, calculate:
- **Expansion Revenue Potential:** Based on seat utilization, CSM pipeline, and cross-sell signals — estimate the dollar value of expansion revenue accessible if marketing programs fully activated this segment
- **Churn Revenue at Risk:** Based on health scores and renewal proximity — estimate ARR at risk if marketing does not intervene
- **Advocacy Value:** Based on NPS scores and reference program gaps — estimate the pipeline value of turning X additional accounts into active advocates (reference calls, case studies, referrals)
- **Marketing ROI Multiplier:** Expected return per $1 of customer marketing spend invested in this segment, accounting for response rate and deal value

**Step 3: Program-to-Segment Matching Matrix**

Map the following 10 customer marketing program types to their highest-impact segments:
1. Onboarding email sequences (milestone-triggered)
2. Monthly product newsletter
3. Feature announcement campaigns
4. Executive Business Reviews (EBR) / QBR marketing support
5. User community (online forum/Slack group)
6. User conference / regional roadshow invitation
7. Upsell/cross-sell campaign sequence
8. Renewal reminder and risk mitigation campaign
9. NPS follow-up and promoter activation program
10. Reference program and advocacy recruitment

For each program × segment intersection, specify:
- Fit score (High/Medium/Low/Not Applicable)
- Expected conversion rate based on segment engagement baseline

**Step 4: Dark Customer Rescue Plan**

## Integration Tips

**CRM (Salesforce / HubSpot):**
- Build the 7-segment taxonomy as a custom field on the Account object; populate via automation rules based on health score, lifecycle stage, and marketing engagement data
- Create segment-specific report folders so CSMs and customer marketing can pull accounts for each segment in real-time
- Configure Salesforce/HubSpot workflows to automatically move accounts between segments as their behavioral signals change (e.g., a Green account whose last login was >30 days ago drops to "Engagement Risk" segment)
- Sync segment data to your MAP (Marketo/HubSpot) to enable segment-based list suppression and targeting

**Customer Success Platform (Gainsight / Totango / ChurnZero):**
- Pull health scores, product usage data, CSM notes, and expansion flags directly into the segmentation model
- Create automated Calls-to-Action (CTAs) in CS platform that trigger customer marketing program enrollment when an account enters or exits a segment
- Share segment data with CSMs via dashboard so they understand which marketing programs their accounts are currently in and can coordinate outreach

**Marketing Automation Platform (Marketo / HubSpot / Pardot):**
- Create a customer-only marketing database partitioned by segment
- Build Smart Lists or Active Lists that dynamically pull accounts into each segment based on real-time behavioral data
- Set up trigger-based enrollment rules so accounts automatically enter the right program sequence when they qualify for a segment

**Product Analytics (Pendo / Amplitude / Mixpanel / Gainsight PX):**
- Feed product usage signals (feature adoption, login frequency, DAU/licensed seat ratio) into the segmentation model via API or CSV export
- Use in-product messaging (tooltips, banners, modals) as a channel for reaching marketing-dark accounts who are still logging in but not responding to email
- Create behavioral cohorts in product analytics that map to your customer marketing segments for deeper usage pattern analysis

**Data Warehouse / BI (Snowflake + Tableau / Looker / Domo):**
- Build a unified customer marketing intelligence dashboard that combines CRM data, CS platform signals, product usage, and marketing engagement into a single segmentation view
- Automate weekly segment refresh so the customer marketing team always has current data without manual pulls
- Create an executive NRR bridge report that shows how customer marketing segment performance is contributing to or detracting from the NRR target

**Zapier / Make (Workflow Automation):**
- Automate alert to customer marketing when an account shifts from Green to Yellow health (triggers at-risk marketing enrollment)
- Trigger advocacy recruitment email sequence automatically when a new NPS Promoter response is recorded
- Alert account CSM in Slack when their account transitions to a high-priority segment (Expansion-Ready, At-Risk) so marketing and CS can coordinate outreach timing

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Segmentation data is incomplete — many accounts are unscored or missing key signals.**
Solution: Prioritize building data completeness for your highest-ACV segments first. For accounts >$50K ACV with missing health scores, mandate that CSMs complete a quarterly health assessment. For email engagement gaps, run a "customer contact audit" to find valid contacts via LinkedIn Sales Navigator or ZoomInfo and update CRM records. Accept 70% data coverage as a workable minimum for initial segmentation — don't wait for perfect data to start.

**Problem: Marketing and CS are operating in silos — segmentation insights aren't being acted on.**
Solution: Establish a bi-weekly customer marketing council with CS ops, and create a shared Slack channel where segment shifts (accounts moving into At-Risk or Expansion-Ready) trigger automatic alerts with a recommended marketing action. Formalize a joint SLA: marketing agrees to enroll at-risk accounts in a rescue sequence within 48 hours of CS flagging them; CS agrees to include marketing engagement data in their renewal risk assessments.

**Problem: The advocacy funnel has very low conversion from Satisfied → Active Advocate, despite good NPS scores.**
Solution: The most common cause is that satisfied customers are never asked — NPS is collected passively and nothing happens with Promoter responses. Fix by implementing a 48-hour NPS Promoter trigger: immediately after a 9-10 score is recorded, the customer receives a short, specific email offering 3 concrete low-friction advocacy options (e.g., "Would you be willing to take a 15-minute reference call next month?" with a one-click "Yes, interested" button). Track response rate weekly and A/B test the framing. Even a 25% response rate to this trigger can double the size of your active advocate pool within two quarters.
