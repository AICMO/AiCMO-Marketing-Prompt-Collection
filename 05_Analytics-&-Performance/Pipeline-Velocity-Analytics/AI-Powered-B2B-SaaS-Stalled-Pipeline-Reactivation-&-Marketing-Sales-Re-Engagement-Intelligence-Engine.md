## Troubleshooting

**Problem: The prompt returns generic reactivation advice rather than deal-specific sequences.**
Fix: Provide richer deal context — include the exact stage name, the specific last activity (e.g., "champion said pricing is 15% above budget"), and the economic buyer's title. The more specificity in the input, the more specific the touch calendar and message angles in the output. The example row format in the Advanced Version is the minimum data required for deal-specific analysis.

**Problem: The 21-day sequence ignores channels you don't actually have access to (e.g., executive events, 6sense).**
Fix: Add a constraint at the top of the Advanced Version: "We do NOT have access to the following: [list channels]. Build the sequence using only: [your available channels]." The prompt will adapt the re-engagement plan to your available toolkit without gaps in the sequence.

**Problem: The reactivation prioritization doesn't match gut instinct on which deals to pursue.**
Fix: The model weights ARR × probability × urgency equally by default. To adjust weighting, add: "Weight reactivation probability 2x more heavily than ARR — we need quick wins this quarter more than large deals." Alternatively, manually override the priority ranking and ask the prompt to justify why your instinct-pick should or shouldn't be ranked higher based on the archetype analysis.
