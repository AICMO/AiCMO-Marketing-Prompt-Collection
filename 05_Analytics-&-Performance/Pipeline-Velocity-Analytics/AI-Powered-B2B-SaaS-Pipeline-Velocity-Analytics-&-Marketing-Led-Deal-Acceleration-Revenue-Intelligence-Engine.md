## Overview
Deploys a comprehensive pipeline velocity analytics system that measures how fast deals move through every stage of the revenue funnel, identifies what's causing stalls, and prescribes the specific marketing interventions — content, programs, signals, and enablement — that accelerate deal closure. Use this when marketing needs to demonstrate impact beyond pipeline sourcing, when sales cycles are lengthening and leadership wants answers, or when you need a rigorous data model that ties marketing investments to deal velocity rather than just lead volume.

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Stall risk scores are inaccurate — flagging healthy deals as at-risk (high false positive rate)**
Solution: Recalibrate signal weights using your last 12 months of closed/won and closed/lost data. Run logistic regression on each signal vs. deal outcome to get empirical weights rather than using the default weights in the model. Also check if your deal stages are too long — if "Technical Evaluation" legitimately takes 35 days for your motion, recalibrate your benchmark accordingly before scoring against it.

**Problem: Sales won't update CRM with stall reasons, making the system data-unreliable**
Solution: Reduce the ask — instead of free-text stall reason notes, create a picklist field of 5-8 standardized stall reasons that reps select in one click. Tie CRM compliance to monthly velocity review access: only reps with >75% SLA compliance get visibility into the marketing intervention queue. Have VP Sales make CRM velocity data entry a stated expectation in the weekly pipeline review call.

**Problem: Marketing intervention volume is too high — team can't fulfill requests without burning out**
Solution: Tighten the Intervene threshold from 66+ to 72+ and reserve only Escalate tier (81+) for human marketing intervention. For Intervene tier, build a self-serve content package library in Highspot or Seismic that AEs access directly without marketing involvement, reducing marketing's queue by ~60%. Also implement a hard cap: no more than 8 Tier 1 interventions per week per marketing team member, with deals prioritized by ACV × stall risk score.
