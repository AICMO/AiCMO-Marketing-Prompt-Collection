## Troubleshooting

**Problem: "We can't tell which pipeline deals were influenced by our G2 profile — the attribution is invisible."**
Solution: Add a mandatory "How did you first hear about us?" field to your demo request form with G2/Capterra as explicit options. Additionally, train BDRs and AEs to ask "Did you see our G2 reviews as part of your evaluation?" in every discovery call and log the response as a custom Salesforce field. Even a 50% response rate will give you statistically significant data within 60 days. Supplement with UTM parameters on your G2 profile CTA (use UTM source=g2 / medium=review-site / campaign=profile) to capture demo requests that click through.

**Problem: "Our review velocity is too slow — we can't generate enough reviews to move our ranking."**
Solution: The highest-converting review request moments are: (1) immediately after a customer goes live / reaches their first success milestone, (2) at the end of a QBR where the customer rated the partnership 8+/10, and (3) when a customer publishes a LinkedIn post praising your product. Build automated triggers for all three in your CSM platform (Gainsight, ChurnZero, or HubSpot). Target customers who have been with you 6–18 months — they have enough experience to write detailed reviews (which G2 weights more heavily in ranking algorithms). Aim for 3 targeted asks per week per CSM. A 30% response rate will generate significant velocity lift.

**Problem: "G2 Buyer Intent data is showing accounts, but our BDRs say the leads are cold and not converting."**
Solution: Raw G2 Buyer Intent requires intent stacking and enrichment before outreach. Layer in three additional signals before contacting: (1) LinkedIn job posting check — is this account hiring for roles that signal the problem your product solves? (2) Recent funding — have they raised in last 6 months (expansion budget)? (3) Tech stack confirmation via Clearbit/Apollo — do they use the integrations your product connects to? Accounts meeting 2+ of these criteria alongside G2 intent convert at 4–8x the rate of intent-only accounts. Build this enrichment into your intent workflow before routing to BDRs.
