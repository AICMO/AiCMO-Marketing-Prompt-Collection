This engine transforms your multi-platform review site presence (G2, Gartner Peer Insights, Capterra, TrustRadius, Software Advice) into a real-time competitive intelligence and buyer intent interception system — extracting pipeline signals from competitor reviews, benchmarking your category performance, and automating review-to-revenue attribution. ## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Review velocity is too low to move Grid position even with a campaign**
*Solution:* Don't chase volume — chase recency weighting. G2's algorithm weights recent reviews 3-4x higher than older ones. Instead of broad outreach, run a "30-day review sprint" targeting your 20 most recent NPS 9-10 customers with a direct ask from your CEO. 20 reviews in 30 days will move your Grid position faster than 100 reviews spread over a year. Also verify your profile is in the correct G2 subcategory — many companies lose Grid position because they're in a category with 500 vendors instead of the specialized subcategory where they'd be a Leader.

**Problem: Review-to-pipeline attribution shows very low numbers despite high review traffic**
*Solution:* Most review site traffic is untagged because buyers navigate directly to the platform (not through your UTM links). Supplement UTM attribution with self-reported attribution: add "How did you first hear about us?" to your demo request form and include review platforms as an explicit option. Also activate review platform lead capture features (G2's "Contact Me" button, Capterra's "Get Pricing" form) which notify you of explicit buyer intent — these are easier to attribute than organic profile views. Expect review site to be underreported in last-touch models; use multi-touch attribution or position-based attribution to capture its true influence on deal progression.

**Problem: Competitor review analysis keeps surfacing generic complaints that don't differentiate us**
*Solution:* Don't analyze average reviews — analyze the reviews from your exact ICP. Filter competitor reviews by reviewer job title (look for "Revenue Operations," "Sales Operations," "VP Sales") and company size (use LinkedIn company size filters when you can identify the reviewer's company). ICP-matched reviews will surface dramatically more specific and actionable pain points than the generic "hard to use" complaints. Also look for reviews from the last 90 days specifically — recent reviews reflect the current product experience, and the complaints that are newest are often complaints that aren't yet addressed by any vendor.
