## Integration Tips

- **HubSpot:** Create a "Review Request" workflow triggered by NPS score ≥9 AND health score ≥80 AND QBR date = 30 days ago. Use custom contact property "Review_Platform_Link" to dynamically insert G2 vs. Capterra vs. TrustRadius URLs based on buyer persona. Build a "Review Published" contact property synced via G2's HubSpot integration to suppress further requests once a review is live.
- **Gainsight / ChurnZero:** Create a CTA playbook that fires to the CSM dashboard when an account hits Tier 1 criteria (NPS 9-10, health ≥85, QBR complete, no open escalations). Include the 3-touch sequence templates and a "review published" success metric so CSMs can mark the CTA complete when the review is live.
- **Salesforce:** Add three fields to the Opportunity object: `Review_Site_Mentioned__c` (checkbox), `Review_Site_Platform__c` (picklist: G2/Capterra/TrustRadius/Other), `Review_Mentioned_Stage__c` (picklist: Awareness/Evaluation/Decision). Build a report: "Pipeline Influenced by Peer Reviews" filtered by `Review_Site_Mentioned__c = True`.
- **G2 Buyer Intent Integration:** In your G2 Advanced/Expert subscription, sync buyer intent accounts into HubSpot/Salesforce via the native integration. Build a dedicated nurture sequence for intent accounts that leads with social proof: "150+ companies like yours use [Product]" → link to G2 profile.
- **Notion / Confluence:** Build a "Review Corpus" database that auto-imports new G2/Capterra reviews weekly (via Zapier + G2 API or manual paste). Tag each review by: use case, persona, sentiment, star rating, competitor mentioned. This becomes the source-of-truth for sales enablement extraction.
- **Zapier:** Automate: New G2 review published → Slack notification to CSM + Customer Marketing → HubSpot contact property updated → Review Corpus Notion database entry created.

## Troubleshooting

**Problem:** Review conversion rate is below 5% even with NPS promoters.
**Solution:** The most common cause is timing. Review requests sent more than 21 days after the peak value moment (QBR, success milestone, onboarding completion) convert at half the rate. Audit your trigger events — if you're sending bulk email to NPS promoters who scored 9-10 six months ago, they've already moved to a different emotional state. Reset to event-triggered asks only. Also verify your G2 review link is going directly to the review form (not the product profile page) — this alone typically improves conversion by 40%.

**Problem:** Reviews are short and generic — they don't mention specific features or outcomes.
**Solution:** Add a "review brief" to your Touch 1 email: "If it helps, here are 3 things other customers mention: (1) the specific workflow or use case this solves for you, (2) a metric or result you've seen (even rough is fine), (3) what made you choose us over other options you considered." This 3-bullet prompt increases average word count from 60 to 120+ words in most programs without violating any platform authenticity policies. Never write the review for the customer or provide text they can copy — this violates G2/Capterra terms and can result in review removal.

**Problem:** Sales reps don't use review links in deals even when you ask them to.
**Solution:** Sales reps need one-click access, not a policy. Add the G2 profile link and a pre-written "social proof email" template directly into Salesforce/HubSpot as a deal-stage task that fires at Stage 3 (evaluation). The template should read like it's from the rep, include a 40-word micro-testimonial from your review corpus that matches the prospect's industry, and have a single CTA to the G2 reviews page filtered by the prospect's vertical. When you make it 10 seconds of copy-paste effort rather than 10 minutes of manual research, adoption goes from 20% to 70%+ within 30 days.
