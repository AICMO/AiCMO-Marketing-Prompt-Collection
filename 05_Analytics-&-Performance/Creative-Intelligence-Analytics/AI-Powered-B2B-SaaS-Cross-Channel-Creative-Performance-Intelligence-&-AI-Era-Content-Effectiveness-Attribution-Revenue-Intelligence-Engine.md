# MEASUREMENT FRAMEWORK

## Module 1: Creative Performance Scoring Matrix

For EACH channel × creative element dimension combination, build a Creative Performance Score (CPS):

**CPS CALCULATION METHOD:**

Step 1 — Engagement Score (ES):
Pull primary engagement metric for each content piece. Normalize to a 0-100 index where 100 = top quartile performance for that channel.
- Blog: Organic pageviews / avg session duration / scroll depth
- LinkedIn: Impressions → engagement rate → click-through rate
- Email: Open rate → click rate → reply rate
- Paid Ads: CTR → landing page conversion rate → cost per click
- Webinar: Registration rate → show rate → Q&A engagement

Step 2 — Pipeline Conversion Score (PCS):
Track each content asset through to pipeline generation:
- Assisted pipeline (content touched by lead before opportunity creation)
- Direct pipeline (content directly attributed to opportunity)
- Influenced pipeline (content engaged by active opportunities)

For each piece of content, calculate:
- Pipeline per 1,000 impressions/views
- Pipeline per $ of content production cost
- Win rate of opportunities where this content was engaged

Step 3 — Creative Performance Score (CPS):
CPS = (Engagement Score × 0.3) + (Pipeline Conversion Score × 0.5) + (Brand Consistency Score × 0.2)

Brand Consistency Score = human-reviewed score for:
- On-brand voice and tone (0-100)
- Accuracy and factual correctness (0-100)
- Competitive differentiation clarity (0-100)

**SCORING OUTPUTS:**

For each creative dimension (headline pattern, value prop angle, format, CTA, emotional tenor):
- Average CPS across all content using that element
- Standard deviation (consistency of performance)
- Sample size (number of content pieces scored)
- Top performing example (specific URL/asset)
- Worst performing example (specific URL/asset)

## Module 3: Creative Fatigue Detection System

Build a creative fatigue early warning system:

**FATIGUE SIGNALS BY CHANNEL:**

PAID ADVERTISING FATIGUE:
- Frequency threshold: Flag campaigns where average frequency >3.5 in LinkedIn, >5 in Meta
- CTR decay rate: Alert when CTR drops >20% week-over-week for 2+ consecutive weeks
- Engagement rate decay: Track 14-day rolling average vs 90-day baseline
- Audience overlap audit: % of target audience seeing same creative across campaigns
- Creative age: Flag any ad creative running for more than [X] days without refresh

ORGANIC CONTENT FATIGUE:
- Topic saturation: Track number of times each core topic has been published in 90 days
- Engagement velocity decay: New posts on same topic cluster getting <70% of baseline engagement
- Keyword cannibalization: Multiple ranking pages competing for same intent
- Audience response patterns: Comment sentiment shift from positive to neutral/critical

EMAIL LIST FATIGUE:
- Unsubscribe spike: Alert when weekly unsubscribes exceed 2x the 90-day average
- Open rate decay cohort: Track open rates for subscribers who joined in last 90, 180, 365 days
- Reply rate decline: Measure conversational engagement as a proxy for list health
- Send frequency vs. engagement cross-tab

**FATIGUE INTERVENTION PLAYBOOK:**

When fatigue is detected, automatically recommend:
1. Creative refresh timeline (immediate / 2 weeks / 30 days based on severity)
2. Replacement creative angle (which dimension to change first: headline, format, CTA, or value prop)
3. Audience segment refresh (which segments to rest vs. continue targeting)
4. Budget reallocation (where to shift spend while refreshing)

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Can't attribute pipeline to specific content assets — attribution model doesn't capture content engagement**
Solution: Implement a Content Engagement UTM architecture where every content asset has a unique UTM tag that gets recorded in the CRM at first and last touch. For blog posts, use a HubSpot or Salesforce form embed with hidden field capturing `utm_content`. For LinkedIn posts, use a short link (bit.ly or UTM.io) that carries the creative formula tag. For email, every link should include the content asset ID. Accept that 30-40% of pipeline will remain unattributed ("dark" influence) and use self-reported attribution surveys for deal-closing content.

**Problem: AI-generated content scoring poorly on engagement but the CMO wants to increase AI production to cut costs**
Solution: Don't fight on volume — fight on formula. Present the data showing that AI-generated *outcome-led* content performs at parity with human, while AI-generated *problem-led* content underperforms 40%. The answer is not "less AI" but "better AI prompts for the right formulas." Run a 60-day experiment: produce 20 AI posts exclusively using the Top 3 formulas with the optimized production templates. The CPS delta will make the case for formula-guided AI content without reducing output volume.

**Problem: Creative scoring is taking too long — team doesn't have time to score every piece of content**
Solution: Automate the Engagement Score using your analytics stack (GA4, LinkedIn, HubSpot) with a weekly data pull script. Score only Pipeline Conversion manually for the top 25% of content by engagement volume (these 25% will represent 80%+ of your pipeline anyway — Pareto principle applies). For Brand Consistency, use an AI brand checker prompt as a pre-publication gate rather than a post-publication score. This reduces the manual scoring burden to ~2 hours/month for a team producing 60-80 content pieces.
