## Overview
Analyzes your churned customer data through a marketing lens to determine which channels, campaigns, personas, and messaging attracted the customers most likely to churn — and produces a prioritized action plan to improve acquisition quality before investing another dollar in demand generation. Use quarterly to hold marketing accountable for LTV, not just pipeline, and before major budget allocation decisions.

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Channel churn rate data doesn't exist in CRM because acquisition source isn't tracked at the account level.**
*Solution:* Before running this prompt, spend 1-2 hours in CRM doing a data fix: for all accounts created in the target period, manually populate "Original Lead Source" using UTM data from the contact's first form fill, first activity, or first session (available in HubSpot Contact Timeline or Salesforce Activity History). If historical UTM data is missing, use marketing automation platform records (HubSpot or Marketo) to reconstruct the first touchpoint. Even a 70-80% complete dataset produces meaningful insights.

**Problem: Churn reasons aren't consistently logged — CS team uses free text, not structured codes.**
*Solution:* Run the prompt with whatever reason data you have, but flag the limitation explicitly. Paste the raw CS notes text (anonymized) into the prompt and ask the AI to taxonomize it using the framework provided. The AI can cluster free-text churn reasons into structured buckets well enough for the analysis to be directionally correct.

**Problem: The output shows that your best-performing channel (e.g., outbound) produces the least churn, but it's also the most expensive — leadership wants to cut it.**
*Solution:* Use Section 1's churn-adjusted LTV analysis to reframe the conversation. If outbound SDR has a $52K LTV vs. LinkedIn's $19K LTV, the real CAC comparison should be CAC-per-LTV-dollar, not just absolute CAC. A channel that costs $8K to acquire but generates $52K of retained revenue outperforms one that costs $5K and generates $19K. Build the CFO-facing version of this comparison from the Section 6 dashboard metrics.
