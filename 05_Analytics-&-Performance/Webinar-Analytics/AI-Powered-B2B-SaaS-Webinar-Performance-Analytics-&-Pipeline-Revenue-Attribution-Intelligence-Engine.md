## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Attendee data isn't matching to CRM contacts, leaving 20–35% of attendees unattributed.**
Solution: The most common cause is email address discrepancies between the webinar registration form and CRM — attendees register with personal or alternate email addresses. Fix: (1) Add a required "work email" field to all registration forms and validate email domain against known personal email providers (gmail.com, yahoo.com, etc.) at registration time. (2) Build a post-event fuzzy matching workflow using first name + last name + company domain to match unattributed attendees to CRM records within 24 hours. (3) For persistent non-matches, route to SDR for manual research — a Priority 1 WES attendee is worth 15 minutes of manual lookup time.

**Problem: WES scores are inflating for low-quality attendees who have high behavioral engagement but poor ICP fit.**
Solution: WES is designed to predict pipeline conversion, not just engagement. If low-ICP contacts are gaming the score by staying for the full session and submitting questions, recalibrate: (1) Increase the ICP fit score weight from 25% to 35% of total WES, reducing behavioral signal weight to 65%. (2) Add an ICP gate to Priority 1+2 tiers — a contact must have both WES ≥ 65 AND ICP tier 1 or 2 to qualify for Priority 1+2 sales actions. (3) After 90 days, run a retrospective: what was the actual opportunity creation rate for Priority 1+2 contacts? Recalibrate weights to maximize predictive accuracy, not just raw engagement.

**Problem: Sales team isn't following up within the WES SLA, undermining the system's value.**
Solution: WES only produces ROI if Priority 1+2 alerts trigger genuine, personalized follow-up. If compliance falls below 85%: (1) Investigate root cause — is the problem volume (too many Priority 1 alerts), quality (SDRs don't believe the alerts are accurate), or tooling (SDR can't see WES data in their daily workflow)? (2) For volume: tighten the WES threshold from 90 to 100 for Priority 1, reducing the alert count. (3) For quality: share the 90-day retrospective showing Priority 1 opportunity creation rate — if it's >60%, SDRs will prioritize. (4) For tooling: add WES score and "Top Signal" (the single most compelling behavioral signal for that contact) directly to the Salesforce task description so the SDR sees context without switching systems.
