Ensure webinar platform events create Salesforce Campaign records with campaign type = "Webinar" and campaign member status progression from "Registered" → "Attended" → "No-Show" → "Watched On-Demand"

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Webinar attendance data isn't linked to CRM opportunities, making pipeline attribution impossible.**
Solution: This is the most common gap and requires a one-time data migration before building the analytics model. First, export your full webinar attendance history from your platform (Goldcast, ON24, Zoom) going back 12 months. Match attendees to CRM contacts by email address. For each matched contact, check if an opportunity was created within 90 days post-attendance and retroactively add a Campaign Influence record. Once the historical data is patched, implement a forward-looking webhook from your webinar platform to CRM so all future attendance is logged automatically at the contact level within 24 hours.

**Problem: The topic cluster model shows contradictory results — high-registration topics have low pipeline conversion, but you're under pressure to keep running them because leadership cites registration volume as the success metric.**
Solution: Build the "Pipeline per Registrant" metric as the primary program scorecard and present it in a side-by-side comparison table at your next QBR. Show: Topic A = 800 registrants, $40K pipeline, $50/registrant. Topic B = 300 registrants, $150K pipeline, $500/registrant. Frame the decision as "we can run 2 Topic B webinars for the same production cost as 1 Topic A webinar and generate 7.5x the pipeline." This reframes the conversation from volume to efficiency and gives leadership the business case language they need to support the pivot.

**Problem: Repeat attendee scoring shows a 3x conversion premium, but sales won't act on the alerts because they don't trust webinar lead quality.**
Solution: Do not ask sales to change their behavior based on data alone. Run a 30-day pilot with one AE or SDR: give them a list of 10 accounts where 2+ stakeholders have attended 3+ webinars each. Ask them to personalize outreach referencing specific webinar topics the contacts attended. Measure their response rate and opportunity creation rate vs. their cold outbound baseline. Present the comparison at the next team meeting. Sales leaders respond to evidence from their own team's results — not marketing analytics reports.
