## Overview
Transforms a product launch from a fire-and-forget event into a precision-managed, data-driven revenue sprint by giving your team real-time performance intelligence during the critical 0-72 hour launch window. Use this when you need an AI-powered command center that monitors launch KPIs hourly, detects early signals of overperformance or failure, orchestrates rapid campaign pivots, and routes intelligence to the right teams — turning the first three days from anxious waiting into autonomous revenue optimization.

SIGNUP VELOCITY MODEL:

For {{SIGNUP_TARGET}} trial signups in 72 hours, model the expected distribution:

Typical B2B SaaS launch signup distribution curve:
- Hours 0-6 (Launch Day Surge): 35-40% of 72-hour signups expected in first 6 hours — driven by email list, owned social, and launch day paid burst
- Hours 6-24 (Day 1 Tail): 15-20% of 72-hour signups — press coverage driving search traffic, social sharing, word-of-mouth
- Hours 24-48 (Day 2): 25-30% of signups — secondary media coverage, partner amplification, social virality
- Hours 48-72 (Day 3): 10-15% of signups — long tail, retargeting, digest newsletters, secondary outreach

TRAJECTORY CALCULATION FRAMEWORK:

At any hour (H) of the launch window, calculate:
- Expected signups by hour H = {{SIGNUP_TARGET}} × (sigmoid distribution curve at H/72)
- Actual signups at hour H = [pull from CRM live count]
- Velocity index = Actual / Expected
  - Velocity Index > 1.25: ACCELERATE — you're ahead of pace, double down on what's working
  - Velocity Index 0.90-1.25: ON TRACK — maintain current plan, monitor closely
  - Velocity Index 0.75-0.89: WARNING — investigate signal source, consider message or audience adjustment
  - Velocity Index < 0.75: INTERVENTION REQUIRED — trigger rapid response protocol (Section 4)

CHANNEL ATTRIBUTION MODEL (first-touch for launch signups):

- Email → best in Hours 1-4 (open-and-click within first few hours)

- Product Hunt → peaks at Hour 3-6 on launch day if on their front page
- Dark Social / Direct → consistent throughout (word of mouth, Slack sharing)

For each signup in the first 24 hours, CRM must capture:

**SECTION 4: RAPID RESPONSE PROTOCOL LIBRARY**

Pre-built decision trees for the most common launch anomalies.

**SCENARIO 1: SIGNUP VELOCITY INDEX < 0.75 AT HOUR 6**

Diagnosis checklist (run in this order before acting):
1. Is the website up and functional? (Check uptime monitor, attempt signup yourself)
2. Is the CRM/attribution tracking working? (Verify test signup gets recorded)
3. Is email confirmed as delivered? (Check email platform — confirmed open, not spam-filtered)
4. Is paid media live and spending? (Check ad platform — any disapprovals or policy holds?)
5. Are tracking parameters correctly set? (Verify UTMs are passing through to CRM)

If all technical checks pass and signups are genuinely slow:
- Rapid action: Email your existing customer base with a personal note from CEO/Founder ("We launched something today that I'd love your opinion on")
- Paid escalation: Increase LinkedIn retargeting budget to target all website visitors in past 30 days
- Partner blast: Emergency activation of 3-5 partners with a "launch day" message template
- Social push: Founder posts a personal LinkedIn thread explaining WHY this was built and WHO asked for it
- Product Hunt: If not already posted, consider immediate PH launch as safety valve

**SCENARIO 2: TRIAL SIGNUPS HIGH BUT ZERO ICP ACCOUNTS IN FIRST 200 SIGNUPS**

Signal: Volume is strong but company.domain enrichment shows consumer/SMB signups, not target ICP

Rapid actions:
- Audit paid targeting: Check LinkedIn Campaign Manager — are job titles correct? Company size filters applied?
- Audience overlap: Are retargeting audiences pulling from organic traffic (potentially lower ICP intent) vs. target account lists?
- Message alignment: Is the hero message calling out the specific persona clearly? ("Built for VP of Finance at 500-5000 person companies" vs. generic "AI for everyone")
- Pricing page gate: Consider adding company size / job title qualifier to free trial form to filter non-ICP at the door
- Signal to Sales: Brief SDR team to filter aggressively — only work ICP-fit signups today; let marketing automation handle the rest

**SCENARIO 3: PR COVERAGE STRONG BUT ZERO INBOUND INCREASE IN SALES TEAM**

Signal: Media is covering it, but it's not driving demand

Diagnosis: Coverage is an awareness play only — typical of "product news" coverage that doesn't include strong buyer call-to-action

Rapid actions:
- Review each article: Is there a direct link to your trial page or demo form? If not, ask the journalist to add one
- Increase retargeting to recent organic visitors (people who came from article traffic are most likely to convert with a second push)
- Launch a "seen us in the news?" LinkedIn paid campaign targeting readers of key publications
- Email list segment: Send a follow-up to existing prospects that references the press coverage ("In case you missed it, [Publication] covered what we've been building")
- Sales outreach: SDR team sends immediate follow-up to all open opportunities referencing the launch coverage

**SCENARIO 4: COMPETITOR LAUNCHES COUNTER-ANNOUNCEMENT WITHIN 6 HOURS**

Signal: Competitor publishes blog, press release, or LinkedIn update directly responding to your launch

Rapid response (within 1 hour of detection):
- Do NOT respond immediately with panic content — let 2 hours pass and assess their traction (social shares, news pickup)
- Gather data: How much engagement is their announcement getting vs. yours?
- If low competitor traction: Stay on original plan, do not amplify their response by mentioning it
- If moderate competitor traction: Activate a "comparison" paid campaign immediately (if pre-approved copy exists) targeting your ICP
- If high competitor traction: CEO/Founder authorizes a "why we're different" direct response (blog post or LinkedIn thread) — factual, specific, confident in tone — publish within 24 hours

**SCENARIO 5: OUTPERFORMANCE — VELOCITY INDEX > 1.50 AT HOUR 6**

Signal: Launch is exceeding expectations significantly

Actions to maintain momentum:
- Accelerate paid spend: Immediately increase daily budget cap by 50-100% — do not let budget limits starve a winning campaign
- AE capacity check: Alert sales leadership that inbound volume is high — are there enough AEs to handle increased demo requests today?
- Social amplification: Push all employees to share the most-engaged organic post right now (provide the specific post URL in Slack)
- PR follow-up: Contact journalists with real-time "X signups in 6 hours" data point — journalists love live performance numbers for round-2 coverage
- Customer amplification: Text/DM top customer advocates and ask for a social post today — real momentum deserves real amplification
- Competitor watch: Alert your sales team — competitor will likely see your momentum and attempt retention campaigns to their own customers

## Troubleshooting

**Issue: Signup data is flowing into the product database but not appearing in CRM or analytics — attribution is broken during the launch window**
*Solution*: This is the most common and most damaging launch failure mode — invisible signups. Run an immediate diagnostic: submit a test signup yourself and trace it through the full tracking chain (UTM parameters → GA4 event → CRM contact creation). Check if the issue is in the UTM passing (URL parameter stripping on redirect?), the CRM webhook (timing out due to high volume?), or the analytics event (firing before page load complete?). The fastest fix is manual: pull signup list directly from product database every hour and manually cross-reference with CRM. Post a Slack message to the team immediately so everyone knows attribution is temporarily unreliable and not to make budget decisions based on CRM data alone until fixed.

**Issue: Launch is on track for trial signups but demo conversion rate is near zero — signups are not converting to booked demos**
*Solution*: High trial volume with zero demo conversion means the product signup experience is not surfacing the demo CTA, OR the audience is self-serve intent (not enterprise-intent). Audit the post-signup experience immediately: does the trial signup confirmation email include a prominent "book a demo" CTA? Does the in-product onboarding flow offer a guided demo option? Check the ICP-fit rate of current signups — if they're non-ICP, trial signups without demo intent is expected. If ICP-fit is high but demo conversion is zero, the friction is in the booking flow: test the Calendly/Chili Piper link, check for calendar integration errors, and verify the demo form is functional. Quick fix: send a direct 1:1 email from an SDR to every ICP-fit trial signup within 2 hours of their signup with a personal meeting invite (not a Calendly link).

**Issue: PR placements are live and performing but they're not generating inbound — traffic from PR is hitting the homepage but not converting**
*Solution*: PR-driven visitors have high intent but low patience. If they arrive on your homepage and don't immediately see the feature they read about, they bounce. Check your homepage hero message — does it match the exact language and promise in the press coverage? Temporarily update the hero CTA to say "See [Product Name] — the AI detection platform featured in TechCrunch today" to make arriving PR-traffic feel immediately validated. Consider a temporary launch-specific landing page that mirrors the PR narrative exactly — headline pulled directly from the TechCrunch article, trial CTA above the fold. Add a site banner: "Just launched: [Product Name] — [Key Differentiator] → Start Free Trial." PR traffic that arrives with specific intent needs immediate validation that they've found the right product.
