## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Can't accurately separate AI-sourced pipeline from human-sourced in CRM**
*Fix:* Implement "Source Attribution" tagging at the deal level before running this analysis. Create a deal custom field: Primary Source = [AI SDR / AI Content / AI Ad Optimization / Human SDR / Human Content / Inbound Organic / Partner]. Retroactively tag the last 90 days of closed-won and open deals using sequence data (Instantly, Salesloft, or Outreach) correlated with CRM deal creation date. Even imperfect tagging is better than no segmentation — flag estimates as "directionally accurate" in your board summary.

**Problem: AI SDR performance looks better than human SDR on meetings booked, but win rate is lower**
*Fix:* This is a lead quality problem, not a volume problem. The AI SDR is likely targeting accounts outside the true ICP (larger/smaller than ideal, different industry mix, or wrong title). Audit the last 50 AI-sourced opportunities: what % matched your core ICP criteria exactly? If <60% match, tighten the enrichment filters and targeting criteria in the AI SDR workflow before optimizing messaging. Quality-adjusted meeting-to-close rate is the north star metric — not raw meetings booked.

**Problem: CFO pushing back on AI agent ROI because pipeline influenced ≠ pipeline sourced**
*Fix:* Present two ROI scenarios side-by-side: (1) Conservative: count only AI-sourced pipeline (deals where the AI agent was the first touch). (2) Full-credit: count AI-sourced + AI-influenced pipeline. Anchor your board recommendation on the conservative number, but include the full-credit calculation as the "realistic contribution estimate." Supplement with the human labor cost avoidance metric — this is always compelling to CFOs: "We generated the equivalent output of 3 additional FTEs at a $137K annual AI spend vs. $540K annual labor cost for human equivalents."
