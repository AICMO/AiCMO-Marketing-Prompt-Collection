## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Signal-to-pipeline attribution is unclear — can't prove which signals drove which deals**

Solution: Implement retroactive signal tagging. Pull all closed-won opportunities from the last 12 months. For each, check 6sense/Bombora history to see if an intent spike occurred 0–90 days before opportunity creation. Run the same check for job change signals (use LinkedIn or ZoomInfo historical data). Create a "signal presence at time of opportunity creation" field in Salesforce. Even imperfect retroactive attribution (covering 60–70% of deals) gives you enough data to make budget decisions. Going forward, ensure every Clay enrollment creates a CRM campaign membership record with the specific signal that triggered it — this makes attribution automatic and audit-ready.

**Problem: Enrichment hit rate is below 65% — SDRs are sequencing contacts with incomplete data and personalization is failing**

Solution: Don't sequence contacts with incomplete enrichment. Add a Clay logic gate: if enrichment hit rate for a record is below 80% confidence (missing 2+ critical fields: email, title, company size, LinkedIn URL), route to a "manual research queue" CRM task instead of auto-enrolling. For strategic accounts (company score >80), have the GTM engineer spend 15 minutes on manual research via LinkedIn before adding to Clay — the ACV justifies the time. For mid-tier accounts, try a secondary enrichment waterfall (Apollo → Lusha → Hunter for email) before accepting incomplete data. Track enrichment failure reasons by source monthly and pressure vendors at renewal with failure rate data.

**Problem: Sequence reply rates have dropped from 5.1% to 2.8% over 3 months despite no workflow changes**

Solution: This is almost always one of three things: (1) your sending domains have accumulated deliverability damage — check Google Postmaster Tools and Microsoft SNDS weekly; if domain reputation has degraded, warm up new domains for 3–4 weeks and gradually migrate sequences; (2) your signal-triggered personalization has become stale — if you've been using the same "I saw your company just raised funding" opener for 6+ months, your audience has been trained to ignore it; rotate openers and test 3 new personalization angles; (3) ICP drift — your signals are now triggering on a broader set of accounts that were once non-ICP; re-run ICP qualification logic in Clay and raise scoring thresholds. Run a 2-week A/B test: top 20% of accounts by signal score vs. all accounts — if the top 20% replies at 5%+ and the bottom 80% is at 1%, you have an ICP targeting problem, not a messaging problem.
