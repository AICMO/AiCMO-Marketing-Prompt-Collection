## SECTION 2: AI SEARCH SHARE OF VOICE CALCULATION

**DEFINING AI SEARCH SOV**:

AI Search Share of Voice = (# queries where your brand is cited ÷ total queries tested) × 100

This differs from traditional SOV because:
- It measures presence in AI-generated answers, not SERP positions
- It's qualitative (citation quality matters as much as citation frequency)
- It varies by query intent category (you may dominate evaluation queries but be invisible in category discovery)
- It's non-deterministic (AI responses vary; use average of 4-week rolling test)

## SECTION 4: CONTENT ASSET GEO PERFORMANCE SCORING

**GEO SCORE METHODOLOGY**:

Score each content asset 0–100 across 5 dimensions (20 points each):

**Dimension 1: Information Density (20 pts)**
- 20 pts: Contains original proprietary data, specific statistics, benchmarks, or research findings
- 15 pts: Contains specific numbers, named frameworks, or named methodologies
- 10 pts: Contains general best practices with some specifics
- 5 pts: Primarily strategic/conceptual without specific data
- 0 pts: Thin, generic, no unique information

**Dimension 2: Direct Answer Format (20 pts)**
- 20 pts: Leads with clear direct answer, uses H2/H3 subheads matching likely query phrases, contains a summary or TLDR
- 15 pts: Has clear answers but subheads don't match queries directly
- 10 pts: Has relevant info but buried in narrative prose
- 5 pts: Mostly narrative, requires reader to extract answers
- 0 pts: No clear answers to specific questions

**Dimension 3: Source Authority Signals (20 pts)**
- 20 pts: Cites authoritative external sources (Gartner, IDC, Forbes, peer-reviewed research), includes expert quotes with named attribution
- 15 pts: Cites some external sources but limited
- 10 pts: Internal data cited as source
- 5 pts: Claims without source citations
- 0 pts: Unsupported assertions

**Dimension 4: E-E-A-T Signals (20 pts)**
- 20 pts: Author bio with credentials, company about page linked, customer proof (case studies, testimonials) present on or linked from page
- 15 pts: Author bio present, minimal credentials
- 10 pts: Company brand present but no author attribution
- 5 pts: Generic company content, no individual attribution
- 0 pts: No E-E-A-T signals

**Dimension 5: Technical AI-Friendliness (20 pts)**
- 20 pts: Schema markup present (Article, FAQ, HowTo), page loads <2.5s, no JavaScript-dependent content, clean HTML structure, meta description matches page content
- 15 pts: Some schema, good load time, mostly clean structure
- 10 pts: No schema but clean HTML, acceptable load time
- 5 pts: Poor technical implementation but content is accessible
- 0 pts: Content blocked or difficult for crawlers/AI to parse

## SECTION 6: GEO OPTIMIZATION ROADMAP

**TIER 1: IMMEDIATE WINS (Days 1–30, existing content optimization)**

Priority 1 — Enrich your top-5 highest-traffic pages with proprietary data:
- Task: Add a unique statistic, benchmark figure, or framework that AI cannot get elsewhere
- Example: If your blog post on "sales forecasting accuracy" currently has generic best practices, add: "In our analysis of 400 SaaS companies using revenue intelligence platforms, teams that update pipeline weekly achieve 23% better forecast accuracy vs. monthly updaters"
- This creates an "anchor fact" that AI search engines cite when answering the query
- Time to implement: 2–4 hours per page | Expected citation improvement: 15–25% citation lift on optimized pages

Priority 2 — Restructure top pages for direct answer format:
- Task: Add a "Key Takeaways" box at the top of each target page (3–5 bullet points directly answering the target query)
- Add H2 subheads that exactly match likely AI query phrasings
- Example: Change H2 "Why Forecasting Matters" → "Why Sales Forecasting Accuracy Drops Below 75% for Most SaaS Teams"
- Time to implement: 1–2 hours per page | Expected citation improvement: 10–20% lift for pages where answer format was a gap

Priority 3 — Add FAQ schema to comparison and evaluation pages:
- Task: Add FAQ schema markup to your comparison pages (vs. competitor pages) and evaluation guides
- FAQ questions should exactly match buyer queries: "Is [Your Brand] better than [Competitor]?", "How does [Your Brand] pricing work?", "What integrations does [Your Brand] have?"
- These pages have the highest AI citation potential for evaluation-stage queries
- Time to implement: 30–60 min per page | Expected citation improvement: Comparison pages with FAQ schema see 30–50% higher AI citation rates vs. pages without schema

**TIER 2: STRUCTURAL IMPROVEMENTS (Days 31–60)**

Build a proprietary benchmark content asset:
- Create one original research report or benchmark study using your own customer data, with specific, citable statistics
- This becomes your highest-citation-potential asset because AI search engines actively seek to cite proprietary data that doesn't appear elsewhere
- Target: 2,000–3,500 words, 8–12 unique statistics with methodology notes, PDF download version for lead capture, HTML version optimized for AI search
- Example: "[Company] Annual SaaS [Category] Benchmark Report: Data from [X] Companies Shows [Key Finding]"
- Expected citation impact: Research-backed assets earn 2–4x the AI citation rate of opinion-based content

Create "Answer Pages" for your 5 highest-priority query categories:
- Build dedicated pages that directly answer the AI query, not pages designed primarily for human browsing
- Structure: Query-matching headline → Direct answer in first 50 words → Detailed explanation → Supporting data → Expert attribution → Related resources
- These pages may have low traditional SEO traffic but high AI citation rates — measure them separately

**TIER 3: SCALE AND INTELLIGENCE (Days 61–90)**

Implement ongoing AI SOV monitoring:
- Deploy an AI search monitoring tool (Profound.io, Otterly.ai, or Brandwatch AI) to track citation rates automatically
- Set up weekly SOV report: your brand vs. top 3 competitors, across 50-query test set
- Create an automated Slack alert: if competitor gains >5% SOV in any query category in a single week, trigger competitive content response

Build AI search attribution reporting:
- Add "How did you first hear about us?" to all demo request and free trial forms, with "AI search (ChatGPT, Perplexity, etc.)" as a response option
- Tag self-reported AI-sourced leads in CRM and track separately: pipeline, ACV, win rate, sales cycle vs. other sources
- Build quarterly AI attribution report for CMO/board presenting estimated + confirmed AI-influenced pipeline

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: AI citation testing produces inconsistent results — the same query shows our brand in AI responses sometimes but not others**

Solution: AI search is non-deterministic by design — language models sample from probability distributions, producing different outputs for identical queries. This is expected behavior, not a data quality problem. Handle it with statistical rigor: test each query a minimum of 3 times across 4 separate weeks (12 total data points per query) before drawing conclusions. Calculate your brand's "citation probability" per query as: (# times cited ÷ # total tests) × 100. Only act on patterns that hold across at least 8 of 12 tests — single-test citations or absences are statistical noise. If you use a monitoring tool like Profound.io, their ensemble testing methodology (50–100 tests per query) provides more reliable citation rate estimates than manual spot-checking.

**Problem: Direct traffic growth is accelerating but we cannot confidently attribute it to AI search — finance wants harder evidence before approving GEO investment**

Solution: Three pieces of confirmatory evidence will satisfy most CFOs: (1) Self-reported attribution data — even 2–3 months of form data showing "AI search" as a source for 8–12% of new leads constitutes hard evidence that AI discovery is happening at material scale. Add the form field immediately; data accumulates quickly. (2) New visitor behavioral analysis — pull GA4 data for new visitors arriving via direct channel to your pricing and comparison pages. If they bounce at <40% rates (typical for AI-referred visitors who arrive with specific intent vs. average direct bounce rate of 55–65%), this is behavioral confirmation that these visitors are pre-qualified, not casual brand typists. (3) Correlation analysis — export 12 months of weekly data showing: [AI SOV score] correlated with [direct traffic volume] with a 2-week lag. If R² > 0.6, you have statistical correlation between your AI search presence and traffic impact. Present this as a confidence interval, not proof — finance will accept a well-constructed estimate over zero data.

**Problem: Our GEO score improvements haven't produced measurable AI citation improvements after 60 days of optimization**

Solution: GEO optimization works on a 60–120 day feedback loop — AI search engines need time to re-crawl, re-index, and update their training representations of your content. If 90 days have passed without improvement, diagnose the bottleneck: (1) Technical indexing — verify your optimized pages are being crawled (Google Search Console → Coverage → check crawl dates) and that your structured data is validating (Rich Results Test). If crawling is infrequent, submit pages manually for re-indexing. (2) Content authority threshold — some query categories require domain authority AND content quality before AI will cite you. Run the query in Perplexity and examine which sources it cites; if they're all DR 70+ domains, build topical authority via digital PR and expert quotes first. (3) Query selection — you may be targeting queries where AI engines have formed strong content associations with competitors and are resistant to change. Shift focus to newer, emerging query categories where no brand has established citation dominance — these are 3–5x faster to capture than entrenched queries.
