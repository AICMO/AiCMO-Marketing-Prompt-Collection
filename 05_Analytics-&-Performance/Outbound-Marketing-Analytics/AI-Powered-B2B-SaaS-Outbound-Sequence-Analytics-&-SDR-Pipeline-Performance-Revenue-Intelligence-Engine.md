ANALYTICAL FRAMEWORK

Apply all of the following analytical frameworks:

**Outbound Funnel Decomposition:** Break down the full funnel from prospect enrolled → email sent → opened → replied → meeting booked → held → qualified → opportunity → closed-won. Identify the single biggest conversion drop and calculate the revenue impact of closing that gap by 20%.

**Channel Attribution Modeling:** Calculate the meetings-per-thousand-touchpoints and pipeline-per-thousand-touchpoints for each channel (email, phone, LinkedIn, video). Rank channels by ROI efficiency, not raw volume.

**Sequence Decay Detection:** For each sequence, plot meeting booked rate by step number. Identify at which step meeting booking probability drops below threshold (< 0.5% per step) — that is where the sequence should end or be restructured.

**Rep Performance Benchmarking:** Using the MEDDICC framework, categorize rep performance gaps as: (1) Activity volume gaps, (2) Message quality gaps, (3) ICP targeting gaps, (4) Objection handling gaps, or (5) Pipeline management gaps. Each requires a different coaching intervention.

**Revenue Attribution:** Use W-shaped attribution (first touch 30%, lead create 30%, opportunity create 30%, other touches 10%) to calculate outbound's true revenue contribution including pipeline influenced, not just pipeline sourced.

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: My sequence platform doesn't break down metrics by step — I only have overall sequence-level data.**
Fix: Use the Quick Copy-Paste version with overall sequence metrics and add this instruction: "I only have overall sequence metrics, not step-by-step data. Based on the overall positive reply rate and meeting book rate, infer where the likely drop-off points are and provide recommendations assuming standard B2B SaaS sequence engagement curves." The AI will apply decay modeling to estimate step performance.

**Problem: The output benchmarks don't match my industry or ACV — my buyers are very different.**
Fix: Add this context block before the metrics: "Important: My ICP is [describe]. This is a highly technical/highly regulated/very senior buyer (C-suite only). Adjust all benchmarks accordingly. Standard B2B SaaS benchmarks may not apply — adjust thresholds based on [low/high] touch sales motion." The prompt will recalibrate its benchmark expectations to your buyer profile.

**Problem: I have data from multiple tools that don't reconcile — Outreach shows different numbers than Salesforce.**
Fix: Default to CRM (Salesforce/HubSpot) as the source of truth for pipeline and revenue metrics. Use the sequencing platform (Outreach/Salesloft) as the source of truth for sequence engagement metrics (opens, replies, meetings booked). Add a note in your input: "Sequence metrics from Outreach, pipeline metrics from Salesforce — there may be a 5–10% discrepancy due to attribution timing." The AI will flag which numbers to use for which section.
