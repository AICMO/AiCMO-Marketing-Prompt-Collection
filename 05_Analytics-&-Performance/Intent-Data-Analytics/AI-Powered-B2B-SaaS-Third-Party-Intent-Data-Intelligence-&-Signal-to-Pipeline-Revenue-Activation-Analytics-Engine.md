## Overview

## Troubleshooting

**Problem:** Intent data generates high account volume but few actual meetings — SDRs dismiss it as noise.
**Solution:** Conduct a 30-day backtest: pull all intent-activated accounts from 60–90 days ago and check how many converted to opportunities. Share the conversion rate data with SDRs visually. If the rate is genuinely low (<5%), audit your ICP firmographic filters — the issue is likely that too many non-ICP accounts are surfacing. Tighten company size, industry, and tech stack filters before expanding outreach volume.

**Problem:** Multiple providers show the same surging accounts, creating duplicate outreach and confusion about attribution.
**Solution:** Implement a "first signal wins" attribution rule in your CRM: whichever provider triggered the first intent alert for an account gets credit. Use the composite scoring model to deduplicate — if an account appears in Bombora AND G2, that multi-signal match raises their composite score rather than creating two separate activation events. Single owner per account, single activation sequence.

**Problem:** Intent data ROI is difficult to prove because sales doesn't consistently log intent-influenced activities in the CRM.
**Solution:** Add a required "Intent Signal Influenced" checkbox field to the Opportunity object in your CRM, with a validation rule that prevents Stage 2+ progression without the field being populated. Train AEs: if they received a Tier 1 intent alert before opening an opportunity, check the box. Reinforce in weekly pipeline reviews. Use the control group methodology from Section 5C to create a parallel proof of ROI that doesn't depend on manual CRM hygiene.
