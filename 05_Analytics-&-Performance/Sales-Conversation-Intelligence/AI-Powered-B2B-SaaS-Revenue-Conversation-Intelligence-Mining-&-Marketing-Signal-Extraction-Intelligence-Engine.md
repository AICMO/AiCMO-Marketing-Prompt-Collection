## Overview
This prompt transforms raw sales call recordings from Gong, Chorus, Salesloft, or Clari into a continuous marketing intelligence engine — automatically surfacing buyer objections, winning talk tracks, competitive mentions, messaging gaps, and content opportunities, then routing each signal into specific marketing campaigns, ad copy updates, and content briefs. Use it when your marketing team is running on assumptions while your sales team is sitting on 10,000+ hours of recorded buyer truth.

## Integration Tips

**Gong Integration:**
- Use Gong's "Smart Trackers" to automatically flag calls mentioning competitor names, objection phrases, or buyer intent signals — export weekly to a shared Notion or Google Sheet for marketing review
- Gong's "Library" feature: Create a "Marketing Intelligence" collection of calls showcasing breakthrough buyer moments — use these for direct quote mining, not just summaries
- Enable Gong → Salesforce sync to enrich CRM records with conversation signals that marketing automation can act on (e.g., "Competitor X mentioned in discovery" → triggers LinkedIn retargeting audience)

**Chorus / ZoomInfo Integration:**
- Use Chorus "Moments" to auto-tag calls with your defined signal taxonomy — export Moment data monthly for statistical analysis
- Chorus → HubSpot integration: Map Moment tags to HubSpot contact properties, enabling automated marketing list segmentation by conversation signal

**Salesloft Conversations:**
- Salesloft Rhythm integration: Conversation intelligence signals can trigger marketing automation steps in the same sequence — e.g., "objection phrase detected in call" → add to HubSpot workflow → send relevant case study

**Clari Copilot:**
- Clari's Deal Intelligence summaries can be batch-exported for marketing analysis — set up a weekly Clari → Google Sheets → Looker Studio pipeline to track signal trends over time

**HubSpot / Salesforce Marketing Activation:**
- Create custom fields for "top_objection_detected," "competitor_mentioned," and "content_gap_flagged" — populated by conversation intelligence data
- Use these fields as list segmentation criteria for marketing campaigns triggered by specific buyer signals

**Content Production Workflow:**
- Route content gap discoveries directly to a Notion content brief template (include: buyer question, verbatim quote, deal stage, ICP, and recommended format)
- Connect to your content calendar tool (Notion, Airtable, CoSchedule) to auto-populate the backlog with call-intelligence-triggered content priorities ranked by signal frequency × deal-stage impact

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Call transcripts are too long or messy to analyze directly**
Solution: Don't use raw transcripts. Instead, use Gong's AI summary exports, Chorus "Smart Highlights," or have sales reps complete a 5-field post-call form (top objection, competitor mentioned, content requested, buyer language used, deal risk). This structured data is faster to analyze and more consistent. Run the AI analysis on the structured output, not the raw transcripts.

**Problem: The insights are interesting but marketing doesn't act on them**
Solution: The bottleneck is almost never insight quality — it's routing and ownership. Assign one person (marketing ops, PMM, or content lead) as the "Signal Owner" who is accountable for turning call intelligence into activated campaigns within a defined SLA. Build a Slack channel `#call-intelligence-→-marketing` where the Signal Owner posts one activated campaign brief per week from conversation data. Make it a KPI: "X% of Q[n] campaigns informed by conversation intelligence."

**Problem: Sample size is too small for statistical conclusions**
Solution: For teams with fewer than 20 calls per month, analyze by quarter rather than month, and focus on qualitative pattern identification rather than frequency statistics. Even 5 calls where the same objection appears verbatim is a meaningful signal. Use frequency thresholds appropriate to your deal volume: for <20 calls/month, a signal appearing in >15% of calls (3+ times) is actionable; for >100 calls/month, use >10% as your threshold.
