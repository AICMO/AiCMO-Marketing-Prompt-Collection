## Overview
Transforms raw MQL data across every source channel into a rigorous demand waterfall analysis that pinpoints exactly where and why leads convert—or don't—from Inquiry through Closed Won. Use this when marketing and sales disagree on lead quality, when CAC is rising with stagnant close rates, when you need to defend marketing budget with channel-level pipeline contribution data, or when your MQL volume is up but pipeline is flat.

## Integration Tips

- **HubSpot**: Use the Attribution Reports Beta (Contact Attribution) to pull first-touch and last-touch source data per deal. Build a custom report with "Deal stage funnel" filtered by "Marketing original source" to recreate the waterfall by channel natively. Set up Workflows with "Delay until" + Slack notifications to enforce SLA routing.
- **Salesforce**: Build the demand waterfall in Salesforce Reports using Campaign Influence with Customizable Attribution. Use Process Builder or Flow to create automated task assignment with timestamp logging for SLA compliance tracking. Einstein Lead Scoring supplements rule-based models with predictive signals.
- **Marketo**: Use the Revenue Cycle Analytics (RCA) or Revenue Cycle Explorer to visualize the full SiriusDecisions waterfall. Marketo's Lead Lifecycle program tracks stage transitions natively — ensure you're logging SAL and SQL disposition timestamps for velocity analysis.
- **Google Sheets / Looker Studio**: For companies without BI tooling, export deal data with channel source, lead score at MQL, stage timestamps, and outcome to Google Sheets. Use pivot tables to calculate stage-by-stage conversion rates by channel. Build a Looker Studio dashboard connecting Google Sheets + Google Ads data for real-time channel efficiency reporting.
- **Clay / Clearbit**: Enrich all MQLs with company size, funding round, tech stack, and headcount growth before routing to SDR — this allows retroactive scoring analysis to identify which firmographic signals actually predict close.
- **Zapier / Make**: Automate MQL alerts: when a lead reaches MQL score threshold in HubSpot, trigger an instant Slack message to the assigned SDR with lead context, score breakdown, and a one-click link to create the activity. Eliminates the most common SLA miss cause (reps not seeing leads in CRM).
- **Gong / Chorus**: Pull conversation intelligence data to correlate SDR call quality scores with MQL-source to identify which lead sources produce prospects most receptive to SDR outreach — this adds a qualitative layer to the quantitative conversion data.

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: I don't have clean stage-transition timestamp data in my CRM, so I can't calculate velocity or accurate stage conversion rates.**
Solution: Don't let perfect be the enemy of good. Start with deal "Created Date" vs. "Close Date" for cycle length, and pipeline stage history logs (HubSpot Activity Timeline / Salesforce Stage History) to reconstruct waterfall transitions. Use a 6-month cohort of closed deals (won and lost) and backfill stage timestamps from activity history — this gives you a directionally accurate baseline. Build the timestamp logging infrastructure now so future cohorts are clean.

**Problem: Sales team disputes the MQL definition and doesn't trust the conversion data — they say marketing is gaming the metrics.**
Solution: This is a governance problem, not a data problem. Convene a joint Marketing-Sales MQL Definition Review with the CRO and CMO. Bring the lead score calibration audit (Section 4 of the output) showing exactly which score bands produce revenue and which don't — let the data define the threshold, not politics. Propose a 90-day joint MQL quality pilot where marketing and sales co-own the MQL definition, track it together weekly, and agree on the success criteria upfront. Shared ownership eliminates the "gaming" accusation.

**Problem: Our partner referral channel has the best economics but we can't scale it beyond 15 leads/month — it's not a real growth lever.**
Solution: This is the most common mistake in channel efficiency analysis: assuming low-volume channels can't scale. Partner referrals typically underperform their potential because there's no dedicated partner enablement program, no MDF (market development funds), and no systematic referral process. Run the [AI-Powered B2B SaaS Affiliate & Partner Revenue Intelligence Engine] to build a formal partner program. Doubling partner referrals from 15 to 30/month is achievable in 90 days with a structured referral incentive program — and at $13 cost-per-closed-won-dollar, every additional partner referral MQL is worth 4.7x a non-branded paid search MQL.
