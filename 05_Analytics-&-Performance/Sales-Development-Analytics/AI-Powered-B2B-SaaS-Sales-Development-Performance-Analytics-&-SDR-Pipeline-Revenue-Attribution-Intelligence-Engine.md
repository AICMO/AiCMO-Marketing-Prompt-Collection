## Overview
Transforms raw SDR activity data (calls, emails, LinkedIn touches, sequences, meetings booked) into a rigorous pipeline attribution model that proves SDR program ROI, identifies your highest-converting reps, sequences, and personas, and pinpoints exactly where your outbound funnel leaks. Use this when diagnosing underperforming SDR teams, justifying headcount expansion, evaluating AI SDR tools vs. human reps, optimizing sequence strategy by segment, or building a data-driven SDR coaching program.

## Integration Tips

- **Outreach / Salesloft:** Export sequence performance reports weekly — key fields: sequences started, steps completed, reply rate by step, meeting booked rate, positive vs. neutral vs. negative reply breakdown by sequence and rep. Build a custom "SDR Efficiency Dashboard" in Outreach Analytics filtering by rep, sequence, and persona tag. Set automated weekly Slack alerts when any rep's meeting-to-SQL rate drops below 50% for the trailing 2-week window.
- **Salesforce:** Create a "SDR Source Attribution" field on the Opportunity object with values: Cold Email / Cold Call / LinkedIn / Intent Trigger / AI SDR / Inbound-SDR / Other. Build a custom report type "SDR Pipeline Attribution" joining Contacts, Activities, Opportunities, and the SDR source field. Schedule this report to email to RevOps and CRO every Monday morning. Add "SDR Accepted" and "AE Accepted" status stamps to the Lead/Contact record to track SQL gate timing.
- **HubSpot:** Use the Sequences analytics panel to pull per-rep, per-sequence reply and meeting booking data. Create a custom "SDR Meeting Quality" property on the Contact record (populated via form after each discovery call): ICP Score (1–10), Pain Articulated (yes/no), Authority Confirmed (yes/no), Budget Range, Timeline. Build a workflow that fires when Meeting Quality score < 6 to alert the SDR manager for coaching review.
- **Gong / Chorus:** Build a "Discovery Quality" scorecard with weighted criteria: asked about current state (25%), quantified pain (25%), confirmed next steps before call end (25%), introduced to economic buyer (25%). Run weekly Gong Deals at Risk report filtered for SDR-sourced opportunities — identify where deal momentum stalled post-handoff to AE. Use Gong keyword trackers for competitor mentions in SDR-booked calls to identify intent signals for competitive displacement tracking.
- **6sense / Bombora / G2 Intent:** Create a CRM workflow: when an account enters your ICP firmographic filter AND shows intent signal (6sense keyword surge, G2 buyer intent, Bombora topic surge), automatically enrich the account in ZoomInfo/Cognism, create a priority Task for the territory SDR, and enroll the top 3 ICP contacts in your highest-converting intent-trigger sequence. Flag these accounts as "Hot" in CRM for daily SDR review. Intent-triggered leads warrant same-day follow-up SLA.
- **Clay / Apollo / ZoomInfo:** Build an automated ICP scoring workflow: for every prospect enrolled in any sequence, Clay pulls firmographic data (headcount, ARR estimate, tech stack, LinkedIn growth signals, recent funding) and calculates an ICP Fit Score (1–100). SDRs should only manually prioritize accounts scoring > 70. Accounts 50–70 go to AI SDR first-touch. Accounts < 50 are disqualified before sequence enrollment — this single change typically reduces wasted activity by 25–35%.

## Troubleshooting

**Problem:** SQL numbers look strong but AE acceptance rate is low (< 70%) and pipeline-to-close rate from SDR-sourced deals is significantly below target.
**Solution:** The SDR team is booking meetings with underqualified prospects to hit quota — a classic "SQL quantity over quality" problem. Implement a two-step SQL gate: SDR completes a 5-field pre-meeting form (ICP score from Clay, pain statement in prospect's own words, authority level of contact, timeline to decision, next step confirmed). AE reviews the form before accepting. Add an "AE Rejection Reason" field to rejected meetings tracked in CRM. After 30 days, analyze rejection reasons by rep — most rejection reasons cluster into 2–3 patterns (wrong persona, vague pain, no budget signal) that become the coaching curriculum. Do not increase quota until meeting quality rate exceeds 65%.

**Problem:** SDR-sourced deals have significantly lower win rates than marketing-sourced deals, making SDR program ROI hard to justify.
**Solution:** First, check if the comparison is fair — SDR-sourced deals often skew smaller company, earlier stage, and shorter relationship (colder entry). Segment the comparison: compare SDR-sourced deals vs. marketing-sourced deals within the same ICP segment, ACV band, and deal stage at the same sales cycle point. If the gap persists after segmentation, the problem is usually: (1) SDRs booking meetings with non-decision makers — check whether the economic buyer is included in the first call; (2) Insufficient hand-off context — SDRs should document the pain, trigger event, and champion motivation in the CRM opportunity record before the AE first call; (3) ICP drift — SDR-sourced prospects are outside your best-fit segment. Pull a firmographic comparison of SDR-sourced won deals vs. SDR-sourced lost deals and use the differences to tighten ICP targeting criteria.

**Problem:** Junior SDRs are ramping 40–60% slower than expected and attriting before reaching full productivity.
**Solution:** Slow ramp is almost always a systems problem, not a people problem. Audit the first 30 days of your ramp program: Are junior SDRs calling real prospects in week 1 or spending 3+ weeks in training? Research shows that SDRs who book their first meeting within 14 days have 40% higher 6-month retention. Implement a "quick win" design: in week 2, give junior SDRs a small batch of warm inbound leads or recently-engaged accounts (visited pricing page, attended webinar) to build confidence with a shorter cold-to-meeting conversion path. Pair each junior SDR with a senior SDR for daily 15-minute sequence reviews and call shadowing for the first 45 days. Track "days to first booked meeting" as the leading indicator for ramp success — target < 21 days.
