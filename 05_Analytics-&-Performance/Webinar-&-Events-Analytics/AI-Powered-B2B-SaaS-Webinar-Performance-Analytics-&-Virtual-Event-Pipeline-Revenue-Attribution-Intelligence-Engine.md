## Integration Tips

**Webinar Platforms (ON24, Demio, Zoom Webinars, Hopin):**
- Export attendee engagement reports (time in session, poll responses, Q&A submissions, resource downloads) directly from platform into a standardized CSV format for this prompt
- ON24 users: Use the ON24 Intelligence API to pull real-time engagement scores and map to CRM contact records within 30 minutes of session close
- Zoom Webinars: Enable the registrant Q&A report and post-session survey to capture NPS; sync attendance data to HubSpot or Salesforce via native Zoom integration

**CRM (Salesforce / HubSpot):**
- Create a custom Campaign field: "Webinar Quality Tier" (Tier 1-4) and update after each program — enables pipeline reporting by program tier in native CRM reports
- Build a Salesforce report: Opportunities with webinar campaign touch within 90 days, segmented by webinar format and topic — run monthly to track pipeline attribution trend
- HubSpot: Use the "Attribution Reports" tool with webinar campaign objects; set webinar registration as a lifecycle stage trigger to initiate enrollment in post-event workflow

**Marketing Automation (Marketo / HubSpot / Pardot):**
- Create post-webinar smart campaigns with audience segmentation: (1) Live attendee — high engagement, (2) Live attendee — low engagement, (3) Registrant no-show, (4) On-demand viewer
- Program-specific follow-up sequences: Map each webinar format to a dedicated email sequence template (customer story follow-up differs significantly from educational follow-up)
- Score acceleration: Add +15 behavioral points in lead scoring for webinar attendance, +25 for high-engagement score (top 20%), +10 for on-demand completion

**Revenue Intelligence (Gong, Clari, Chorus):**
- Tag calls as "webinar follow-up" in Gong to measure conversation quality and close rate correlation with webinar attendee status
- Clari: Create a pipeline "webinar-sourced" segment to track forecast contribution separately — gives CMO a real-time view of webinar pipeline health

**Analytics & Reporting (Looker, Tableau, Google Sheets):**
- Build a live Webinar Program Intelligence Dashboard: Feed program data weekly, track Tier distribution, pipeline/registrant trend by format and topic
- Set automated alert: If any program's MQL rate drops below 5% by Day 30 post-event → trigger marketing ops review and follow-up sequence audit
- Quarterly benchmark card: Compare program metrics against prior quarter and prior year to identify seasonal patterns and improvement trajectories

**Zapier / Make.com Automation:**
- Trigger: Webinar attendee with engagement score >70 → Immediately enrich in Clearbit/ZoomInfo → Notify assigned SDR in Slack with account context → Auto-create task in Salesforce
- Trigger: Webinar ends → Auto-send on-demand access link to all registered no-shows within 2 hours → Track link clicks as re-engagement signal → Route click-throughs to nurture workflow

## Troubleshooting

**Problem:** Attribution data is incomplete — CRM opportunities aren't consistently tagged to webinar campaigns, making pipeline attribution unreliable.
**Solution:** Audit the webinar-to-CRM sync immediately. Ensure every webinar registrant is created as a campaign member in Salesforce (or associated with a HubSpot campaign) at registration — not just at attendance. This single step makes all downstream attribution possible. Run a retrospective data cleanup: pull the registrant list from your webinar platform for the past 4 quarters, match by email to CRM contacts, and retroactively tag opportunities created within 90 days. Use this prompt with partial data and flag attribution gaps explicitly — imperfect data with caveats is still far more actionable than no analysis.

**Problem:** Show rates are consistently low (below 30%) across all programs, undermining engagement and conversion metrics.
**Solution:** Show rate is a registration quality and promotion timing problem, not a content problem. Run three parallel diagnostics: (1) Check registration-to-event gap — registrants who sign up more than 14 days in advance show at half the rate of those who register within 7 days; restructure promotion cadence to weight heavily toward the final 5 days; (2) Audit reminder sequence — most programs underinvest here; test a 5-touch reminder sequence (Day -7, Day -3, Day -1, Day of at 2 hours, Day of at 15 minutes) with calendar hold + SMS option; (3) Validate ICP match — low show rates often indicate a volume-over-quality registration problem; implement mandatory company/title fields at registration and score registrants in real time.

**Problem:** Webinar programs produce strong MQL numbers but poor downstream opportunity conversion and win rates — marketing and sales are fighting about lead quality.
**Solution:** This is a lead handoff and follow-up speed problem, not a webinar quality problem. Analyze the time-from-registration to SDR outreach — if it's more than 24 hours for live attendees, you're losing 60-70% of conversion potential. Implement a same-session hot lead queue: anyone who attends live with an engagement score above 60 receives an SDR call within 2 hours of session close. Build a shared Webinar Intelligence Brief (auto-generated from webinar Q&A and poll data) that SDRs receive before making follow-up calls — this gives them the context to have a relevant conversation rather than a generic "did you enjoy the webinar?" outreach. Track and report MQL-to-opportunity conversion rate by webinar program separately — this creates accountability and proves the quality problem is downstream of marketing, not within the webinar program itself.
