## Overview
Transforms your organic search data into a revenue attribution model — mapping keyword rankings, organic traffic segments, and content assets to pipeline creation, sales opportunities, and closed-won revenue. Use this when the CFO questions SEO ROI, when you need to prioritize which organic pages and keywords to double down on, or when you're building the business case for content and SEO investment against paid media alternatives.

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Organic sessions are high but organic-attributed pipeline is near zero**
This is almost always a last-touch attribution problem, not an SEO problem. Your organic content is creating awareness and consideration touches that don't get credit when demo requests come via direct or paid retargeting. Solution: Switch to multi-touch attribution in HubSpot or Salesforce, enable first-touch source tracking, and add a "how did you hear about us?" question to your demo request form. Run this prompt again with multi-touch data — organic contribution typically increases 2-4x.

**Problem: Cannot connect keyword rankings to specific pipeline opportunities**
Most CRMs don't natively capture keyword-level organic data. Workaround: (1) Ensure all paid search campaigns use keyword-level UTMs — this creates comparison data for what organic equivalents should look like. (2) Use Clearbit Reveal or Koala.sh to identify organic visitors by company — match to your CRM accounts to see which target accounts are researching you organically before any form fill. (3) Implement self-reported attribution ("How did you find us?") on your highest-intent form pages.

**Problem: SEO team says content quality is the bottleneck, but this prompt says link acquisition is the priority**
Both can be true simultaneously — use the content scorecard output to separate the diagnosis. If HIDDEN GEM pages (high conversion, low traffic) are the primary opportunity, link acquisition wins. If TRAFFIC TRAP pages (high traffic, low conversion) dominate, content quality/intent alignment is the fix. The prompt will tell you which scenario applies based on your data. If the output is ambiguous, run the analysis separately for branded vs. non-branded organic traffic segments.
