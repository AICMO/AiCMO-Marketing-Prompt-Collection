## Troubleshooting

**Problem: ICP engagement rate is below 2.5% despite seemingly relevant content**
*Solution:* First audit LinkedIn Campaign Manager audience targeting — confirm you're targeting the right job functions (check both primary AND secondary job function categories), correct seniority levels (use "Director" + "VP" + "CXO" explicitly), and company size ranges. Overly broad targeting (e.g., "All Marketing" instead of "Product Marketing + Revenue Marketing") is the #1 cause of low ICP rates. Second, review creative: LinkedIn's algorithm suppresses TLA posts that read like ads — posts with product screenshots, pricing language, or obvious promotion consistently underperform organic-style content. Third, check creator follower composition — a creator with 80% followers from personal network may have low ICP alignment even with correct targeting overlays.

**Problem: Can't attribute pipeline to TLA because sales cycles are 6–12 months and accounts engage early**
*Solution:* Shift from pipeline sourced attribution to pipeline acceleration measurement with extended lookback windows (up to 180 days). Measure three leading indicators instead: (1) Average time from TLA engagement to demo request for TLA-touched accounts, (2) Proposal acceptance rate at accounts with ≥3 TLA engagements vs. 0 engagements, (3) Number of stakeholders engaged at TLA-touched accounts vs. non-touched accounts when opportunity opens. These proxy metrics build the business case for early-funnel TLA investment before long-cycle attribution catches up.

**Problem: Creator engagement is declining after 3–4 months despite consistent posting**
*Solution:* Creator fatigue has three root causes — audience saturation (same people seeing the same creator repeatedly), content formula repetition (algorithm and audience pattern-match the creator's style), or topic exhaustion (same themes over and over). Fixes by cause: For saturation, expand audience targeting or rotate the creator to a new campaign for 30 days. For formula repetition, deliberately break the creator's pattern — if they always post text, switch to carousel; if they post lessons-learned, shift to a behind-the-scenes series. For topic exhaustion, conduct a creator brainstorm session to surface fresh angles, ideally tied to recent customer wins, product launches, or industry events. Also check posting frequency — most B2B creators peak at 2–3 TLA-boosted posts per week; daily boosting accelerates fatigue.
