## Overview
This prompt transforms your executive podcast guest appearance program from a brand activity into a measurable revenue driver. It builds a systematic framework to pre-score podcast opportunities before committing, track pipeline attributed to each appearance across direct and dark funnel channels, measure audience-to-ICP fit, and optimize your executive speaking circuit for maximum qualified pipeline generation — fully automatable as a quarterly analytics workflow.

## Integration Tips

**CRM (Salesforce / HubSpot):**
- Create a custom field "Podcast Source" on Contact and Opportunity records. Train SDRs to ask "How did you first hear about us?" on every discovery call and log podcast show names when mentioned. Set up a workflow: if Contact Source = "Podcast", auto-create a task to research which episode aired nearest to their MQL date.
- Build a Salesforce report: "Pipeline sourced by podcast appearance" filtered by "Lead Source = Podcast" and "Close Date = this quarter." Run this monthly to track CRIS accuracy vs. direct attribution.

**LinkedIn Sales Navigator:**
- After each appearance airs, use Sales Navigator to search for followers of the podcast's host/show page who match your ICP (title + company size + industry filters). Export to a list and hand to SDRs for the Day 7 activation sequence. Most shows have a LinkedIn company page or the host has a significant following — this is your free ICP-matched audience.

**Google Search Console / Semrush:**
- Export branded search query volume weekly. Create a time-series chart with podcast air dates marked as events. Identify statistically significant lift windows (>20% above 4-week prior baseline) that correlate with appearances. This is your primary dark funnel proxy signal.

**Gong / Chorus:**
- Set up a tracker keyword for each podcast show name. Run a monthly report: "How many discovery calls in the last 30 days mentioned [show name]?" This captures buyers who reference the podcast in the call but weren't tracked via UTM.

**Shield Analytics / Taplio (LinkedIn analytics tools):**
- Track executive LinkedIn follower growth daily. Export weekly. Build a spreadsheet correlating follower spikes to appearance dates with a 7-day lag. After 6 months of data, you'll have a statistically calibrated relationship between episode ICP quality and follower conversion rate that improves dark funnel estimates.

**Zapier / Make.com:**
- Automate: When a new Contact is created in HubSpot with Source = "Podcast" OR "Referral" with a note containing a show name, automatically create a task for the SDR to add them to the podcast activation sequence in Outreach/Salesloft.

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: I have almost no direct attribution — no UTM links were created for past appearances. How do I retroactively estimate pipeline?**
Use the time-windowed proxy correlation method: for each past appearance, pull (1) inbound demo requests in the 21 days post-air vs. the 4-week prior baseline, (2) LinkedIn follower growth in the 7 days post-air vs. weekly baseline, (3) any CRM contacts created in 30 days whose "heard about us" survey response mentions podcasts or audio. Apply the CRIS formula with a 0.4x dark funnel confidence multiplier (lower than normal because you're missing the click-through signal). Accept that retrospective measurement will undercount true influence by 30-50%, and use the analysis primarily to identify relative performance ranking between shows rather than absolute pipeline values.

**Problem: The show I want to appear on has no listener data and won't share download statistics.**
Use proxy scoring: pull the host's LinkedIn follower count and engagement rate (comments + shares ÷ impressions on last 10 posts). A host with 20K followers and 3%+ engagement rate likely reaches a deeply engaged audience of 600+ per post — which for a B2B niche show is meaningful. Check Apple Podcasts and Spotify for public review counts and ranking in category charts. Look at guests the show has previously had: if past guests are your ICP peers (VP Sales at $20M-$100M SaaS companies), the audience is likely similar. Run the opportunity scoring rubric on what you do know and proceed if score is ≥60 even with incomplete data.

**Problem: My CEO's calendar is full and I can't book more than 2 appearances per quarter. How do I maximize impact with limited availability?**
Shift from quantity to hyper-selectivity. Apply the opportunity scoring rubric ruthlessly: only accept shows scoring ≥80. In the current quarter, pre-negotiate 2 high-ICP-fit shows with the full post-appearance activation package (unique landing page, host LinkedIn promotion, SDR activation sequence). Then add the CMO or CTO to your speaker rotation with a clearly defined show category (CMO covers marketing/GTM shows, CTO covers technical/engineering shows). Two perfectly targeted appearances by the right exec will outperform six mediocre appearances by any exec — the Revenue Architecture vs. Operators Podcast comparison in the example output proves this mathematically.
