## Overview

## Integration Tips

**HubSpot:**
- Create a custom webinar performance property on Contacts to track which webinar(s) each contact attended, their show rate, and post-event CTA engagement
- Build a workflow that scores contacts based on webinar engagement signals and routes high-WQS contacts to sales immediately
- Use HubSpot reporting to build a "Webinar-to-Pipeline" dashboard that tracks MQL conversion from webinar attendee lists by segment

**Salesforce:**
- Create a Campaign Hierarchy in Salesforce for each webinar series with child campaigns per episode to track influence across multi-touch journeys
- Build a custom Campaign Influence model that weights webinar attendance based on funnel stage (TOFU webinar attendance gets 10% weight; BOFU demo webinar gets 40% weight)
- Add a "Webinar Quality Score" field on Campaign records to track and compare program performance

**Zoom / ON24 / Goldcast:**
- Export full engagement data (Q&A, polls, chat, attendance duration) after each webinar and upload to your CRM within 24 hours
- Tag attendees with engagement tier (High: 75%+ attendance + Q&A; Medium: 50-75% attendance; Low: <50%) and trigger different post-event nurture sequences

**Notion / Airtable (Program Management):**
- Build a Webinar Intelligence Database that stores all historical performance data, speaker scorecards, topic ratings, and format test results
- Create a "Webinar Selection Scorecard" template that sales and marketing teams fill out when proposing new webinar topics to ensure alignment with proven success patterns

**Zapier / Make:**
- Automate the post-webinar data pipeline: Zoom → Google Sheets (raw data) → HubSpot (contact updates + CTA enrollment) → Slack (team notification with WQS score)
- Set up automated alerts when a webinar's post-event pipeline conversion drops below your program average (e.g., <15 pipeline-influenced contacts per 100 attendees) — triggers immediate review meeting

## Troubleshooting

**Problem:** Limited historical data makes speaker and format analysis inconclusive (fewer than 10 webinars).
**Solution:** Supplement your own data with industry benchmarks. Use the format selection guide prescriptively based on funnel stage goals rather than purely from historical data. Start tracking the Webinar Quality Score on your next 3 webinars to build your internal baseline before making major program changes.

**Problem:** CRM data doesn't reliably track which webinars influenced pipeline because reps don't log webinar attendance as a touchpoint.
**Solution:** Implement Salesforce or HubSpot Campaign Influence tracking with automated webinar data sync (Zoom/ON24 native integrations or via Zapier). In the short term, cross-reference your webinar attendee lists against Closed-Won and Open Pipeline accounts manually to build a directional attribution view.

**Problem:** Your webinar audience includes a mix of customers, prospects, and partners making pipeline attribution murky.
**Solution:** Segment your attendee analysis by CRM contact type before running the pipeline attribution analysis. For net-new pipeline measurement, filter to contacts who were NOT existing customers at time of attendance. Track customer attendees separately as an expansion/retention signal, not as demand generation pipeline.
