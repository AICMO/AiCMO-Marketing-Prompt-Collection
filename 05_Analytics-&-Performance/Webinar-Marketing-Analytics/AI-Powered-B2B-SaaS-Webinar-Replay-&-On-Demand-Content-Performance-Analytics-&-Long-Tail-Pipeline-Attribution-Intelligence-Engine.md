## Troubleshooting

**Problem:** Most replay views are anonymous (no identity), making pipeline attribution impossible.
**Solution:** Implement a progressive gate strategy — let viewers watch the first 10 minutes ungated, then present a soft gate ("Enter your email to continue") at the 10-minute mark. Pair with LinkedIn Insight Tag retargeting on replay pages to reach anonymous viewers with sponsored content. Target 20–35% identity rate as a realistic baseline for ungated replay programs; anything above 15% on ungated content is above industry median.

**Problem:** Replay pipeline attribution creates political conflict with sales — reps claim they sourced deals that marketing's replay data shows as "influenced."
**Solution:** Implement a dual-credit attribution model: first SDR/AE outreach gets Sales Sourced credit; if a replay view occurred ≥7 days before the opportunity creation date, Marketing gets Influenced credit. Neither attribution cancels the other. Use Salesforce Campaign Influence with a "Replay View" campaign type and document the credit-sharing rules in a joint Marketing-Sales SLA signed by CMO and CRO.

**Problem:** Replay views spike immediately after the live event email (Day 1–3) then flatline — no sustained long-tail engagement.
**Solution:** Your replay is not being discovered organically. Audit: (1) Are replay landing pages indexed by Google? Run site:yoursite.com/webinars in Google Search Console. (2) Are replay page titles optimized for buyer intent queries, not internal event titles? "Q2 2026 Customer Summit Replay" → "How SaaS CFOs Automate Board Reporting [Webinar]". (3) Are replays inserted into email nurture sequences at the 30-day and 60-day stage, not just in the immediate post-event follow-up? Long-tail replay views require active distribution — they do not self-sustain.
