# AI-Powered B2B SaaS Affiliate Partner Quality Scoring & Traffic Intelligence Revenue Engine

**Difficulty:** Advanced | **Time:** 25 min | **Tags:** b2b, affiliate, analytics, partner-quality, fraud-detection, revenue-attribution, saas

## Overview
This prompt transforms raw affiliate partner data into a comprehensive quality scoring and traffic intelligence system, revealing which affiliate partners drive genuine ICP-fit pipeline versus vanity traffic, commission fraud, or low-converting noise. Use it monthly to tier and prune your affiliate partner base, quarterly to restructure commission agreements, and in real-time to flag fraud patterns before they contaminate your CRM.

## Integration Tips

- **PartnerStack:** Use the Partner Performance API to pull monthly conversion data per partner. Export the "Conversions Detail" report and cross-reference `partner_id` with CRM opportunity data using `email` as the join key. Set up automated webhooks to push conversion events to Salesforce or HubSpot the moment they occur, tagging the originating partner on the contact record.
- **Impact.com:** Use the "Reporting → Partner Performance" dashboard filtered by custom date range. Export via CSV or use the Impact REST API (v3) to pull impression, click, and conversion data by `partner_id`. Build a custom attribution comparison report using Impact's "Path Analytics" feature to detect last-click vs. assisted attribution discrepancies that may indicate cookie hijacking.
- **HubSpot:** Tag all affiliate-sourced contacts with a custom property `Affiliate_Partner_Name` using UTM parameter capture (configure UTM auto-capture in HubSpot Settings → Forms → Pre-populate Fields with `utm_affiliate`). Build a contact list segment for each affiliate partner and run a "MQL Quality by Affiliate Partner" report using the Contact Attribution report filtered by original source = "Other Campaign" + UTM campaign contains partner name.
- **Salesforce:** Create a custom Campaign Type = "Affiliate" and assign all affiliate-sourced leads to partner-specific campaigns using UTM `utm_affiliate` capture via web-to-lead forms. Build an Opportunity Influence report joining Campaigns + Opportunities + Campaign Members to calculate true affiliate-influenced pipeline per partner with closed-won revenue segmentation.
- **Google Analytics 4:** Set up a custom dimension `affiliate_partner` populated via the `utm_affiliate` UTM parameter. Build a GA4 exploration report comparing engagement rate, session duration, and pages/session across the affiliate partner dimension to identify traffic quality discrepancies without requiring CRM integration — ideal for early fraud detection.
- **Zapier/Make automation:** Build a monthly affiliate quality scoring pipeline: (1) Pull partner performance data from your affiliate platform API, (2) Pull MQL-to-SQL rates per partner from CRM API, (3) Calculate quality composite scores in a Google Sheets formula engine, (4) Pass the structured partner data table to the Claude API with this prompt, (5) Post the generated tier scorecard and fraud flags to Slack #affiliate-marketing and email the summary to your partner manager automatically.

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Can't verify whether affiliate-sourced MQLs match ICP — no job title or company size data captured at conversion.**
Solution: Implement a 2-3 question form enrichment on your affiliate landing pages capturing company size (dropdown: 1-50 / 51-200 / 201-1,000 / 1,000+) and primary job function. Alternatively, auto-enrich affiliate-sourced MQLs using Clearbit Reveal, Apollo, or ZoomInfo enrichment triggered on `lead_source = affiliate` in your CRM the moment a record is created. Compare enriched company size and title against your ICP definition. Even 60% enrichment coverage gives sufficient signal to rank partner ICP fit — you don't need 100% enrichment to identify which partner types attract the wrong audience.

**Problem: One partner's conversion rate is 4x the program average — how do I distinguish genuine excellence from fraud?**
Solution: Run a 3-part fraud investigation before taking action: (1) Check IP diversity — if >20% of conversions share the same IP address block using your CRM's IP capture or a tool like Clearbit/ipinfo.io, flag for fraud review. (2) Check email domain diversity — if >30% of MQL emails share the same domain or use generic providers (gmail, yahoo) at unusual rates for your B2B ICP, flag for verification. (3) Check CRM progression rate — genuine high-conversion partners show above-average SQL rates. If the partner has a conversion rate 4x the average but an SQL rate below 50% of program average, it almost certainly indicates incentivized or fabricated conversions. A genuinely excellent partner shows high conversion rate AND high SQL rate AND strong ICP fit — all three signals together indicate real quality.

**Problem: More than half of enrolled affiliate partners are dormant — they signed up but never generated a conversion.**
Solution: Segment dormant partners by time-since-enrollment and run a tiered reactivation campaign: (1) Partners dormant <90 days receive an activation kit — 3 pre-written promotional post templates, a co-branded landing page with their affiliate link pre-populated, and a 10-minute quick-start video guide. (2) Partners dormant 90-180 days receive a personal outreach from your partner manager with a time-limited commission uplift (e.g., 5% higher commission for next 30 days as activation incentive). (3) Partners dormant >180 days with zero historical conversions are silently terminated — no notification needed for partners who never activated, and their slots can be reallocated to recruited partners. Implement a 30-day activation checkpoint into your partner onboarding workflow going forward, with an automated Day 15 follow-up delivering the activation kit to partners who haven't yet sent their first click.
