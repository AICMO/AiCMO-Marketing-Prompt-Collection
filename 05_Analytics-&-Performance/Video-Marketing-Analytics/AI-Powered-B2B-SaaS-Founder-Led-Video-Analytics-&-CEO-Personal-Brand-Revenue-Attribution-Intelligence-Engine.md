# AI-Powered B2B SaaS Founder-Led Video Analytics & CEO Personal Brand Revenue Attribution Intelligence Engine - Measure the Dollar Value of Your Founder's Camera Time

**Difficulty:** Advanced | **Time:** 20-30 min | **Tags:** founder-led growth, video analytics, CEO personal brand, dark social attribution, revenue attribution, b2b saas, pipeline measurement, thought leadership ROI

## Overview

This prompt deploys an autonomous analytics agent that ingests LinkedIn video, dark social, CRM, and sales conversation data to calculate the true revenue impact of founder/CEO video content programs. Use it when your CEO is posting 3-5 videos per week but you can't quantify what it's worth in pipeline dollars, when the board asks for ROI on the founder's time, or when you need to decide whether to scale or scale back the founder content investment.

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: CRM shows almost zero founder content attribution despite strong LinkedIn engagement metrics.**
Solution: LinkedIn native video generates zero trackable clicks — it is inherently a dark social channel. Implement self-reported attribution immediately: Add "How did you first hear about [Company]?" as a required field in every demo request form and as a standard question in the first 60 seconds of every discovery call. Train the sales team to log the response in HubSpot/Salesforce notes. Expect 4-6 weeks to accumulate enough data for meaningful analysis. In the interim, use the dark social shadow attribution model from Module 2 to estimate impact.

**Problem: Founder is posting consistently but LinkedIn audience is not growing — video views plateau.**
Solution: This is a distribution problem, not a content problem. Audit: (1) Is the founder engaging with comments within 60 minutes of posting? (Early engagement tells the LinkedIn algorithm to boost the post.) (2) Are employees sharing and commenting on posts? Implement an employee advocacy micro-campaign — 5 employees who comment meaningfully on every video can 3-5x organic reach. (3) Is the content being distributed beyond LinkedIn? Embed top-performing videos in SDR outreach sequences, email nurture campaigns, and the website homepage. Off-platform distribution compounds on-platform reach.

**Problem: Founder-influenced pipeline doesn't show better deal quality than non-influenced — no ACV or velocity advantage.**
Solution: This suggests the founder's content is attracting the wrong ICP or the content topics are not aligned with the buyer persona who controls the budget. Run a content audit: Pull the LinkedIn follower demographics (company size, seniority). If the majority of engaged followers are individual contributors rather than VP+/C-suite titles, the content is building awareness in the wrong audience tier. Reorient content topics toward problems that senior economic buyers own (revenue predictability, board-level reporting, competitive market position) rather than problems that practitioners own (workflow efficiency, feature adoption). Test the new positioning for 60 days before reassessing.
