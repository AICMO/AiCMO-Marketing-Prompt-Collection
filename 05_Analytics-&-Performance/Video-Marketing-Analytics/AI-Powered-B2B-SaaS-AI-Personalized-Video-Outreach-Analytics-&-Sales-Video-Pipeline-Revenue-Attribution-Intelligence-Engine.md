## Overview

This prompt deploys an autonomous analytics agent that ingests AI-personalized video outreach performance data (HeyGen, Synthesia, Loom, Vidyard, Sendspark) alongside CRM pipeline data to reveal which video approaches, personalization depths, and sales stages generate the highest reply rates, meeting bookings, and closed revenue — so you can scale what works and eliminate what doesn't.

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Low video open rates (<25%) despite high send volume**
Diagnosis: Subject line or deliverability issue, not a video quality problem.
Fix: Audit subject lines against spam trigger words (avoid "video inside," "watch this," "free"). Test plain-text subject lines with no video mention. Check domain warm-up status — video link emails have higher spam risk. Switch from embedded video links to thumbnail images linking to hosted page.

**Problem: High view rates but low reply rates (<4%)**
Diagnosis: Hook is compelling but CTA is weak or ask is too big.
Fix: Audit the final 15 seconds of every video — is the CTA specific and low-friction? Replace "Would love to connect for a 30-minute call" with "Would a 15-minute Tuesday or Thursday work to see if this is relevant?" Add a P.S. text below the video with a direct calendar link. Test ending with an open question vs. a direct meeting request.

**Problem: Strong individual rep results but poor team-wide adoption**
Diagnosis: Rep resistance is tool-related (camera anxiety or time investment) or coaching-related (no clear ROI feedback loop).
Fix: Share weekly leaderboard of video reply rates vs. text reply rates at team level — social proof from peers drives adoption faster than manager mandates. For camera-anxious reps, implement HeyGen AI avatar as the default starting point. Set a "3 videos per week" minimum as a KPI tracked in the weekly pipeline review, not as a punishment metric but as a success enabler.
