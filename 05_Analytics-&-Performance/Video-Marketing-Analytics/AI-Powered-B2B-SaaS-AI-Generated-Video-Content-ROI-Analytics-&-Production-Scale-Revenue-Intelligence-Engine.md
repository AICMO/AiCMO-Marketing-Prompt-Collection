## Troubleshooting

**Problem: AI video completion rates are consistently 20-30% below human-produced videos, making the ROI case hard to defend.**
Solution: First, isolate whether the gap is in the first 5 seconds (hook failure—fix your AI video opening prompt to lead with a sharp, curiosity-gap statement or data point) vs. mid-video (pacing failure—break long AI videos into shorter segments under 90 seconds for social and email) vs. near the end (CTA placement—move your CTA to the 70% mark, not the final frame). Run a 30-day A/B test with one specific fix at a time. If the gap persists above 20% after prompt optimization, consider a hybrid production model: AI-generated B-roll and voiceover with a 5-second human-produced hook at the start.

**Problem: The attribution tool isn't capturing AI video touches because Wistia/Vidyard and the CRM aren't syncing video view data reliably.**
Solution: Implement the Wistia JavaScript embed API to fire a custom event to Google Tag Manager when a user hits the 30%, 50%, and 80% watch marks. Route those GTM events to GA4, then connect GA4 to your attribution tool or CRM via the Salesforce/HubSpot GA4 native connector. As a fallback, export weekly Wistia engagement CSVs, match email addresses to CRM contacts, and create a manual "Video Touch" campaign activity log in Salesforce. This gives you a working attribution model while the full integration is being built.

**Problem: Personalized AI outreach videos are generating high reply rates but not translating to booked meetings—the hand-off from video reply to meeting is leaking.**
Solution: The issue is almost always the reply-to-booking sequence, not the video itself. Ensure every personalized AI video email includes a direct calendar embed link (Calendly or Chili Piper) inside the email body AND the video landing page. Add an AI-generated follow-up sequence: if no meeting is booked within 48 hours of a positive reply, trigger a second personalized video responding to the specific question or objection raised in the reply. Track "reply-to-booked" rate as a separate metric from raw reply rate to isolate the leak.
