## Integration Tips

- **Wistia/Vidyard → Salesforce:** Use native integrations to push individual viewer activity (name, company, watch %), creating video touchpoints on Contact and Lead records. Map watch milestones (25%, 50%, 75%, 100%) as custom activities for Bizible/attribution tagging.
- **YouTube → GA4 → CRM:** Implement UTM parameters on all YouTube video descriptions and end-screen CTAs. Connect GA4 audiences (YouTube video viewers) to HubSpot or Salesforce via GA4 Audiences sync for retargeting and attribution.
- **LinkedIn Video → HubSpot:** Use LinkedIn Insight Tag to capture LinkedIn Video viewers as website visitors. Build HubSpot lists of contacts who've viewed specific LinkedIn videos using form submissions + UTM tracking on post-click landing pages.
- **Vidyard → Outreach/Salesloft:** Use Vidyard's native SDR integrations to insert personal video thumbnails into sequences and track plays, completions, and CTA clicks as engagement signals fed back to sequence step logic.
- **Looker Studio / Tableau:** Build a unified video analytics dashboard by connecting YouTube Analytics API, LinkedIn Marketing API, Wistia API, and Salesforce opportunity data. Use Supermetrics or Funnel.io as the data layer for non-engineering teams.
- **Zapier automation:** Trigger Slack alerts to the content team when a video crosses pipeline thresholds (e.g., "Competitor comparison video just influenced a $120K opportunity") to reinforce production priorities in real time.

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Video views exist but no pipeline attribution is tracked in CRM.**
Solution: The most common cause is missing Wistia/Vidyard-to-CRM integration or UTM parameter gaps on YouTube/LinkedIn CTAs. Audit the three conversion pathways: (1) gated demo CTAs in video end screens → form submission → CRM; (2) Wistia viewer identification via email match; (3) LinkedIn retargeting audiences built from video viewers → form fill → CRM. Implement at least one before running attribution reports.

**Problem: Completion rates are high but pipeline influence is near zero.**
Solution: High completion with zero conversion is almost always a targeting problem, not a content problem. The video is reaching non-ICP audiences (job seekers, students, competitors). Run Wistia or LinkedIn analytics to check viewer company size and industry. If YouTube, review the Traffic Sources report—organic search audiences often skew away from your ICP. Shift distribution toward LinkedIn paid promotion with ICP audience targeting overlaid on the high-completion video.

**Problem: Attribution data conflicts—Bizible says one video influenced a deal, Salesforce says the contact never watched it.**
Solution: Multi-touch attribution tools like Bizible attribute at the Contact level, not the Account level. In B2B buying committees, an influencer may watch the video without being the tracked Contact on the Opportunity. Enable account-level video attribution by matching viewer email domains (from Wistia) to Account domains in Salesforce, then attributing video touches at the Account level using Bizible's account-based attribution model.
