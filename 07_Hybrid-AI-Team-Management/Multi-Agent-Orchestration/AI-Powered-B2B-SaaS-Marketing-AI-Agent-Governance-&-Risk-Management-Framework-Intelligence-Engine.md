## Overview
This prompt designs a comprehensive governance and risk management framework for B2B SaaS marketing teams running autonomous AI agents — covering risk tiering, brand safety protocols, compliance guardrails, audit trails, human oversight models, and incident response playbooks. Use it when deploying or scaling AI agents for demand gen, content, ABM, or outbound so that speed of automation never outpaces your ability to control, audit, and correct it.

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Sales team doesn't trust AI-sourced leads and rejects MQLs regardless of quality**
Solution: The trust issue is almost always caused by 1-2 high-visibility bad incidents (like named account contamination), not sustained quality problems. Fix this with transparency: share the Weekly AI Governance Scorecard directly with sales leadership, show them the named account suppression list is working, and run a joint "AI quality vs. human quality" comparison on a sample of 50 leads. Data beats perception.

**Problem: Tier 3 escalations are creating a bottleneck — everything needs approval and agents stall**
Solution: You have over-tiered your risk matrix. Tier 3 should be <5% of all actions. Audit your tier assignments: if routine actions like "send follow-up email to prospect who downloaded a whitepaper" are in Tier 3, recalibrate. Build a "pre-approved action library" — common actions that have been individually approved once can be whitelisted for future autonomous use, with logging only.

**Problem: No one is running the weekly governance review because it takes too long**
Solution: The 15-minute weekly review only works if the dashboard is pre-built and auto-populates. Don't build a checklist people fill manually — build an automated report in your BI tool or even Google Sheets with data connectors that pulls the 10 key metrics automatically. The human's job is to look at the dashboard and flag exceptions, not to gather data. If setup is blocking you, start with just 3 metrics: named account incidents (must be 0), budget variance (must be <5%), and brand violations caught (track the trend).
