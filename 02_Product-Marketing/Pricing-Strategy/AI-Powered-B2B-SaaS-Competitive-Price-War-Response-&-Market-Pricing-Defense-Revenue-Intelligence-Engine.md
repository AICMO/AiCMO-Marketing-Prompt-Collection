## Overview

When a direct competitor cuts prices, launches a free tier, or runs an aggressive pricing campaign, most B2B SaaS teams react too slowly — losing deals, spooking renewal accounts, and allowing the competitor's narrative to set the market conversation. This engine builds a complete, AI-powered competitive pricing defense playbook: threat assessment, customer risk segmentation, value reinforcement messaging, sales objection responses, commercial counter-moves, and proactive pipeline protection campaigns — activated within 48 hours of a competitive pricing event.

Use it when a competitor announces a price reduction, launches a freemium tier, runs a "rip and replace" campaign targeting your install base, or when win/loss data signals that pricing is suddenly appearing as a top objection.

## SECTION 1: THREAT INTELLIGENCE ASSESSMENT

Analyze the competitive pricing move across four dimensions:

**1A. Strategic Classification**
- Is this a tactical discount (one-time, short-lived) or strategic repositioning (permanent new market approach)?
- Pricing-to-value signal: Does their pricing reflect a real cost advantage, a margin sacrifice, or a feature-for-feature downgrade?
- Sustainability assessment: Can they sustain this pricing given their known burn rate, funding, and margin structure?
- Precedent: Have they done this before? What happened?

**1B. Market Timing Analysis**
- Why now? Connect the timing to their recent events (funding, product launch, leadership change, losing market share, etc.)
- Category impact: Will this move accelerate or slow overall market growth?
- Analyst/press reaction: How will Gartner, Forrester, and trade press interpret this?

**1C. Intent Scoring Matrix**

**The Value:** Anchor to specific, quantifiable outcomes your customers achieve.

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Sales team reverts to discounting instead of using the messaging framework.**
Solution: Create a "pricing concession gate" in the deal desk process — require competitive documentation (competitor's pricing proof, AE's completed ABV objection response, VP approval) before any discount is approved. The friction of the process naturally pushes reps toward the messaging playbook first. Run a 30-minute sales team enablement session within 72 hours of launching the playbook.

**Problem: The messaging feels defensive or weak when deployed in real conversations.**
Solution: The most common cause is reps using the messaging as a script rather than an outline. Run 3-4 role-play scenarios in your next weekly sales meeting where reps practice the Acknowledge-Bridge-Value structure until it feels conversational. The goal is internalizing the logic, not memorizing lines. Also check: are the proof points specific enough? "Fastest implementation" is weak — "customers live in 14 days vs. industry average of 45 days" is strong.

**Problem: Competitor's free offer is converting at-risk SMB customers faster than expected.**
Solution: Accelerate the commercial response timeline — move from "Value Add Bundle" to activating Option C (new packaging tier) faster than originally planned. Convene an emergency pricing committee with your CEO, CFO, and VP Sales. Present the 30-day metrics and propose a concrete SMB-specific offer with a sunset date. Frame internally as "protecting the segment while we build more defensible differentiation" rather than "reacting to a competitor."
