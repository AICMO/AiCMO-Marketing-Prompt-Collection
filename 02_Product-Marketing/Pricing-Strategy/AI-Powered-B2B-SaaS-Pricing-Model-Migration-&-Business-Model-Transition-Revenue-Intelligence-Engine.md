## Overview
Designs a complete, AI-orchestrated playbook for migrating a B2B SaaS product from one pricing model to another — per-seat to usage-based, flat fee to tiered, or freemium to value-metric — while minimizing churn, preserving NRR, and accelerating revenue capture. Use it when the board has approved a pricing transformation, when unit economics signal the current model is leaving money on the table, or when a competitive shift forces a pricing response.

## Success Metrics

- **Zero surprise churn**: Every churned account should have had at least 2 touchpoints before churning — no account should cite "I didn't know" as the reason
- **NRR preservation**: Post-migration NRR should be within 5 percentage points of pre-migration NRR at the 90-day mark
- **Migration completion rate**: >90% of accounts actively migrated (not relying on auto-migration) within 60 days of go-live
- **Expansion signal**: At least 20% of migrated accounts show usage-triggered expansion within 6 months
- **Internal adoption**: All CSMs and AEs complete enablement training before migration begins

## Troubleshooting

**Problem:** Customers are requesting exceptions beyond the defined grandfathering policy, and the CS team is approving them inconsistently.
**Solution:** Publish the exception decision matrix internally and create a Slack channel or ticket workflow where all exception requests are reviewed by the VP of Revenue before approval; track exception rates by CSM to identify pattern-based training needs.

**Problem:** The migration email sequence is generating high open rates but low response / action rates — customers are reading but not acting.
**Solution:** Add a personalized subject line with the customer's account name and a concrete number ("Your Nexus account impact: estimated $2,400/year savings"); add a calendar booking link directly in the email body rather than asking them to contact their CSM; A/B test sending from the CSM's personal email vs. company domain.

**Problem:** The sales team is sandbagging renewals — holding deals until after migration go-live to avoid the pricing conversation.
**Solution:** Update the commission plan to treat migrated renewals at the same rate as new business for 90 days post-migration; add a SPIFF for AEs who proactively convert accounts to the new pricing before go-live; track and share a leaderboard of proactive migrations in the weekly revenue review.
