## Overview
Designs a complete channel partner pricing architecture — partner tier discounts, deal registration protection, channel conflict prevention, marketplace pricing, and co-sell incentive structures — that enables partner-sourced revenue without cannibalizing direct sales or destroying margins. Use it when launching a partner program, restructuring reseller discounts, entering cloud marketplaces, or resolving channel conflict that is causing direct-sales friction.

## Integration Tips

- **Salesforce CPQ / DealHub / Conga:** Configure partner tier discounts as locked price rules in CPQ. Set absolute margin floor as a hard stop — any quote below it triggers an approval workflow. Map deal registration status as a custom object linked to Account and Opportunity, with automatic discount application on approval.
- **Impartner / Allbound / PartnerStack:** Use your PRM (Partner Relationship Management) system to enforce tier status, gate deal registration by qualification criteria, and deliver MDF claim workflows. Sync PRM data to Salesforce daily so direct reps see partner pipeline alongside their own.
- **AWS Partner Network (APN) / Azure Partner Center / GCP Partner Advantage:** Enroll in co-sell tracks (AWS ACE, Microsoft Solution Provider, GCP co-sell) and configure your private offer engine. Set pricing templates in your marketplace seller portal to ensure list price parity. Use AWS CPPO (Channel Partner Private Offer) for reseller-issued private offers.
- **HubSpot / Salesforce (for comp):** Create a custom deal source field (Direct / Partner-Assisted / Partner-Sourced / Marketplace) and configure your comp admin system to pay appropriate commission splits based on this field. Automate audit emails to AE and partner when deal source changes post-registration.
- **Stripe / Chargebee:** If partners bill customers directly and remit to you, configure a reseller billing SKU with the appropriate net price. If you bill customers directly and pay partner rebates separately, set up a separate partner rebate billing schedule synced to your quarterly close.
- **Notion / Confluence:** Publish the Partner Pricing Policy as a living document. Tag each section with an "Owner" (VP Partnerships for deal reg policy; VP Sales for rules of engagement; Finance for margin floor). Schedule a quarterly review in your partner advisory board calendar.

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Direct reps refuse to accept co-sell arrangements even when a partner registered the deal first.**
Solution: This is a compensation and culture issue masquerading as a pricing policy problem. The root cause is almost always that direct reps earn zero or reduced commission on partner deals, making them feel like partners are "stealing" their quota. Fix the comp plan first: direct reps should earn at minimum 25-50% of their normal commission on partner-registered deals where they provide any material assistance. Then audit the last 10 channel conflicts — most will resolve when reps see the comp math working in their favor. Update the escalation policy to include an SLA where failure to respond within 48 hours means auto-enrollment as co-sell participant (removes the incentive to stonewall).

**Problem: Our top reseller is threatening to leave because they claim our partner margins don't cover their cost to serve.**
Solution: Request a cost-to-serve analysis from the partner: what's their average deal cycle, how many hours do they invest pre-sale, what's their implementation cost? Then benchmark their claimed economics against industry norms (mid-market B2B SaaS resellers typically need 25-35% gross margin to cover cost of sales). If they are genuinely margin-negative, you have three options: (1) increase their tier discount, (2) compensate with MDF tied to pipeline generation to reduce their cost-to-serve, or (3) restructure their deal registration to include faster protection windows that improve their win rate. Avoid blanket discount increases as these set precedent — use targeted MDF and deal reg improvements first.

**Problem: A partner is pricing our product below our floor in their customer quotes.**
Solution: This is a contract violation, not just a policy problem. Your partner agreement must explicitly state the minimum resale price (the floor) and the consequences of violation. Immediate response: (1) notify the partner in writing via their partner manager, (2) suspend their deal registration access for 30 days on first offense, (3) escalate to VP Partnerships who will hold an executive review with the partner's leadership. If the partner continues, invoke the termination clause with a 90-day notice. The threat of losing partner status is more powerful than any fine — use it. Long-term prevention: lock the floor in your partner portal's quoting tool so that quotes below the floor simply cannot be generated.
