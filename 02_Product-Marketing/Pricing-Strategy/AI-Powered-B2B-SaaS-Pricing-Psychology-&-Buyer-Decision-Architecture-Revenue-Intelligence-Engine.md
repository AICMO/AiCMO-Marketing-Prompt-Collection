## Overview
Applies behavioral economics, prospect theory, and decision science to design a B2B SaaS pricing presentation strategy that reduces cognitive friction, neutralizes loss aversion, and increases perceived value — without changing a single dollar of your actual price. Use this when pricing page conversion is stagnant, sales reps lose too many deals at the price reveal, or when you're launching a new pricing tier and want it to feel inevitable rather than expensive.

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Reps feel the psychological framing is manipulative and resist using it.**
Solution: Reframe internally — behavioral economics describes how human decisions actually work, not how to trick people. Share Kahneman's research on System 1 vs. System 2 thinking with the sales team. Every pricing presentation already uses psychology (whether intentionally or not). This playbook makes those choices intentional and calibrated to help buyers make decisions that are genuinely in their interest. Role-play the objection scenarios and let reps experience how anchoring actually reduces friction for the buyer, not just for the seller.

**Problem: The loss aversion framing feels too negative and buyers disengage.**
Solution: Loss aversion statements work best when they're factual and buyer-acknowledged, not salesperson-asserted. Instead of telling buyers what they're losing, ask questions that lead them to articulate the loss themselves: "What's the cost if this problem stays unsolved for another year?" Buyer-generated loss statements are 3-5x more persuasive than seller-generated ones. Adjust the scripts to use questions that surface the loss rather than declarations that state it.

**Problem: The decoy tier recommendation requires a product/pricing change we can't execute quickly.**
Solution: You can create a "psychological decoy" in your proposal without changing your published tiers. In a custom proposal, present three package options: a reduced-scope version at lower price (your existing Starter/Pro), your current recommended tier, and a fully loaded version with premium services at a higher price. The premium option serves as the anchor even if it's not a published SKU — it's a custom scoped proposal option that makes the middle look like the obvious choice.
