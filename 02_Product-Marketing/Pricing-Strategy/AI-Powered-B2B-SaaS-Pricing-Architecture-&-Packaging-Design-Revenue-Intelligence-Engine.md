## Integration Tips

- **Stripe / Paddle / Chargebee**: Map each tier directly to a product ID in your billing system. Use the add-on architecture to create modular billing objects that can be attached at checkout.
- **HubSpot / Salesforce**: Create deal stage properties tied to pricing tier interest. Route deals to the correct sales sequence based on which tier a prospect engaged with on the pricing page.
- **Figma / Webflow**: Use the pricing page copy architecture to brief designers — provide headline, sub-headline, CTA copy, and FAQ content as a single handoff document.
- **Gong / Chorus**: Tag calls where pricing came up with the competitive positioning narrative labels. Track which responses correlated with closed-won outcomes.
- **Mixpanel / Amplitude**: Instrument your product to fire events at 70% and 90% of usage limits — feed these into your email automation to trigger upgrade nudge campaigns.
- **Notion / Confluence**: Store the feature fencing decisions and their rationale in a pricing decision log — update quarterly with win/loss data to track which fencing decisions drove conversions vs. churn.
- **Zapier / Make**: Automate pricing page CTA routing — if a visitor clicks "Enterprise" CTA, trigger a Salesforce lead record creation and notify the relevant AE within 5 minutes.

## Troubleshooting

**Issue: The output tiers feel generic — not differentiated enough from competitors**
Solution: In the Advanced version, add verbatim competitor pricing page URLs and the exact language from their tier descriptions. Ask the engine to explicitly contrast each tier decision against competitor equivalents, citing specific feature differences rather than general positioning language.

**Issue: Sales team isn't using the positioning narrative, reverting to ad-hoc discounting**
Solution: Extract only Deliverable 8 (Competitive Positioning Overlay) and run it through a separate prompt asking it to rewrite each narrative as a 30-second verbal response with a confidence-first structure. Turn the output into a Gong snippet library or Highspot card so reps can pull it in-call.

**Issue: Pricing tiers work for new business but aren't driving expansion from existing customers**
Solution: Add to the Advanced prompt: "Design an expansion pricing motion specifically for customers who have been on Tier 1 for 6-18 months. Include: the in-app trigger event that initiates the upsell, the email sequence that supports it (3 touches), and the expansion CTA to present during QBRs." Feed usage data from your product database as context.
