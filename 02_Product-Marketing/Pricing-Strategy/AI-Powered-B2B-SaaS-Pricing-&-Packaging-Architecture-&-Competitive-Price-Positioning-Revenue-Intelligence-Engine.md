## Overview
Designs a complete SaaS pricing and packaging architecture — value metric selection, tier logic, price-to-win positioning, and pricing page psychology — that maximizes revenue capture across all customer segments. Use it when launching a new product, repositioning against aggressive competitors on price, adding a new tier, or running a pricing page overhaul.

**Output Example (Tier Architecture excerpt):**

**RECOMMENDED VALUE METRIC: Per Active User + Platform Fee**

Scoring winner: Per active user (not total seats) — aligns billing with actual value delivered. Customers with 200 employees but 40 expense filers pay for 40, not 200. This is a significant competitive differentiator against Expensify's all-seat billing and creates immediate budget justification.

**Tier Architecture:**

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: The tier recommendation doesn't map cleanly to our existing feature set.**
Solution: Run Deliverable 2 first with your current feature inventory pasted in as a constraint. Ask the model to "work within these existing features" before designing the tier gates. Then use the output to identify which features need to be built to make the packaging architecture work as designed.

**Problem: Sales team rejects the pricing because it's higher than what they're used to selling.**
Solution: Run Deliverable 3's "Price Objection Playbook" with your sales team before launch. The resistance is almost always fear of objections they don't know how to handle, not genuine belief the price is wrong. Role-play the 5 objection scenarios in your next sales call. Track win rates for the first 30 days post-launch before drawing conclusions.

**Problem: The output recommends usage-based pricing but our finance team says it creates unpredictable revenue.**
Solution: Add this constraint to your prompt: "Design a hybrid model — flat annual platform fee that covers base usage, with consumption-based overage above defined thresholds." This gives you the expansion revenue benefits of usage-based pricing with the ARR predictability your CFO needs. Most mature SaaS companies (Snowflake, Datadog, Twilio) use this hybrid model for exactly this reason.
