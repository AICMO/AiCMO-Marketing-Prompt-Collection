## Troubleshooting

**Problem:** Champion is enthusiastic, but internal communications aren't gaining traction.
**Solution:** The issue is almost always distribution authority, not content quality. Ensure the champion routes communications through a C-suite executive sponsor signature (CEO or COO). Pair every async email with a live session — async-only campaigns fail in complex organizations. Add manager accountability: each department manager should receive a weekly utilization report for their team with a clear target to hit.

**Problem:** IT security review is blocking rollout or creating months-long delays.
**Solution:** Proactively provide a "Security FastTrack Kit" before IT asks — pre-completed HIPAA/GDPR/SOC 2 risk assessments, BAA, penetration test summaries, and direct reference contacts at peer organizations who completed security review. Reduce the IT team's workload to near-zero. Establish a 5-business-day review SLA in writing with an executive escalation path if the review stalls. Assign your Security Architect to be available for a 30-minute technical call within 48 hours of request.

**Problem:** Adoption launches strong but stalls at 30-40% after initial excitement fades.
**Solution:** The "30-day dip" is universal — it signals absence of reinforcement, not bad product fit. Deploy the Day-30 momentum email on schedule, publicly celebrate the first cohort of power users by name, and schedule an "Advanced Workflows" session for early adopters to discover high-value features. The manager layer is usually the stall point — give each manager a simple weekly utilization dashboard and a 5-minute "team check-in" script to run in existing standups. Stalling signals more reinforcement is needed, not more initial communication.
