## Overview

Designs the 2–4 week internal revenue team readiness sprint that runs parallel to your launch execution — building battle cards, demo scripts, objection handlers, expansion plays, and training cadences so your AEs, SDRs, and CSMs can immediately capitalize on launch day momentum rather than scrambling to learn the new product post-announcement.

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Reps ignore launch enablement assets and default to old messaging**
Solution: This means the new assets aren't demonstrably better than what they already know. Do a live comparison in a team meeting: run a cold call using old messaging, then using launch messaging, and measure which gets a longer conversation. Make adoption a manager accountability metric by tracking "launch messaging usage rate" in your conversation intelligence tool. Create a public leaderboard showing which reps are booking the most demos using launch messaging — social proof and competition drive adoption faster than mandates.

**Problem: CSM expansion sequence gets low response rates from existing accounts**
Solution: CSMs who are over-quota-carrying (and feel like sales) will execute expansion plays reluctantly and it shows in the copy. Audit 5 of the sent emails — are they copying the template verbatim without personalization, or are they modifying with account-specific context? Require CSMs to add one sentence of genuine account-specific observation (e.g., "I noticed your team's call volume tripled in Q3 based on your usage data") before the template copy begins. Response rates typically jump 30–50% when CSMs add a single authentic personalization sentence.

**Problem: AEs pass certification but struggle in live customer conversations**
Solution: Certification tests knowledge — not skill. If reps know the answer but can't deliver it fluently under pressure, add a mandatory 30-minute recorded peer-to-peer practice session where one rep plays the skeptical buyer and the other handles objections live. Review sessions in your conversation intelligence tool and flag any rep where the "wow moment" isn't landing within the first 8 minutes of demo — those reps need one additional coaching session before running independent customer calls.
