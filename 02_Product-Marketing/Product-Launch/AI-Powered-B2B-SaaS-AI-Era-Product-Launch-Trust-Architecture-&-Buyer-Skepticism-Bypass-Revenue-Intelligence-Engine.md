## Troubleshooting

**Problem: "Our beta customers don't want to be named in case studies"**
Solution: Offer three proof tiers: (1) fully attributed with logo, (2) named executive quote but no logo (most common compromise), (3) anonymized with industry/size only. For tier 3, make up in specificity what you lose in attribution — "a 900-person SaaS company in ITSM reduced mean time to forecast from 4 days to 6 hours" is more credible than "an enterprise customer improved efficiency." Build contractual proof rights into your beta/early customer agreements from day one.

**Problem: "The AI objection handling playbook isn't being used by sales — it's too long"**
Solution: Compress the playbook into a "Skeptic Cheat Sheet" — one laminated card (or Notion page) with the top 3 objections and a two-sentence response + one proof asset link per objection. The 40-page playbook is for training; the cheat sheet is for live calls. Add a 10-minute "AI Objection Sprint" to your weekly AE standup — one objection handled in role-play, every week.

**Problem: "Our launch messaging is landing flat — buyers aren't engaging"**
Solution: Test your messaging in community before launch. Post the core value proposition as a genuine question in 2-3 relevant Slack communities or LinkedIn groups (not as a promotion — as a "we're thinking about this problem, does it resonate?"). If you get zero engagement, the message isn't landing. If practitioners respond with "this is a real problem," you've found resonance. This community pre-testing takes 2 weeks and costs nothing.
