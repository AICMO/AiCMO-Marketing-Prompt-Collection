## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Hacker News post gets less than 50 upvotes and minimal traction**
Solution: Analyze the post timing (best: Tuesday/Wednesday 8-10am ET), title framing (Show HN posts that describe the developer problem outperform those that describe the product), and comment quality in the first hour (HN algorithms favor posts with fast early engagement from real accounts). Repost with adjusted framing in 4-6 weeks — one failed HN launch doesn't define a product. Consider a "Tell HN: How we X" narrative post instead, which often generates more discussion than product launches.

**Problem: Developer signups are high but time-to-first-API-call is >48 hours, indicating DX friction**
Solution: Run a 5-person "getting started test" — have developers unfamiliar with your product attempt the getting started guide while screen-sharing, and note every point of confusion. The most common issues: authentication complexity (reduce to one copy-paste API key), missing "working example" code (add a 100% working curl command as the first example), and unclear prerequisite requirements (list exact versions of dependencies). A 10-minute improvement in time-to-first-API-call typically doubles 30-day activation rates.

**Problem: Developer community engagement drops off after launch week**
Solution: Launch weeks always spike and then drop — the goal is to establish a floor, not maintain a spike. Design a 90-day community health program: weekly office hours (developer Q&A live stream), bi-weekly "integration spotlight" (feature a community member's use case), monthly community challenge (build something interesting with X), and a quarterly "community roadmap" vote where members influence the product direction. Communities that give members real influence retain 3x better than communities that are primarily brand-managed channels.
