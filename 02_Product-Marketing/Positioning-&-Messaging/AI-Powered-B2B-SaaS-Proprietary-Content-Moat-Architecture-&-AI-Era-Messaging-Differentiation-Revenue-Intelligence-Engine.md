## Troubleshooting

**Problem: "We don't actually have any proprietary data — we're pre-product-market fit and can't access the customer data at scale."**
Solution: Shift to expertise moat content. Interview your 5 most experienced team members for their most specific, controversial operational beliefs. Publish one 1,500-word "Unpopular Opinion" piece per founder/executive per quarter. Originality of perspective is a proprietary asset even when data isn't available. Document your implementation failures, not just successes — most competitors only publish success stories.

**Problem: "Our content team passes the Anti-Generic Filter but the content still feels like everyone else's."**
Solution: The filter is necessary but not sufficient. Apply the "Bold Claim Test" — every piece must include at least one claim that a reasonable person in your industry would initially push back on. If your content generates zero controversy, it's creating zero signal. Audit the last 10 pieces: if zero generated LinkedIn comments disagreeing with your position, your editorial threshold is too cautious.

**Problem: "Competitors are copying our frameworks within weeks of us publishing them."**
Solution: Your Tier 1 structural moats are working but your Tier 2 expertise content is too easy to replicate. Shift Tier 2 content toward documented experiments with specific data ("We tested X vs. Y across 23 customer implementations and here's what we found") rather than frameworks alone. Experiment data is much harder to fake than frameworks. Also: publish your research methodology — competitors copying your conclusions without your methodology will produce visibly inferior outputs that damage their credibility.
