## Troubleshooting

**Problem: The AI output feels generic — "we're the only platform that..." clichés everywhere.**
Fix: Provide more specific win/loss data, actual customer quotes, and concrete competitive details in the CONTEXT section. Generic input produces generic output. Paste 3-5 verbatim customer quotes about why they chose you — the AI will mirror their language and produce far more distinctive copy.

**Problem: The positioning statement sounds like it could describe any SaaS company in the space.**
Fix: Run the "Would this statement also describe [Competitor A]?" test. If yes, the differentiation isn't sharp enough. Return to Step 1 and push harder on the Differentiation Mapping exercise — specifically, identify one "Breakthrough" differentiator (something competitors structurally cannot copy in 12 months) and build the positioning around it.

**Problem: Sales isn't adopting the new messaging — reps fall back to their old talk tracks.**
Fix: The output lacks persona-specific, conversational language. Rerun Step 3 (Buying Committee Message Map) with actual Gong/Chorus transcript examples for each persona included in the CONTEXT. Messaging that sounds like how buyers actually talk is adopted; messaging that sounds like a PMM wrote it at a desk is ignored.
