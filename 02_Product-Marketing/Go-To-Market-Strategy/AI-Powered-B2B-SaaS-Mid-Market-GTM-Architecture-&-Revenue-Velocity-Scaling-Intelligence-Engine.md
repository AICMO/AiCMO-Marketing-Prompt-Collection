## SECTION 2: MID-MARKET GTM MOTION DESIGN

### 2A. The Hybrid Inside-Sales + Digital Demand Architecture
Design the motion that combines marketing-generated demand with SDR-led outbound in a coordinated, non-duplicative system:

**Marketing's Role:**
- Creates awareness and intent before SDR ever sends an email (target: 60% of outbound prospects have seen 2+ marketing touches before first SDR contact)
- Generates inbound pipeline from content, paid, and community channels
- Runs account-level intent monitoring and serves account intelligence to SDRs in real time
- Owns the "Marketing-Assisted Outbound" (MAO) playbook: when marketing runs targeted ads to a prospect account at the same time the SDR is running an outbound sequence

**SDR's Role:**
- Executes outbound to trigger-event accounts identified by marketing
- Supplements inbound with multi-channel follow-up for high-intent web visitors
- Provides field intelligence back to marketing (what objections are they hearing / what content is resonating in conversations)

**Marketing-Assisted Outbound (MAO) Playbook:**
1. Marketing identifies trigger-event accounts and loads them into [intent data platform / 6sense / Bombora]
2. Marketing launches targeted LinkedIn ads + retargeting to contacts at that account (3-week campaign before SDR touches)
3. SDR begins outbound sequence in Week 4 with reference to content (not cold — warm)
4. Marketing monitors account-level engagement score; if score drops, ads refresh with new content angle
5. AE receives account intelligence brief before discovery call with top 3 conversation starters based on account's engagement history

**Handoff Criteria (Marketing → SDR → AE):**
| Stage | Criteria | Owner | SLA |
|-------|---------|-------|-----|
| Marketing Qualified Account (MQA) | [Intent spike + ICP match + 2+ content engagements] | Marketing | Deliver to SDR within [4 hours] |
| Sales Development Qualified (SDQ) | [SDR has had 1 connected call / prospect replied to sequence / booked meeting] | SDR | Book meeting within [48 hours of MQA receipt] |
| Sales Accepted Lead (SAL) | [AE confirms: budget authority confirmed / use case is real / timeline is within 90 days] | AE | SAL within [24 hours of meeting held] |
| Sales Qualified Opportunity (SQO) | [Discovery complete / MEDDPICC criteria 70%+ populated / mutual action plan agreed] | AE | SQO within [5 business days of SAL] |

### 2B. The Mid-Market MEDDPICC Qualification Layer
Mid-market deals move fast but fail when qualification is skipped. Deploy a lightweight MEDDPICC framework calibrated for the mid-market context:

- **M — Metrics**: What 3 KPIs does the buyer use to measure success? Get these before the demo. (Mid-market buyers measure ROI in months, not years — ask "What does success look like in 6 months?")
- **E — Economic Buyer**: Has the AE spoken directly to the person who signs? In mid-market, this is often 1-2 levels up from the champion. If the AE hasn't spoken to the EB, the deal is not qualified.
- **D — Decision Criteria**: Get the formal and informal evaluation criteria in writing by end of discovery. Mid-market buyers frequently make the criteria list after talking to you — shape it early.
- **D — Decision Process**: Map the approval path. Mid-market = typically 2-3 approvals (champion + manager + finance). Identify if IT/Security sign-off is required. If yes, add 14 days to cycle.
- **I — Identify Pain**: Quantify the pain in dollars. If the champion can't articulate the cost of inaction, they're not a champion — they're a contact.
- **C — Champion**: A champion can access the EB, speaks positively about you internally, and shares internal dynamics. In mid-market, champions are typically new hires trying to prove value in their role.
- **C — Competition**: Know who else is on the shortlist by end of discovery. If the prospect won't share who else they're evaluating, treat this as a risk flag.
- **P — Paper Process**: Get the contract review and signature process mapped by Week 2 of the deal. Mid-market contracts hang in legal even at $40K ACV. Ask: "If we reach agreement on terms, what does the signature process look like and who needs to sign?"

## SECTION 3: MID-MARKET DEMAND GENERATION ENGINE

### 3A. Channel Prioritization Matrix (Top 4 Channels for Mid-Market)

**Channel 1: LinkedIn Ads + Thought Leader Ads (Highest ROI for Mid-Market B2B)**
- Why this channel: Mid-market buyers (VP/Director level) are active LinkedIn users who consume industry content and vendor content before engaging with sales
- Specific tactics:
  - Thought Leader Ads (TLAs) from company executives (not the brand page) targeting lookalike audiences of current best customers
  - Retargeting campaigns for website visitors from ICP companies (use Clearbit/6sense for account-level targeting)
  - Sponsored content featuring customer case studies from companies the target company recognizes ("Similar companies to you achieve X in Y days")
  - LinkedIn Document Ads with downloadable ROI benchmark reports (gate to capture intent signal)
- Budget allocation recommendation: 35-45% of paid media budget
- Expected CPL: $[180-$350 for mid-market]
- Pipeline attribution: 45-day attribution window from ad click to opportunity creation

**Channel 2: Intent-Data-Driven Outbound (Signal-Based, Not Volume-Based)**
- Why this channel: Mid-market buyers research 3-6 vendors before engaging any. By the time they reach your website, they've already formed opinions. Intent data lets you intercept them before the shortlist forms.
- Specific tactics:
  - Layer 6sense / Bombora intent signals on top of your ICP list — prioritize accounts showing intent for your category keywords in last 30 days
  - Combine with trigger events (job changes, funding, competitor reviews) for multi-signal accounts
  - SDR sequences for multi-signal accounts achieve 2-3x reply rates vs. cold outbound
  - Email subject lines that reference the specific intent signal without revealing the data source: "Noticed [Your Company] is [growing quickly / re-evaluating their [category] stack] — most companies your size..." 
- Budget allocation: $18-35K/month for intent data platform(s)
- Expected CPL: $[220-400] blended for SDR-touched intent-sourced pipeline
- Pipeline attribution: First-touch or U-shaped (give intent data platform partial credit when signal predates outreach)

**Channel 3: Webinars + Virtual Events (Demand Capture + Relationship)**
- Why this channel: Mid-market buyers attend webinars to learn — they convert to pipeline when the webinar addresses a specific operational challenge they're actively trying to solve
- Specific tactics:
  - "Benchmark + Insight" webinar series: "State of [Category] for Mid-Market 2026" with original research (positions you as authority, attracts in-market buyers)
  - Customer panel webinars: 3 mid-market customers explain how they evaluated and implemented (use existing happy customers as co-hosts — turns them into advocates)
  - Competitor alternative webinar: "Evaluating [Category] Tools in 2026: What Mid-Market Finance Teams Need to Know" — attracts buyers actively shopping
  - Post-webinar ABM sequence: Every attendee from a Tier 1/2 target account gets a personalized follow-up from an SDR within 24 hours with account-specific resources
- Expected show rate: 35-45% of registrants
- Pipeline contribution: 15-25% of webinar attendees from ICP companies convert to pipeline within 60 days

**Channel 4: Field Events + Executive Dinners (High ACV Acceleration)**
- Why this channel: Upper mid-market buyers (ACV >$75K) are won in person. Digital campaigns create awareness; in-person events create urgency and accelerate close.
- Specific tactics:
  - Co-locate with 2-3 industry conferences per quarter (SaaStr / Dreamforce / industry-specific events) — sponsored dinner the night before main sessions
  - Host city-specific "CXO Peer Dinner" series (8-12 executive peers, facilitated discussion, no pitch) in 5-6 target cities/quarter
  - Sponsor a speaking slot at the event or run a "breakfast briefing" session for 30-50 attendees on a tactical topic
  - Follow-up: Every event attendee from a Tier 1 account receives a personalized "What we discussed" summary from the AE within 48 hours
- Cost per qualified conversation: $[800-$2,500] (high absolute cost but highest deal velocity)
- Pipeline attribution: Field-event sourced pipeline closes 30-40% faster than digital-only

### 3B. The Mid-Market Content Engine
Mid-market buyers are busier than enterprise buyers but more self-directed than SMB. They want proof and brevity:

**High-Conversion Content Types for Mid-Market:**
1. **One-Page ROI Snapshot** — "Companies like [Customer] achieved [Result] in [Timeframe]." One page, three data points, done. Used by AEs before demos and in sequences.
2. **Vertical-Specific Case Studies** — 400-600 words, same industry as prospect, emphasize implementation speed and team size (mid-market cares about ease of implementation, not enterprise feature depth)
3. **Competitive Comparison Guides** — "Why [Your Company] vs. [Competitor A] for [200-1,000 employee] Companies" — captures evaluation-stage buyers searching alternatives
4. **ROI Calculator** — Self-serve interactive tool showing payback period based on prospect's inputs (captures intent data, warms up SDR conversation: "I saw you ran our ROI calculator and estimated X — is that in the right range?")
5. **"30-Day Quick Win" Playbook** — Downloadable guide showing how a mid-market team gets value in the first 30 days (addresses #1 mid-market objection: implementation complexity)

## SECTION 4: ACCOUNT-BASED MARKETING AT SCALE (1:FEW)

### 4A. The Mid-Market ABM Tier Structure
Unlike enterprise ABM (1:1), mid-market ABM operates at 1:Few — batched personalization for groups of 20-50 similar accounts, not custom campaigns per account.

| Tier | Account Count | Personalization Depth | Marketing Investment/Account | SDR Coverage |
|------|--------------|----------------------|------------------------------|--------------|
| Tier 1 (Strategic Targets) | 25-50 accounts | Fully customized: personalized landing page, custom executive outreach, account-specific content | $[3,000-$5,000]/account | Dedicated SDR, AE-involved in outreach |
| Tier 2 (Priority Targets) | 100-150 accounts | Industry-personalized: vertical-specific sequences, industry webinar invites, segment-specific ads | $[800-$1,500]/account | SDR pool, 30-touch multi-channel sequence |
| Tier 3 (ICP Accounts) | 200-500 accounts | Persona-personalized: role-specific email nurture, retargeting ads, intent monitoring | $[200-$400]/account | Automated sequences, SDR only on intent spike |

### 4B. 60-Day Account Progression Blueprint (Tier 1)
Design the sequence that takes a cold Tier 1 account from zero awareness to booked discovery call:

**Weeks 1-2: Awareness & Intelligence**
- Launch targeted LinkedIn ads to 5-7 contacts at account (decision-maker + champion personas)
- SDR identifies champion and key contacts using LinkedIn Sales Navigator + Clay enrichment
- Marketing intelligence brief delivered to SDR: company news, tech stack, recent hiring, intent spikes
- No outreach yet — let ads run and build awareness before first contact

**Weeks 3-4: Warm-Up**
- SDR sends connection request to champion on LinkedIn with a relevant insight (not a pitch): "Noticed [Company] is [hiring for X / expanded into Y market] — wrote something relevant on [topic]"
- Marketing serves retargeting ads featuring a case study from the same industry as this account
- If champion engages with any content — record in CRM, trigger SDR email within 24 hours

**Weeks 5-6: Direct Outreach**
- SDR sends personalized email sequence (3-touch over 10 days):
  - Email 1: Insight-led, references a public challenge the company faces, offers relevant resource
  - Email 2: Case study from identical peer company with specific metric outcomes
  - Email 3: Direct ask for 20-minute exploratory call — reference what marketing ads they've seen (tastefully)
- Phone call attempt on Day 2 and Day 7 of email sequence
- LinkedIn DM on Day 5 if no reply to email

**Weeks 7-8: Executive Escalation**
- If no response from champion, AE sends executive-to-executive LinkedIn note to the economic buyer
- Marketing escalates: direct mail physical mailer to top 5 Tier 1 accounts with no response (personalized, physical, premium format)
- Last attempt: "Breaking up" email from SDR — sets a date after which they'll stop outreach for this quarter (creates urgency)

**Weeks 9+: Maintain, Monitor, Re-activate**
- Account moves to Tier 2 cadence: quarterly nurture, intent monitoring, webinar invitations
- Re-activate immediately on any trigger event (new hire, intent spike, competitor review)

### 6C. Forecasting Accuracy Model

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: SDRs are burning through ICP accounts and outreach isn't converting — reply rates under 3%**
Solution: The ICP list is likely too broad and lacks trigger-event filtering. Run the ICP Trigger-Event Filter from Section 1B: only outreach to accounts showing 2+ trigger events simultaneously. Also check the Marketing-Assisted Outbound timing — SDRs should not start cold outreach until accounts have seen at least 2 LinkedIn ad impressions over 2 weeks. If MAO isn't running, launch it before increasing outreach volume.

**Problem: Demo-to-close win rate is under 20% and no-decision is the most common loss reason**
Solution: The deal is not qualifying pain sharply enough in discovery. Enforce the MEDDPICC "I — Identify Pain" step: before any demo, AEs must have a quantified pain statement agreed to by the champion ("Our current process costs us approximately $[X] in [wasted time / errors / rework] per month"). If the champion can't quantify the pain, they are not a champion — escalate to find one who can. Also implement the POC/Quick Win offer from the Fieldnote example: replace full-scope demos with a scoped 30-day proof of value on one use case.

**Problem: Mid-market accounts are going cold after the demo — AE loses contact with the buying committee**
Solution: Deploy a "Champion Enablement Kit" immediately after every demo: a short deck (5 slides max) the champion can use to present the case internally, a 1-pager comparison vs. the status quo (not vs. a competitor), and an ROI calculation pre-populated with numbers from the discovery call. The champion's job is to sell your product internally — make it as easy as possible. Add a weekly check-in calendar invite with the champion (optional but marked "brief touchpoint") for the first 4 weeks after the demo to maintain momentum without being pushy.
