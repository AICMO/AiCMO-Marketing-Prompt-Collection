## Troubleshooting

**Problem:** Suppliers join and get verified but never complete their profile or post their first listing
**Solution:** Implement an "Activation Score" (0-100) that tracks listing completeness — require a minimum score of 60 for public visibility. Deploy an automated 3-email onboarding sequence triggered at days 1, 4, and 7 post-verification with specific completion instructions. If supplier hasn't reached score 60 by day 10, trigger an AI-generated "Profile Review" that audits their incomplete sections and sends a personalized gap analysis. Track this metric weekly as "Supplier Activation Rate."

**Problem:** Buyers register and browse but don't initiate a transaction (window shoppers with no conversion)
**Solution:** Analyze browse-without-transaction session recordings (Hotjar or FullStory) for behavioral patterns. The three most common causes: (a) Price uncertainty — add instant ballpark pricing estimates to search results, (b) Trust deficit — add buyer guarantee banner above the fold on all search and profile pages, (c) Request friction — if the request form is >5 fields, cut it to 3 and collect the rest post-match. A/B test each fix independently with a minimum 200-session test size before declaring a winner.

**Problem:** Supply liquidity is severely uneven — some categories have 20+ suppliers while others have 1-2, creating poor buyer fill rates in thin categories
**Solution:** Build a real-time "Category Heat Map" dashboard that flags any category with fewer than 5 qualified suppliers as "Constrained." Automatically trigger a targeted supply acquisition micro-campaign for constrained categories using the same outreach sequence from Campaign Blueprint 1, filtered specifically by constrained category expertise. In parallel, temporarily hide constrained categories from buyer search results (or add a "Notify me when available" waitlist mechanic) to prevent buyer trust damage from empty shelf experiences. The waitlist creates urgency for supplier recruitment — you can show suppliers "127 buyers waiting for suppliers in [category]."
