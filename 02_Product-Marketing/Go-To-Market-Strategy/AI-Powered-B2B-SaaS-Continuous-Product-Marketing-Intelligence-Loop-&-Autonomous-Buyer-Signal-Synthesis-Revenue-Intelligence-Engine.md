## Integration Tips

- **Salesforce / HubSpot CRM**: Export closed-won and closed-lost deal data monthly with custom fields for "Primary Win Reason" and "Primary Loss Reason" — make these required fields for AEs to populate before marking a deal closed. Feed this directly into Signal Layer 1.
- **Gong / Chorus / Clari**: Pull monthly "topics discussed" and "objections raised" reports from conversation intelligence tools. Many platforms have built-in analytics exports that map to Signal Layers 1 and 2.
- **Notion / Confluence PMM Wiki**: Create a live "PMM Intelligence Brief" page updated monthly. Use Zapier or Make to auto-populate deal data from CRM into a structured template. Tag each Intelligence Brief with date and link to resulting asset updates.
- **Slack PMM Intelligence Channel**: Pipe G2/Capterra new reviews, competitor LinkedIn mentions, and industry news via RSS/Zapier into a Slack channel. Pull from this channel for Signal Layer 3 and 4 inputs monthly.
- **Google Alerts + SparkToro**: Set up Google Alerts for each competitor name + "pricing," "launch," "announcement" — feed these alerts into Signal Layer 4 competitor activity section monthly.
- **Typeform / Qualtrics Win/Loss Survey**: Build a 3-question post-deal survey automatically triggered when a deal closes (both won and lost). Auto-summarize responses with AI and inject into Signal Layer 1 each month.
- **Linear / Jira PMM Sprint Board**: Feed the "PMM Sprint Backlog" section output directly into your sprint board as tickets, with signal source linked as context for each ticket.

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Signal data is sparse — AEs aren't filling in win/loss reasons in CRM**
Solution: Start with just 3 data points: (1) primary win/loss reason (required CRM field with a dropdown, not free text), (2) which competitor appeared in the deal (required field), (3) one sentence on buyer's primary pain (optional free text). Layer complexity over 90 days. Until CRM data is reliable, run a 15-minute monthly "PMM Signal Sync" call with 3-5 AEs instead of relying on CRM exports alone.

**Problem: The output is too generic — recommendations don't feel specific enough**
Solution: The quality of output directly correlates with the specificity of customer verbatims in Signal Layer 3. Generic inputs produce generic outputs. Even 3-5 direct quotes from real customer conversations will dramatically sharpen the Buyer Language Harvest and Positioning Drift Analysis. If verbatims aren't available, pull them from Gong call transcripts, NPS survey responses, or CS hand-off notes from the past 30 days.

**Problem: PMM team doesn't have time to run this monthly**
Solution: This prompt is designed to be run in one 90-minute working session once per month. Reduce the signal collection burden by assigning 15-minute monthly responsibilities: Sales Ops owns CRM data export, one AE owns sales team intelligence, one CSM owns customer verbatims, one PMM owns market intelligence monitoring. Rotating the collection task distributes load while keeping the synthesis in PMM hands.
