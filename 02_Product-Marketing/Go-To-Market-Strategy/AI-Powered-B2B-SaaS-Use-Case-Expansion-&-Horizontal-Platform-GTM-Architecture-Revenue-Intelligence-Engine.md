**6-month scale trigger:**
- 10+ logos closed in new use case
- Average ACV ≥ 80% of core use case ACV
- NPS from new use case customers ≥ 55
- 30%+ of new use case pipeline is coming from internal expansion (existing customers)

**Ongoing platform health metrics:**
- Multi-use-case attach rate (% of customers using 2+ use cases): Target 40%+ within 18 months of expansion launch
- NRR for customers using 2+ use cases vs. 1 use case (should be 15-20 points higher)

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Sales reps default to selling the core use case even when calling on new personas.**
Solution: This is a training and incentive issue, not a PMM issue. Implement two fixes simultaneously: (1) Create a specific "new use case discovery question framework" that reps can use as a script — do not ask them to wing it. (2) Temporarily adjust quota credit to give 1.5x quota credit for new use case deals during the validation phase. Money changes behavior faster than training.

**Problem: New persona buyers are confused about why a "legal AI tool" (or whatever core positioning is) is calling them.**
Solution: Your platform narrative architecture has a coherence gap. Pause outbound until you've updated your positioning hierarchy. The new persona should arrive at your website and immediately see content written for them. The homepage should not scream "legal AI" if you're selling to CS leaders. Add a "For CS Teams" page, update nav to show "Solutions" with use case specific pages, and brief all reps on the new positioning before re-launching. If the problem persists, consider whether you need a distinct sub-brand or product line name for the expansion use case.

**Problem: Expansion pipeline is growing but deals are taking 3x longer to close than core use case deals.**
Solution: Diagnose whether the delay is at discovery, evaluation, or procurement. If discovery is slow: your ICP for the new persona is too broad, you're talking to tire-kickers — tighten your trigger signals. If evaluation is slow: you lack sufficient proof for the new persona's specific risk concerns — invest in 1-2 detailed case studies with hard numbers. If procurement is slow: you're selling to a new budget owner who doesn't control discretionary spend, and this may require a different deal structure (e.g., add-on to existing contract vs. new contract) or executive sponsorship from the original buyer to smooth the path.
