## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Partner alliance manager is unresponsive and co-marketing never gets traction.**
Solution: Bypass the alliance manager by building relationships directly with 3-5 partner solutions consultants or pre-sales engineers who close deals with your joint ICP. Give them a deal win (refer a customer to them) before asking for co-marketing support. Bottom-up momentum in partner ecosystems accelerates top-down program approval faster than any formal proposal.

**Problem: Joint co-marketing assets feel generic and don't resonate with partner's customers.**
Solution: Interview 3 shared joint customers for 20 minutes each before building any asset. The most valuable copy is direct quotes about the workflow they had before the integration vs. after. Generic "better together" messaging loses to specific before/after customer stories with numbers. Your best co-marketing asset is always a 1-page case study with a name, logo, and three quantified outcomes.

**Problem: MDF budget gets approved but then pipeline attribution is disputed — partner claims credit, you claim credit, nobody trusts the data.**
Solution: Agree on attribution rules IN WRITING before spending a single dollar of MDF. Document: what counts as "partner-sourced" (partner intro = partner sourced), what counts as "partner-influenced" (partner touched but you found the lead), and what happens with co-sell deals where both teams worked the account. Use a shared Google Sheet updated weekly as the single source of truth both teams sign off on monthly.
