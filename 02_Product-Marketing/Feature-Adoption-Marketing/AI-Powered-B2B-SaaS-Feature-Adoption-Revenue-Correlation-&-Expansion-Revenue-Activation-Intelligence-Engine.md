## Troubleshooting

**Problem: We don't have granular product analytics data (no Amplitude/Pendo — just login counts).**
Solution: Start with the data you have. Login frequency, support ticket themes, and NPS verbatims can serve as proxy signals for feature adoption. Build a simplified version of the ERS using 5 signals (logins/week, support ticket topic, NPS score, seats used, days since last login). Add richer feature-level data as you instrument your product properly. Even a basic 5-signal model will outperform zero-signal marketing.

**Problem: Product and Marketing don't agree on which features are "power features" — product team says everything is equally important.**
Solution: Run the correlation analysis with your existing data rather than debating it. Pull your 20 accounts with highest expansion MRR over the last 18 months and your 20 accounts with the lowest (or churned). Compare their feature usage profiles. The differences will be obvious and data-backed — this removes the opinion from the conversation and makes the power feature list defensible.

**Problem: Our CSMs feel threatened by automated expansion campaigns — they worry marketing is "going around them."**
Solution: Design the system as CSM-enhancement, not CSM-replacement. Every automated campaign should include a CSM notification that fires before or simultaneously with the customer outreach. Give CSMs the option to suppress any automated message if they're actively working the account. Frame the ERS as a tool that gives CSMs visibility into accounts they wouldn't have otherwise flagged for expansion — not as something that removes their judgment from the process.
