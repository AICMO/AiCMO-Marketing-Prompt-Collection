## Overview
This prompt builds an AI-powered intelligence system that mines your product usage data to identify adoption patterns competitors cannot replicate, then converts those patterns into quantified differentiation proof, updated battlecards, and competitive campaign briefs. Use it when you're losing deals on "we're all basically the same" comparisons, or when your competitive positioning relies on feature assertions rather than customer outcome evidence.

## Integration Tips

- **Amplitude / Pendo / Mixpanel:** Build saved reports for each Tier 1/2 feature with adoption depth metrics (DAU/MAU ratio, weekly session frequency per seat, workflow integration depth). Export quarterly to refresh competitive claims.
- **Gong / Chorus:** Use call intelligence to track how often sales reps use adoption proof language in competitive deals and correlate with win rate. Create a "competitive differentiation" call library of winning conversations.
- **Salesforce:** Add a "Competitive Deal: Adoption Proof Used" field to opportunity records. Track which specific proof assets were used and cross-reference against deal outcome. Run quarterly analysis to identify highest-impact proof content.
- **G2 / Capterra API:** Automate quarterly mining of competitor reviews using keyword searches for your differentiated features. Tools like Phantombuster, Clay, or Bardeen can automate collection; AI can classify and summarize themes.
- **Highspot / Seismic / Showpad:** House adoption-proof battlecards as "living documents" with auto-expiration reminders 90 days after last data refresh. Track rep usage by competitive deal type.
- **Clay / Apollo enrichment:** Identify accounts in your TAM currently using competitor products via technographic data. Route these accounts into competitor-targeted campaigns that lead with adoption benchmark data.
- **Notion / Confluence:** Build a competitive intelligence wiki with adoption data, proof claims, and source evidence — structured so any PMM or rep can find the exact claim and data source within 60 seconds.

## Troubleshooting

**Problem:** Product analytics data shows feature adoption rates but we can't link adoption to business outcomes (renewals, expansion, win rate).
**Solution:** Build a data join in your data warehouse or BI tool (Looker, Tableau, Sigma) linking product analytics user IDs to Salesforce account IDs. Even a basic cohort comparison — "accounts with Tier 1 adoption renewed at X% vs. Y% overall" — is sufficient to build the first proof claim. Start with renewal rate and NRR as the outcome metrics; both are reportable without complex attribution.

**Problem:** Competitors have similar feature names on their website and reps can't articulate why our "adoption depth" matters if the feature technically exists in both products.
**Solution:** Shift the framing from feature existence to feature outcome: "Both products have a revenue forecast module. But 82% of our customers use ours in their weekly exec review — because it updates automatically from CRM and call data, with zero manual input. If your team doesn't use the feature, the feature doesn't exist." Have reps ask in competitive deals: "Can [Competitor] show you customer evidence that their [feature] is used this frequently? Not a screenshot — an adoption benchmark?" This reframes the conversation from spec sheets to usage proof.

**Problem:** We don't have enough customer data points to publish a statistically valid benchmark report in year one.
**Solution:** Start with a hybrid data approach: combine your internal product telemetry (even 50+ accounts is enough for directional benchmarks with appropriate caveats) with a customer survey (email 200 customers, target 80+ responses) and publicly available data from G2, Capterra, and relevant industry surveys. Frame it as "based on data from [N] professional services teams and [N] G2 reviews" — transparency about sources builds credibility. Publish volume 1 now; let the data grow annually.
