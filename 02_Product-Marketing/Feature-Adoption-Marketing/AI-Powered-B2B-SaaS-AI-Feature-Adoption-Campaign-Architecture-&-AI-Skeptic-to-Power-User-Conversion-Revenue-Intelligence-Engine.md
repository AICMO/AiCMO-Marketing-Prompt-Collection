## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Users open AI adoption emails but don't activate the feature.**
Solution: The gap is between intent and action. Add a deeplink that takes the user directly to the AI feature pre-loaded with their real data (not a demo or empty state). Every extra click after the email reduces activation by ~40%. Work with product/engineering to create a "one-click AI launch" URL tied to the specific user's context.

**Problem: High trial rate but abandonment after 2-3 uses ("tried it, didn't stick").**
Solution: Users are likely hitting the "review tax" — it feels like more work to check AI output than just do the task. Shift messaging from "AI saves you time" to "AI helps you avoid the mistake you'd never catch until it was too late." Loss aversion is 2× more motivating than efficiency gains for this segment. Also, ensure the UI shows what the AI prevented (e.g., "This AI flag caught a deal going dark — you would have noticed in 11 days"), not just what it did.

**Problem: CS team doesn't have time to run the AI adoption program alongside their regular responsibilities.**
Solution: Create a "minimal CS lift" version of the program: fully automated email sequences require zero CS action, with CS only involved at two points: (1) responding to replies and (2) extracting an AI win story during a QBR that was already scheduled. Provide CSMs with a 1-page "AI talking points" sheet they can use when AI naturally comes up in conversation. The goal is to make the program invisible to CS, not add to their plate.
