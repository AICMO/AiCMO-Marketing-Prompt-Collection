## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Customers agree verbally but never complete the review**
Solution: The biggest drop-off is between agreement and action. Deploy a two-step process: (1) Send the review link immediately via email during or directly after the conversation while the customer is engaged; (2) set a CSM follow-up reminder for 5 days later to personally check in and offer to answer any questions about the review process. Always include the platform's direct review link with the customer's work email pre-populated if possible. Some platforms allow you to pre-fill reviewer details to reduce friction.

**Problem: Review quality is thin — customers write 2-3 sentence reviews that don't help in enterprise evaluations**
Solution: Create a one-page "Review Guide" PDF that explains what makes a helpful review for their peers (longer reviews with specific use cases, quantified outcomes, and implementation experience are more useful to other buyers — frame this as helping other data/security/ops professionals in their shoes). Share this guide with the direct review link and a note: "The most impactful reviews mention the specific problem you were solving, the ROI or time saved, and what the implementation experience was like." Never tell them what rating to give.

**Problem: We're getting reviews but mostly from our smallest customers, not enterprise accounts that Gartner weights**
Solution: GPI explicitly weights reviewers from larger organizations in the "Distinguished Vendor" calculation. Re-segment your Tier 1 outreach list to prioritize accounts with 1,000+ employees. If these accounts have lower NPS or usage scores, this is a signal to engage CS leadership in customer health recovery before asking for reviews. Consider an "Enterprise Advisory Review" program: offer select enterprise customers a 1:1 call with your CPO to discuss the product roadmap in exchange for their commitment to write a GPI review. This is policy-compliant because the value exchange is access and influence, not compensation for the review itself.
