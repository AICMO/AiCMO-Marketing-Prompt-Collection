## Overview
This prompt engineers a complete B2B SaaS pricing and packaging architecture — from value metric selection and willingness-to-pay research through package design, price point optimization, and sales discount governance. Use it when launching a new product, redesigning existing pricing, expanding to new segments, or adding an AI-powered tier. Pricing is the highest-leverage PMM activity: a 1% improvement in price realization generates 11% improvement in operating profit (McKinsey).

- Stage: Series B, $22M ARR, 340 customers

- Win rate vs. top competitor (Ironclad): 38%

**Output excerpt:**

**VALUE METRIC RECOMMENDATION:** Contracts processed per month (not seats)

*Rationale:* Seats penalizes small legal teams who process high volumes of high-value contracts — Veloq's highest-value customers. Contracts processed aligns revenue with the actual value delivered (legal risk reduction per contract). Ironclad uses seats; switching to contracts creates a differentiation story ("we charge for outcomes, not people") and captures more revenue from high-volume users while reducing churn from low-volume customers who currently feel per-seat is unfair.

**PACKAGE DESIGN:**

**Essentials** — $0.85/contract processed/month (minimum $299/month)
- Contracts up to 50/month, standard templates, e-signature, basic workflow
- AI review: 5 contract reviews/month (taste of AI, creates upgrade desire)
- Designed for: companies doing 30-80 contracts/month, no dedicated legal ops
- Upgrade trigger: hits 50 contract limit OR needs AI review beyond 5/month

**Professional** — $0.65/contract processed/month (minimum $899/month)
- Contracts up to 250/month, custom templates, approval workflows, Salesforce integration
- AI review: unlimited (this is the AI monetization tier)
- Clause library, risk scoring dashboard, reporting
- Designed for: companies doing 80-300 contracts/month with dedicated legal ops
- Upgrade trigger: multi-team management, SSO, audit requirements

**Enterprise** — Starting at $36,000/year (custom contract)
- Unlimited contracts, unlimited AI review
- SSO, RBAC, audit logs, SLA guarantee, dedicated CSM, custom integrations
- Designed for: 500+ employee companies with complex legal workflows
- Minimum: 300+ contracts/month or $36K ACV

**AI TIER RECOMMENDATION:** Bundle unlimited AI review into Professional and Enterprise (Option A)
- Rationale: AI review is Veloq's primary competitive differentiator. Gating it as an add-on reduces trial conversion and creates friction. Make it unlimited in Pro+ to accelerate upgrade from Essentials and justify price premium over Ironclad. Cost to serve AI is $0.08/contract — acceptable margin at Professional pricing.

**ASP IMPACT PROJECTION:**
- Current blended ASP: $112/seat/month (after 26% avg discount)
- Projected blended ASP under new model: $0.55/contract average (blended with minimums) → $940/month for a typical 150-contract/month Mid-Market customer vs. current $900 (5-6 seats × $150)
- High-volume customers (500+ contracts/month): $2,750/month vs. current $1,800 (12 seats)
- Revenue uplift from repricing high-volume segment: estimated +$1.8M ARR in year 1

## Integration Tips

**HubSpot / Salesforce CRM:**
- Create custom deal fields for: Discount Justification Code, Discount % Approved By, Tier Sold, Contract Term
- Build dashboards tracking ASP by rep, segment, and deal source — review in weekly pipeline calls
- Set up automated flag when any deal exceeds discount authority threshold (e.g., >20% flags to Sales Manager in Slack via Zapier)

**Chargebee / Stripe Billing:**
- Configure usage-based metering at the value metric level before launch — requires 60-90 days lead time
- Set up automated usage alerts to customers approaching plan limits (trigger upgrade conversations before they hit the wall)
- Grandfathering: tag legacy customers in billing system and exclude from automatic price increase rules

**Notion / Confluence (Internal Pricing Hub):**
- Maintain a living pricing page with: current price points, discount matrix, approved justifications, competitive pricing tracker
- Update quarterly — pricing is never "set and forget"
- Link from sales playbook so reps always access current version

**Gong / Chorus (Conversation Intelligence):**
- Tag calls where pricing is mentioned as a primary topic
- Analyze: at what deal stage is pricing first discussed, which objections appear most, which reps handle pricing objections best
- Use successful pricing conversations as coaching material for new reps

**G2 / Trustpilot:**
- Monitor reviews mentioning "price" or "expensive" — early indicator of price sensitivity before it becomes churn
- Respond to pricing objections in public reviews with value narrative

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Sales team resists new pricing and continues to discount at old levels**
Fix: The discount governance framework only works if leadership enforces it publicly. Run a pricing launch SKO session (use the Sales Kickoff prompt in Sales Enablement) with role-played objection handling at list price. Share weekly ASP leaderboard — positive peer pressure works. Make the first quarter a "soft launch" with coaching, not punishment, then enforce authority matrix strictly from Q2.

**Problem: Willingness-to-pay research shows prices are too high but leadership doesn't want to reduce**
Fix: Reframe research as segmentation data, not a ceiling. If SMB WTP is $299/month but Enterprise WTP is $3,000/month, the answer is better segmentation (SMB-only tier or freemium funnel) not a single price reduction. Pricing is not one number — it's a portfolio. Also validate whether survey respondents matched your actual ICP (respondents often understate WTP by 20-30% vs. actual purchase behavior).

**Problem: Existing customers are angry about transition to new pricing model (e.g., seats to usage-based)**
Fix: Grandfather existing customers at current pricing for minimum 12 months with advance notice. Provide a conversion calculator showing what they'd pay under new vs. old model — if the new model is better for them, lead with that data. For customers who'd pay more, offer a multi-year lock at old pricing to convert annual contract before transition. Frame the transition as "new customers pay by [VALUE METRIC], you keep what you have."
