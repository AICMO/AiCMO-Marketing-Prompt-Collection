## Overview
This prompt architects a complete outcome-based pricing model — from defining measurable customer outcomes and designing guarantee structures through risk-sharing mechanics, pricing floors, cap architecture, and the sales motion required to sell pay-for-results contracts. Use it when you want to differentiate on value certainty, accelerate enterprise deals by eliminating budget risk, or monetize AI-driven results that are now reliably measurable. Outcome-based pricing is the fastest-growing pricing motion in B2B SaaS (2025-2026): companies that tie price to outcomes report 23% higher win rates and 2.1x higher NRR vs. seat-based peers — but a poorly designed results model creates financial exposure, legal complexity, and customer disputes that destroy trust and margin.

- Structure: Customer pays [X%] of dollar value of outcomes delivered

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: "We can't establish a clean baseline — customers don't have historical data in a consistent format"**
Fix: Design a 30-day "Baseline Discovery Sprint" as part of the pre-contract process — a paid, time-bounded engagement ($5,000-$15,000 depending on complexity) where you instrument the customer's environment, pull the baseline data, and co-sign it before the main contract begins. This converts a data problem into a paid service motion and ensures baseline integrity. Customers who won't invest in a baseline sprint are often the same ones who would dispute outcome measurements later — qualifying them out protects you.

**Problem: "Sales team reverts to fixed pricing under deal pressure because outcome-based deals take longer to close"**
Fix: This is an incentive and training problem. Three interventions: (1) Pay AEs 15-20% higher commission rate on outcome-based deals vs. equivalent fixed deals — the ACV premium funds this. (2) Create a "guaranteed ACV" commission rule: AEs earn commission on guaranteed floor ACV at signing, with a bonus payout when outcome threshold is verified (aligns rep incentives with customer success). (3) Remove the option to quote fixed pricing for deals >$50K ACV without VP approval — scarcity drives behavior change faster than training.

**Problem: "A customer is claiming they achieved the outcome independently of our product and wants to dispute the attribution"**
