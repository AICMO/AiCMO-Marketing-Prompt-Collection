# AI-Powered B2B SaaS Multi-Year Contract Marketing & Annual Commitment Upgrade Revenue Intelligence Engine - Convert Annual Customers to 2-3 Year Commitments Before Renewal

## Overview
This prompt designs and executes a proactive multi-year contract marketing program — identifying which customers are upgrade-ready, what incentives convert them, and how to run AI-orchestrated campaigns that move customers from annual to 2-3 year commitments 90-180 days before renewal. Use it when your CFO wants more ARR predictability, when your churn risk is rising, or when you're preparing for a fundraise or M&A event that rewards revenue quality over short-term bookings. Multi-year contracts reduce effective churn by 3-5x, improve CAC payback periods, and add 15-25% to company valuation multiples.

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Customers say "yes" to multi-year pitch but deals stall at legal/procurement for months**
Solution: Develop a pre-negotiated "standard multi-year addendum" with pre-approved language for 80% of contract scenarios — reduces legal back-and-forth from 6 weeks to 1 week. Work with your legal team in Q1 to create a self-serve contract template for deals under $150K ACV. For enterprise, assign a dedicated contract coordinator who owns the admin track while commercial team focuses on relationship.

**Problem: Multi-year conversion rates are high but GRR improvement isn't materializing (customers are still churning at term end)**
Solution: Multi-year contracts compress but don't eliminate churn — they shift it to commitment end. If you're converting customers who are actually at-risk and using multi-year discounts as retention tools, you've created a 2-3 year delayed churn problem. Audit your readiness scoring model: ensure Risk tier customers are excluded from multi-year campaigns entirely. Introduce a "health floor" rule: no multi-year offers for any account below 70/100 health score, regardless of renewal date proximity.

**Problem: Sales team is underperforming on multi-year conversion — CSMs don't want to push customers**
Solution: This is a framing problem, not a sales problem. Reframe the program internally: multi-year is a customer benefit program, not a sales technique. Give CSMs economic proof that multi-year customers are better customers (higher NPS, lower escalations, more expansion, more referrals). Introduce a "multi-year bonus" for CSMs tied to program enrollment, not just AE quota, so the incentive aligns with the relationship ownership. Most importantly: replace "pitch" language with "offer" language in all internal enablement.
