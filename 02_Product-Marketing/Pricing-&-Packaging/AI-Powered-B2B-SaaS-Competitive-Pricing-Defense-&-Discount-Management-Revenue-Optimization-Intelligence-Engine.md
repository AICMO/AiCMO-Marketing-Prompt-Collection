## Integration Tips

- **Salesforce**: Build the discount authorization matrix as a Salesforce Approval Process with auto-routing based on Opportunity discount field. Use custom fields to capture discount justification taxonomy for analytics.
- **Gong/Chorus**: Create Smart Trackers for competitor name mentions + pricing language ("cheaper," "budget," "price," "too expensive"). Route flagged calls to managers for coaching.
- **Clay**: Build a workflow that monitors G2 reviews mentioning competitor pricing changes and routes alerts to the pricing team Slack channel weekly.
- **HubSpot**: Use Deal Properties to track discount depth and justification category. Build a Workflow that triggers a deal desk notification when discount exceeds threshold.
- **Google Sheets/Looker**: Build the weekly pricing analytics report as a connected sheet pulling from Salesforce via API. Automate the Monday morning email to revenue leadership with the prior week's pricing metrics.
- **Notion/Confluence**: Publish the objection response library as a searchable database with filters by competitor and objection type. Integrate with Guru or Highspot for in-the-moment rep access during calls.

## Troubleshooting

**Problem**: Reps ignore the discount authorization process and promise discounts verbally before approval, creating approval requests that are already committed.
**Solution**: Add a CRM field "Discount Discussed With Prospect" that auto-alerts the manager when a rep submits a discount approval after the verbal commitment stage. Train managers to redirect (not honor) unauthorized commitments. The key is consequence + process, not just process.

**Problem**: Competitive pricing objections feel like "we're just expensive" when reps try the TCO approach and buyers don't engage.
**Solution**: The TCO approach fails when delivered as a spreadsheet exercise. Reframe it as 3 questions in conversation (see the Business Case Builder above). If a buyer won't engage in a 3-question ROI conversation, the problem is not price — it's that the business case isn't compelling enough. Escalate to discovery, not discount.

**Problem**: The prompt generates generic objection responses that don't match our specific competitor's actual differentiators.
**Solution**: Feed the AI actual G2 review data from your competitor's page (copy 10-15 reviews mentioning price or limitations), your own Gong call transcripts where price was raised, and your 3 most recent competitive win/loss call summaries. The more specific the input, the sharper the counter-narrative output.
