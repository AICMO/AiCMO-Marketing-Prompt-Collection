## Overview
This prompt automates the deal desk function for B2B SaaS companies — from handling non-standard enterprise pricing requests through custom quote generation, multi-year ramp structuring, discount justification, and CFO-ready commercial proposals. Use it when your enterprise sales team faces complex pricing negotiations, custom bundling requests, multi-product deals, or any situation where standard pricing page tiers don't apply. Deal desk bottlenecks are the #1 cause of quarter-end revenue slippage in B2B SaaS companies above $10M ARR.

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Sales team bypasses deal desk and gives discounts verbally before formal approval**
Fix: This is a process enforcement issue, not a tool problem. Make unapproved discounts in contracts legally unenforceable by having Finance reject POs that don't match CRM-approved pricing. Run a "pricing integrity" training session at the next Sales QBR — frame it as protection for the rep ("if you promise something we can't deliver, it becomes a legal issue for you, not just a quota issue"). Publish a weekly leaderboard showing "ASP vs. list by rep" — social accountability is more effective than policy alone.

**Problem: Deal desk process adds too much friction and slows deal velocity**
Fix: The culprit is usually the approval chain, not the analysis. Streamline by (1) pre-approving a "standard deal desk playbook" — if the deal fits ramp template + expansion commitment, the AE can execute without VP approval, (2) setting hard SLAs: deal desk responds within 4 business hours or the AE gets auto-escalation authority, (3) building CPQ so that ramp deals and value-add deals auto-generate without deal desk review — only truly custom requests need human review.

**Problem: Multi-year ramp deals get signed but customers don't hit expansion milestones, making Year 2 step-up a renewal fight**
Fix: The expansion commitment was set incorrectly — either the milestone was unrealistic or CSM wasn't involved in structuring it. Fix prospectively: bring your CSM into every deal desk conversation so they validate that the expansion commitment is achievable. Fix retrospectively: for at-risk accounts, proactively renegotiate the Year 2 step-up 90 days before it hits — convert the risk into a "here's what we can do" conversation rather than a renewal crisis. Track "expansion milestone attainment rate" quarterly and use it to calibrate future ramp deal structures.
