## Integration Tips

**HubSpot:**
- Use custom contact properties: EHR Platform, Health System Type (IDN / community / academic), Bed Count, HIMSS Attendance Year — these enable segmentation for conference follow-up and persona-specific nurture
- Set up conference workflows: trigger sequences for contacts who scanned badge at HIMSS booth, with different tracks by buyer role captured at scan

**Salesforce Health Cloud:**
- Health Cloud's native care coordination data model can be extended with custom fields for marketing-sourced pipeline attribution — work with RevOps to map conference lead source codes to opportunity records
- Health Cloud's Account hierarchy maps naturally to IDN structure: Parent Account (health system) → Child Accounts (individual hospitals) — critical for ABM reporting at the system vs. facility level

**Definitive Healthcare / Strata Decision Technology:**
- Use Definitive Healthcare's API to enrich CRM accounts with bed count, system revenue, EHR platform, and key executive contacts — this enables account scoring without manual research
- Strata Decision's benchmarking data can inform which health systems are focused on cost reduction (your financial ROI message) vs. quality improvement (your clinical outcomes message)

**HIMSS Digital / HIMSS Analytics:**
- HIMSS Analytics EMRAM (Electronic Medical Record Adoption Model) scores are publicly available — target Stage 6-7 systems for advanced technology like ambient AI; target Stage 4-5 for interoperability and data analytics products
- HIMSS Digital's buyer's guide listing: submit product for inclusion in the official HIMSS conference app and attendee search directory

**Bombora for Healthcare Intent:**
- Bombora's healthcare technology intent topics (EHR Optimization, Clinical Documentation, Revenue Cycle Management, Remote Patient Monitoring) can be used to identify health systems actively researching your category — prioritize these accounts for outbound sequences in the weeks before HIMSS

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Case studies are generating opt-outs from clinical references who cite HIPAA concerns.**
Solution: Healthcare marketing teams frequently over-request from clinical references. Implement a tiered reference model: Tier 1 = anonymous quote (low ask, no approval friction), Tier 2 = named quote with legal review, Tier 3 = full case study with outcomes data. Always have legal pre-approve the anonymization language and send customers the final version for sign-off before publication. For AI-generated case study drafts, include explicit instruction: "Use only de-identified language — refer to the customer as 'a 350-bed regional health system' not by name unless [CUSTOMER LEGAL APPROVAL = YES]."

**Problem: The prompt generates generic messaging that doesn't reflect the specific clinical workflow problem.**
Solution: The advanced version requires specific product context to generate differentiated clinical messaging. Before running, complete a thorough internal interview: pull your top 3 customer win/loss interviews, identify the exact language customers used to describe the problem (not your language — their language), and paste those verbatim quotes into the Product Description field. The AI will mirror that language, which creates buyer-resonant messaging vs. vendor-speak.

**Problem: The conference strategy output doesn't account for the specific year's HIMSS conference format changes (virtual vs. in-person ratio, session track restructuring).**
Solution: HIMSS changes its conference format annually. Before running this prompt, visit HIMSS.org for the current year's exhibitor prospectus and session track descriptions. Paste the relevant sections into the prompt as additional context: "This year's HIMSS conference details: [paste prospectus excerpt]." The AI will then generate a conference strategy calibrated to the specific year's format rather than a historical template.
