## Overview

Builds a complete, AI-executable go-to-market playbook specifically engineered for B2B SaaS companies targeting mid-market accounts (100–1,000 employees, $15K–$150K ACV). Use it when you are launching into mid-market for the first time, repositioning from SMB or enterprise, or need to accelerate pipeline velocity without an enterprise-scale team or budget.

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: The ICP scorecard is producing too many Tier 1 accounts (SDR team can't handle the volume).**
Solution: Tighten the firmographic and technographic criteria — raise the Tier 1 threshold from 24 to 27 points, or add a hard cap of 50 Tier 1 accounts per quarter. Run the scoring rubric against your existing closed-won accounts to validate that the highest-scoring accounts are actually your best customers; adjust weightings if the data shows misalignment.

**Problem: The outbound sequence is generating low reply rates (<4%) even after implementation.**
Solution: First, check deliverability — if open rates are also low (<25%), the issue is inbox placement, not copy. Run a deliverability audit (MailTester, MXToolbox) and warm a new sending domain if needed. If open rates are acceptable (>35%) but replies are low, the issue is relevance — rewrite the Day 1 hook to reference a specific trigger event (funding, job posting, news) rather than a generic pain statement. Add one personalization token tied to something visible on their LinkedIn or company website.

**Problem: The playbook produces a comprehensive strategy but the internal team can't execute all of it simultaneously.**
Solution: Run the 90-day plan through a prioritization filter using the pipeline velocity formula — identify which single lever (# opportunities, ACV, win rate, or cycle length) is furthest from benchmark, then sequence the playbook to address that lever first. Defer all programs that don't directly impact the primary lever until month 4. A focused team executing 40% of this playbook well outperforms a stretched team executing 100% of it poorly.
