## Integration Tips

**TikTok Shop:**
- Connect Shopify store via TikTok Shop app (Shopify App Store) to sync inventory, orders, and fulfillment in real time — no manual SKU management
- Use TikTok Shop Affiliate Center API to pull creator GMV data into your attribution dashboard daily via Zapier or Make automation
- Set up TikTok Shop coupon codes per creator tier for additional attribution layer beyond UTM (buyers sometimes screenshot, share, or use promo codes outside the tracked click path)

**Klaviyo / Email:**
- Tag social commerce purchasers in Klaviyo with source attribute (tiktok-shop, ig-shopping, live-event) to build separate post-purchase flows with social-native language and creator shoutouts
- Trigger "we saw you found us on TikTok" welcome email variant for social commerce acquirees — conversion rate 40%+ higher than generic welcome email

**Notion / Google Sheets:**
- Creator roster management: Notion database with fields for tier, GMV YTD, next video due date, seeding status, exclusivity terms, and contract expiry
- Weekly performance review: Google Sheets dashboard auto-populated via TikTok Shop CSV export and Zapier; shared with creators as leaderboard screenshot via group DM

**Gorgias / Customer Support:**
- Flag orders from social commerce channels in Gorgias with "TikTok Shop" tag to measure CSAT and return rates by acquisition channel (social commerce buyers have different return behavior than paid social buyers)

**Live Shopping Tools:**
- TikTok LIVE: native, no additional tool required; use TikTok Studio desktop app for streaming quality
- Simulcast to Instagram Live simultaneously via StreamYard (free tier) to maximize reach without duplicating effort
- CommentSold or Buywith.com for more advanced live shopping features if event GMV exceeds $10K/show

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Creators are posting but not generating clicks or GMV**
- Root cause is almost always one of three things: (1) product tag is not properly pinned in the video, (2) hook fails in first 2 seconds and video is swiped, (3) CTA is too soft ("check it out" vs. "it's in my TikTok Shop, linked right here")
- Fix: Pull the 3 lowest-performing creator videos and have AI analyze the hook, product visibility, and CTA against your brief. Send personalized feedback with one specific change to make on the next video. Do not give generic feedback — creators ignore it.

**Problem: TikTok Shop orders are cannibalizing Shopify/website revenue rather than adding new customers**
- This is a dark social attribution problem, not a true cannibalization problem in most cases
- Fix: Run a post-purchase survey for 30 days. If ≥ 60% of TikTok Shop buyers answer "I had never heard of [Brand] before seeing this TikTok," cannibalization fear is unfounded. If ≥ 40% say they were "already planning to buy from your website," add a Shopify-exclusive loyalty incentive to shift high-intent buyers back to your owned channel and protect margin.

**Problem: Live show viewership is high but GMV conversion is low (under 1%)**
- Root cause is usually (1) no live-only pricing mechanic to create urgency, (2) CTA is buried at the end of a 60-minute show, or (3) product demo is too long and viewers are dropping before the buy moment
- Fix: Add 3 flash "live-only" price drops at minutes 10, 30, and 60. Move the first buy-now CTA to minute 8, not minute 45. Cap demo segments at 12 minutes each. Announce countdown timers out loud, not just on-screen graphic.
