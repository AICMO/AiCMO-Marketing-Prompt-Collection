# AI-Powered B2B SaaS Webinar-to-Video Content Repurposing System & On-Demand Pipeline Generation Intelligence Engine

**Difficulty:** Advanced | **Time:** 25-30 min | **Tags:** b2b, video, webinar, content-repurposing, demand-generation, pipeline, seo, youtube, automation, content-operations

## Overview
This prompt engine transforms recorded webinars into a systematic, AI-driven video content repurposing machine that generates ongoing pipeline from existing recordings. Use it when you have a library of webinar recordings sitting underutilized and want to convert each one into 20+ content assets, build a YouTube channel with compound search authority, and create on-demand lead generation that works around the clock without additional production cost.

6. ON-DEMAND GATING STRATEGY:
- Recommended gate level (ungated / soft gate / hard gate) with rationale based on funnel stage

Format as a production-ready content brief that a solo content manager can execute in one week.

## Troubleshooting

**Problem:** YouTube video gets <200 views in first 30 days despite good optimization.
**Solution:** YouTube's algorithm cold-starts new channels — promotion is required. Embed the video in your highest-traffic blog post, send the broadcast email (this traffic signal teaches YouTube the audience), and ask your top 10 advocates to watch, like, and comment within the first 48 hours. The algorithm weights early engagement velocity heavily.

**Problem:** Email subscribers engage with clips but don't convert to demos.
**Solution:** The gap is typically funnel stage mismatch. Map your video CTAs to buyer readiness: video-top audiences should get soft CTAs (download framework template), not hard CTAs (book a demo). Add a middle-step offer — a checklist, diagnostic quiz, or free audit — that converts warm viewers before asking for a sales conversation.

**Problem:** Attribution shows video content influenced deals but sales team dismisses it as "marketing vanity metrics."
**Solution:** Pull closed-won deals from the last 90 days and show which ones had a video content touchpoint. Calculate the average deal size for "video-touched" vs. "non-video-touched" deals. If video-touched deals have higher ACV or faster sales cycles (common finding), that's the ROI case. Present deal-level data, not aggregate view counts — sales leaders respond to specific deal examples, not totals.
