# AI-Powered B2B SaaS Executive Earned Media Presence & Guest Content Distribution Intelligence Engine

**Difficulty:** Advanced | **Time:** 25-30 min | **Tags:** earned-media, podcast-guest, thought-leadership, executive-brand, video-interviews, content-distribution, dark-social, pipeline-attribution, B2B-SaaS

## Overview

Deploys an AI-powered system to build a systematic earned media presence program for B2B SaaS executives — covering opportunity identification, guest pitch creation, spokesperson preparation, appearance repurposing, and pipeline attribution. Use this when you want to generate top-of-funnel awareness and dark social demand by placing your executives on podcasts, video interview programs, and expert panels without increasing headcount or paid media spend. This is distinct from hosting your own branded podcast (outbound audience building) — this is about earning placement on established audiences that already trust the host.

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Pitch emails are getting ignored — response rate below 5%**
Solution: The most common cause is leading with the company/product instead of the HOST's audience value. Rewrite your pitch so the first two sentences are entirely about what the HOST's audience will gain — zero mention of your company or product until paragraph 3. Also personalize the opening hook to a SPECIFIC recent episode (cite a timestamp or quote from the host). Generic pitches that could apply to any show get deleted immediately.

**Problem: Executive got booked but gave a poor interview — too promotional, not enough insight**
Solution: The "signature story bank" and bridge technique library (Module 3B and 3C) prevent this, but only if the executive rehearses — not just reads. Run a mock interview 24 hours before the appearance where a colleague asks unprepared questions. The executive should be able to answer all 15 prep questions without notes. Also establish a "no product pitch" rule during the appearance — any time the executive mentions the product name, the content gets edited or the host disengages. Keep appearances insight-led: the product earns trust through the quality of the thinking, not direct mention.

**Problem: Content repurposing is producing too much content that sounds generic**
Solution: The repurposed content must be built from the ACTUAL quotes from the specific interview, not paraphrased from the topic. Feed the full verbatim transcript to the AI agent rather than a summary. Pull the 3-5 most surprising or counter-intuitive things the executive said and build each piece of content around those specific moments. If the executive didn't say anything genuinely surprising during the appearance, the pre-appearance prep (Module 3A and 3B) needs to be stronger before the next booking.
