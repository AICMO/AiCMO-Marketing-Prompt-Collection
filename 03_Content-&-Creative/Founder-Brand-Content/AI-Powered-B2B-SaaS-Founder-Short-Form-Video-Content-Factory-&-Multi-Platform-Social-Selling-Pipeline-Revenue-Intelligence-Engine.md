## Integration Tips

- **Notion + AI Agent:** Store scripts in Notion database, use Claude/GPT to generate platform variants automatically from the master script
- **Descript/Riverside:** Record all 10 scripts in one session, auto-transcribe, then push transcripts back into your AI pipeline for repurposing
- **HubSpot CRM:** Create a custom property "Founder Content Engaged" — any contact who comments or DMs from founder video gets tagged and enrolled in a dedicated nurture sequence
- **Zapier workflow:** Comment keyword (e.g., "SIGNALS") on LinkedIn → triggers Zapier → sends DM automatically via linked social tools → creates HubSpot contact record with source attribution
- **Salesforce:** Add "Founder Video" as a Lead Source value; track influenced pipeline in campaign attribution reports
- **Buffer/Hootsuite:** Schedule the week's 10 videos across platforms using optimal posting times (LinkedIn: Tue-Thu 7-9am and 12-1pm; TikTok: 6-10pm; YouTube Shorts: anytime — it's search-driven)
- **Beehiiv/Substack:** Monthly newsletter to subscribers: "Here's what I said on video this month that got the most debate" — repurpose top 3 scripts as written posts

## Troubleshooting

**Issue: Scripts sound generic and not like the founder's voice**
- Solution: Before running the prompt, record a 5-minute unscripted voice memo of the founder explaining the insight out loud. Feed the transcript to the AI and instruct it to "rewrite these scripts in the exact speaking style of the following transcript." The AI will mirror their cadence, vocabulary, and sentence structure precisely.

**Issue: Comment CTAs generate engagement but DMs don't convert to meetings**
- Solution: The Day 1 DM is doing too much selling. Restructure: Day 1 = deliver value only (no ask), Day 3 = share one relevant story ("we saw this work for a company just like yours"), Day 7 = make the ask. The conversion problem is almost always premature ask timing, not offer quality.

**Issue: Video views are high but no comment CTA responses (< 1% conversion)**
- Solution: The keyword CTA needs to be more specific and the reward needs to be more tangible. Instead of "comment PLAYBOOK," try: "I built a 47-point checklist our top customers use every Monday — comment 'MONDAY' and I'll send the exact sheet." Specificity (47-point, every Monday, exact sheet) increases response rates by 2-4x versus vague resource promises.
