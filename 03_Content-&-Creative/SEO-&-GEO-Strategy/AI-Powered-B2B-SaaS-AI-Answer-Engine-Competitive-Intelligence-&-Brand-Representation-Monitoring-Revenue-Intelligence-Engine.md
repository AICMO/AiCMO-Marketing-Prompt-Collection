## Troubleshooting

**Problem:** AI engine responses vary significantly between runs of the same query — making it hard to establish a reliable baseline.
**Solution:** Run each query 3 times within a 10-minute window and use the median response. AI engines have response variance; single-run scores are misleading. For Perplexity and ChatGPT, use a fresh session (clear conversation context) for each query to avoid context carry-over affecting results.

**Problem:** Your brand is mentioned in AI responses but described inaccurately — and publishing new content doesn't seem to change AI outputs quickly.
**Solution:** AI engine training data has a lag of weeks to months. Prioritize getting accurate descriptions published on high-authority third-party sites (G2, TechTarget, industry publications) rather than your own domain — AI engines weight external validation heavily. Entity consistency matters: ensure your company name, product category, and key claims appear identically across 10+ authoritative external sources.

**Problem:** A competitor is dominant in AI responses despite having weaker content than you — you can't figure out why.
**Solution:** Run the competitor's brand name through Perplexity and look at the "Sources" section — this tells you exactly what content AI is pulling to recommend them. The answer is usually: analyst report coverage (Gartner, Forrester), a single viral research report they published, or heavy G2 review volume with keyword-rich review text. Match whichever of these is driving their score.
