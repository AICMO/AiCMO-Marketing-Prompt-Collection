## Troubleshooting

**Problem:** Answer passages are well-written but AI engines still aren't citing our content.
**Solution:** Citation requires authority signals, not just content quality. Check three things: (1) Is the page indexed and crawlable? Run `site:yourdomain.com [page URL]` in Google. (2) Does your domain have enough authority relative to who's currently being cited? Check who AI cites for your target query and compare their DA/traffic to yours — if there's a 20+ point gap, outreach for backlinks is the priority, not more content. (3) Are your answer passages buried below promotional content? AI engines skip pages that read as sales copy — move your answer passage to the top of the page, before any product mentions.

**Problem:** AI engines cite us but describe our product incorrectly or with outdated information.
**Solution:** This is a training data vs. RAG architecture problem. For ChatGPT/Claude (training-based), you cannot update what they "know" quickly — focus on getting accurate descriptions in high-authority third-party sources (Wikipedia, G2, major publications) that influence future training runs. For Perplexity and Google AIO (RAG-based), ensure your homepage and product pages have clear, accurate structured data (Organization schema with description, Product schema) — these engines index in near-real-time and will pick up corrections within days to weeks.

**Problem:** We're generating lots of content but seeing no citation lift after 60 days.
**Solution:** Most GEO programs stall because they create content without building authority. Content without authority = invisible to AI. Audit your last 60 days: if you published 8 articles but secured 0 backlinks from the target citation stack (industry publications, analyst sites), the content is not being weighted. Pause content production for two weeks and run an authority sprint: pitch 10 guest articles, secure 3 data partnerships, and get your brand mentioned in 5 authoritative third-party pieces. Citations follow authority, not content volume.
