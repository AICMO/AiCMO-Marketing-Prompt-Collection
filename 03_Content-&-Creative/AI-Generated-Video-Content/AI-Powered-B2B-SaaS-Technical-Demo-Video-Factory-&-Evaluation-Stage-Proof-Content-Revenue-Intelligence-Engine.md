## Integration Tips

**HubSpot:**
- Create a custom contact property: `demo_video_chapter_reached` (number)
- Use HubSpot Video or Vidyard integration to track watch % per contact
- Trigger enrollment into "high-intent" sequence when `chapter_reached >= 4`
- Set deal property: `video_influenced = true` when video watched pre-close

**Salesforce:**
- Add `Demo_Video_Watched__c` (checkbox) and `Demo_Video_Completion__c` (percent) to Opportunity
- Build a report: "Opportunities with video completion >50%" vs. close rate
- Create a Task auto-log when champion shares video (tracked via Vidyard/Loom share link)

**Vidyard / Loom:**
- Use Vidyard's Room feature to create personalized video playlists per account
- Track individual viewer data — know which buying committee members watched
- Use Loom's engagement analytics (comments, reactions) to qualify champions
- Set up webhook: chapter 6+ completion → Slack alert to AE/SDR

**Synthesia / HeyGen (AI Video Production):**
- Create a "base video" template per use case (3-4 templates total)
- Use variable text overlays to personalize: company name, industry metrics, use case label
- Produce 15-20 variants per quarter using different avatars for different buyer personas
- A/B test: Human-style presenter vs. screen-recording + voiceover — measure completion rate

**Notion / Confluence (Production Workflow):**
- Build a Video Brief Template as a Notion database
- Fields: Use Case, Persona, Competitor, Key Metric, Champion Quote, Production Status
- Track each video's pipeline attribution in a linked Deals table

**Zapier Automation:**
- Trigger: New Opportunity created in Salesforce with `Stage = Evaluation`
- Action 1: Find best-match demo video from Vidyard library based on `Industry` + `Use Case` fields
- Action 2: Create and send personalized email with video link via HubSpot
- Action 3: Log activity to Salesforce opportunity timeline

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Watch rates are low (<25% completion)**
Solution: The hook is failing. Test 3 different opening scenarios — one with a specific late-night incident story, one with a cost-of-inaction stat, one with a question. Run for 50 views each. The scenario that resonates with your exact ICP will pull completion rates above 40%. Also audit video length — anything >6 minutes for a top-of-demo video will lose B2B buyers; cut ruthlessly.

**Problem: Prospects watch but don't convert to demo requests**
Solution: The CTA architecture is broken. Audit your Chapter 6 (CTA segment) — are you offering ONE clear next step or multiple? For evaluation-stage buyers, two-option CTAs perform best: "Watch the 90-second customer result summary" OR "Book a 30-minute custom walkthrough." Eliminate all other options. Also check if your proof story (Chapter 5) matches the prospect's industry — mismatched social proof actively kills conversion.

**Problem: Sales team won't use the videos in their outreach**
Solution: The adoption problem is a workflow integration problem, not a content quality problem. Make it zero-friction: build a Salesforce or HubSpot "one-click" workflow where AE/SDR selects `Use Case` + `Persona` from a dropdown and the system auto-drafts the email with the correct video and chapter links pre-populated. Measure adoption by tracking video sends in CRM. If less than 60% of opportunities have a video sent by Day 14 of evaluation, the workflow is too manual.
