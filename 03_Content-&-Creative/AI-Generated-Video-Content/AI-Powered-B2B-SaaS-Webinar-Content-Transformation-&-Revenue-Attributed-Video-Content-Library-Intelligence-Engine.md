## Integration Tips

- **HubSpot**: Create a workflow triggered by webinar registration status (attended vs. no-show) to enroll contacts into the correct email sequence automatically; use custom properties to tag pipeline influenced by each asset
- **Salesforce + Gong**: Tag clips used in sales outreach in Gong activity notes; build a Salesforce report tracking "content touchpoints before closed-won" to prove attribution
- **Vidyard / Loom**: Upload the full recording and individual clips to Vidyard for tracked video viewing — integrate with HubSpot to fire lead scoring events when prospects watch >50% of a clip
- **Notion / Airtable**: Build a content asset tracker with columns for: asset type, publish date, channel, UTM link, 30-day metrics, pipeline attributed — update weekly via Zapier automation pulling from HubSpot
- **Zapier / Make**: Automate clip distribution — when a YouTube upload is complete, auto-create LinkedIn post drafts in Buffer or Hootsuite; auto-add asset to Airtable content library
- **Transistor / Buzzsprout**: If webinar audio is high quality, auto-publish as a podcast episode with show notes generated from the blog post draft
- **Descript / Opus Clip**: Use AI to auto-identify the best 60-90 second clips from the full recording based on energy, quotability, and topic density — feed these timestamps into the prompt as "most-engaged moments"

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Output assets feel generic and don't reflect the actual session insights**
Solution: Paste the verbatim transcript (or key 10-15 minute excerpts) directly into the prompt rather than summarizing key points. The model performs significantly better with raw quotes and specific data points than with paraphrased summaries. Use Descript or Otter.ai to generate a clean transcript first.

**Problem: LinkedIn clips get low engagement despite high-quality content**
Solution: The first 3 seconds are everything on LinkedIn video. Rewrite the hook to lead with a counterintuitive stat or a direct challenge to a common belief ("Most people think X — here's why that's wrong"). Avoid starting with "In this clip..." or "Today we're talking about..." Test 3 different hooks for your top-performing clip to identify which pattern your audience responds to.

**Problem: Non-attendee email sequence has low open rates (<15%)**
Solution: The subject line for Email 1 should reference something specific that happened in the session — not just the title. Example: "The CFO who kills 60% of software deals (she explained why)" outperforms "Watch the replay: [Webinar Title]" by 2-3x. Personalize the preview text to reference the recipient's company or role using HubSpot tokens.
