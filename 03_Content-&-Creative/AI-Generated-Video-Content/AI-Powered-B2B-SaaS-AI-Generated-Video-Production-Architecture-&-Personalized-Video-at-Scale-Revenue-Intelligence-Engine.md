## Troubleshooting

**Problem: AI avatar videos feel uncanny or robotic, resulting in lower reply rates than expected**
Solution: Three fixes — (1) Use HeyGen's "Expressive" avatar mode with natural head movements and eye contact calibration. (2) Keep scripts conversational and short-sentence — AI avatars stumble on complex sentences. Write as if you're texting, not writing an email. (3) Add a genuine personal element that AI can't fake: have the SDR record a real 5-second "PS" using Loom and attach it to the end of the AI video. This hybrid approach combines production scale with human authenticity and consistently outperforms purely AI or purely human video.

**Problem: Personalization feels generic even when variables are injected ("I noticed you're the VP of Ops at [Company]" is the most common offender)**
Solution: Upgrade your personalization tier. The company name and title are Tier 3 personalization — they signal "I merged you from a list." Tier 1 personalization requires a real trigger event: a LinkedIn post they published, a specific job listing they posted, a news article about their company, a funding announcement. Build a Clay workflow that auto-searches LinkedIn and Google News for each prospect 48 hours before video generation runs, surfacing a recent trigger event to inject into the hook. If no trigger event exists, hold the video and move that prospect to a Tier 2 segment script instead of sending a weak Tier 1 video.

**Problem: Video production costs are scaling faster than pipeline ROI**
Solution: Audit the video type mix monthly. Calculate cost-per-video-influenced-opportunity for each video type separately — personalized outreach, ABM Tier 1, ad creative, evergreen demo. In most programs, Tier 3 programmatic videos and ad creative deliver the lowest cost-per-outcome; Tier 1 ABM videos have the highest cost but also the highest average deal value in the influenced pipeline. Shift budget toward the formats with the best cost-per-influenced-ARR ratio, not just the cheapest per-unit cost. Typically, reducing Tier 1 bespoke videos from 30 to 15 accounts per month and reinvesting in Tier 3 programmatic volume produces a better program-wide ROI.
