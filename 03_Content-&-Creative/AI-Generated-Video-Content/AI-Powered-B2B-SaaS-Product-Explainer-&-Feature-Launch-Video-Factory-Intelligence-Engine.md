## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Scripts feel too long or dense when the AI avatar reads them aloud.**
Solution: Aim for 130 words per minute maximum. Run the script through a word count tool and divide by your target duration in minutes. If over 150 wpm, cut every "that," "also," and "additionally." Replace compound sentences with two short sentences. The dead air between sentences is not waste — it's processing time for the viewer.

**Problem: The [SCREEN:] cues are too complex to record without a professional editor.**
Solution: Simplify to 3 UI moments maximum per 60-second video. Each screen moment should show only the moment *after* the action (the result state), not the full click sequence. Record with Loom at 1.5x zoom on the relevant UI panel only. A blurred background with a sharp product UI outperforms a full-screen recording in A/B tests.

**Problem: Distribution copy sounds the same across all channels, or the AI output is generic.**
Solution: Anchor every channel variant to the primary pain of the person *reading* on that channel. The LinkedIn caption is written for the buyer scrolling their feed on mobile at 7pm. The in-app tooltip copy is written for a user mid-workflow who has 8 seconds of attention. The sales email is written for a champion who needs to forward it to their CFO. Rewrite with that specific reader in mind and the copy immediately becomes more specific.
