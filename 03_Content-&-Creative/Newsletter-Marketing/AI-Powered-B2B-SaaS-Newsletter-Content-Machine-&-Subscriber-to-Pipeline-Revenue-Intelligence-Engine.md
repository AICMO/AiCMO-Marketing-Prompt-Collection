## Overview

This prompt deploys an AI agent to architect, produce, and continuously optimize a B2B SaaS newsletter that functions as a revenue engine — not a vanity metric. It generates every editorial element (issue strategy, headline, sections, CTAs, subject lines) aligned to pipeline goals, subscriber lifecycle stage, and buying-intent signals. Use it to launch a new newsletter, systematize an existing one, or transform a low-engagement publication into a top-of-funnel pipeline machine.

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Open rates are declining despite consistent content quality**
*Likely cause:* List fatigue, deliverability degradation, or subject line formula becoming predictable.
*Fix:* Run a re-engagement campaign (5-issue sequence with a high-value lead magnet) to reactivate inactive subscribers. Simultaneously, rotate your subject line frameworks — if you've been running curiosity-gap SLs for 8+ weeks, switch to specific-data-point or contrarian for 4 issues. Check your sender score using MXToolbox and warm up a secondary sending domain if reputation is below 90.

**Problem: High open rates but near-zero pipeline conversion**
*Likely cause:* Audience-product mismatch (subscribers love the content but aren't buyers), or CTAs are buried/friction-heavy.
*Fix:* Audit your subscriber source mix — if most subscribers came from a viral piece that attracted non-ICP readers, segment your list by company size/title using a data enrichment tool (Clearbit, Apollo) and suppress non-ICP contacts from pipeline CTA sends. Separately, A/B test CTA placement: move the pipeline CTA to position 2 in the email (immediately after the hook, not at the bottom) for one issue and measure click-through lift.

**Problem: The AI-generated content sounds generic and doesn't match your editorial voice**
*Likely cause:* Insufficient voice and context input in the prompt.
*Fix:* Before running the Advanced Version, create a "Voice Calibration Block" — paste 3 of your highest-performing past issues into the prompt context, label them "These are examples of our newsletter voice and quality standard," and then run the generation. Additionally, populate the Tone and Voice fields in the Quick Version with 5 specific adjectives and 3 specific things to avoid (e.g., "Never use 'journey,' 'holistic,' or rhetorical questions that start with 'But what if?'"). Re-run the generation with this context.
