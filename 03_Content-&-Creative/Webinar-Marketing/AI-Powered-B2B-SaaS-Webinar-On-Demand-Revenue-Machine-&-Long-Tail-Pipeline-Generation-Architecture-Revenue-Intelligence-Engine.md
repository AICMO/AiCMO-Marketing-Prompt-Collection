## Overview
Transforms a single live webinar recording into a 6-12 month autonomous demand generation engine — building an on-demand content ecosystem that generates qualified pipeline long after the live event ends. Use this when you've produced a high-value webinar and want to extract 10x more pipeline from that investment through strategic replay distribution, SEO-optimized on-demand landing pages, content atomization into 25+ derivative assets, and automated nurture tracks for on-demand viewers — turning a one-time event into a permanent demand channel.

## Integration Tips

- **Wistia or Vidyard:** Embed the on-demand replay in these platforms rather than YouTube for native HubSpot/Salesforce video intelligence integration. Both platforms capture per-viewer engagement data (% watched, chapters re-watched, moments paused) and sync to your CRM contact record automatically — enabling the behavioral lead scoring model in this playbook. Set up email gates within the player for ungated distribution strategies.
- **HubSpot:** Create a "Webinar On-Demand" contact property group. Build a Smart List that segments contacts by: webinar replay viewed + % completion + form score. Create a workflow that assigns a "Hot / Warm / Cool" property value based on the scoring model, triggers the appropriate email nurture sequence, and creates a task for the assigned SDR with a "webinar intelligence summary" including what they watched and their stated challenge. Use HubSpot's Multi-Touch Revenue Attribution report to measure pipeline influenced by the on-demand content.
- **Salesforce + Pardot/Marketing Cloud:** Build an Engagement Studio program (Pardot) or Journey Builder flow (Marketing Cloud) triggered by webinar replay form completion. Use Salesforce custom objects to track: Webinar Title, View Date, View Percentage, and Pipeline Created. Create a Salesforce report for "Webinar On-Demand Pipeline" as a permanent revenue channel report visible to the CMO and CRO.
- **Google Search Console + Looker Studio:** After publishing SEO derivative content, track ranking progress in Search Console. Build a Looker Studio dashboard that combines: GSC impressions/clicks for derivative blog posts, GA4 on-demand landing page sessions segmented by source, HubSpot MQL data filtered by "Webinar On-Demand" campaign, and pipeline data exported from CRM. Review monthly to optimize underperforming channels.
- **LinkedIn Campaign Manager:** Create matched audience segments from: (a) on-demand page visitors who didn't convert, (b) email nurture subscribers who haven't converted after 45 days, (c) lookalike audiences based on converted on-demand viewers. Run carousel ad and video ad campaigns targeting these segments with derivative webinar content to create second-touch engagement.
- **Notion or Airtable:** Use as the central on-demand content production tracker. Build a table with: Asset Type, Target Channel, Status (Not Started / In Production / Published), Publish Date, UTM Link, Performance Notes. Share with the sales team to give them a real-time view of which content assets are available for deal support.

## Troubleshooting

**Problem:** On-demand replay landing page has high traffic but low form conversion rate (<10%)
Solution: The form is appearing before viewers experience enough content value to justify submitting their information. Run a 2-week A/B test: Variant A keeps current form placement, Variant B moves the form to appear after a 3-minute preview clip auto-plays (give them a taste of the insight before asking for contact details). If conversion rate doesn't improve with either variant, reconsider the gating strategy — try email-only gate or ungated with in-video CTAs. The landing page headline may also be too generic; verify that the headline includes at least one specific, quantified claim from the webinar rather than just the topic.

**Problem:** On-demand content generates views and form completions but MQL-to-pipeline conversion rate is poor (<5%)
Solution: The audience is consuming on-demand content out of topic curiosity, not active purchase intent. Audit the source of on-demand traffic — if >60% is coming from branded or organic keyword traffic (people already familiar with your brand), your audience may be too early in their buying journey. Add more bottom-funnel signals to the form: "What's your timeline for addressing [pain area]?" and score aggressively on timeline signals. If your SDRs are not referencing the specific webinar content the prospect watched in their outreach, adoption of the viewing intelligence data is failing — run a 30-minute SDR training on how to use video engagement data in outreach personalization.

**Problem:** Content atomization stalls after the first 2-3 assets — team loses momentum
Solution: The on-demand content production workload is getting distributed across too many team members without a clear owner. Assign one person as "webinar on-demand producer" with a 30-day sprint mandate and a pre-built asset checklist. Use an AI-assisted workflow: (1) Upload the webinar transcript to an AI tool, (2) Run a series of specific extraction prompts for each asset type (carousel hooks, thread ideas, email segments), (3) Edit and brand the AI output rather than writing from scratch. The goal is to produce the full 25-asset suite in 12-15 hours of total work using AI assistance — if it's taking longer, the prompts are too open-ended. Use the specific asset specifications in this playbook as prompt inputs for the AI tool.
