## Integration Tips

- **HubSpot:** Create a unique UTM tracking URL per partner using HubSpot's tracking URL builder. Set up a workflow that assigns `Lead Source = [Partner Name]` to any contact who registers via partner UTMs. Create a Deal property `Webinar Co-Marketing Source` to track pipeline attribution through close.
- **Salesforce:** Build a Campaign Hierarchy: parent campaign = "Q4 Webinar Series," child campaigns = "Webinar — [Topic] — Gong Source" and "Webinar — [Topic] — Our Source." Use Campaign Member Source field to distinguish registration channels.
- **Zoom Webinars / ON24:** Set up separate registration links for each partner (unique UTM per link). Export attendee data with custom fields for partner source tag so it syncs cleanly to your CRM.
- **Notion / Google Docs:** Build the Partner Webinar Runbook in a shared Notion workspace with your partner — both teams can see status of each promotional action in real time. Use a Kanban view: To-Do → In Review → Published → Done.
- **Zapier:** Automate the post-event pipeline report: trigger when a deal in HubSpot crosses $0 pipeline value, filter by campaign = webinar, and log to a Google Sheet that both your team and your partner can view.
- **Slack:** Create a shared Slack channel with your partner team (use Slack Connect) for 30-day promotion coordination. Pin the promotional calendar and flag each action item as it's completed.

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Partner agrees to co-host but their promotional commitment is weak (1 email instead of the agreed 3).**
Solution: Set promotional commitment in writing before finalizing the partnership — include it in the Term Sheet as a checklist. Use a shared Google Sheet or Notion table where both teams mark each promotional action complete. If a partner consistently under-delivers, tier them down (Tier 2) for future programs and increase your own promotional budget to compensate.

**Problem: UTM tracking breaks or a large portion of registrations arrive as "direct" with no UTM source.**
Solution: Set up redirect tracking at the URL level (use a link shortener like Bitly or your marketing automation's built-in link tracker) so even if a registrant clicks from a forwarded email or social share, the original source is preserved. Build a "last non-direct touch" attribution rule in HubSpot/Salesforce so that if UTM is missing, the system looks back 30 days for the most recent campaign touch.

**Problem: Partner's legal or procurement team stalls on data sharing agreements — delaying go-live.**
Solution: Remove the legal dependency by using a permissioned opt-in on the joint registration page: "I agree to share my contact information with [Partner Name] and [Your Company]." This gives both parties permission to contact the registrant without a company-to-company data sharing contract. Move all company-level data sharing conversations to a post-event relationship (after you've proven value). Most legal holds dissolve once the event has already been successful.
