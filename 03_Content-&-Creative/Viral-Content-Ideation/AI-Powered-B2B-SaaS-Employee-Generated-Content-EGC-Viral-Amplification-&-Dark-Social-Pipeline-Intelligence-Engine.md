## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Employees don't want to post because they fear saying something wrong**
*Solution:* Build a simple 3-point pre-post checklist (No confidential customer data. No financial claims. No competitive attacks). Pre-approve 8 starter posts for each employee — they can post verbatim or customize. Emphasize "your personal brand benefits YOU even if you leave the company." Make participation opt-in with visible appreciation for those who do participate (Slack shoutouts, recognition in all-hands).

**Problem: Program starts strong then dies after week 3 (participation decay)**
*Solution:* This is the #1 failure mode of EGC programs. Fix it with a weekly "content drop" — every Monday, each employee receives their 3 AI-generated post options for the week, pre-written, requiring 10 minutes of personalization. Remove the "what should I write about" friction. Add gamification: monthly "most influential post" award with real incentive (extra PTO day, gift card, executive lunch). Show pipeline impact: "Sarah's post last month was mentioned by 3 prospects in discovery calls — that's $450K in influenced pipeline."

**Problem: LinkedIn penalizes coordinated engagement and suppresses posts**
*Solution:* LinkedIn's algorithm detects "engagement pods" with simultaneous reactions. Stagger amplification: first employee comment at T+5min, second at T+15min, third at T+45min, etc. Comments must be substantive (3+ sentences, add a new insight) — generic "great post!" comments signal inauthentic behavior and reduce reach. Never use third-party "pod" automation tools — these violate LinkedIn's Terms of Service and risk profile suspension.
