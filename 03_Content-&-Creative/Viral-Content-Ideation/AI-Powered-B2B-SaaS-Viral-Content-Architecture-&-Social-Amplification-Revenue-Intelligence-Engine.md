## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Posts get likes but no comments, stalling algorithm distribution**
Fix: Seed 3-5 comments from colleagues within the first 20 minutes using genuinely substantive responses (not "great post!"). Ask a direct controversial question at the end of the post body — not in the CTA — that forces a yes/no take. Remove external links from the post body (put them in first comment).

**Problem: High impressions but zero demo requests from content**
Fix: The content is entertaining but not "buying signal" adjacent. Audit whether your top posts connect to a customer pain your product solves, or if they're too broadly educational. Introduce one monthly "conviction post" that directly addresses a buyer's top objection — this converts at 3-5x the rate of pure thought leadership.

**Problem: Content performs well on LinkedIn but generates no pipeline from dark social**
Fix: You're likely missing the community seeding step. Set up a Slack notification that fires immediately when a post publishes, triggering manual shares by 2-3 team members in target communities within 60 minutes. Track dark social attribution using a unique Bitly or UTM-tagged link specifically for community shares vs. organic profile traffic.
