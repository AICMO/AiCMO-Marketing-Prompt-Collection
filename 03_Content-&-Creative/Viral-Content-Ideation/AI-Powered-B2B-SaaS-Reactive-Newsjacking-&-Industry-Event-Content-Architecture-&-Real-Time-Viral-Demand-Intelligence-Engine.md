## Integration Tips

- **Slack + Make/n8n Automation:** Set up a Slack channel where team members drop breaking news links. Trigger this prompt automatically via webhook → AI generates all 7 assets → posts draft outputs to a review channel. Human approves in one click, then publishes via Buffer or Hootsuite.
- **Google Alerts → Zapier → AI Draft:** Configure Google Alerts for 10-15 competitor names, key analysts, and regulatory bodies. When an alert fires, Zapier triggers this prompt, feeds the news snippet, and emails a draft content pack to the content lead.
- **HubSpot Triggered Email Workflow:** When this prompt generates the email asset, import it directly into a HubSpot Smart List Workflow targeting cold MQLs. Schedule send for 8-10 AM next business day if the event breaks outside business hours.
- **Salesforce / CRM Outreach Task Creation:** The sales snippet output can be pasted into an outreach sequence template and assigned as a "timely" task to the 25 most relevant open opportunities by your SDR manager, prioritized by deal value.
- **Notion Content Hub:** Store all newsjacking outputs in a Notion database tagged by event type, ICP segment, and performance metrics. Build a library of high-performing reactive posts to train future prompts with real examples.
- **Editorial Calendar Block:** Reserve a standing "reactive slot" in your content calendar each week — a pre-approved, empty slot that can be filled within 60 minutes by this engine when a significant event breaks. Remove the approval bottleneck in advance.

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: The content feels forced — the connection between the news and our product is not natural.**
Solution: Switch the Strategic Framing from B (Contrarian) or D (Validation) to C (Practical Guide). Instead of tying your product to the event, focus purely on helping the ICP navigate the news with actionable steps. Brand awareness comes from being genuinely useful — the product mention can be subtle or absent in the initial post.

**Problem: We're consistently 4-6 hours behind competitors, missing the peak engagement window.**
Solution: Pre-build your response infrastructure. Set up monitoring for 20 news triggers (competitor names, regulatory bodies, major publications) before any event breaks. Pre-write "fill-in-the-blank" hook templates and voice guidelines so the AI prompt can generate in under 5 minutes once the event surfaces. Designate one "content rapid response" owner per quarter who has pre-authorized publish access without approval delay.

**Problem: The sales team isn't using the conversation accelerator snippets.**
Solution: Reduce friction to zero. Paste snippets directly into Salesforce/HubSpot as pre-written email templates tagged "TIMELY — use before [date]." Add a Slack notification to the #sales channel with the event summary and one-click copy of each snippet. Run a 5-minute stand-up to brief the team on the narrative before they start outreach.
