## Troubleshooting

**Problem: SMEs consistently miss contribution deadlines, creating production bottlenecks**
Solution: Reduce friction by defaulting to the smallest possible ask (voice memo or 3 Slack sentences instead of Google Doc). Create a "Top 10 Standing Questions" bank per SME — questions so aligned to their expertise that they can answer instantly without prep. Schedule recurring 15-minute monthly "content contribution slots" on SME calendars in advance rather than making one-off requests. Have a backup plan: if the SME misses their deadline, use their past talks, LinkedIn posts, or Gong call recordings to extract their perspective via AI synthesis.

**Problem: AI-generated content lacks originality — feels generic, doesn't reflect brand voice**
Solution: Your AI prompts are likely missing three critical inputs: (1) a specific, differentiated point of view (force the AI to take a counterintuitive stance, not describe both sides neutrally), (2) concrete examples from your customer base or product experience (AI can't invent authentic specifics), and (3) your brand voice system prompt with explicit red flags (words/phrases to avoid, tones to reject). Add a "What's our unique POV on this topic that a competitor couldn't credibly claim?" field to every content brief — this forces the AI to anchor on genuine differentiation rather than generic expertise.

**Problem: Content ops system is built but team isn't using it — reverting to ad-hoc habits**
Solution: The system is likely too complex or has too many new tools to adopt simultaneously. Roll out in phases: start with just the editorial calendar and brief template for 30 days before adding AI production workflows. Make the system the path of least resistance: if writers have to do extra steps to bypass the system, they'll use it. Create a "Done is better than perfect" culture where the system's structure is more valuable than its completeness — a 70% perfect brief published on schedule beats a 100% perfect brief that's two weeks late. Run weekly 15-minute content ops standups to troubleshoot friction points in real time.
