## Overview
This prompt transforms a single high-quality long-form content asset (research report, webinar transcript, case study, podcast episode, or blog post) into a complete, channel-optimized suite of 25–40 derivative assets mapped to buyer journey stages, with publishing sequencing, UTM attribution, and CRM integration instructions. Use it weekly as part of your content operations workflow to maximize ROI from every piece of original content your team produces.

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: AI-generated derivative content sounds generic and doesn't match brand voice**
Solution: Before running the prompt, paste 2–3 examples of your highest-performing past content into the "brand voice" section of the Advanced Version and explicitly tell the model "Match this tone and style." Add specific brand voice anti-patterns: "Never use words like 'leverage,' 'synergy,' or 'empower'" and list 3–5 phrases your brand does use regularly.

**Problem: The prompt produces too many assets and team can't execute on all of them**
Solution: Run only Tier 1 assets in the first pass. Add this instruction to the prompt: "For this run, only produce [A] LinkedIn Article and [C] Email Nurture Sequence. Mark other assets as 'queued' with one-line descriptions." Use the Tier 2–4 assets in subsequent weeks as the source content continues to be distributed — great content has a 6–12 month publishing runway with rotating derivative formats.

**Problem: Sales team isn't using the SDR sequence or insight brief**
Solution: The most common cause is that sales wasn't involved in defining the ICP pain or angle. Before running the Advanced Version, interview 2–3 reps about the most common objections and questions they hear — paste these verbatim into the "Key data points to prioritize" field. When derivative assets speak directly to objections reps hear daily, adoption increases dramatically. Also: share the SDR sequence performance data weekly in Slack; reps adopt what's proven to work.
