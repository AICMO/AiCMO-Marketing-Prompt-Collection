## Integration Tips

**HubSpot:**
- Use HubSpot Campaigns to tag all distributed content touchpoints with a unified campaign ID — this enables cross-channel pipeline attribution across organic, paid, email, and advocacy distribution
- Build HubSpot workflows that auto-tag contacts who engage with amplified content and enrich them with "content engagement score" — route high-score contacts to SDR follow-up sequences
- Connect HubSpot blog analytics to content classification scoring by pulling 30-day traffic and lead conversion data into a shared Google Sheet that updates the tier assignment logic weekly

**LinkedIn Campaign Manager:**
- Maintain an always-on matched audience segment built from HubSpot ICP contact list — use this as the base targeting layer for all paid amplification campaigns to ensure budget stays focused on known ICP segments
- Set up Campaign Manager automated rules to pause ads when CPC exceeds $12 or when impressions drop below 500/day — prevents budget waste without manual monitoring
- Use LinkedIn's Predictive Audiences feature to expand ICP reach 2-3x without sacrificing targeting precision

**Slack + Zapier:**
- Build a Zapier workflow that triggers a Slack notification to #content-amplification channel whenever a blog post exceeds 200 organic pageviews in its first 72 hours — this creates a real-time signal for the team to consider paid amplification
- Create a weekly Zapier automation that pulls top 5 performing content pieces from GA4 and posts them to a #share-this-week Slack channel with pre-written share copy for each — removes all friction from employee advocacy

**Hootsuite / Buffer:**
- Use Hootsuite's bulk scheduling feature to pre-schedule 30 days of derivative social content for each major asset — load the full 90-day distribution calendar at publication rather than managing day-by-day

- Use Gong's "topics" feature to identify which deal stages correlate with specific content topics — feed this intelligence back into content classification scoring to improve amplification priority accuracy

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Employee advocacy participation falls off after launch week**
Solution: The decay is structural, not motivational. Fix it by automating the reminder system rather than relying on employee memory. Build a 4-week Slack automation sequence post-publish that sends personalized (not mass) messages with new share copy variants at days 3, 10, and 21 after each major asset launch. Also add a monthly "top advocates" recognition post in the company all-hands Slack channel — social recognition drives more consistent behavior than any incentive program.

**Problem: Paid amplification spend depletes in the first 2 weeks, leaving nothing for breakout organic content**
Solution: Implement strict budget pacing rules: allocate only 60% of monthly budget in the first 15 days, holding 40% in reserve for amplifying organic breakouts that appear in the second half of the month. Set LinkedIn Campaign Manager daily budgets at 70% of your target daily spend — LinkedIn's algorithm will naturally pace, and you won't overspend early. Review pacing every Monday morning and rebalance.

**Problem: Content classification scoring feels subjective and the team disagrees on tier assignments**
Solution: Objectify the scoring system by converting qualitative criteria into measurable inputs. Replace subjective "strategic alignment" scores with binary criteria: (1) Does this content address a top-3 ICP pain point? (2) Does it include original data or proprietary insight? (3) Is the topic currently trending in ICP communities? Score 1 point per yes — a 3/3 is Tier 1, 2/3 is Tier 2, 1/3 or 0/3 is Tier 3. Have the content team score each piece at briefing stage, not after publication, so distribution planning can begin before the asset launches.
