## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Completion rate is below 50%**
The most common cause is too many inputs or inputs that require research the buyer doesn't have handy (e.g., "What is your current CAC?"). Audit every input: can the buyer answer it in under 10 seconds without leaving the page? If not, either add a tooltip with a formula to calculate it, replace it with a proxy input they know (e.g., "How many sales reps do you have?" instead of "What is your total sales labor cost?"), or remove it entirely and use your industry benchmark as the default. Target: buyer should never need to open another tab to complete your calculator.

**Problem: Gate conversion below 30%**
The results are not compelling enough, or the gate is too early. Fix 1: audit the headline — does it show a specific dollar number or just a percentage? Dollar figures convert 40-60% better than percentages. Fix 2: A/B test showing full results vs. gating one-third of the breakdown — most buyers will provide their email to see the hidden value levers. Fix 3: strengthen the gate value proposition — replace "Download your report" with "Get a shareable PDF your CFO can review" — internal sharing intent dramatically increases gate willingness.

**Problem: SDR follow-up response rate is low despite calculator data**
The outreach is not using the calculator data specifically enough. If the opening line is "I saw you ran our ROI calculator" — stop there. Every word after that must reference their specific inputs or outputs: "I saw you calculated $847K in selling hours recovered for your 25-person team — that's on the high end for teams your size, which usually tells me [specific reason]." Generic references to "you showed interest in our tool" have the same response rate as cold outreach. The calculator data is only an advantage if you use the exact numbers.
