## Troubleshooting

**Problem:** Pitch response rate is below 10% despite following the templates.
**Solution:** The most common failure is leading with speaker credentials instead of leading with episode value. Rewrite Touch 1 to spend the first 60% of the email on what the LISTENER will get from the episode, not who the guest is. Run the "listener selfishness test": would a listener who has never heard of your company care about this episode? If no, rewrite the angle. Also audit subject lines — if open rate is < 30%, the subject line is the bottleneck, not the body.

**Problem:** High booking rate but low landing page conversion (< 15%).
**Solution:** The in-episode CTA is almost always the issue, not the landing page. Audit whether: (1) the vanity URL is actually memorable and was stated clearly twice during the episode, (2) the offer is genuinely different from what listeners can Google for free, (3) the CTA was delivered in a natural segment of the conversation (not bolted on at the end when listeners have already stopped paying attention). Ask hosts to mention the resource in their own words during a natural conversation moment, not read a scripted CTA — listener response rates are 3–5x higher for organic host mentions.

**Problem:** Pipeline attribution shows good MQL volume but zero SQLs from podcast after 90 days.
**Solution:** This indicates a segmentation failure — the podcast audience is interested in the topic but isn't your ICP. Audit the show tier selection: Tier 3 shows often attract aspiring practitioners who match the topic but not the budget or company size. Reweight the program toward Tier 2 shows where sponsoring companies align with your ICP profile. Also check if the nurture sequence is advancing or stalling — if Email 5 open rate is below 20%, the sequence is losing people before they reach the sales conversation invitation. Compress to 4 emails or add more value in Emails 2–4.
