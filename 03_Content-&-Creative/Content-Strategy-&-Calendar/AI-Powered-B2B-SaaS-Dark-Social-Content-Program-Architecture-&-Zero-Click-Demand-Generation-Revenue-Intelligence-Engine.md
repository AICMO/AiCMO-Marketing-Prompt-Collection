## Overview
Builds a complete zero-click content program architecture that distributes full-value content inside LinkedIn, Slack, Discord, WhatsApp groups, and private communities — generating measurable pipeline without relying on website clicks or gated asset downloads. Use this when your attribution data shows "direct/none" is your #1 pipeline source, your gated content CPL is rising while MQL quality falls, and you suspect buyers are finding you through channels you can't track — because they are.

Build production-ready frameworks for each format:

**Format 1: LinkedIn Text Thread (The Contrarian Take)**
- Structure: Hook (1 sentence) → Unpopular opinion statement → 5-7 supporting points with specific data/examples → Open-ended question → Zero promotional CTA
- Ideal length: 800-1,200 characters

**Format 2: LinkedIn Document/Carousel (The Tactical Breakdown)**
- Structure: Page 1 (Bold problem statement) → Pages 2-8 (Step-by-step breakdown with original frameworks, data, visuals) → Page 9 (Surprising insight) → Page 10 (Soft question — no "click here" link)
- Ideal specs: 8-12 slides, 16:9 or 4:3, text-dominant with 1-2 data points per slide
- Production template: Framework for converting any executive 30-minute interview into a carousel in 90 minutes
- What gets shared: Slides that contain "steal this" frameworks buyers can reuse in their own work

**Format 3: LinkedIn Native Newsletter (The Weekly Briefing)**
- Structure: 1-sentence hot take → 3 things happening in the industry this week (opinionated commentary, not news recap) → 1 framework or template readers can use → 1 question for readers to answer in comments
- Delivery: LinkedIn Newsletter (subscribers receive in-app + email notification) — zero website required
- Production template: 45-minute weekly production workflow from news aggregation → draft → executive polish → publish
- Subscriber growth playbook: How to grow from 500 to 5,000 subscribers in 90 days without paid promotion

**Format 4: Slack Community Post (The Expert Ask)**
- Applies to: Pavilion, CFO Connect, Revenue Collective chapters, industry-specific communities
- Structure: Context (why you're posting) → Specific question or insight → Genuine request for peer experience (not a pitch) → Offer of something useful in return (template, data, framework)
- Tone requirements: Must pass the "does this sound like a vendor?" test — all posts from personal profiles, never company accounts
- What gets DMs: Posts that reveal a surprising data point or share a counterintuitive framework get direct outreach from potential buyers

**Format 5: LinkedIn Poll (The Opinion Splitter)**
- Structure: Controversial binary question relevant to the buyer's job → 3-4 genuinely contested answer options → Follow-up comment with your take → Tagging 3-5 relevant practitioners
- Why it works: Polls generate 3-4x more impressions than text posts because LinkedIn amplifies early engagement velocity
- Production: Can be produced in 5 minutes; stack-produce 4 polls at once in monthly planning session
- Signal to watch: Comments from ICP personas who reveal their internal processes while explaining their vote

**Format 6: The Stat Drop (Short-Form Data Post)**
- Structure: 1 surprising statistic from your own data or original research → 2-3 sentence explanation of why it matters → Question to readers
- Source: Pull from customer conversations, product usage data, internal survey data, or commissioned research
- Why it works for dark social: Screenshots of specific statistics are shared in WhatsApp groups and Slack channels constantly
- Production: Requires a "stat vault" — a running document of data points collected from customer calls, internal reporting, industry research

**Format 7: The Response Thread (Piggybacking Trending Conversations)**
- Structure: Find a trending post or news item in your category → Write a 400-800 character response post that adds a layer of insight (not agreement/disagreement theater) → Tag 2-3 practitioners with context
- Why it works: Gets amplified by the original poster's audience while building your own reputation for thoughtful analysis
- Production: Requires a daily 10-minute "signal scan" — monitoring 5-7 accounts for content to respond to

**Format 8: The Compiled Resource (The Dark Social Gift)**
- Structure: "I compiled X resources on [topic] so you don't have to" → List format with brief commentary on each → No gating, no form, no link to anything — the entire list lives in the post
- Why it's shared in private channels: This format gets copy-pasted into Slack/WhatsApp channels constantly because it's self-contained
- Production: Quarterly deep research project (4-6 hours) that produces 12-16 posts across 3-4 months

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Executive spokespeople are posting inconsistently — weeks of silence followed by bursts**
Solution: Inconsistency is the #1 dark social program killer. Implement a "content float" system — always have 4 weeks of approved content pre-scheduled in LinkedIn's native scheduler. When the float drops below 2 weeks, the content manager triggers an emergency 60-minute content session to refill it. Never let an executive's calendar be the bottleneck for publishing. If executives won't commit to 2 hours/month of content review, the program won't work — and that's a leadership conversation, not a content problem.

**Problem: Content engagement looks good (likes, comments) but warm inbound isn't increasing**
Solution: Engagement metrics and pipeline are disconnected in dark social because sharing happens in private — you'll never see the LinkedIn post get screenshot and shared in 14 Slack channels.
