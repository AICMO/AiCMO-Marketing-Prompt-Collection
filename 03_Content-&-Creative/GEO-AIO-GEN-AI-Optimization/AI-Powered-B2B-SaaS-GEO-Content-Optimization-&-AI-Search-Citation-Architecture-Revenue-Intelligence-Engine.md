## Integration Tips

- **CMS (WordPress, Webflow, HubSpot CMS)**: Deploy FAQ and HowTo JSON-LD via a custom code block in your page template — no plugin required. Test with Google Search Console's URL Inspection tool immediately after deployment to confirm schema is recognized.
- **Screaming Frog + GEO Audit**: Run a Screaming Frog crawl of your content cluster before optimizing. Export the page list to a Google Sheet, add a GEO Score column, and prioritize pages that (a) already rank in top 5 organically but (b) are not being cited in AI answers — these are highest-leverage GEO optimization targets.
- **Notion Content Database**: Track your GEO optimization queue in Notion — columns: Page URL, Primary Target Query, Current GEO Score, Optimization Status, AI Citation Status (check weekly), Next Review Date. Link each page to its GEO audit output for easy reference.
- **Perplexity API Monitoring**: Use Perplexity's API to programmatically query your 10 target queries weekly and store results in a Google Sheet. Flag any week where a competitor gains a new citation or your page loses its citation — this triggers an immediate content review sprint.
- **Google Search Console**: After deploying schema markup, monitor the Rich Results report in GSC weekly. Schema impressions and clicks are a leading indicator of AI citability — pages with schema that Google validates are significantly more likely to appear in AI Overviews.

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: The page has a high GEO score after optimization but AI engines still cite a competitor instead.**
Solution: GEO is a competition, not just a checklist. Even a perfectly optimized page loses to a competitor with more topical authority (more content on the topic, more backlinks, more citations across the web). Run a competitor content audit: how many pages does the competitor have covering this topic cluster? If they have 20 pages and you have 2, build the cluster first. The GEO audit optimizes individual pages — cluster depth is the strategic foundation.

**Problem: Answer-Ready Blocks appear in AI answers but without attribution to our company.**
Solution: AI engines sometimes extract the answer but not the attribution if the company name appears only at the end of the block or is weakly associated with the claim. Move the company name to the first or second sentence of each Answer-Ready Block, and ensure it's paired with the specific claim ("Nexus data shows..." rather than "According to our research..."). Also audit your entity consistency — if the AI engine doesn't have strong associations between your company name and this topic, attribution will be weak.

**Problem: Google Search Console shows schema errors after deploying the JSON-LD.**
Solution: The most common errors are: (1) FAQ answers exceed 1,500 characters — truncate to under 1,000 to be safe; (2) HowTo steps lack a `url` property — add anchor links to each step; (3) Nested JSON formatting errors — validate with Google's Rich Results Test (search.google.com/test/rich-results) before and after every deployment. If errors persist, strip all smart quotes and replace with standard ASCII quotes — CMS text editors frequently corrupt JSON-LD.
