# AI-Powered B2B SaaS Webinar-to-Content Engine & Post-Production Asset Factory Revenue Intelligence Engine

**Difficulty:** Advanced | **Time:** 20-30 min | **Tags:** webinar, content-repurposing, content-atomization, demand-generation, b2b-saas, pipeline, revenue, post-production, ai-automation, multi-channel

## Overview
This prompt transforms a single recorded webinar (transcript + recording) into a full pipeline of 20+ distribution-ready content assets — blog posts, LinkedIn posts, short video clips, email sequences, sales enablement one-pagers, podcast episodes, and more. Use it immediately after every webinar to maximize the demand-generation value of your content investment and ensure no insight stays locked in a recording that 90% of your audience will never watch.

   - 3 buyer insights with supporting quote
   - ROI or outcome metrics mentioned by speakers

6. **SOCIAL MEDIA CAROUSEL** (LinkedIn / Instagram, 8-10 slides)
   - Slide 1: Hook statement
   - Slides 2-8: One insight per slide with speaker attribution
   - Slide 9: Summary / key takeaway
   - Slide 10: CTA slide

7. **PODCAST EPISODE REPURPOSE**
   - Episode title and description for audio-only version
   - Chapters list with timestamps
   - Show notes structure with links and resources mentioned

8. **DISTRIBUTION CALENDAR**
   - 30-day publishing calendar showing which asset goes on which channel on which day
   - Frequency recommendations per channel

Format each asset as ready-to-publish copy. Use real speaker quotes verbatim where available. Every asset should drive traffic back to the on-demand recording or a demo/trial CTA.

**CONTENT ATOMIZATION PROTOCOL:**

Run the following modules in sequence. Each produces distribution-ready output.

---

**MODULE 1: INSIGHT EXTRACTION & CONTENT MINING**

Before generating any asset, perform a structured analysis of the transcript:

A. **Quotable Moments Matrix** — Extract 15 quotes ranked by:
   - Quotability score (1-5): novelty, specificity, emotion, memorability
   - Buyer stage relevance: TOFU (awareness), MOFU (consideration), BOFU (decision)
   - Speaker authority weight: customer voice > external expert > internal expert

B. **Framework / Model Identification** — List every named framework, 3-step process, or decision model mentioned. These become carousel posts, infographics, and blog section headers.

C. **Data Point Inventory** — Compile every statistic, benchmark, or original data point. These become standalone LinkedIn posts, email subject lines, and sales proof points.

D. **Objection Map** — Identify every challenge or objection discussed and how it was addressed. These feed directly into sales battlecard updates and email nurture sequences.

E. **SEO Keyword Opportunities** — From the transcript topics, identify 5 high-intent keyword phrases a buyer would search when researching this topic.

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: The blog post reads like a webinar recap, not a useful article.**
Fix: Restructure around buyer questions, not speaker flow. Ask: "What problem is my buyer trying to solve when they search for this topic?" Reorganize sections to answer that question first, then weave in speaker quotes as supporting evidence. The webinar happened — the article must stand alone.

**Problem: LinkedIn posts feel flat and get no engagement.**
Fix: The hook is almost always the problem. Rewrite the first line using one of three formulas: (1) counterintuitive claim — "Most [job title] are getting [thing] backwards." (2) Specific number — "[X]% of teams we surveyed said [surprising finding]." (3) Pattern interrupt — "[Common belief]. Wrong." Never start with "We hosted a webinar..." — that's a press release, not a LinkedIn post.

**Problem: Sales team isn't using the enablement assets.**
Fix: Deliver the assets the way sales reps consume information — in Slack, in Salesloft/Outreach as a snippet, or as a Salesforce Chatter post on relevant opportunity records. A PDF in Google Drive is invisible. A pre-loaded email template in their sequencer that's already personalized? That gets used. Reduce activation friction to zero.
