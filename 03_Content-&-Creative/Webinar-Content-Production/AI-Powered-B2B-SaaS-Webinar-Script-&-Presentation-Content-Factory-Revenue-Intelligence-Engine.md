## Success Metrics

- **Webinar show rate:** 40-55% of registrants attending live (industry average: 35-40% — good webinars beat this)
- **Engagement rate:** 60%+ of attendees participating in at least one poll or chat interaction
- **Drop-off rate:** Less than 20% of attendees leaving before the CTA section (last 15 minutes)
- **Conversion rate:** 5-15% of live attendees taking the primary CTA action within 7 days (varies significantly by CTA type)
- **Pipeline influence rate:** 30-50% of attendees with any pipeline activity within 30 days (for mid-funnel audiences)

- **Content repurposing yield:** One webinar producing 8-12 downstream content assets

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: The AI-generated script sounds stiff or overly formal — not how a human would actually present**
Solution: Add this instruction to your prompt: *"Write the script entirely in informal spoken English. Use contractions throughout (it's, you're, we've). Include natural hesitations and rhetorical questions. If a sentence is longer than 20 words, break it into two. Imagine you're explaining this to a smart friend over coffee, not presenting at a board meeting."* Also feed in a sample of your speaker's actual speech patterns (a Gong call transcript works well) and ask the AI to match the voice.

**Problem: The webinar runs long — pacing is off and the CTA gets cut**
Solution: Run the generated script through a text-to-speech tool and time it — typical conversational delivery is 120-140 words per minute. If it's over-length, add to your prompt: *"The presentation portion is [X] minutes. Cut the script to [X*130] words maximum while preserving all five acts. Mark each section with [TIME CHECK: X MIN] so I can pace live delivery."* Always protect the CTA section — cut from the middle acts before touching the close.

**Problem: Poll participation is low (under 30% of attendees responding)**
Solution: Three common causes: (1) Poll launches too late — attendees need a verbal warm-up: *"I want to take a quick pulse of the room — I'll give you 30 seconds to answer this."* (2) Poll question is too complex — simplify to a binary or 3-choice answer. (3) Poll platform has friction — test the participant experience before the event. Ask the AI to rewrite polls with simpler answer choices and add the verbal prompt to the script.
