DELIVERABLE 1: GUEST INTELLIGENCE & SOURCING SYSTEM

Apply the "Audience Quality Vetting" framework before booking any guest:

A) Guest Selection Criteria (score each 1-10, minimum 35/50 total):
1. **Audience ICP alignment** — Do their LinkedIn followers, newsletter subscribers, or podcast audience match our ICP title + company size? (Not just "VP level" but specifically our ICP)
2. **Engagement authenticity** — What's their content engagement rate? (Minimum 3% LinkedIn engagement for their last 5 posts. Large follower count with <1% engagement = wrong signal)
3. **Topic authority** — Have they published original POV content on the topic we're covering in the last 90 days? (Avoid guests who will give generic talking points — we need opinion-forward content)
4. **Network cross-pollination** — Does their audience have <40% overlap with our existing email list? (We want net-new reach, not re-engagement of our database)
5. **Co-promotion commitment** — Are they willing to promote to their audience? (Require commitment to: 2 LinkedIn posts + 1 newsletter mention minimum before booking confirmation)

B) Guest Research Protocol (automate with Clay + LinkedIn Sales Navigator):
Step 1: Pull the guest's LinkedIn connections list filtered by [ICP title + company size] — estimate: what % of their connections match our ICP?
Step 2: Check their last 10 LinkedIn posts: engagement rate, topic relevance, comment quality (are their commenters ICP-relevant?)
Step 3: Check if they have an email newsletter — if so, request audience demographics before confirming booking
Step 4: Google them + "[Company Name] OR [Competitor Name]" — are they already co-marketing with a direct competitor?

C) Guest Outreach Sequence (target: 70%+ booking acceptance)

**Initial DM/Email — Subject: Quick question about [specific topic they posted about]**

Worth a quick call to see if the fit is there? Here's my calendar: [link]"

**Follow-up (Day 5 if no response):**
"Bumping this up — adding the specific topic angle we were thinking: [1-2 sentence topic description]. Also happy to share our typical attendee breakdown if that's helpful context."

**Final follow-up (Day 10):**
"Last nudge on this, [First Name] — if timing isn't right, happy to revisit for [Month+1] instead. Just let me know."

D) Pre-Event Guest Prep Package (send 1 week before webinar):

DELIVERABLE 2: SHOW RATE MAXIMIZATION SYSTEM

Industry average show rate for B2B webinars: 25-30%. Target: 40-50%.

The show rate gap is almost entirely an expectation-setting and reminder system problem, not a registration quality problem.

A) Registration Experience Architecture

- Confirmation email (immediate): NOT a generic calendar invite. DELIVERABLE 3: LIVE CONVERSION ARCHITECTURE

The conversion architecture runs in 3 distinct phases during the live event:

A) The Value Hook (Minutes 3-5): Anchor the ROI, not the agenda
Instead of walking through slides, open with: "By the end of the next 45 minutes, you'll have [specific, quantified outcome]. Here's how I know that's realistic: [2-sentence proof point with specific customer metric]."

This primes the audience to evaluate the content through a "can I do this?" lens rather than "is this interesting?" The shift from passive learning to active application multiplies pipeline conversion.

B) Mid-Webinar Intent Signal Pulse (Minute 20-25): Run 3 live polls

**Poll 1 — Problem Awareness (Segmentation):**
"When it comes to [problem we solve], where would you say your team is?"
A) We've identified this as a priority for the next 90 days
B) We're actively working on it but stuck
C) We know it's important but haven't started
D) Honestly, not on our radar yet

**Poll 2 — Solution Awareness (Qualification):**
"Have you evaluated any tools or approaches to solve [specific problem]?"
A) Yes — actively evaluating solutions now
B) We tried [approach] but it didn't work
C) We've done research but haven't committed to an approach
D) No — still in problem-definition mode

**Poll 3 — Urgency (Pipeline Timing):**
"When would you be looking to implement something like what we're discussing today?"
A) This quarter — we have a project starting now
B) Next quarter — planning now for [Q+1]
C) 6-12 months out
D) Just learning for now

→ Poll responses feed directly into your CRM as custom field data for lead scoring. "A" answers on all 3 = hot hand-off to SDR within 2 hours of webinar end.

C) The Last 10 Minutes — CTA Architecture
Do NOT end with "thanks for joining!" End with a structured offer:

1. **Resource delivery** (minute 40): "I'm dropping the [specific asset] in the chat right now. You can also get it here: [URL]. This is the tool we built based on today's framework."

2. **Social proof pivot** (minute 43): "Before I wrap, I want to share what [Customer Name] did with this exact framework. They went from [problem metric] to [outcome metric] in [timeframe]. Here's the key thing they did differently..." (45-second story)

3. **Explicit CTA** (minute 45): "If you answered [A or B] to any of our polls today, we've built a 30-minute working session specifically to map this to your situation. It's free, it's focused, and [team member name] will be the one leading it — not an SDR. [Calendar link in chat right now.] We have [X] slots available for the next 2 weeks."

4. **Recording + replay pitch** (minute 47): "We're sending the recording to everyone who registered within 24 hours. If you want the follow-up resource packet, reply to that email and we'll include [bonus asset]."

DELIVERABLE 4: POST-WEBINAR PIPELINE CONVERSION SYSTEM

Segment all registrants into 3 tracks immediately after webinar:

**Track A: Hot Leads** — Attended + answered Poll 1 "A" or "B" + Poll 3 "A" or "B"
**Track B: Warm Leads** — Attended but mixed signals OR no-showed with high engagement score
**Track C: Nurture** — No-showed + low engagement OR attended + all "C"/"D" poll answers

**Track A: Hot Lead 5-Email Sequence**

Day 0 (within 2 hours — from SDR, not automation): Personal email referencing what they answered in the polls: "Saw you answered [X] on our poll about timing. I'd love to spend 20 minutes mapping what [Guest] shared to your specific situation. Here's my calendar for this week: [link]"

Day 1 (from marketing): Thank you + recording + specific call-to-action: Subject: "The recording + the question you asked" — reference the specific Q&A question if they asked one. Include recording link. CTA: "Based on your [poll answer], I thought this 2-min segment at [timestamp] was most relevant for you."

Day 3: Customer proof asset. "Thought this was worth sharing — [Customer] had [similar situation to poll answer A]. Here's what they did." Link to case study. CTA: "Worth a quick call to see if the same approach applies to your situation?"

Day 7: Content + soft CTA. Relevant piece of content that extends the webinar topic. End: "We're hosting a follow-up office hours session for people who attended [webinar name] — 5 spots left. [link]"

Day 14: Final direct outreach. "Last reach on [webinar topic]. Happy to let this sit if the timing isn't right — but if anything has shifted in the last two weeks, just reply and I'll reach out."

**Track B: Warm Lead Sequence**
Same cadence but softer. Day 0 = from marketing (not SDR). Day 1 = recording. Day 3 = content asset. Day 7 = CTA for next webinar in series. Day 14 = "still interested?" with content.

**Track C: Long Nurture (90-Day)**
Monthly touchpoint. Alternate between: new piece of relevant content, upcoming webinar invitation, customer story. No hard CTA until they engage.

## Troubleshooting

**Problem**: Guests accept the invitation but don't actually promote the webinar to their audience, resulting in only your existing database attending and no net-new reach.
**Solution**: Make co-promotion a contractual (if not literally legal) condition of the invitation. Send the guest's 2-3 LinkedIn posts pre-written and ask them to customize and post — not draft their own. Schedule a 15-minute "promotion check-in" call at Day -10 to see their posts and confirm their newsletter slot. If a guest doesn't want to promote, they're not the right guest — the audience access is the whole point.

**Problem**: Show rate remains low (below 30%) despite the full email sequence.
**Solution**: The email sequence addresses deliverability and reminder logistics, but the root cause of low show rates is almost always weak topic-audience fit. If your registrants are not the same persona who would urgently want to attend a 45-minute live event on this specific topic, no reminder sequence will fix it. Audit your last 3 webinar topics: are they "we're sharing a framework" (weak) or "here's what you can do with this specific problem by Tuesday" (strong)? Reframe topics as outcomes, not education.

**Problem**: The prompt produces a generic post-webinar sequence that feels like standard marketing automation and doesn't convert.
**Solution**: The entire system depends on personalization data from the live polls. If you're not running the mid-webinar polls, you can't segment into Track A/B/C and every follow-up becomes generic. Add this requirement before running the next webinar: the polls are non-negotiable. Also: the Track A Day 0 email MUST come from a human (SDR or the host), not from marketing automation — SDR name, personal tone, specific reference to their poll answer.
