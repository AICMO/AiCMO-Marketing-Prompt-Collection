## Integration Tips

**HubSpot:** Create a custom contact property `EGC_First_Touch` and a UTM parameter `utm_source=linkedin_egc&utm_medium=video&utm_content=[creator_name]`. When a prospect clicks a link from an employee's LinkedIn video and fills out a form, capture the creator attribution. Build a report showing EGC-sourced contacts vs. all contacts by conversion rate. This data is your business case for scaling the program.

**Slack:** Create three channels: `#egc-ideas` (anyone drops a video concept, team upvotes), `#egc-submitted` (videos awaiting review — automated 24-hour reminder), `#egc-published` (celebrate every published video with a Slack bot that shows view count at 24 hours and 7 days). The Slack integration makes EGC feel like a team sport, not a marketing task.

**Notion:** Build a Creator CRM inside Notion — one row per creator with: videos published, cumulative impressions, best-performing video link, topics they own, next scheduled video, and a "coaching notes" field for the program manager. Review this weekly. Creators who haven't posted in 14 days get a personal check-in, not a reminder email.

**LinkedIn Analytics + Google Sheets:** Export creator-level LinkedIn analytics weekly (impressions, engagement rate, follower growth). Track in a shared Sheet visible to all creators — friendly competition drives volume. Add a "Creator Leaderboard" that resets monthly so new contributors can win.

**Zapier / Make.com:** Automate the celebration loop: when a video hits 1,000 views → Slack notification in `#wins` → HubSpot note added to any contacts who engaged → SDR Slack alert if an ICP-company employee liked or commented. The automation closes the loop between content performance and pipeline action.

**CapCut for Teams / Adobe Express:** Create a branded template library that employees access in one click — company color lower-thirds, branded intro frame, thumbnail template. This reduces production time by 15 minutes per video and maintains visual consistency without micromanaging creator style.

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Employees sign up for the program but never film their first video.**
Fix: The friction is psychological, not technical. Run a 30-minute "First Video Day" where 8 employees film their first video live on a Zoom call together. Peer presence removes 80% of solo-posting anxiety. The first video is always the hardest — make it a group activity, not a solo homework assignment. Celebrate everyone who publishes regardless of view count.

**Problem: EGC videos get strong engagement but zero pipeline attribution.**
Fix: You're missing the conversion bridge. Add a DM trigger CTA to every video ("Comment [KEYWORD] and I'll send you X") and set up a Zapier automation to log each keyword commenter in HubSpot with `egc_lead=true`. Alternatively, train creators to use a LinkedIn newsletter signup as their CTA — newsletter subscribers are opt-in pipeline that you own. Pure brand content doesn't create pipeline without a structured next step.

**Problem: A creator posts something off-brand or inaccurate and it spreads before review.**
Fix: Prevention beats correction. First, ensure every creator completes the Brand Safety Bootcamp before their first post — 30 minutes covering the Red/Yellow/Green content taxonomy. Second, add a step in the production SOP: "Before posting, read it aloud and ask: Would I say this on a customer call?" Third, have a rapid-response protocol — if off-brand content goes live, the program manager comments immediately with a clarifying statement and DMs the creator with a no-blame correction process. Speed and tone matter more than perfection. Programs that over-correct after mistakes lose creators permanently.
