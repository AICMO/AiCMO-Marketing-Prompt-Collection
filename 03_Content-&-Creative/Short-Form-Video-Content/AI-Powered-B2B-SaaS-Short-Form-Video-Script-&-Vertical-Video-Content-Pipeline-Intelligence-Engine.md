## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Videos get views but no comments or CTA conversions.**
Fix: Your CTA is too passive. Replace "check the link in bio" with a specific keyword comment prompt ("comment COACHING below") or a direct question ("What does your manager spend most of their week on? 👇"). Keyword CTAs convert 3–5x better than link CTAs on LinkedIn because they drive algorithmic amplification alongside lead capture.

**Problem: Hook view rate is strong (>40%) but watch-through drops before 20 seconds.**
Fix: The problem agitation (seconds 3–15) is too abstract or too long. The ICP doesn't feel seen fast enough. Rewrite using a specific, visceral scenario: name a day of the week, name a tool they use, name a meeting they hate. Specificity holds attention. Run two hook variants on the same video in the first week to separate hook performance from content performance.

**Problem: Content isn't reaching the right ICP — wrong commenters, wrong engagement.**
Fix: Your hook is too broad. Add job title or company-stage specificity in the first 3 seconds: "If you're a VP of Sales at a 100–500 person SaaS company..." will reduce reach but double qualified engagement. LinkedIn's algorithm rewards comment quality and saves over raw views for B2B targeting — optimize for ICP engagement signals, not vanity metrics.
