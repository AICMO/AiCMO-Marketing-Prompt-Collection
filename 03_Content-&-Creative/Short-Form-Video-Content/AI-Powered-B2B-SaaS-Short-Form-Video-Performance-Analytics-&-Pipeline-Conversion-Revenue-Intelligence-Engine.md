## Troubleshooting

**Problem:** UTM links in LinkedIn captions get zero clicks even on high-view videos.
**Solution:** LinkedIn suppresses link reach on posts with URLs in the caption. Move your CTA link to the first comment (post it yourself immediately after publishing) and reference it as "link in first comment." Also add the link to your LinkedIn bio and reference "link in bio" in the video CTA. This typically 3–5x your UTM click rate.

**Problem:** Can't match LinkedIn video viewers to CRM contacts because LinkedIn doesn't export individual viewer data.
**Solution:** LinkedIn protects individual viewer identity. Use a proxy approach: (1) Export post "reactions and comments" — these users consented to identity. (2) Run LinkedIn Matched Audiences upload using your full CRM contact list, then target that audience with your boosted videos. (3) Use LinkedIn Insight Tag on your website to build a "website visitor who also engaged with LinkedIn video" audience — this is your best overlap signal. (4) For high-value accounts, monitor LinkedIn page follower growth correlated with video publish dates as a proxy for target account awareness.

**Problem:** BVPS scores are inconsistent — some low-view videos score high, some high-view videos score low.
**Solution:** This is working correctly. BVPS deliberately deprioritizes raw view count in favor of quality signals. A video with 800 views but 35% completion, 12 ICP comments, and 8 UTM clicks is genuinely more valuable than a viral video with 50K views, 8% completion, and 0 UTM clicks. Use BVPS to guide production decisions, not platform vanity metrics. Present BVPS to your CEO alongside view counts to reframe what "video success" means for pipeline-focused B2B programs.
