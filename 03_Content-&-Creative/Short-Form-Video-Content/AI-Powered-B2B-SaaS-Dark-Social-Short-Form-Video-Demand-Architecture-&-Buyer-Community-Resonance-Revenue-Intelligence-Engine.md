## Overview

Use this when you need to generate pipeline from buyers who have already decided they want your category before they ever fill out a form, by making content your ICP can't resist forwarding to their team and peers.

Trigger a workflow that tags these contacts with `source_category: dark_social` and routes them to a dedicated nurture track optimized for already-warm buyers. Use tools like Mention, Brand24, or manual keyword alerts (set up in relevant public channels) to track when your brand name, product category, or key pain-point phrases appear in communities you have access to. Create a weekly log of mention volume and context. Spike in mentions 2-4 weeks before a pipeline surge = dark social is working.

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Videos are being seeded in communities but no one engages or shares them.**
Fix: The content is being perceived as brand promotion, not peer value. Apply the "brand name test": remove your company name from the first 60 seconds and ask if the video still stands as valuable content for any practitioner regardless of vendor. If the value disappears without your brand name, the content is advertising. Dark social content must be useful before it is branded. Rewrite the script leading with a framework, observation, or data point that is universally true — your product is the optional continuation, not the premise.

**Problem: Champions say they'll share the internal sell kit but nothing happens.**
Fix: The kit is too long or requires the champion to do work. Reduce the entire kit to three elements: (1) a 90-second video that ends with an explicit question the champion can pose to their manager, (2) a single-sentence context blurb they can paste above the video link, (3) a two-sentence follow-up question they can ask their manager after they watch. The champion's job should require zero creativity — just copy-paste and forward.

**Problem: Community seeding works but new pipeline doesn't attribute to dark social efforts.**
Fix: The pipeline is appearing but attribution is being lost. Implement the self-reported attribution question across ALL new pipeline touchpoints — not just website forms. Train SDRs and AEs to ask "how did you first hear about us?" in the first 2 minutes of every discovery call and log the verbatim answer in CRM notes. Mine these notes monthly for dark social signals. Most companies ask this question once during onboarding — by which point buyers have forgotten the original source. Ask it during the buying process while memory is fresh.
