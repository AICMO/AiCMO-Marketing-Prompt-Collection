**Difficulty:** Advanced | **Time:** 20-30 min | **Tags:** short-form-video, content-repurposing, video-marketing, demand-generation, B2B-SaaS, LinkedIn, YouTube-Shorts, content-operations

## Overview
Transforms existing long-form video assets — webinars, podcasts, customer interviews, keynotes, and demos — into a steady stream of high-performing short-form clips optimized for LinkedIn, YouTube Shorts, and Instagram Reels. Use this when you want to multiply the pipeline impact of every piece of video content your team produces without adding headcount or production budget.

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Clips feel like corporate marketing content, not authentic video**
Solution: Prioritize VULNERABILITY and AHA MOMENT extraction signals over DATA PUNCH clips. Buyers trust authenticity over polish. The 86-second clip where a customer admits a mistake they made will outperform the polished "here are our results" clip 3:1. Rewrite all hooks to start with the human, not the data.

**Problem: High view counts but zero demo requests from clips**
Solution: The pipeline mechanism is missing. Review your 30-day calendar and ensure each clip has a clear "conversion path" — either a direct link in caption to a demo landing page, a comment-triggered DM sequence, or a retargeting campaign set up against video viewers. Views without a next step are brand awareness, not demand generation. Add a conversion mechanism to every clip before republishing.

**Problem: Content team can't sustain 8 clips/month per video asset**
Solution: Build the Repurposing Operations Playbook in Section 7 as a weekly AI-assisted workflow. The 3–4 hour human time investment should primarily be editing, not creation. If the team is spending more than 4 hours per video asset on repurposing, the AI prompt inputs in Steps 2–4 are too vague. Ensure the transcript fed into the AI includes specific timestamps, speaker names, and data points — the more context the AI has, the higher quality its first draft output.
