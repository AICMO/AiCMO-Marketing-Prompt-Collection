## DELIVERABLE 3: Listening Engagement Trigger Playbook

TRIGGER LEVEL 0 — EMAIL OPENED, LINK NOT CLICKED (48-hour window):

Escalation: If no response after 3 touch attempts, add to long-cycle newsletter segment

TRIGGER LEVEL 1 — ABANDONED LISTEN (Clicked link, <30% completion):
Interpretation: Topic interest but time/format mismatch
Action 1 (Day 2): Send written executive brief — 3 bullet insights from the episode
Action 2 (Day 4): Offer a shorter episode on the same topic (target: <15 minutes)
Action 3 (Day 7): "What would make this more useful?" message — personalized question about their specific situation
Flag: Add to SDR task queue for manual personalization assessment

TRIGGER LEVEL 2 — ENGAGED LISTEN (30-70% completion):
Interpretation: Genuine interest, likely a topic they're actively working on
Action 1 (Day 1): Automated follow-up email: "You may have caught the part where [guest] mentioned [X] — here's the framework they referenced"
Action 2 (Day 3): Send complementary episode that goes deeper on the aspect they likely reached
Action 3 (Day 5): Offer related tool, calculator, or assessment tied to the episode topic
Platform Action: Trigger MQA (Marketing Qualified Account) flag in ABM platform; elevate account to next tier if currently Tier 2
Rep Alert: Notify owning SDR with context and suggested outreach window (within 72 hours)

TRIGGER LEVEL 3 — POWER LISTEN (>70% completion):
Interpretation: High engagement, equivalent to reading a full white paper — treat as inbound intent signal
Immediate Actions (within 2 hours):
- Slack/Teams alert to AE/SDR: "[Name] at [Company] just listened to 84% of Episode [X] — [episode topic]. Their company just [recent trigger event]. Here are 3 questions to ask in the next 24 hours."
- CRM: Automatically increase account score by [X] points; add to priority calling queue

Action 2 (Day 2, if no response): LinkedIn DM from AE as peer-to-peer outreach
Action 3 (Day 4): Invite to exclusive "extended conversation" — live Q&A with the episode guest, framed as a small group peer roundtable

TRIGGER LEVEL 4 — SERIES BEHAVIOR (3+ episodes consumed within 30 days):
Interpretation: This account is self-educating — they're building a business case or evaluating solutions
Treatment: Escalate immediately to executive briefing track
Action 1: CMO or VP Marketing personal outreach (not SDR)

## Troubleshooting

**Problem:** Executives aren't clicking podcast links in cold emails — open rates are acceptable but audio CTR is near zero
**Solution:** The primary channel mismatch is the issue — executives rarely click audio links in cold email from strangers. Switch your cold outreach primary channel to LinkedIn DM, where audio content shares feel more natural and peer-to-peer. Use email only for warm follow-up after a LinkedIn connection is established or after prior engagement. Additionally, lower the commitment ask: instead of linking to the full episode, share a 90-second audio clip (Podscribe, Audacity, or Descript) of the single most compelling moment — with the full episode as a secondary optional link. State the time commitment explicitly: "90-second clip" performs 3× better than "listen to our podcast."

**Problem:** Listening analytics are incomplete — can see that the episode was accessed but cannot track individual completion rates or attribute anonymous listens to specific companies
**Solution:** Implement a two-layer tracking approach: (1) For anonymous public listeners, install Podscribe's tracking pixel on your podcast host and enable IP-to-company matching via 6sense or Clearbit — this identifies the company behind 40–60% of anonymous listens. (2) For Tier 1 named accounts, create private podcast feeds via Hello Audio — each account gets a unique RSS URL you share directly, giving you perfect individual-level analytics with zero attribution gap. These two layers combined capture 80%+ of meaningful listening data from your ICP.

**Problem:** Sales reps are receiving Power Listen alerts but not acting on them within the 24-hour window — the opportunity is getting lost to rep inertia
**Solution:** The alert format is likely too long and the required action is too ambiguous. Redesign the alert to be a single actionable Slack card: "[First Name] at [Company] listened to 87% of Episode [X] on [Topic]. Their [trigger event]. Here are your 3 talking points. Reply to claim this account." Add a one-click CTA — a pre-written LinkedIn DM they can send in 15 seconds. Create a weekly "Podcast Intelligence Leaderboard" showing which reps booked meetings from podcast signals that week — competitive gamification drives 2–3× faster rep adoption than process mandates alone.
