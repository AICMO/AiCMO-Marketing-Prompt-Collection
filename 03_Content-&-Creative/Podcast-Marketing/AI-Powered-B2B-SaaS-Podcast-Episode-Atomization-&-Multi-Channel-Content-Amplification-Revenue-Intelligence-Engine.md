This prompt turns a single podcast episode into 15+ pieces of pipeline-generating content distributed across every channel your buyers inhabit — automatically. 6. **EMAIL NEWSLETTER SNIPPET** (150-200 words)

## Integration Tips

- **Riverside / Descript:** Export transcript automatically after recording; feed directly into the Quick Copy-Paste version. Most platforms now offer AI summaries — use these as a starting point but run the full atomization prompt for depth.
- **HubSpot:** Create a custom "Episode" object; attach all UTM-tracked asset links to track which content piece each contact engaged with before converting. Use smart lists to segment contacts who listened vs. read vs. watched.
- **Notion:** Build an Episode Database with properties for each asset type (status: draft/review/live). Use Notion AI to generate first drafts from the prompt output, then review before publishing.
- **Zapier:** Automate: when a new YouTube video is published → trigger Slack notification to sales → attach the episode brief. Also: when a LinkedIn post gets >50 likes → add to "top performers" Notion database for content pattern analysis.
- **Buffer / Hootsuite:** Schedule all 7 LinkedIn posts and 3 threads at once using the 14-day calendar output. Tag guest handles so they can approve and re-share.
- **Google Sheets Attribution Tracker:** Create one row per episode with columns for: publish date, total assets deployed, total reach (manual pull), UTM clicks, pipeline sourced (from CRM), and CAC per episode. Review quarterly to find which episode themes drive the most pipeline.
- **Descript:** Use the short-form clip hooks to brief your video editor on exactly where to cut. Descript's auto-highlight feature can identify the moments you identified in the prompt.

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: The AI output is generic — it doesn't capture the guest's specific frameworks or voice.**
Solution: Front-load the prompt with a "key moments" section before the full transcript. Pull 5-10 verbatim quotes you want emphasized and add them as "PRIORITY QUOTES — use these in at least 3 assets each." The model will anchor to specific language rather than paraphrasing.

**Problem: LinkedIn posts feel too salesy or corporate — not getting engagement.**
Solution: Activate the "Post 4: Contrarian/hot take" format first. Engagement on B2B LinkedIn in 2026 comes from POV, not announcements. Remove product mentions entirely from the first 4 posts in the sequence; let the episode's insight carry the value. Add your product CTA only in posts 5-7 after audience trust is established.

**Problem: Sales team is not using the Slack brief in outreach.**
Solution: Add a "Copy/paste this message" block to the brief — literally the exact outreach message they can send, with [placeholder] for prospect name and company. Reduce cognitive load to zero. Also add a "Best accounts to send this to" section using language from your ICP definition so reps know exactly who to target, not just what to say.
