## Overview
Designs and operationalizes a complete B2B SaaS podcast strategy — from format positioning and guest sourcing, through episode production workflows and content atomization, to pipeline attribution and compounding SEO — as a fully autonomous demand generation channel. Use this when you want to build a podcast that functions as a consistent top-of-funnel pipeline engine, not just a brand awareness vanity project.

Build my complete podcast revenue engine:

1. **PODCAST FORMAT & POSITIONING** — Define the show concept that wins in a crowded B2B podcast space: a differentiated show title and tagline that your ICP would subscribe to before ever hearing an episode, the core format (practitioner interviews / roundtable debates / solo thought leadership / customer case studies / hybrid), the 3-sentence "why this show exists" that differentiates it from every other podcast your ICP already follows, and the specific content pillar topics that map directly to your buyer's top 5 decision-making questions.

2. **IDEAL GUEST PROFILE & SOURCING SYSTEM** — Build the repeatable guest pipeline: the exact title, seniority level, company stage, and "credibility signal" that makes a guest worth booking (not anyone with a LinkedIn presence — the practitioners your ICP actually trusts), 5 specific naming examples of ideal guests by role and why your ICP respects them, a cold outreach email template that gets a 40%+ reply rate from busy executives, and a systematic sourcing workflow using LinkedIn Sales Navigator, Twitter/X lists, newsletter authors, and conference speaker rosters to surface 10 bookable guests per month.

3. **EPISODE PRODUCTION WORKFLOW** — Design the end-to-end production system a single marketer can operate without a production agency: pre-interview research brief template (company background, recent LinkedIn activity, 5 questions tailored to their specific experience, 2 contrarian debate topics), interview question framework that reliably produces 3+ quotable moments per episode, post-production checklist (transcript → show notes → chapter markers → clip selection), and the 48-hour post-recording turnaround workflow from raw audio to published episode with all derivative assets ready.

4. **CONTENT ATOMIZATION INTO 15+ ASSETS PER EPISODE** — Extract maximum pipeline value from each recording:
   - 3 LinkedIn posts (one hook-driven insight carousel, one short video clip with caption, one text-only "what I learned from [Guest Name]" post)
   - 2 email newsletter segments (one teaser before publish, one deep-dive insight 3 days after publish)
   - 1 SEO-optimized blog post using the episode transcript (targeting 1 high-intent keyword your ICP searches during evaluation)
   - 3 short-form video clips for LinkedIn and YouTube Shorts (specify which moments in a standard episode structure reliably produce clip-worthy content)
   - 1 sales enablement asset (the 90-second clip or quote that sales can use in outreach to prospects experiencing the specific pain discussed)
   - 1 Twitter/X thread from the guest's most contrarian take
   - Show notes page optimized for organic search with full transcript and internal links to product pages

5. **GUEST-TO-PIPELINE CONVERSION SYSTEM** — Turn podcast guests into revenue opportunities: the post-recording thank-you email sequence that keeps guests engaged as brand advocates, how to identify when a guest's company is in-market based on their engagement patterns after the episode goes live, the "guest network expansion" playbook where each guest refers 2-3 additional guests who are also ICP-fit accounts, and the subtle CTA architecture within episodes that drives listeners to book demos without feeling like a sales pitch (the "curiosity gap" method that converts 3-5x better than direct CTAs).

6. **PIPELINE ATTRIBUTION SYSTEM** — Measure podcast's actual revenue contribution: UTM tracking setup for all podcast-driven traffic, the listener-to-lead conversion tracking methodology using dedicated landing pages with episode-specific CTAs, the CRM tagging framework to track "podcast-influenced" pipeline separate from "podcast-sourced" pipeline, and the 6-week podcast attribution window logic that captures dark social impact where listeners engage weeks after hearing an episode.

7. **90-DAY LAUNCH & DISTRIBUTION PLAN** — Specific week-by-week execution to launch with momentum: pre-launch strategy (record 5 episodes before announcing, build subscriber waitlist), launch week promotional playbook (simultaneous LinkedIn + email + guest cross-promotion to hit the charts), the ongoing distribution cadence across Apple Podcasts, Spotify, YouTube, and the B2B community channels where your ICP is most active (specific Slack communities, newsletters, subreddits).

Output as a complete, execution-ready system. Every section must be specific enough that a single marketing manager can begin implementation tomorrow using AI tools — no vague brand awareness goals, only measurable pipeline outputs.

### OUTPUT 1: SHOW CONCEPT & COMPETITIVE POSITIONING

Deliver the following:

**Show identity system:**
- Show title: 3 options, each with a different positioning angle (authority, community, insider access)
- Tagline: One sentence that makes an ICP-fit practitioner immediately understand why this show is different from the 3 existing shows they already follow
- Show description (150 words): What you cover, who you feature, what listeners consistently learn — written for Apple Podcasts and Spotify directory pages optimized for keyword discovery
- The "unfair advantage" this show has that competitors cannot replicate: typically a host's unique access, company's proprietary data, or distinctive guest network

**Content pillar architecture:**
Map each content pillar to:
- The specific buying stage question it answers (Awareness / Consideration / Decision)
- The podcast episode format that works best for that pillar (debate, case study, how-to, prediction)
- 3 specific episode title examples that your ICP would click based on this pillar
- How content from this pillar connects back to your product's core use case without being promotional

**Differentiation from competitive shows:**
For each of the 3 identified competitor shows, specify:
- What they cover well (do not duplicate this)
- What they consistently miss (your white space)
- One specific episode type you will own that they cannot

### OUTPUT 2: GUEST ACQUISITION SYSTEM

**Ideal Guest Profile (IGP) scorecard:**
Score potential guests 0-100 across these dimensions:
- ICP alignment: Does their company and role match your buyer profile? (0-30 pts)
- Audience credibility: Do they have a LinkedIn following, published writing, or conference speaking history your ICP respects? (0-20 pts)
- Content value: Do they have a specific story, result, or framework they can share that maps to your content pillars? (0-25 pts)
- Network value: Could they refer additional high-IGP guests? (0-15 pts)
- Pipeline potential: Is their company a potential customer or expansion account? (0-10 pts)

Minimum threshold for booking: 65/100

**Guest sourcing channels and workflows:**
For each sourcing channel, provide:
1. LinkedIn Sales Navigator: Exact Boolean search strings for your ICP by title, seniority, company stage, and engagement signals (recently posted, shares thought leadership content)
2. Conference speaker rosters: Which 5 conferences your ICP speaks at — and the process for identifying speakers by reviewing session abstracts for content pillar alignment
3. Newsletter authors and community moderators: Specific newsletters your ICP writes or curates, and the pitch to cross-promote an episode
4. Guest-to-guest referral: The post-recording email that asks for 2 warm introductions without being transactional
5. SDR collaboration: How to involve your sales team in identifying pipeline-stage accounts whose VP would benefit from being featured (creates relationship with ICP while generating content)

**Outreach sequence:**
- Email 1 (cold): Subject line, body copy (150 words max), and the single specific signal you reference that shows you actually know their work — not a generic podcast pitch
- Email 2 (follow-up, Day 7): Different angle — focus on what they get from being on the show (audience reach, content assets you create for them)
- LinkedIn DM (if email goes unanswered): 3-sentence version of the pitch
- Booking confirmation email: Includes pre-interview questionnaire to surface the 3 specific stories you'll focus the conversation on

**Booking velocity target:** 8-10 booked guests/month to sustain biweekly publishing cadence with 4-week lead time buffer

### OUTPUT 3: EPISODE PRODUCTION SYSTEM

**Pre-interview brief template (complete):**
For each episode, generate:
- Guest background summary (3 paragraphs from public sources — LinkedIn, podcast appearances, published writing)
- Their publicly stated opinions on the 3 content pillars you'll cover — and where they might disagree with conventional wisdom (source for contrarian debate)
- 5 warm-up questions to establish rapport in the first 5 minutes
- 8 core questions that reliably produce quotable, specific answers — structured as "Tell me about a time when..." and "What do most [job titles] get wrong about [topic]?"
- 3 "challenge" questions that push back respectfully to surface the guest's most confident takes
- Specific signal to watch for that means the conversation is going in a direction that produces clip-worthy content

**Recording setup and quality standards:**
- Minimum technical requirements for acceptable audio quality (guests often record from suboptimal setups)
- The exact pre-recording checklist email to send guests 24 hours before recording
- Backup recording setup in case guest's audio fails
- The 3 recording mistakes that kill episode quality and how to prevent each

**Post-production workflow (48-hour turnaround):**
- Hour 0-2 (Day of recording): Raw audio → AI transcription → transcript review for accuracy
- Hour 2-6: Episode editing brief (what to cut, what to keep, where to add chapter markers)
- Hour 6-12: Show notes draft from transcript → SEO optimization → CTA placement
- Hour 12-24: Clip selection (identify 3-5 moments ≤90 seconds that work as standalone social content)
- Hour 24-36: All derivative assets drafted (LinkedIn posts, email segments, sales clip, blog post outline)
- Hour 36-48: Final episode review, scheduling, and distribution queue setup

**Quality control checklist before publishing:**
- Audio quality passes threshold (no background noise, consistent volume, clean edits)
- Show notes include target keyword in H1, first paragraph, and 2-3 subheadings
- Transcript published on episode page (SEO and accessibility)
- Internal links: show notes link to minimum 2 relevant product pages and 2 previous episodes
- CTA: One primary CTA per episode (demo / newsletter / content download — not all three)
- Guest review: Guest has approved quotes used in promotional copy

### OUTPUT 4: CONTENT ATOMIZATION SYSTEM (15+ ASSETS PER EPISODE)

For a standard 35-minute practitioner interview episode, specify:

**LinkedIn content (7 posts per episode):**
1. Pre-launch teaser post (2 days before): "Next week on [Show Name], [Guest] told me something that completely changed how I think about [Topic]. One quote: '[Specific pull quote from episode]'. Episode drops [Day]." — Hook formula and 150-word post template
2. Launch day post — host (Episode 1): The single most counterintuitive insight from the episode as a carousel or text post. Include the moment in the episode that produced it (timestamp reference helps on video podcast)
3. Launch day post — guest: Pre-written post the guest can share with 1-click approval (they almost always share if you write it for them). Optimized for their audience, not yours
4. Clip post Day 3: 60-90 second video clip posted natively to LinkedIn with closed captions. Caption formula: problem statement → guest's solution → CTA to full episode
5. "Key lesson" post Day 7: One specific framework or mental model from the episode, reformatted as a standalone insight. No explicit episode reference needed — functions as organic thought leadership for the host
6. Stat/data post Day 14 (if episode contained specific data): "[Guest] shared a stat that stopped me: [Specific statistic]. Here's what it means for [ICP job title]..."
7. Repurposed insight post Day 30: The episode's most evergreen insight recycled as a new angle — position it as "something I keep coming back to from a conversation a month ago"

**Email newsletter (3 segments per episode):**
1. Preview email (48 hours before publish): Subject line formula, 150-word teaser that builds curiosity without spoiling the best insight, CTA to subscribe to podcast
2. Launch email (episode publish day): Full show notes summary for subscribers who prefer reading, key quotes formatted for skimmability, primary CTA (demo / content download)
3. "One insight" email (7 days post-publish): Single framework or mental model extracted from the episode, presented as standalone value with no podcast branding necessary — builds newsletter subscriber LTV independent of podcast listen rates

**SEO blog post (1 per episode):**
- Target keyword selection: How to identify the 1 keyword from the episode topic that has search volume from your ICP during evaluation stage (not just high volume — intent-qualified volume)
- Blog post structure: Headline, intro hook (use the guest's most quotable moment), 5 H2 sections that answer the keyword's implicit question, guest quote integration throughout, internal links to 2 product pages and 2 related episode pages, conclusion with primary CTA
- Word count target: 1,200-1,800 words (enough for SEO without padding)
- Time to index estimate: 30-60 days for new domains, 7-14 days for established domains — plan launch-week distribution accordingly

**Sales enablement clip (1 per episode):**
- The 60-90 second clip that maps to a specific sales objection or competitor comparison your reps face weekly
- How to identify which moment in the recording produces this clip (hint: when the guest describes a problem your product solves from a practitioner's perspective, it's more credible than your sales rep saying it)
- The SDR outreach template that embeds this clip: "I noticed you [signal — e.g., posted about CI/CD pipeline speed last week]. A VP Eng I spoke with on our podcast shared exactly how they solved this. 90 seconds: [link]"
- CRM activity logging: How to track when prospects engage with sales-shared podcast clips

**Additional assets:**
- Twitter/X thread: The guest's most debatable claim, structured as 8-12 tweets with the thread opener designed to generate replies from other practitioners in the ICP community
- YouTube upload: Full episode video (if video podcast) with timestamp chapters, SEO-optimized title and description, end screen linking to 2 related episodes
- Internal Slack post: Weekly digest to sales team of the most pipeline-relevant episode moment and the accounts where this topic is active in current deals

### OUTPUT 5: GUEST-TO-PIPELINE CONVERSION ARCHITECTURE

**Post-recording nurture sequence (for guests who are ICP-fit accounts):**

Email 1 — Day of recording:
Subject: "Your episode is in post-production — here's what's coming"
Content: Thank-you, episode publish timeline, preview of the assets you're creating for them (written LinkedIn post, clip), and one follow-up question that extends the relationship without being sales-y

Email 2 — Episode publish day:
Subject: "[Podcast Name] — Your episode is live (+ assets attached)"
Content: Episode link, guest's pre-written LinkedIn post ready to share, clip file, and one personal observation about a specific thing they said that was most valuable. NO product mention.

Email 3 — Day 14:
Subject: "Quick update from [Their Name]'s episode"
Content: Engagement metrics (listens in first 2 weeks, LinkedIn reach from the post), any listener questions or replies that came in. This creates reciprocity and is a natural transition to: "A lot of listeners resonated with the [specific challenge] you mentioned — we actually built [feature] specifically for that problem. Would a 15-minute chat be relevant?"

**Intent signal monitoring for guest accounts:**
Track these signals in your CRM or intent tool:
- Guest's company visits your pricing page within 30 days of episode publish
- Guest or colleague from same company engages with your LinkedIn ads
- Guest's company appears in G2 "alternatives to [Your Product]" category traffic
- Guest's company opens 2+ emails in your newsletter within 30 days

When 2+ signals fire: Route to assigned AE as "warm podcast guest — approach as relationship, not cold outreach"

**Listener-to-pipeline conversion:**
- Episode CTA architecture: One soft CTA per episode (newsletter, related content download, community join) + one hard CTA in show notes (demo request). Never both in the same episode.
- Dedicated podcast landing page: A single URL your host mentions in every episode with a listener-exclusive content offer (template, framework, report) that gates with email. This is your primary podcast list-building mechanism.
- Podcast listener nurture track (separate from standard nurture): 6-email sequence starting the day they download the listener offer, focused on the content pillar of the episode they listened to, progressing to soft demo CTA at email 4.

Company: Retool — Low-code internal tools platform for engineering teams

Competitors: Appsmith, DronaHQ, Budibase, custom-built solutions

**Output Example (Quick Version, Section 1 — Show Format & Positioning):**

Show concept: **"Internal Infrastructure" — The show for engineering leaders building the systems that power their product team**

Tagline: *Conversations with VPs Eng and platform engineers about the internal tools, systems, and decisions that 10x engineering productivity — and the ones that cost them 6 months of technical debt.*

Format: 35-minute practitioner interviews, biweekly. Format rationale: Your ICP makes vendor decisions in 30-45 minute windows. A show at exactly their decision-making attention span signals you understand how they work.

Why this show is different from existing shows your ICP listens to:
- **vs. Software Engineering Daily**: They interview anyone interesting across the full software stack. You interview practitioners making the specific decision your product solves. Narrower = more valuable to the right audience.
- **vs. The Pragmatic Engineer Podcast**: Focuses on individual career development and product engineering. You focus on the infrastructure layer beneath the product — engineering productivity, internal tooling, platform engineering.
- **vs. Ship It!**: Infrastructure and DevOps focus, rarely covers internal tooling and developer experience. That's your white space.

Content pillar Episode examples:
1. *"We deleted 40,000 lines of internal tooling code in one quarter — here's what we replaced it with"* (Pain recognition stage)
2. *"Why we stopped building admin panels from scratch after 5 engineers quit over tooling frustration"* (Consideration stage)
3. *"The internal tooling audit we do before every Series B: how to identify what to build vs. buy"* (Decision stage — directly maps to Retool's category)

Guest archetype for this show: VP Engineering or Platform Engineering Lead who has made a visible "build vs. buy" decision on internal tooling and can speak specifically to the engineering team productivity outcomes.

## Integration Tips

- **Riverside.fm or Squadcast**: Use for remote recording — both produce high-quality audio and video simultaneously. Riverside's AI transcription integrates directly with your show notes workflow.
- **Transistor or Buzzsprout**: Podcast hosting with analytics that tracks listener geography, listening apps, and episode completion rates. Transistor integrates with Zapier for automated CRM contact creation.
- **Zapier**: When a new listener submits the podcast landing page form → create/update CRM contact → add to "Podcast Listener Nurture" sequence → tag as "Podcast Sourced" → notify SDR if company matches ICP criteria.
- **HubSpot**: Set up a custom "Podcast Engagement" property. Workflows: if contact submits podcast offer form → enroll in 6-email podcast nurture → after email 4, trigger internal notification to assigned AE if lead score ≥ 45.
- **Salesforce + Pardot**: Create podcast UTM-specific campaign in Salesforce. Build a Pardot engagement program for podcast listeners with branching logic based on content pillar (episode topic) → routes to the SDR cadence aligned to the relevant product use case.
- **Descript**: AI-powered audio/video editing tool that lets a non-editor marketer produce professional episodes. Edit by editing the transcript text — no timeline experience required.
- **Notion**: Guest pipeline tracker with IGP score columns, booking status, episode publish dates, and post-episode intent signals. Link each guest record to their CRM contact.
- **Taplio or Shield Analytics**: Track LinkedIn performance of podcast-derivative posts to identify which episode topics drive the most engagement from your ICP — feeds directly back into content pillar prioritization.

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Episode downloads are growing but pipeline attribution shows zero podcast-sourced leads**
Solution: The tracking infrastructure is broken, not the podcast. Audit: (1) Is the podcast landing page URL actually unique and trackable vs. your main website? (2) Are UTMs being passed through to your CRM when the listener offer form is submitted? (3) Is the "Podcast Sourced" CRM tag being applied to contacts who come through the podcast-specific URL? Fix tracking before optimizing content — you may be generating pipeline that's being attributed to "direct" or "organic."

**Problem: Can't book enough quality guests — outreach reply rate is below 15%**
Solution: Three fixes in order of impact: (1) Your pitch is too vague — the first email must reference something specific the guest has written or said publicly (a LinkedIn post, a conference talk, a published article). Generic "I love your work" pitches fail at >90% rate. (2) Your show's guest-value proposition is unclear — state explicitly what they get: a professionally produced episode asset they can use for their own personal brand, a LinkedIn post written for them, a clip. (3) Activate your SDR team — have sales book 2 guests/month from active pipeline accounts where being featured on the podcast is positioned as a relationship gesture, not a sales tactic.

**Problem: Guest episodes perform well but solo episodes (host-only) underperform**
Solution: Solo B2B podcast episodes fail when they feel like repurposed blog posts read aloud. Reframe solo episodes as "briefings" — 12-15 minutes maximum, structured as: (1) one specific signal or data point from the market that week (2) what it means for your ICP (3) one contrarian take that challenges conventional wisdom. Frame the host as a market intelligence source, not a content publisher. If solo episodes still underperform, eliminate them — guest episodes perform better for 90% of B2B shows, and consistency of format builds subscriber habit.
