## Overview
Converts your B2B podcast audience from passive listeners into an active pipeline engine by designing in-show conversion architecture, listener data capture systems, retargeting infrastructure, and a revenue attribution framework that proves podcast ROI to CFOs and CMOs. Use this when you have an existing podcast (or are launching one) and need to close the loop between listenership and closed-won revenue.

DELIVERABLE 1 — IN-SHOW CONVERSION ARCHITECTURE

A. EPISODE CTA SYSTEM DESIGN

Design a rotating 3-CTA system where different episodes emphasize different conversion goals:

Tier 1 — EDUCATION CTA (use in 40% of episodes)
Purpose: Convert curious listeners into known email contacts
Trigger: Best for educational/tactical episodes where listeners want to save resources

Script framework:
"Before we wrap — [Guest Name] mentioned [specific tactic from episode]. We turned it into a [worksheet/template/checklist] you can run with your team in 20 minutes. It's free, it's in the show notes at [podcast-domain]/[episode-number]."

Conversion architecture: Episode-specific landing page → Resource download → Email capture → Immediate delivery + "you might also like" episode → 7-day nurture sequence → Behavioral score trigger if they visit pricing page within 30 days

Expected conversion: 2–4% of downloads opt-in to episode-specific resource

Tier 2 — COMMUNITY CTA (use in 35% of episodes)
Purpose: Move listeners into a trackable owned community (Slack, Circle, LinkedIn Group, newsletter)
Trigger: Best for episodes touching on peer comparison, shared frustrations, or "what are others doing?"

Script framework:
"If you're wrestling with [episode theme], you're not alone — we run a [community name] where [ICP title]s share [specific type of knowledge]. It's free to join and the conversation is way more candid than anything on LinkedIn. Link in the show notes."

Conversion architecture: Community landing page → Approval/signup → Tagged in CRM as "Podcast Community Member" → Account enriched via Clearbit/Clay → High-ICP members flagged for SDR warmup sequence

Expected conversion: 1–2% of downloads, but these contacts convert to pipeline at 3.4× the rate of cold leads

Tier 3 — DIRECT OFFER CTA (use in 25% of episodes — highest-intent topics only)
Purpose: Convert listeners actively evaluating solutions
Trigger: Best for episodes covering "how to choose a vendor," "implementation," or "making the business case"

Script framework:
"If this episode hit close to home — if you're actively solving [specific problem] right now — we offer a [free assessment/30-minute working session/personalized ROI analysis] where we [specific deliverable, not a sales call]. No pitch. You'll leave with [specific takeaway]. Book at [podcast-domain]/[session-slug]."

Conversion architecture: Booking page with 3 qualifying questions → Routes to BDR calendar → Tagged in CRM as "Podcast High-Intent" → Sales outreach same business day with episode context prepopulated

Expected conversion: 0.5–1.5% of downloads, but these are demo-qualified at point of booking

B. EPISODE LANDING PAGE ARCHITECTURE
For every episode, build a dedicated page at [podcast-domain]/ep-[number] with:
- Episode title + 200-word SEO summary (discoverability beyond your existing listeners)
- Embedded player + full transcript (Google indexing + accessibility)
- One primary CTA matching the episode's tier designation
- LinkedIn/Twitter share with pre-populated guest quote
- "Listen to this if you're struggling with [problem]" framing — intent signal for retargeting segmentation
- Hidden UTM parameters: episode number, CTA tier, publish date

This page is your retargeting pixel drop point. Every visitor is cookied and identifiable before they ever fill out a form.

DELIVERABLE 2 — FIRST-PARTY DATA CAPTURE ENGINE

A. LISTENER DATA FUNNEL LAYERS

Layer 1 — Anonymous Listeners (majority of your audience)
You cannot identify them individually. But you can:
- Drop retargeting pixels on every episode page
- Track which episodes drive the most page dwell time (signal: high-engagement topics = ICP resonance)
- Identify referring domains (which newsletters, shows, or communities send you listeners)

Layer 2 — Episode Page Visitors (10–15% of downloads click through to show notes)
Install 6sense, Clearbit Reveal, or RB2B on all episode pages. These tools deanonymize at the company level — you'll see "someone from [Company Name] read the episode about [Topic]" with no form required.

Priority trigger: If a visitor from a target account visits 2+ episode pages within 30 days → create a CRM activity → flag for SDR outreach with podcast context ("Someone from [Company] has been reading your podcast content on [Topics] — here's what we know").

Layer 3 — Resource Downloaders and Community Members (opted-in contacts)
These are your highest-value listeners. Segment in CRM:
- "Podcast Subscriber": downloaded resource from any episode
- "Podcast Community Member": joined community from episode CTA
- "Podcast High-Intent": booked a session from Tier 3 CTA
Lead score additions: +15 for Podcast Subscriber, +25 for Community Member, +50 for High-Intent Booker

B. PODCAST NEWSLETTER AS OWNED AUDIENCE ASSET
Every episode page includes a persistent opt-in: "Get the next episode + a one-page insight summary — join [X] subscribers."
This newsletter is YOUR email list, not Spotify's or Apple's. It is the only podcast audience asset that survives platform changes.
Newsletter format: 200-word episode summary, key quote pulled to LinkedIn, downloadable resource from Tier 1 CTA, one soft product use case reference tied to the episode theme.

Google Display:

Shows: age range, gender, country, episode completion rates

Shows: downloads by episode, date, geography, listening apps

Run a 10-question survey linked from 1 episode per quarter. Use Typeform or Airtable with conditional logic.

Required questions:
1. Job title (free text)
2. Company size (employees) — picklist: 1–50 / 51–200 / 201–1,000 / 1,001–5,000 / 5,000+
3. Industry (picklist relevant to your ICP)

5. Are you currently evaluating [product category] solutions? Yes, actively / Yes, casually / No / Already a customer of [Company]

7. Which episode topic was most valuable? (free text)

9. What topic do you most want us to cover? (free text)
10. Your email (optional — "to receive a summary of the survey results")

## Troubleshooting

- **Problem: Episode landing pages receive traffic but email conversion rates are below 15%.** Solution: The resource offer is not specific enough to the episode. Listeners need to feel the asset was built *for that exact conversation*, not pulled from a generic content library. Audit: Does the CTA script name the guest and reference a specific tactic they shared? Does the landing page headline match the CTA script word-for-word? Replace generic lead magnets ("Our B2B Marketing Guide") with episode-specific assets ("The [Guest First Name] Forecast Confidence Worksheet — from Episode #[X]"). Specificity is the conversion multiplier.

- **Problem: Salesforce shows zero podcast-influenced opportunities after 60 days of running the system.** Solution: The demo request form attribution question is not working correctly. Check: Is "Podcast" an explicit option in a forced picklist (not a free-text field)? Free-text "how did you hear?" fields miss 80%+ of podcast attribution because respondents write "online" or "social" instead. Second: audit the most recent 20 closed deals manually in Gong transcripts — search for your podcast name. You will almost certainly find dark social mentions that were never logged. Use these to seed the attribution fields retroactively and calibrate your expected attribution rate going forward.

- **Problem: LinkedIn Matched Audience from episode page visitors never reaches the 300-contact minimum to activate.** Solution: Episode landing pages lack enough organic or referral traffic. Two fixes: (1) Publish a long-form transcript post targeting a "[Topic] complete guide" keyword — organic search traffic can 5–10× page visits vs. show notes alone. (2) Run a $300 LinkedIn Sponsored Content campaign pointing directly to your 3 highest-performing episode pages instead of your homepage — this seeds the retargeting audience within 2–3 weeks and creates a compounding traffic base as the algorithm learns your ICP.
