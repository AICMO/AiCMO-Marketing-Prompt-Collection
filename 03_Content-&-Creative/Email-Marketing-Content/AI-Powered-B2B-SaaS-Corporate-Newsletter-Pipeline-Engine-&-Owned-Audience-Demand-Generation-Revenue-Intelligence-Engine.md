## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Open rates are stuck below 25% despite good list quality**
Solution: The problem is almost always the subject line, not the content. Run a 30-day subject line intensive: every issue A/B tests 2 radically different subject line styles (question vs. statement, specific number vs. concept, first person vs. third person). Also audit preview text — it contributes 15–20% of open rate lift independently. Audit send time: for VP/C-suite ICP, Tuesday–Thursday 8–9am and 5–6pm in recipient's local timezone consistently outperforms midday sends.

**Problem: Subscribers open but don't click — low click-to-open rate (< 8%)**
Solution: This is a CTA placement or offer problem, not an engagement problem. Diagnose by heat mapping your email template (Litmus or Email on Acid). Most B2B newsletters bury the CTA in the footer after 800+ words. Move your primary CTA to the end of Section 1 (after the first 200–300 words). Make CTA copy specific to what they just read ("Get the template we mentioned above") rather than generic ("Book a demo"). Also test click-only sends: one issue per quarter that's entirely CTA-forward — a curated list of 5 resources, each with a specific click. This trains subscriber behavior.

**Problem: Newsletter MQL rate is < 0.5% — subscribers aren't converting to pipeline**
Solution: This is a subscriber quality or CTA architecture problem. Run a 2-week diagnostic: (1) Pull firmographic report on all current subscribers — what % match ICP criteria? If < 40% match ICP, subscriber acquisition is pulling the wrong audience; fix acquisition channels before CTA optimization. (2) If subscriber quality is fine, the CTA offers don't map to subscriber intent. Newsletter readers are in "learning mode," not "buying mode" — replace hard demo CTAs with intent-revealing content CTAs (ROI calculator, benchmark report, peer comparison tool). These generate more clicks AND identify higher-intent subscribers. (3) Check the newsletter MQL definition: if the engagement threshold is too low (e.g., just 1 open + 1 click), you're routing low-intent contacts to sales and burning SDR capacity. Raise the bar.
