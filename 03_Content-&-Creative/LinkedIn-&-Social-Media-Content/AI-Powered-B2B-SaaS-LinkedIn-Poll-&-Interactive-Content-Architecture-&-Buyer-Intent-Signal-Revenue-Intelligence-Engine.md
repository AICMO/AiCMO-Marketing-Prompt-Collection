## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Polls getting fewer than 100 votes despite >5,000 followers**
Solution: Poll distribution is determined by initial engagement velocity — the first 2 hours matter most. Post polls at Tuesday–Thursday 8–10am in your ICP's time zone. Immediately engage with the first 10–15 commenters personally to trigger the algorithm. Ask 2–3 colleagues to vote and comment within the first 30 minutes. Even a single reply to your poll from a person with 10,000+ followers can 3x your reach.

**Problem: High vote volume but no ICP match — lots of students, job seekers, non-buyers voting**
Solution: Your poll questions are too broad or the framing doesn't qualify the audience. Add specificity that self-selects for ICP: instead of "What's your biggest sales challenge?" try "If you're managing a quota-carrying team of 10+ reps, what's your forecast accuracy?" The specificity filters out non-buyers before they vote. Also re-evaluate your follower base — if your audience is heavily non-ICP, the poll program needs to be paired with a follower acquisition strategy targeting ICP profiles first.

**Problem: Responder outreach getting no replies or feeling intrusive**
Solution: The most common mistake is messaging too quickly or being too direct. Wait until the poll closes (never DM during active poll window — it feels predatory). Lead with an insight specific to their vote, not a question about their company. Reference the community context ("You were one of 847 people who voted...") to create social proof. If your first message gets no reply after 4 days, send a second with a free resource — not a follow-up asking if they saw your message.
