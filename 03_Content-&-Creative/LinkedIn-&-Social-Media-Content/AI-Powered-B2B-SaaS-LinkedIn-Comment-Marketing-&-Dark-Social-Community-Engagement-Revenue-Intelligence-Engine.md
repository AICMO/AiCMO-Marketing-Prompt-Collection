## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Comments get high likes but no replies or DM conversions.**
Solution: The comment is delivering value but not creating a "curiosity gap." Audit your comment endings — replace declarative statements ("This is why pipeline reconciliation fails") with diagnostic questions ("Is the stage definition problem your root cause, or is it data latency?"). Questions that require the reader to share context to answer them generate 3-4x more replies than statements.

**Problem: Post authors never engage with comments, even on high-scoring posts.**
Solution: You're likely targeting posts from high-follower creators who get hundreds of comments and can't respond to all. Shift 60% of comment volume to mid-tier posts (500-5,000 impressions, 10-50 comments) where the post author is actively engaged in the thread. Comment engagement rate is inversely correlated with post author follower count above 10,000.

**Problem: LinkedIn flags or restricts account for "spammy behavior."**
Solution: LinkedIn's algorithm penalizes accounts that post identical or near-identical comments across multiple posts. Ensure each comment is contextually customized to the specific post (at minimum, change the opening line and the closing question). Never post more than 25-30 comments per day from a single account. Distribute comment volume across multiple team members rather than concentrating on one "comment marketer" account. Avoid posting comments within the first 30 minutes of a post going live (LinkedIn briefly treats rapid mass engagement as bot behavior).
