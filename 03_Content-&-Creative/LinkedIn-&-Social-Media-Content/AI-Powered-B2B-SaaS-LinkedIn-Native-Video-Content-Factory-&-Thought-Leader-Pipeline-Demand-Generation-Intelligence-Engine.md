## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Videos getting <500 views after 24 hours**
Solution: The algorithm prioritizes dwell time and early engagement velocity. Diagnose using this checklist: (1) Is your hook creating enough pattern interrupt? Rewrite the first 3 seconds — it should feel surprising or counterintuitive. (2) Did you post at a suboptimal time? For B2B SaaS audiences, Tuesday/Wednesday 7-9am and 12-1pm in your ICP's timezone consistently outperform. (3) Are you engaging back in the first 30 minutes? The algorithm scores creator-to-commenter response time — respond to every comment within 60 minutes of posting. (4) Try a direct-to-camera selfie-style video vs. a more polished setup — authenticity often outperforms production quality on LinkedIn.

**Problem: High views but zero pipeline signals (no DMs, no demo requests)**
Solution: Views without pipeline usually means your content is interesting but not buyer-activating. Apply the "so what" test to every video: after watching, can a viewer clearly identify themselves as having the problem you solve? Add one of these pipeline-trigger mechanisms: (1) End your video with a specific diagnostic question ("If you answered yes to X, your team may have Y problem — DM me 'analysis' and I'll send you our 3-question diagnostic"). (2) Create a companion LinkedIn post linking to a free tool or template — gated with email capture. (3) Start a comments-only offer: "First 10 people who comment [keyword] get a 20-minute live teardown of their [relevant area]."

**Problem: Running out of ideas after 4-6 weeks**
Solution: Use the "Idea Engine Flywheel" — video content never runs out when you build systematic idea capture: (1) Every customer call: note 1 thing the customer said that would resonate with other customers → video idea. (2) Every sales call: note 1 objection or question raised → "myth vs. reality" video idea. (3) Every industry news story: your hot take → reactive video within 24 hours. (4) Every competitor move: what this means for buyers → analysis video. (5) Every team internal debate: what your team disagrees about → "inside the room" video. Build a shared Notion/Airtable with these trigger categories — your team collectively generates 20+ ideas per week without effort.
