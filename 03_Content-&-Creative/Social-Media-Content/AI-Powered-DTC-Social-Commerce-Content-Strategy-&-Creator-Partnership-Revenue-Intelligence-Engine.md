## Integration Tips

- **TikTok Shop Seller Center + Affiliate Program:** Set up your creator affiliate program directly in TikTok Shop Seller Center — creators apply to promote your products, you approve them, and they earn commission on every TikTok Shop purchase driven by their content. At 5-10% commission on a $50-100 AOV, this is the most scalable creator acquisition model for DTC brands.
- **Triple Whale / Northbeam:** For DTC brands spending $30K+/month on paid social, add a third-party attribution layer to see true creator-influenced revenue — these tools stitch together first-click, last-click, and view-through attribution that native platform tools miss. Without this, you'll systematically undervalue creator content.
- **Grin / Modash / Insense:** Creator relationship management platforms let you manage seeding, contracts, content approvals, payment, and performance tracking in one system. At 20+ active creators, managing this in spreadsheets creates errors and missed follow-ups that damage creator relationships.
- **Klaviyo / Attentive (post-purchase flows):** Build a post-purchase automation that asks buyers where they heard about you — specifically offering "TikTok / Instagram / Creator video" as options. Self-reported attribution consistently shows social commerce influence on purchases that pixel-based attribution misses, especially on iOS devices.
- **Shopify + TikTok and Meta Shopping channels:** Install both native shopping channel apps from the Shopify App Store for seamless product catalog sync — your inventory, pricing, and product descriptions update automatically across TikTok Shop and Instagram Shopping without manual maintenance.
- **Canva Brand Kit + CapCut:** Brief creators on your brand kit in Canva (colors, fonts, logo placement guidelines) and provide CapCut templates with your audio branding pre-loaded — this reduces editing time for nano-creators who produce high-quality content but don't have professional tools.
- **Slack + Notion Creator Hub:** Build a private Slack channel or community for your top 20-30 creators — share trending sounds early, give first access to new products, and create a sense of insider community. Creators who feel like partners, not vendors, create 3-4x more content and convert at higher rates.

## Troubleshooting

**Problem:** Creators are accepting free product and never posting, or posting once and going silent.
**Fix:** This is a brief and expectation-setting failure, not a creator quality issue. At the time of seeding outreach, be explicit that content is encouraged but not required — and then follow up at Day 7 post-delivery with a specific, low-friction content prompt ("Quick question — have you had a chance to try it yet? If you film anything, even a 15-second reaction, we'd love to see it and will reshare it"). The follow-up DM with a specific prompt converts silent recipients into creators at 3-4x the rate of waiting passively. Also: seed people who already create content in your category — check their grid before reaching out.

**Problem:** Creator content is posting but showing zero TikTok Shop attributed revenue despite strong view counts.
**Fix:** Three likely causes: (1) Your product listing in TikTok Shop has friction — product photos are suboptimal, pricing appears inconsistent with your website, or shipping time shows 7+ days which kills impulse conversion. Audit each listing using TikTok Shop's listing score tool. (2) Creators are not properly tagging your products in their posts — run a content audit to verify product tags are appearing. Send creators a 30-second screen recording showing exactly how to tag products in TikTok Shop creator tools. (3) Your product price point requires more than one touchpoint to convert — use TikTok Shop's "save for later" and wishlist data to identify viewers who are in consideration but not ready to buy, then retarget them with a time-limited offer.

**Problem:** TikTok organic content performs well (high views, saves) but website traffic and conversions remain flat.
**Fix:** You have a conversion bridge problem, not a reach problem. Viewers are not motivated to leave TikTok. Fix this by: (1) Moving your primary conversion CTA from "link in bio" to TikTok Shop native checkout — every product-forward video should have a shop product tagged so viewers can checkout without leaving the app. (2) For brand awareness content that drives profile visits, add a pinned video that specifically walks viewers through how to purchase (short "how to get it" tutorial). (3) Run a 72-hour TikTok Shop flash sale announced in a video — this creates urgency that overcomes the "I'll buy it later" friction that kills link-in-bio conversion.
