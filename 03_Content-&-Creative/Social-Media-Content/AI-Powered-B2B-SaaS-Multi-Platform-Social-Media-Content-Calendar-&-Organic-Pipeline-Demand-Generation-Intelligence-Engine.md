- Zero dark social self-reports: add friction to find where ICP is consuming content privately

**Red (Pivot Required):**
- < 1% engagement rate after 6 weeks: full content audit, ICP validation, voice reset
- Social-sourced pipeline = 0 after 90 days: UTM structure is broken or ICP targeting is wrong
- Employee advocacy adoption < 30%: program requires management mandate or redesign

## Integration Tips

**HubSpot:**
- Create a Social Media Interactions custom object to track which contacts engage with social posts before requesting a demo
- Build a workflow that flags contacts with 3+ LinkedIn ad interactions + social website visits as "social-warm" for SDR prioritization
- Set up UTM tracking in HubSpot to capture `utm_medium=organic-social` as a Last Touch and Any Touch attribution dimension

**Salesforce:**
- Add "Social Influenced" as a Campaign Influence type with a custom attribution model (recommended: 10% credit for social touches in multi-touch)
- Use Salesforce Social Studio or integrate with Sprout Social to log social interactions on Contact records automatically
- Create a Social Pipeline dashboard showing Opportunities where social was a touch in the last 90 days

**Buffer / Hootsuite / Sprout Social:**
- Use AI-assisted content scheduling: upload 30 days of content at once, let the tool identify optimal posting times per platform based on your specific audience's online behavior
- Enable "best time to publish" AI recommendations and A/B test posting times in Month 2

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Posts are getting impressions but no engagement, and almost no website clicks.**
Diagnosis: Content is too brand-forward or educational without being specific enough to your ICP's daily reality. ICPs are scrolling past because the content could have been written by anyone in your category.
Fix: Audit your last 20 posts — highlight every sentence that could only be said by your specific company with your specific customer base. If less than 30% of sentences qualify, rewrite using "I've seen X happen to Y customers" framing with actual data points. Add a more specific first-line hook tied to a pain point your ICP feels at 8 AM on a Tuesday.

**Problem: Employee advocacy program launched but only 2 out of 15 advocates are posting consistently.**
Diagnosis: Activation friction is too high (writing a post from scratch), or employees don't feel confident in their personal brand, or there's no management accountability signal.
Fix: Reduce the minimum viable action: send advocates pre-written posts with a one-line customization instruction. Create a "Steal This Post" Notion page updated weekly with 3 ready-to-publish options. Have the CMO or VP Marketing activate first publicly — social proof works inside companies too. Add advocacy participation to quarterly team OKR check-ins (not as a performance metric, but as a team health signal).

**Problem: Self-reported attribution shows "LinkedIn" but UTM data in HubSpot shows zero LinkedIn-sourced demos.**
Diagnosis: UTM links are not being applied consistently — either posts don't include tracked links, the bio link isn't UTM-tagged, or the CRM is stripping UTM parameters on form submission.
Fix: Audit the full tracking chain: (1) check that the company LinkedIn bio link is UTM-tagged and routing correctly, (2) verify that HubSpot forms are passing UTM parameters through to the Contact record (requires hidden UTM fields in form settings), (3) for posts without clickable links (LinkedIn limits link reach), route users to a vanity URL (e.g., yourdomain.com/linkedin) that immediately redirects with UTM parameters appended.
