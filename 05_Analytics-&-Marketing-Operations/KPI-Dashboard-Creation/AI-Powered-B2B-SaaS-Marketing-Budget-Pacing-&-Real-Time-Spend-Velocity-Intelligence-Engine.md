## Troubleshooting

**Issue: Pipeline numbers don't match across systems (CRM vs. marketing attribution tool shows different pipeline figures)**
Fix: Clarify your attribution model before running this prompt. Use a single source of truth (typically CRM, not marketing platform). Note in the prompt: "Pipeline figures are first-touch attributed, pulled from Salesforce as of [date]." This gives the AI the right framing to avoid double-counting interpretation errors.

**Issue: The reallocation recommendation suggests moving budget between channels but our contracts are locked (annual commitments to agencies or platforms)**
Fix: Add a "Budget Constraints" subsection to your prompt listing locked commitments and their amounts: "Agency retainer: $18K/month locked through Dec. LinkedIn contract: $25K/month committed minimum." The model will then only recommend reallocation of truly flexible spend.

**Issue: Quarter-end pacing looks great but we're generating pipeline that won't close until next quarter, creating a timing mismatch**
Fix: Add "Average days from MQL to Closed-Won: [X] days" and "Current quarter close date: [date]" to your context. Ask the AI to segment pipeline into: (a) likely to close this quarter, (b) will close next quarter, (c) early-stage. This creates a more honest pipeline coverage picture for the CFO.
