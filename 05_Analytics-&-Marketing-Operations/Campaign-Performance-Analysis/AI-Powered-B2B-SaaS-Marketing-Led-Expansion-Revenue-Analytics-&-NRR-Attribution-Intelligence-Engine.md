## Integration Tips

**Salesforce:**
- Build a "Marketing Expansion Influence" campaign field on Opportunity records—log all marketing touchpoints within 180 days of expansion close
- Create a "Marketing NRR Dashboard" report: filter Opportunities by Type = "Expansion" + Campaign Influence > 0; group by Campaign Name to see program ROI
- Automate expansion trigger alerts: when a customer contact visits the Pricing page (tracked via Pardot/HubSpot web tracking), create a Salesforce Task for the CSM

**Gainsight / ChurnZero / Totango:**
- Configure health score components to include marketing engagement signals: certification completion (+15 pts), webinar attendance in last 60 days (+10 pts), email CTR trend (+5 pts)
- Set up CTAs (Calls-to-Action) triggered by marketing signals: "Champion completed certification → Schedule QBR" or "Account hasn't engaged with any marketing in 90 days → CSM outreach"

**HubSpot (Customer Marketing):**
- Use List Segmentation to create "Expansion-Ready" smart lists: certified AND newsletter clicker AND attended webinar in last 90 days → enroll in expansion nurture sequence
- Set up Deal-based attribution reporting: associate contact email engagement to Expansion deals using HubSpot's attribution reporting with a 180-day lookback window

**Marketo / Eloqua:**
- Build a "Customer Engagement Score" in addition to standard lead score—track engagement depth (certifications, events, content downloads) as a separate dimension
- Use Revenue Cycle Analytics to model the path from marketing program enrollment → expansion deal at the account level

**Pendo / Amplitude (Product Analytics):**
- Create cohorts by marketing touchpoint: "Users who received in-app feature campaign" vs. control group—measure feature adoption rate and downstream expansion correlation
- Build a "Power User" cohort based on session frequency + feature breadth; cross-reference with marketing engagement to find expansion-ready accounts

**Google Sheets / Looker Studio:**
- Build the Program Scorecard as a live dashboard: pull expansion deal data from Salesforce, marketing program data from MAP, engagement data from CSP into BigQuery → visualize in Looker Studio with program ROI updated weekly

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Can't separate marketing influence from CS-led expansion—all credit goes to CSM**
Solution: Implement a "first marketing touch" attribution field on Expansion Opportunities in CRM. Log the first post-sale marketing program that engaged the account (email click, webinar registration, certification enrollment) as the marketing influence source. Even if CS closes the deal, marketing gets credit for the signal that triggered the conversation. Present this as "Marketing-Influenced NRR" rather than "Marketing-Sourced NRR" to avoid territorial conflict.

**Problem: Sample size too small—only 40 expansion deals makes correlation data unreliable**
Solution: Expand the analysis to 24 months of data and supplement with qualitative evidence. Conduct 5-10 expansion customer interviews asking: "What made you decide to upgrade?" Look for mentions of specific programs. Use testimonials as supporting narrative alongside thin quantitative data. Build in a note that the attribution model will become statistically reliable at 80+ expansion events—set a timeline for when the data will be actionable.

**Problem: CS leadership pushes back that marketing had no role in their expansion deals**
Solution: Run a controlled comparison: pull all expansion deals where the account had zero marketing engagement in the 180 days prior vs. deals where they had at least 3 marketing touches. If engaged accounts expand at a higher rate, the data makes the argument. If not, marketing's post-sale investment needs strategic reassessment. Present this analysis to CS leadership as a collaborative finding, not a political argument—frame it as "here's how we can make each other more effective."
