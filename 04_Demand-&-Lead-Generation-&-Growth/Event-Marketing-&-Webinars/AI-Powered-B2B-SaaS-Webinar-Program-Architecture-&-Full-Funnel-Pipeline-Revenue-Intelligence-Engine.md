## Overview

- Marketo smart campaign: trigger on "Attends Webinar" event → fire scoring rules → update Salesforce lead score field → assign to correct queue based on tier

- Sequences tool: build Tier 1 and Tier 2 templates; auto-enroll via Workflow based on tier property

**Zoom Webinars + Zapier:**
- Zapier: Zoom Webinar → attendee data → update contact in CRM → trigger scoring workflow
- Key data fields to capture from Zoom API: join time, leave time, poll responses, Q&A submissions, chat activity

**Restream / StreamYard + Content Atomization:**
- Record to cloud → auto-send to Descript for transcript + AI highlight clips
- Descript AI: identify top 5 moments by audio energy and silence gaps → export as clips
- Zapier: Descript complete → notify content team in Slack → create Asana tasks for distribution

- Create a "Webinar" campaign type and tag every opportunity with the sourcing webinar name

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: High registration, low show rate (below 25%)**
Fix: The most common cause is misaligned topic-to-audience fit — you're reaching people who are curious but not ready to invest 60 minutes. Test: (1) Shorten format to 30-minute "office hours" style; (2) Add a specific, tangible deliverable in the registration confirmation ("You'll walk away with a completed procurement maturity scorecard"); (3) Segment your reminder emails — personalize the "what's in it for you specifically" line based on registrant job title. Also audit your registration source mix — paid traffic converts at lower show rates than organic and referral.

**Problem: Tier 1 attendees aren't converting to opportunities**
Fix: Usually a sales follow-up timing or personalization failure. Audit whether Tier 1 emails are actually going out within 2 hours (check automation logs), and review the email copy for generic language. The follow-up MUST reference a specific moment from the event — a poll result the attendee participated in, a question they submitted, or a statistic from the session relevant to their industry. If timing and personalization are fine, the issue may be ICP mismatch — review job titles of Tier 1 attendees against your actual ICP definition.

**Problem: CFO/CRO disputes webinar pipeline attribution**
Fix: The argument usually centers on whether the webinar "created" the opportunity or just "touched" it. Resolve by presenting both sourced and influenced numbers separately — never blend them. For sourced: only count opportunities where the first CRM-logged interaction was the webinar (or webinar registration). For influenced: count any open or closed opportunity where an attendee is a contact, within a 90-day attribution window. Provide a "closed-won sample" — show 3-5 actual deals that trace back to webinar attendance, with the full deal timeline, to make the number concrete and auditable.
