## Overview
Design and operate a systematic in-person executive event program — intimate dinners, hosted roundtables, and field events — that uses AI to select the right accounts, send hyper-personalized invitations, orchestrate the night-of experience, and convert attendees into qualified pipeline within 48 hours. Use this when your webinar attendance-to-pipeline rate is plateauing, when enterprise deals require human relationship acceleration, or when you need a repeatable field event motion that generates $500K+ in pipeline per event.

   - Lead with relationship context: reference last interaction (webinar attendance, content download, prior meeting)
   - Send from AE or CSM who owns the account relationship
   - Calendar integration: embed Calendly-style one-click RSVP directly in email body

   Tier 3 — Existing customers / champions:
   - Frame as VIP appreciation, not pipeline event
   - Note: do not send pipeline-focused follow-up to Tier 3 attendees post-event

3. EVENT EXPERIENCE DESIGN (AI-GENERATED CONTENT ASSETS)
   The event must feel like an exclusive peer forum, not a sales dinner. Produce:

   Venue selection criteria:
   - Private dining room or semi-private section (max 20 guests, optimal 12-15)
   - No screen projectors or branded signage — removes "sales event" perception
   - Food quality score: Michelin-starred or equivalent (associate quality with your brand)
   - AV: none required except background music

   Moderator guide (AI-produced, customizable per city/vertical):
   - Opening framing script (2-minute host introduction): thank attendees for time, set the "Chatham House rules" norm (what's said in the room stays in the room), establish peer-to-peer forum tone
   - 4 discussion questions designed to surface paint points naturally — without mentioning your product:
     * "What's the one operational challenge your team is still solving manually that you assumed AI would have fixed by now?"
     * "How has [relevant macro trend] changed your planning horizon for [relevant function]?"
     * "What's the biggest internal obstacle to getting a new initiative approved right now?"
     * "What would you want the vendor community to understand about how you actually evaluate new solutions?"
   - Note-taking brief: assign a marketing team member to capture exact language used — this feeds directly into messaging and sales talk tracks

   Attendee pre-event briefing (AI-generated per invitee):
   - 1-page intelligence brief on each attendee: company news in past 30 days, LinkedIn activity, role context, likely pain points based on industry + company size
   - Distribute to all hosts 48 hours before event
   - Include 2-3 conversation starters relevant to each guest

4. NIGHT-OF EXECUTION CHECKLIST (AI-AGENT MANAGED)
   - Guest confirmation re-sent 48 hours before and morning-of (personalized, not generic reminder)
   - Seat chart generated based on strategic pairing: put your most credible reference customer next to your highest-priority target account
   - RSVP drop-off recovery: if a key Tier 1 guest cancels within 72 hours, auto-trigger backup invite sequence from waitlist
   - Name card data: each place card should include guest's company, title, and one personal detail host can use to open conversation
   - Real-time attendance tracking: host marks confirmed arrivals in mobile CRM app; AI agent updates pipeline influence record immediately

5. 48-HOUR POST-EVENT PIPELINE CONVERSION ENGINE
   Triggered automatically when event ends (host marks event complete in CRM):

   Within 2 hours of event end (same evening):
   - Personal thank-you SMS or WhatsApp from the host executive — one sentence, no ask
   - For Tier 1 accounts only: AE receives "dinner brief" — 3 bullet summary of what that specific guest said, their apparent pain, and a recommended opening for tomorrow's follow-up

   Within 24 hours (next morning):
   - Personal email from AE (not automated-looking) referencing one specific thing the guest said at dinner
   - Include one piece of content directly relevant to the topic they raised — not a product one-pager, a credible third-party or customer reference
   - CTA: "Would it make sense to continue this conversation one-on-one? I have 20 minutes on [Tuesday] or [Thursday]."
   - LinkedIn connection request from host executive (personalized note referencing the dinner)

   Within 48 hours:
   - If meeting accepted: AE moves account to "Meeting Scheduled" stage; marketing suppresses from drip nurture
   - If no response: one follow-up email at 48 hours, then silence for 5 business days before re-engaging via LinkedIn
   - Tier 3 (customer guests): CSM sends a "great to see you" note, flags any expansion signals heard during conversation to customer success team

6. PIPELINE ATTRIBUTION & ROI MODEL
   Define how the AI agent should attribute and track field event pipeline:

   Attribution rules:
   - New pipeline: if an account was Tier 1/2 target with no open opportunity, and a new opportunity is created within 90 days of event, tag as "Event-Sourced" with 100% source credit
   - Pipeline acceleration: if an account had an open opportunity stalled for 45+ days, and the deal progresses or closes within 60 days post-event, tag as "Event-Influenced" with 50% assist credit in revenue reporting
   - Revenue reporting: track "pipeline created per event," "cost per qualified opportunity," "average deal size (event-sourced vs. non-event)," "close rate (event-sourced)"

   Target benchmarks to include in your program:
   - Cost per event (all-in): $8,000–$25,000 depending on city and guest count
   - Pipeline sourced per event target: $750,000–$2,500,000
   - Attendance-to-meeting rate target: 40–60% of Tier 1/2 attendees book a follow-up meeting
   - Meeting-to-opportunity rate target: 50–70%
   - Pipeline ROI target: 20–50x event cost

   Dashboard metrics (auto-updated in CRM by AI agent):
   - Invitations sent → response rate → attendee count → Tier 1 show rate
   - Post-event meetings booked (7-day, 30-day, 90-day windows)
   - Pipeline created (event-sourced + event-influenced)
   - Closed-won revenue traced to event (6-month and 12-month view)

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Low invitation acceptance rate (<15%) for cold executives**
Fix: Your invitation is too generic or too long. Cold executive invitations must be under 80 words in the email body, must name-drop 2 confirmed attendees of equal or greater seniority, must reference one company-specific signal (recent news or post), and must frame the event as a peer conversation — not a vendor dinner. Remove all product references. If acceptance is still low, the event host may lack credibility with this persona — consider co-hosting with a customer executive or an industry analyst.

**Problem: High no-show rate (>20%) among confirmed RSVPs**
Fix: Add a 48-hour and day-of confirmation touchpoint (text message, not email), and ensure the event feels exclusive and scarce enough that canceling feels socially costly. Executive no-shows almost always signal the invitation process was too easy (no personal connection to the host). Require a phone or video call with the AE as part of the RSVP process for your top 5 Tier 1 accounts — this creates commitment and surfaces any objections before event day.

**Problem: Lots of great conversations at dinner, but AEs not converting to meetings**
Fix: The bottleneck is the 24-48 hour window. Check whether AEs are sending generic thank-yous vs. referencing specific dinner conversation. Implement the mandatory "night-of debrief form" that captures exactly what each guest said and auto-populates the follow-up email draft. If AEs still underperform, add a 24-hour "meeting booked" checkpoint as a field marketing KPI — no meeting booked within 24 hours triggers a field marketing manager follow-up directly to the guest.
