## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Our study data shows boring findings — nothing counterintuitive or press-worthy.**
Solution: Re-examine your data segmentation. Nearly every dataset contains a surprising sub-group finding. Try cutting your data by company size (smallest vs. largest companies), by industry vertical (contrast two industries with expected similarity), by geography (U.S. vs. European differences), or by seniority (what frontline workers believe vs. what executives believe). If raw data is genuinely flat, supplement with 5-10 in-depth qualitative interviews to surface unexpected anecdotes that give "color" to findings, then build survey questions around confirming or disconfirming those anecdotes.

**Problem: Journalists are ignoring our press release and embargo pitches.**
Solution: The problem is almost always the lead stat. Your headline finding must make a journalist think "I didn't know that, and my readers will be surprised." Test 5-7 different headline framings with the same data before pitching — try the dollar-quantification angle ("Companies waste $X on..."), the counterintuitive angle ("Despite X assumption, Y is actually true"), the trend reversal angle ("For the first time since X, Y is happening"), and the urgency angle ("New data: X will cost companies $Y by [near-future date]"). Also confirm you are pitching the right reporters — your study needs to land with a journalist who writes exclusively about your beat, not a generalist.

**Problem: We're getting press coverage but can't attribute any pipeline to it.**
Solution: Implement the "Research Attribution Stack" immediately: (1) Add UTM parameters to all coverage URLs you control (press releases, sponsored content); (2) For earned coverage you can't UTM-tag, monitor branded search volume lift 7-30 days after major placements — even a 15% lift is attributable to specific coverage; (3) Require your SDR team to log in CRM when a prospect references the study in any interaction (call, email reply, LinkedIn); (4) Add "How did you hear about us?" to your demo request form with "Saw your research report" as an option. Within 90 days you will have enough data to show research-to-pipeline correlation.
