*Op-Ed Writing System:*

Apply the "PAVE" structure for every op-ed:
- **P**rovocation (200-300 words): Open with the counterintuitive claim. Do not introduce yourself or your company. Start in the middle of the argument.
- **A**rgumentation (600-900 words): Three pillars of evidence, each ending with an implication. Use data in specific, non-generic forms ("73% of the 847 operations leaders we surveyed" not "most leaders say"). ## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Editors keep rejecting pitches as "too promotional" even though we're not mentioning the product.**
Fix: The POV itself is the problem — it's too closely aligned with your product's value proposition. Editors can detect when an argument has been reverse-engineered from a product feature. The contrarian thesis must be a genuine belief the executive holds, independent of what your product does. Test: Can the executive argue this POV if the product didn't exist? If not, rebuild the POV from first principles.

**Problem: Getting placements in trade publications but can't break into tier-1 (HBR, WSJ, Forbes).**
Fix: Tier-1 placements require proprietary, exclusive data. Without a research study, benchmark report, or platform data that no one else can access, editors have no reason to take you over the 50+ other pitches they receive daily. Invest in a 500-respondent survey ($5-10K) focused on a specific question your buyers care about. A single statistic that contradicts a widely-held assumption is worth 20 recycled opinion pieces.

**Problem: Executive agrees to do the program but keeps missing writing deadlines, creating a pitch-to-delivery gap.**
Fix: Implement the "Executive Voice Capture" system — record a 20-minute conversation with the executive where they explain their thesis out loud to a smart colleague. Transcribe with Otter.ai or Grain. Send transcript to a ghostwriter who drafts the op-ed in the executive's voice. Schedule a 30-minute "polish session" (not a writing session) where the executive reviews and adjusts. This reduces executive time from 4 hours to 45 minutes per article.
