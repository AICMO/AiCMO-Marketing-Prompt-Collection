## Troubleshooting

**Problem: No Tier-1 journalists respond to embargo briefing requests**
Solution: The offer isn't differentiated enough or the journalist list is wrong. Audit: (1) Confirm each journalist has written about your specific market in the last 60 days — if not, they're the wrong journalist. (2) Rewrite your pitch to lead with the customer outcome data point, not the product description. (3) Offer a 1:1 call with the customer spokesperson (Tracy Vasquez, not the CEO) — practitioners-talking-to-practitioners gets response when vendor outreach doesn't. (4) Try LinkedIn DM instead of email — response rates for cold journalist outreach on LinkedIn are often 2–3x higher than email for B2B tech.

**Problem: Press coverage is published but drives minimal website traffic**
Solution: Coverage is running in low-traffic or wrong-audience publications. Immediate fix: (1) Check the Domain Authority and monthly traffic of publications where coverage ran — anything under DA 40 or 50K monthly visitors is unlikely to drive meaningful referral traffic. (2) Use the earned coverage to unlock amplification: take the WSJ or Forbes article URL and use it in LinkedIn Sponsored Content targeting your ICP — paid amplification of earned coverage typically generates 3–5x more impressions than organic sharing alone. (3) Pitch byline articles to higher-traffic publications using the existing coverage as credibility proof.

**Problem: Launch day coverage contradicts key messages or introduces factual errors**
Solution: Move fast — errors compound. (1) Within 1 hour of spotting an error, contact the journalist directly via the email used for the briefing. Be specific: "Paragraph 3 says our accuracy is ±5% at 90 days — the correct stat is ±2%. Here's our product documentation." Journalists correct factual errors quickly when they receive clear, sourced corrections. (2) Do NOT post corrections publicly or call out the journalist on social media — this kills the relationship and triggers defensiveness. (3) If the error is material and the journalist is unresponsive within 4 hours, contact the editor listed on the publication's masthead.
