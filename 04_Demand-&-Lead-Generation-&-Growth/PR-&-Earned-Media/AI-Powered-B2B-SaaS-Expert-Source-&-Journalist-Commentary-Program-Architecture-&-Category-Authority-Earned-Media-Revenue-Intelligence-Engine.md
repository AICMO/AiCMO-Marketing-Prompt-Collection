## Troubleshooting

**Problem:** HARO and Qwoted queries aren't relevant to our category, so we can't respond.
**Solution:** Expand your relevance definition. Most categories have 3-5 adjacent topics that journalists frequently connect to your category. A revenue intelligence company can legitimately comment on sales productivity research, economic headwinds affecting B2B growth, AI in the workplace, and CRO/CFO priorities — not just "sales intelligence." Build a relevance map of 20 adjacent topics and monitor for those too.

**Problem:** We're submitting responses but never getting cited.
**Solution:** Common failure modes: (1) Response too long — journalists want 2-4 sentences, not paragraphs. (2) No contrarian angle — if your quote supports conventional wisdom, it's skippable. (3) No credentials in the submission — always include your title, company ARR/scale signal, and one specific data point. (4) Responding to queries after the deadline — set up real-time alerts, not daily digests.

**Problem:** Our executives won't commit time to media, so the program stalls.
**Solution:** Use AI to do 80% of the work. Pre-build a library of 20 approved "standing quotes" the executive has pre-authorized for use without per-instance approval. For HARO responses, use AI to draft, then have a communications manager submit — the executive only reviews if it's Tier 1 and requires a personalized response. Set a 10-minute/week calendar block for executive review of the week's opportunities.
