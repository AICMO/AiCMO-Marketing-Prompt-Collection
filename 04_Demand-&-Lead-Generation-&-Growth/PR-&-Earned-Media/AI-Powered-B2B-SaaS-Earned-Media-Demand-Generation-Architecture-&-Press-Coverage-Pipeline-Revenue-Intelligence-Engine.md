## Troubleshooting

**Problem: We land coverage but see no pipeline impact — how do we know if PR is working?**
Solution: Coverage without activation is a tree falling in the forest. Implement the 24-hour amplification SLA immediately: for every piece of coverage, within 24 hours you must (1) post organic social with the CEO, (2) send a Slack alert to SDR team with outreach templates, and (3) activate paid amplification to ICP audiences. Run a 90-day correlation analysis: compare weeks with tier-1 coverage to weeks without — look for branded search volume lift (+20-40% is normal), direct traffic surges, and SDR reply rate improvement on coverage-referencing sequences. If correlation is absent after 90 days, the publication doesn't reach your ICP — change your target outlet list.

**Problem: Journalists never respond to our pitches — response rate is under 5%**
Solution: You are pitching the wrong story to the wrong journalist at the wrong time. Run a pitch audit: pull your last 10 pitches and score each against three criteria — (1) Does this story serve the journalist's beat and readers, not just your company? (2) Is there a hook that would make a reader click? (3) Is there data or a contrarian angle that makes it genuinely newsworthy? Most company pitches score 2/10 on all three. Rebuild your pitch template to lead with the insight and data, not the company announcement. Spend 10 minutes researching each journalist's recent articles before pitching — reference a specific piece. Response rates should reach 15-25% with personalized, reader-first pitches.

**Problem: Our CEO won't do media — how do we build a PR program without an executive spokesperson?**
Solution: Build a multi-voice media strategy. Identify 3-4 internal experts (VP Sales, Head of Data Science, Senior CSM with strong customer stories) and position them as subject matter authorities on specific topics. Brief each on their domain and prepare them with a personal POV and 3 data-backed talking points. Reserve CEO quotes for tier-1 wire releases and major announcements only. In parallel, launch a contributed byline program — most major publications accept articles written by non-CEO executives. Bylines from your VP of Product or Head of Revenue Science can generate equal or superior pipeline impact to CEO quotes in trade media.
