## Troubleshooting

**Problem: The founder's content sounds like company marketing, not a person.**
Fix: Run every post through this test — "Could this content exist if the company didn't exist?" If no, rewrite. The founder should be writing about the category, the industry, the profession — not the product. The product earns the right to be mentioned only when it's genuinely the best example of a principle they're teaching.

**Problem: Engagement is high but no pipeline is being generated.**
Fix: The content is attracting the wrong audience (peers and vendors, not buyers). Review the follower base composition — are ICP titles represented? Adjust content topics toward buyer pain, not practitioner curiosity. Add explicit "who this is for" framing in profile and newsletter about page.

**Problem: The founder doesn't have time to keep the cadence.**
Fix: Implement the "voice note → AI draft → 30-minute review" workflow strictly. The founder should never write from scratch — only react, refine, and approve AI drafts. If still time-constrained, reduce cadence to 2 LinkedIn posts/week + 1 newsletter bi-weekly before skipping weeks entirely.
