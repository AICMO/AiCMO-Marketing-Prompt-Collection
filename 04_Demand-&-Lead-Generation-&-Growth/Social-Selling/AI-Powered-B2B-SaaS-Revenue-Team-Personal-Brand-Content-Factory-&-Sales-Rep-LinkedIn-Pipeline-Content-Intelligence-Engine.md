## Troubleshooting

**Problem:** Reps write one or two posts and then stop because it "isn't working."
**Solution:** Set the expectation upfront that LinkedIn personal brand is a 90-day compound investment. In week 1-2, impressions are driven mostly by your existing network — which may not be ICP-rich. Momentum builds as the algorithm learns your content category and ICP contacts start following. Show reps their profile view trendline (not just post likes) — profile views from ICP accounts are the leading indicator that the content is working, even before comments roll in. First pipeline influence typically appears in week 6-8.

**Problem:** AI-generated posts all sound the same — generic, vendor-y, or interchangeable between reps.
**Solution:** The quality of the prompt input determines the quality of the output. Run the advanced version of this prompt with specific rep details: their background, their strongest opinions, the exact quote from a prospect call that stuck with them, their personal career story. Generic prompts produce generic posts. The more specific the voice context — "Sarah thinks most HR teams have been sold a lie about how long it takes to implement a new ATS" — the more distinct the post. Build a "Voice Capture Interview" for each rep (15 minutes of questions) and feed the answers into the AI as context.

**Problem:** Posts get decent engagement from peers (other sales reps, vendors) but not from actual ICP buyers.
**Solution:** This is the most common LinkedIn failure mode — creating content for the "LinkedIn-y" crowd instead of the actual buyer. Review each pillar: does the content speak to the DAILY REALITY of a CHRO or VP TA, or does it speak to the LinkedIn marketing culture? Test with this filter: "Would a busy CHRO who hasn't posted on LinkedIn in months still stop scrolling for this?" If the answer is no, you're writing for the feed, not the buyer. Shift from marketing best practices content to practitioner observations — "Here's what I heard from 15 CHROs last month about [pain point]" outperforms "Here are 5 LinkedIn tips" every time in ICP reach.
