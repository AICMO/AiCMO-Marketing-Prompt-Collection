## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Subscriber growth stalls after initial launch spike (common at 300-500 subscribers)**
*Fix:* The launch announcement exhausts your immediate network. Switch from broad launch content to "issue excerpt" posts — publish the most shareable section of each issue as a standalone LinkedIn post with a subscribe CTA at the end. These posts compound over time and reach non-followers through shares. Also audit your post timing: LinkedIn newsletter posts perform best Tuesday-Thursday, 8-10am in your target timezone. If you're posting outside these windows, test shifting schedule for 30 days.

**Problem: High subscriber count but low pipeline conversion (lots of readers, no meetings)**
*Fix:* This indicates audience-ICP mismatch. Pull your subscriber list demographics from LinkedIn Creator Analytics — if your subscribers are skewing toward job seekers, students, or non-buyer roles, your content is attracting the wrong audience. Audit your last 5 issues: are you writing for practitioners instead of economic buyers? Adjust the editorial angle toward content that economic buyers share (benchmark data, board-level frameworks, team management content) and away from practitioner tactics. Also check if your "warm subscriber" DM sequence is actually being executed — this is where most pipeline conversion fails.

**Problem: Newsletter author's DM sequences aren't converting to meetings despite high intent scores**
*Fix:* The most common failure is the DM read as a sales pitch despite the non-salesy framing. Audit the Step 1 DM: does it mention any product, pricing, or solution language? Remove it entirely. The first DM must be 100% curiosity-based — ask about their situation without any implication you can solve it. If replies are happening but Step 3 (the soft ask) is getting ghosted, the issue is trust gap — you need more issues between Step 2 and Step 3. Extend the warm subscriber sequence from 8 days to 21 days and add 2 more value-add touchpoints (share a relevant article, congratulate them on a LinkedIn achievement) before the meeting ask.
