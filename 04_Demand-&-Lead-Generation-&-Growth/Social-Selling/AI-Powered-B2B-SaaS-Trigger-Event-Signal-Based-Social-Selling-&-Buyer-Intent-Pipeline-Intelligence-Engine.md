# AI-Powered B2B SaaS Trigger-Event Signal-Based Social Selling & Buyer Intent Pipeline Intelligence Engine

## Overview

This prompt architects a fully automated, signal-based social selling system that intercepts B2B buyers at the exact moment a trigger event (job change, funding round, tech stack switch, competitive churn signal, hiring surge) indicates purchase readiness. Use it when you need to build a real-time intent-driven outreach motion that converts external buying signals into pipeline before competitors even detect them.

Design a complete AI-powered trigger-event social selling system that detects, prioritizes, and acts on buyer intent signals across LinkedIn, news sources, job boards, funding databases, and tech stack intelligence platforms.

1. TRIGGER EVENT LIBRARY (rank by purchase intent, High/Medium/Low):
   - Job change triggers: New VP/Director hiring into a role we sell to — map which job titles signal which pain
   - Funding triggers: Series A/B/C announcements — define funding stage to product-fit mapping

   - Tech stack change triggers: BuiltWith/Clearbit data showing removal of a competing tool
   - Company expansion triggers: New office openings, international expansion announcements, acquisition news
   - Executive mandate triggers: LinkedIn posts by C-suite mentioning a pain point we solve
   For each trigger: define the signal source, detection method, time-to-act window, and outreach persona (SDR/AE/executive/marketing).

   - Build a composite intent score (0-100) combining: trigger event type (40%), seniority of triggered contact (25%), ICP firmographic fit (20%), recency of signal (15%)
   - Define score thresholds: 80+ = immediate AE outreach, 60-79 = SDR sequence, 40-59 = marketing nurture, <40 = watch list

3. TRIGGER-SPECIFIC OUTREACH SEQUENCES:
   For each of the following triggers, write a complete 4-touch sequence (LinkedIn connection request + comment + DM + InMail fallback) with exact copy:
   A. New VP of [TARGET FUNCTION] hired at an ICP account — reference their mandate and first 90-day pressures
   B. Company just raised Series B — acknowledge growth challenge, position ROI narrative
   C. Competitor G2 review complaint — reference the specific pain without naming the competitor directly
   D. Company posted 5+ [RELEVANT DEPARTMENT] job openings in 30 days — reference the team build-out challenge
   Each sequence: personalize opening to the trigger, lead with insight not product, include one credibility anchor (customer name or stat), CTA is a perspective share not a meeting ask on touch 1-2.

4. SOCIAL LISTENING & CONTENT INTERCEPT PLAYS:
   - Design an AI-powered social listening workflow: monitor LinkedIn posts from ICP prospects mentioning pain keywords, competitor names, or industry trends — trigger a value-add comment within 2 hours
   - Write 10 comment templates by pain category that add genuine insight (not self-promotional) and create a reason to connect
   - Define when a comment play should escalate to a DM (prospect replies to comment, likes 3+ of your posts, shares your content)

5. SIGNAL AGGREGATION TECH STACK:
   - Map the AI-native tool stack for each signal source: Clay (data enrichment + waterfall), Apollo/LinkedIn Sales Nav (contact identification), Bombora/G2 Buyer Intent (in-market signals), Crunchbase/PitchBook (funding triggers), BuiltWith (tech stack intelligence), Gong/Chorus (call intelligence for expansion signals)
   - Design the automated workflow: Signal detected → Clay enrichment → Scoring model → Route to correct sequence → Personalized outreach generated by AI → Rep review + send
   - Specify which steps are fully autonomous vs. require 30-second rep approval

6. MEASUREMENT FRAMEWORK:
   - Define KPIs by funnel stage: Signal detection rate, Signal-to-sequence rate, Sequence-to-reply rate, Reply-to-meeting rate, Signal-sourced pipeline ($), Signal-sourced closed-won ($)

## Integration Tips

- **Clay:** Core enrichment hub — build the signal scoring formula as a Clay formula column. Use Clay's AI column to auto-generate trigger-personalized first-touch copy for rep review. Connect via Zapier to your LinkedIn outreach tool and CRM.
- **LinkedIn Sales Navigator:** Create saved searches with "Changed jobs in past 30 days" filter for champion job change signals. Set up "Account news" alerts for funding triggers. Export contact lists to Clay weekly for enrichment and scoring.
- **HubSpot/Salesforce:** Create custom properties: "Trigger Event Type," "Trigger Date," "Signal Priority Score," "Signal Source Tool." Use these for pipeline attribution reporting and closed-won analysis by signal type.
- **Expandi or Dripify:** Load trigger-specific sequences as LinkedIn automation campaigns. Use conditional logic: if accepted in <24 hours → send DM variant A (warm), if accepted in >3 days → send DM variant B (re-engage context).
- **Slack:** Build a `#signal-alerts` channel with Zapier automation pushing Priority 1 signal cards (contact name, company, trigger type, score, pre-written DM) for one-click AE review and deployment.
- **Gong/Chorus:** Tag discovery calls with "Signal Source" for win-rate analysis by trigger type. Use call intelligence to refine which trigger narratives resonate most in opening conversations.

## Troubleshooting

**Problem:** LinkedIn restricting account due to too many connection requests
**Solution:** Stay under 20 connection requests per day per account. Use message-matched requests (always include a note referencing the trigger event — it improves accept rates AND reduces spam flags). Consider running signals across 2-3 team LinkedIn accounts if volume is high. Warm up new Sales Navigator accounts over 30 days before running full signal volume.

**Problem:** Signal scores are accurate but reps aren't working the queue — Priority 1 signals going stale past the 4-hour SLA
**Solution:** Reduce rep cognitive load. Instead of asking reps to log in to a scoring dashboard, push the signal as a Slack card with the pre-written message already drafted and a single "Approve & Send" button via Zapier. Remove the decision — the AI has already personalized it; the rep just reads and approves in 30 seconds. Gamify: weekly leaderboard of signal-sourced meetings in the #revenue-team Slack channel.

**Problem:** Trigger events detected but contact data is incomplete — can't find LinkedIn profile or email for the triggered prospect
**Solution:** Build a Clay waterfall enrichment sequence: LinkedIn URL → Apollo → Clearbit → Hunter → PDL → RocketReach. For funding triggers specifically, Crunchbase often has incomplete contact-level data — supplement with a Boolean LinkedIn search for "[company name] + [target job title] + [city]" to find the correct decision-maker. For champion job change triggers, your CRM should already have the champion's LinkedIn URL from the previous deal — use that as the Clay input anchor.
