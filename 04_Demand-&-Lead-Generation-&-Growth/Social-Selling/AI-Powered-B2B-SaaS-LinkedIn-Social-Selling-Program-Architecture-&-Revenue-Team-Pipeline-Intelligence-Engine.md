# AI-Powered B2B SaaS LinkedIn Social Selling Program Architecture & Revenue Team Pipeline Intelligence Engine

## Overview

This prompt engineers a fully automated, AI-driven LinkedIn social selling system that transforms your entire revenue team — AEs, SDRs, CSMs, and executives — into a coordinated pipeline-generating machine using intent signals, behavioral triggers, and persona-matched content. Use it when you need to build a scalable, measurement-driven social selling motion that produces qualified pipeline without cold calling or spray-and-pray outreach.

Design a complete AI-powered LinkedIn social selling program for our revenue team that includes:

## DELIVERABLE 3: COLD OUTREACH SEQUENCE ARCHITECTURE (by persona)

**Sequence Design Principles:**
- Never pitch in the first 3 touches — earn attention, then earn trust, then earn the ask
- Every touch must deliver standalone value even if they never respond
- Pattern-interrupt subject lines perform 3x better than "Following up" — never use that phrase
- The optimal cadence is 5-7 touches over 21-28 days; beyond that, returns diminish sharply

**Full 5-Touch Sequence for Persona A (Economic Buyer):**

*Touch 1 — Day 1: Connection Request (no note, or ultra-short note)*
Option A (no note): Connect with no message — a clean connection request from a credible profile converts at 35-45% for warm ICP vs. 12-18% with a templated note
Option B (short note, 200 chars max): "Fellow [industry] person — connecting because we work with [2-3 similar companies] and your post on [topic] resonated. No pitch, just a valuable connection to have."

*Touch 2 — Day 3: Value Comment (on their recent post)*
Find their most recent post. Leave a comment that adds genuine value — cite a data point, share a contrarian angle, or ask a thoughtful question. Do NOT mention your product. Goal: get them to notice you as a smart peer, not a vendor.
Example framework: "[Agree/disagree with their point] — we see this with [X] out of [Y] companies we talk to. The variable that usually determines which side of that equation they end up on is [insight]. Curious whether you've seen the same."

*Touch 3 — Day 7: First DM (insight-led, no ask)*
"[First name] — thanks for connecting. Saw your post on [topic from Touch 2]. We recently did a study of [X] companies at your stage and found [counterintuitive stat related to their pain]. Thought you'd find it interesting — happy to share the full breakdown if useful. No strings."

*Touch 4 — Day 14: Content Share (relevant, specific)*
"[First name] — following up on the data I mentioned. We just published [specific piece of content: benchmark report / teardown / case study] that directly addresses [pain point from their posts]. [2-sentence summary of the most relevant insight for their role]. Here's the link: [URL]. Let me know what you think."

*Touch 5 — Day 21: Direct Ask (low friction)*
"[First name] — I've shared a couple of things that I hope have been useful. The reason I've been reaching out is that we help [ICP job title] at companies like [2 relevant logos] [specific outcome: e.g., reduce forecast error by 30% in 60 days]. Worth a 15-minute conversation to see if there's a fit? I'll send a calendar link — you pick the time that works. If the timing isn't right, no worries — happy to stay connected."

*Fallback Logic:*
- If they engage with Touch 2 comment (like or reply): Advance Touch 3 to Day 5 instead of Day 7 and reference their engagement directly ("Your reply to my comment made me think...")
- If they reply to Touch 3 DM but don't commit: Send Touch 4 immediately with a direct reference to their reply — do not wait 7 days
- If they click the link in Touch 4 but don't respond: Add a "I see you checked out the [content]" opener to Touch 5 (Sales Nav Smart Link tracking enables this)
- If no engagement across all 5 touches: Move to 90-day passive nurture sequence (quarterly value touchpoint, no direct ask for 3 months)

**Full 5-Touch Sequence for Persona B (Technical Champion):**

*Touch 1 — Day 1: Connection with context note*
"[First name] — work with [RevOps / SalesOps / your function] teams at companies like [relevant logo 1] and [relevant logo 2] on [their pain area]. Connecting because we likely run into the same challenges. No pitch — just a useful connection."

*Touch 2 — Day 3: Engage with their content OR a relevant group post*
Comment in a shared LinkedIn group or on their post with a specific technical insight about their pain area. Goal: signal you understand their world at a practitioner level.

*Touch 3 — Day 7: DM with a tactical resource*
"[First name] — we built a [framework / checklist / teardown] specifically for [their title]s dealing with [specific tactical problem]. It's a 3-page PDF — no form, no gate. Here it is: [link]. The [specific section] is probably most relevant to you based on your LinkedIn profile and posts."

*Touch 4 — Day 14: Case study + peer proof*
"[First name] — thought you'd find this useful: [similar company to theirs] had the same problem you're working on ([inferred from their posts/profile]). Here's what they did and the result: [2-sentence outcome summary]. Full case study: [link]."

*Touch 5 — Day 21: The Ask — framed as peer consultation, not a demo*
"[First name] — I've been sharing some resources and wanted to be direct: we work with [title]s at [their company type] on [specific outcome]. Our team does a free 30-minute [stack audit / workflow review / benchmark comparison] for [their role] — no product pitch, just analysis of your current setup and what's working vs. not. Worth it if you're evaluating anything in this space? Calendar link: [URL]."

A prospect is "warm" and ready for direct DM outreach when they cross ONE of the following thresholds in the last 30 days:

|-----------------|-----------|---------------|

**DM Template 1 — For Prospect Who Commented on Your Post:**
"[First name] — appreciate your comment on my post about [topic]. Your point about [specific thing they said] was interesting — that's actually something we see come up constantly when working with [their title]-level folks at [their company type]. [Add one-sentence insight that builds on their comment]. Would it be valuable to compare notes? Happy to share what we're seeing across [X] companies in this space."

**DM Template 2 — For Prospect Who Liked 3+ Posts:**
"[First name] — noticed you've been engaging with a few of my posts about [theme]. Since you seem to be thinking about [that topic], thought I'd reach out directly. **DM Template 3 — For Prospect Who Viewed Your Profile:**
"[First name] — saw you stopped by my profile. We're probably connected through [mutual connection / industry / shared challenge]. I work with [their title]s at companies like [relevant logos] on [specific outcome]. If any of that resonates, worth a quick conversation? Or if you were just looking — no worries, happy to connect either way."

**Sales Qualification Handoff Rule:**

A warm prospect becomes a Sales-Qualified Social Lead (SQSL) when they:
1. Respond to a DM AND express interest in learning more OR ask a product question, OR
2. Click a product-related Smart Link (pricing page, case study, demo page) in any DM exchange, OR
3. Reply to a DM AND their company matches ICP on 3+ firmographic criteria (size, industry, tech stack signal)

At SQSL status: Move conversation to phone or video within 24 hours. AE takes ownership. Log interaction in CRM as "Social Selling — LinkedIn DM" activity source.

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Connection request acceptance rates are below 25% despite targeting ICP.**
- Root cause: Profile credibility gap or generic connection note. Audit the rep's LinkedIn headline, profile photo, and About section — if it reads like a resume or a salesperson's pitch, ICP prospects decline. Rewrite the headline using the outcome formula ("I help [ICP title] achieve [outcome]") and eliminate all product/vendor language from the connection note. Also audit targeting precision: are you sending to actual ICP or adjacent roles? Tighten the Sales Navigator filter to include company size AND industry AND seniority level simultaneously. Expected improvement: 10-15 percentage points in acceptance rate within 2 weeks.

**Problem: DMs are getting no replies even though prospects accepted the connection.**
