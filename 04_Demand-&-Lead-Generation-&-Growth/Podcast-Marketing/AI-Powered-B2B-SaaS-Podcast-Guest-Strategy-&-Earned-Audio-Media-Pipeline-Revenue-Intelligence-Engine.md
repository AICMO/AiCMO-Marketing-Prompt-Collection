## Troubleshooting

**Problem:** Pitching shows but getting no responses (under 5% reply rate)
**Solution:** Audit subject lines — they must reference a specific episode the host published, not a generic topic. Personalization is the #1 predictor of pitch success. Use the "Mutual Connection/Listener Angle" template and reference a specific guest or insight from a recent episode. Also check that the show is actively publishing (must have released an episode within 30 days).

**Problem:** Getting booked but post-episode traffic is negligible (under 50 sessions)
**Solution:** The CTA is too weak or too complex. Replace anything requiring more than one step with a single, high-value URL that's easy to remember and type. Use vanity URLs (e.g., yourcompany.com/[showname]) that redirect to your UTM-tagged landing page. Also, run post-episode amplification on LinkedIn within 24 hours of publish — the host's share + your story post is what drives traffic.

**Problem:** Pipeline influence impossible to measure — contacts not getting tagged
**Solution:** Implement UTM-to-CRM mapping immediately. Every podcast CTA URL must include UTM parameters. Create a HubSpot workflow: if `utm_medium` contains "guest", set `Podcast Influenced = Yes` and stamp the `Podcast Show Name` from `utm_campaign`. Without this infrastructure in place before pitching, attribution is impossible retroactively.
