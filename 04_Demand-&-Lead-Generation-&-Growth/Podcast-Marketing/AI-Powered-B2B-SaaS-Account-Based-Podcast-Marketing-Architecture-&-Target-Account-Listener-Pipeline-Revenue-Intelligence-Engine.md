## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Guest acceptance rate is below 15%**
Solution: The invitation is likely positioned as a marketing activity rather than a genuine editorial opportunity. Audit your outreach — remove any language implying promotion of your product. Add social proof (recent notable guests). Send from the host's personal email, not a marketing alias. Most importantly, make the episode topic directly relevant to the guest's current public focus (reference their recent LinkedIn posts or speaking topics).

**Problem: Podcast intent score triggers aren't generating enough sales activity**
Solution: The score threshold may be too high, or the automation isn't delivering enough context to make sales action easy. Lower threshold for BDR alert to 50 (from 60). Enhance the alert with AI-generated personalized opening lines so reps can act in under 2 minutes. Run a weekly "Podcast Pipeline Review" on Monday — 15 minutes where sales leader reviews top 5 podcast-engaged accounts with the BDR team.

**Problem: Can't identify which target account employees are listening**
Solution: Most podcast platforms don't provide individual-level listener data. Build your identification layer through: (a) email show notes to your subscriber list and track by contact; (b) use LinkedIn retargeting pixel on show notes pages to build a matched audience you can cross-reference against your target account list; (c) add a frictionless CTA ("Text EPISODE to get today's show notes") that captures contact info; (d) host a private "Insider Community" in Slack or Circle that requires email for access — this becomes your listener identification engine.
