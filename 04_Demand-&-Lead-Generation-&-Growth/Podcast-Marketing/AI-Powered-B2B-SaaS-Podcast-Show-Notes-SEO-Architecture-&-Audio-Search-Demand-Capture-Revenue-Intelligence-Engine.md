## Troubleshooting

**Problem:** Show notes pages are getting crawled but not ranking — stuck below position 40 after 90 days
**Solution:** The page is likely missing topical authority signals. Audit the internal link structure: does the show notes page link out to relevant product pages and blog content? Does your website have 3+ other pieces of content on the same topic (Google rewards topical depth)? Add the FAQPage schema if not already present — this alone can push pages into SERP features that drive clicks. Also check page speed: podcast show notes with large transcript sections often fail Core Web Vitals. Use lazy-loading for images and implement a "Read More" accordion for transcripts over 3,000 words.

**Problem:** Strong organic traffic but near-zero lead captures (conversion rate under 1%)
**Solution:** The CTA offer is misaligned with the episode content or audience temperature. A cold organic visitor who searched for "b2b buyer journey statistics" is not ready for a product demo — they want educational content. Replace bottom-of-funnel CTAs (demo, trial) with middle-of-funnel offers (benchmark report, calculator, template) that deliver immediate standalone value. Also check CTA placement: if your form is only below the full transcript (3,000+ words down the page), most visitors never see it. Add an inline CTA block after the first 2-3 key insights, when reader engagement is highest.

**Problem:** Guest is not sharing the episode — co-promotion kit going unused
**Solution:** Reduce friction to zero. Most guests intend to share but procrastinate because they have to write something from scratch. Send the guest kit with content already pre-written and pre-formatted for copy-paste — one LinkedIn post draft (not a template, a complete draft in their voice based on what they said in the episode), one Twitter/X thread, and a simple "share this link" direct message. Frame the email subject as "Your episode is live — 2-click share kit inside" and send within 90 minutes of publish, before the guest's attention moves to something else. Follow up once at Day 3 if no share.
