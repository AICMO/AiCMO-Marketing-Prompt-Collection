### Deliverable 2: AI-Powered Content Production Workflow

Design the full production system for each exec tier:

**Tier 1 — Full Production Support (Low Content Comfort Level)**
Executive inputs: 1x 10-15 minute voice memo or Loom per week on their chosen topic.

Production workflow:
1. **Transcription** → Use [Otter.ai / Whisper API] to produce full transcript with speaker cleanup
2. **Insight Extraction** → AI prompt: "Extract the 3 most insight-rich observations from this transcript. Identify any data points, counterintuitive claims, or frameworks. Flag any moments where the speaker uses unique language or phrases that should be preserved. Output as bullet list."
3. **Content Generation** → AI produces:
   - 3 LinkedIn post drafts in exec voice (text-only format, <1,200 chars, each with different hook style: data hook / story hook / contrarian hook)
   - 1 LinkedIn carousel outline (5-7 slides, headline + key point per slide)
   - 1 newsletter paragraph (250 words, fits in existing company newsletter)
   - 3 short-form video script segments (45-90 seconds each, for LinkedIn video or TikTok/YouTube Shorts)
4. **Human Review** → Editor refines 2 of the 3 LinkedIn drafts to match exec voice. Time: 20 minutes.
5. **Scheduling** → Post schedule loaded into [Taplio / Buffer / Authory] with optimal send time based on exec audience engagement data
6. **Repurposing Queue** → Approved carousel assets sent to designer via [Figma / Canva template]. Video scripts sent to exec for 3-minute Loom recording.

**Tier 2 — Collaborative Production (Medium Comfort Level)**
Executive inputs: Monthly 30-minute content planning session + reviews drafts before posting.

Production workflow:
1. **Monthly Content Brief** → AI generates 4-week content calendar based on exec's 4 themes: 2 posts per theme per month (8 posts/month total), with topic suggestions, hook angles, and supporting data sources
2. **Executive Review Session** → Exec selects 8 approved topics, adds personal anecdotes or data points via voice note or written comments
3. **AI Draft Production** → Full draft for each post using exec's input + their voice profile
4. **Edit-then-Post** → Exec reviews draft, makes 5-10 minute edits, posts personally (increases authenticity signal to algorithm)
5. **Thread Expansion** → AI converts approved posts into comment threads (2-3 follow-up comments the exec can post to extend reach)

**Tier 3 — Light-Touch Support (High Comfort Level)**
Executive writes their own content; AI provides optimization layer:
1. **Pre-Post Hook Analysis** → Exec submits draft; AI scores hook strength (1-10), predicts engagement rate based on format/length, suggests 2 alternative hooks
2. **Optimal Timing Recommendation** → Based on exec's audience activity data, AI recommends best 3 posting windows this week
3. **Cross-Platform Repurposing** → AI automatically generates newsletter paragraph, Twitter/X thread, and podcast talking points from every approved LinkedIn post
4. **Comment Response Drafts** → First 2 hours after post goes live, AI drafts responses to comments > 5 words for exec to approve/edit. Speed of response is the #1 algorithmic signal.

### Deliverable 5: LinkedIn Thought Leader Ads (TLA) Integration

**Organic → Paid Amplification Decision Rules**
Only amplify posts that meet both criteria:
- Organic engagement rate > 2.5% (engagements / impressions × 100)
- ICP relevance score > 7/10 (does the post directly address ICP pain points or ICP's job responsibilities?)

**TLA Audience Architecture**
For each approved exec post, create 3 audience segments:
1. **Retargeting warm audience**: Website visitors (last 90 days) + CRM contacts not yet in pipeline
2. **ICP lookalike audience**: LinkedIn matched audience from CRM ICP contacts → create 3x lookalike
3. **Cold ICP audience**: Job title + seniority + industry + company size targeting matching your ICP definition

**Budget Allocation Formula:**
Monthly TLA budget per exec = (LinkedIn followers × 0.003) + $500 baseline
Example: Exec with 8,000 followers → (8,000 × 0.003) + $500 = $524/month TLA budget

**A/B Test Roadmap:**
- Month 1-2: Test audience segment (retargeting vs. cold ICP — expected winner: retargeting by 2-3x CTR)
- Month 3-4: Test post format (text-only vs. document/carousel — expected winner: carousel by engagement)
- Month 5-6: Test CTA (no CTA → "See how we approach X" → "Get the full framework")
- Month 7+: Optimize winning combinations; test new exec personas

**Creative Rotation Rule:**
Replace TLA creative every 14 days OR when CPM increases >20% (sign of creative fatigue). Always select next batch from top 3 organic performers in the prior 2-week window.

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Executive posts feel inauthentic when AI-drafted, followers notice and engagement drops**
Fix: Implement "voice lock" — have each exec create a 10-post "voice corpus" of content they wrote themselves with no AI. Use these as few-shot examples in every AI prompt. Rule: exec must edit at least 20% of every AI draft before posting, specifically changing any phrase that "doesn't sound like them." Authenticity is maintained through editing, not through writing everything from scratch.

**Problem: LinkedIn reach drops significantly after first 2-3 months despite consistent posting**
Fix: This is the "LinkedIn plateau" — common after initial follower spurt. Diagnosis: check if exec is primarily posting to existing audience (text posts only, low comment volume).
