## Overview

Builds a fully AI-automated, multi-channel nurture system for prospects in the active evaluation stage — when they're shortlisting vendors, running POCs, completing demos, or moving through a formal RFP process. Deploy immediately after a demo, trial activation, or pricing page intent spike to accelerate conversion before a competitor wins the deal.

Create 4 evaluation-stage micro-segments. For each segment define:

- Core motivation driver (what they care most about at this moment)
- Optimal nurture cadence (total sequence length, days between touches, channel mix)
- Priority content themes that convert this segment
- Disqualification criteria (when to exit this sequence and re-route)

Segments to design:
A. CHAMPION-LED / INTERNAL SELLING MODE: Champion is bought in, needs help selling to their own leadership team. Key challenge: building internal business case, not evaluating your product.
B. PRICE-SENSITIVE / ROI-REQUIRED: Prospect is comparing quotes and has requested pricing from multiple vendors. Budget approval is the bottleneck.
C. TECHNICAL EVALUATION / SECURITY-HEAVY: IT, security, or engineering team is running due diligence. Feature depth, compliance, and integration reliability are primary concerns.

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Sequence engagement drops sharply after Day 7, and reply rates are below 2%.**
Solution: Prospects are receiving generic messages that don't acknowledge their specific evaluation situation. Audit whether the competitor tag is being populated correctly in your CRM from demo call notes. If ≥40% of prospects don't have a competitor field populated, the sequence is sending generic messages. Fix: Add a Day 1 triggered email that explicitly asks "Are you evaluating any other platforms alongside Velora?" and use the reply to route to the correct competitive track. Also check subject line personalization — evaluation-stage sequences that include the prospect's company name or a specific pain reference in the subject line see 2.4× higher open rates than generic subject lines.

**Problem: Sales team ignores marketing escalation signals and doesn't follow up on Tier 1 triggers within the required window.**
Solution: The escalation protocol doesn't have accountability built in. Fix: Add a Slack alert to AE's direct channel (not a shared team channel) with the exact behavioral signal, prospect name, company, deal value, and a one-click "I'm calling now" acknowledgment button. Set an automatic follow-up Slack message if no acknowledgment within 4 hours: "This deal may be at risk — competitor evaluation signal detected, no follow-up logged." Track follow-up compliance in CRM with a `Escalation_Followed_Up__c` field and include in weekly pipeline review.

**Problem: Winning demos and winning sequences but still losing deals to the primary named competitor at the final stage.**
Solution: The evaluation criteria shaping in the nurture sequence is not being reinforced in the sales conversation. Run a 2-session JTBD interview with the last 5 closed-lost deals to that competitor. Identify the specific decision criteria the economic buyer used that you weren't addressing. Update the economic buyer sequence (Track B) to explicitly name those criteria and pre-answer them before the final decision meeting. Additionally, ensure the Evaluation Criteria Guide asset (delivered on Day 5) is being used by champions in their internal presentations — add a tracking link to see if the economic buyer opened it.
