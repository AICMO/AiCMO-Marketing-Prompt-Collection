## Overview
This prompt designs a full connected TV (CTV) and streaming advertising program for B2B SaaS companies — targeting named accounts and ICP personas with precision across Hulu, Peacock, Paramount+, Roku, Samsung Ads, and programmatic CTV inventory. Use it when you need to reach VP- and C-suite buyers during their personal screen time, warm target accounts ahead of outbound, or drive brand lift in accounts that are dark to your digital channels. CTV is the fastest-growing B2B demand creation channel in 2025–2026 and requires fundamentally different architecture than paid search or LinkedIn.

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Low account match rate (below 40%)**
Solution: Supplement account list targeting with firmographic audience segments (industry, company size, job function) from DMP providers like Oracle Data Cloud or Bombora. This ensures impression delivery even when IP matching fails for remote/hybrid work environments. Target a combined reach of 70% of your account list through deterministic + probabilistic matching.

**Problem: Completion rate below 75% (ad skipping or abandonment)**
Solution: Audit your creative for front-loading — the first 3 seconds must earn attention. Most CTV inventory is non-skippable, so low completion rates indicate the content is playing in low-quality or connected-but-not-watched environments. Increase inventory quality filters in your DSP (exclude long-tail apps, require IAS/MOAT viewability verification) and activate premium PMP deals with verified publishers. Also check if frequency is too high (above 12/month) causing viewer fatigue.

**Problem: No measurable pipeline lift after 90 days**
Solution: First, verify your attribution window and exposure threshold are calibrated correctly — a single impression does not constitute "influence." Require minimum 5 impressions in a 30-day window. Second, check that CTV is coordinated with other channels — CTV alone rarely drives action; it needs LinkedIn, email, or outbound running simultaneously to create the surround-sound effect that converts to meetings. Third, ensure your sales team is actually activating on the CTV-warmed account list — if AEs aren't following up, CTV impressions don't convert.
