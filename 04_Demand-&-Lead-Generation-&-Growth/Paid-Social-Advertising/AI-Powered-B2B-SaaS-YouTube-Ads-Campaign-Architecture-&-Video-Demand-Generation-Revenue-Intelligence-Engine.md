## DELIVERABLE 1: FULL-FUNNEL CAMPAIGN ARCHITECTURE

Design the YouTube campaign structure across three funnel stages:

### Stage 1: Demand Creation (Top of Funnel — Awareness)

**Campaign Type:** Video Reach Campaign or Awareness campaign optimized for Reach & Frequency
**Goal:** Reach unreached ICP prospects with brand and problem-framing content before they actively search

**Campaign Settings:**
- Bid strategy: Target CPM (aim for $8–$15 CPM for B2B audiences)
- Ad formats: 6-second bumper ads + 15-second non-skippable (run simultaneously)
- Frequency cap: 3–4 impressions per user per week maximum (B2B buyers tune out faster than consumers)
- Budget allocation: 25% of total YouTube budget

**Targeting Layer:**
- Custom intent audiences: Users who searched Google for [competitor name] reviews, [category] software, [job-specific pain point queries] in the past 30 days
- In-market segments: Business Software, Enterprise Technology (Google's pre-built B2B segments)
- Demographic overlay: Age 25-54, employed, household income top 50% (proxy for professional working status)
- Placement exclusions: Gaming channels, entertainment channels, channels under 1,000 subscribers (B2B buyers are not watching gaming content during research)

**Success Metrics:**
- Reach rate: % of estimated addressable ICP audience reached per month
- Frequency: 3–5 impressions per unique viewer per month (below 3 = insufficient recall, above 6 = diminishing returns)
- Brand lift: Run Google Brand Lift study quarterly ($25K+ spend threshold) to measure aided awareness and purchase intent lift

### Stage 2: Demand Capture (Mid-Funnel — Consideration)

**Campaign Type:** Video View Campaign or Discovery Campaign
**Goal:** Re-engage website visitors, content consumers, and competitor-searchers with proof-of-value content

**Campaign Settings:**
- Bid strategy: Target CPV (Cost Per View — pay only when viewer watches 30+ seconds or full video)
- Ad formats: 30-second and 60-second skippable in-stream ads; YouTube discovery ads
- Frequency cap: 5–7 impressions per user per month
- Budget allocation: 50% of total YouTube budget

**Targeting Layer (Priority Order):**

*Priority 1 — Retargeting (Highest Intent):*
- YouTube channel viewers: Anyone who watched 25%+ of any of your YouTube videos in the past 90 days
- Website visitor retargeting: Site visitors via Google Ads remarketing tag, segmented by page visited (pricing page visitors = highest intent, served more aggressive CTA)
- CRM contact retargeting: Customer Match upload of all MQLs and SQLs who haven't yet booked a demo — serve them video case studies and ROI narratives

*Priority 2 — Competitive Research Audience:*
- Custom intent: Users who searched "[Competitor A] vs [Your Company]", "[Competitor A] alternatives", "[Competitor A] pricing", "[Category] software reviews" in past 14 days
- YouTube viewers of competitor channels: Target viewers of your top 3 competitors' official YouTube channels

*Priority 3 — Lookalike Expansion:*
- Customer match lookalike: Upload your closed-won customer list, use Google's "Similar Audiences" (now "Optimized Targeting") to reach new prospects with similar digital behavior
- Engaged persona audiences: Build custom audience of users who visited G2/Gartner/Capterra category pages for your software type

**Success Metrics:**
- View rate: 25–40% for skippable ads (below 20% = creative problem; above 45% = strong hook)
- Engaged view rate: % watching 30+ seconds (benchmark: 35–55% for B2B content that leads with a specific pain point)
- View-through conversions: Demo requests attributed to YouTube impressions within 7-day view window
- CPL (Cost Per Lead): Target $150–$350 for demo request depending on ACV

### Stage 3: Pipeline Acceleration (Bottom of Funnel — Decision)

**Campaign Type:** Video Action Campaign (VAC) or Performance Max with video assets
**Goal:** Convert high-intent accounts in active evaluation into demo requests or trial signups

**Campaign Settings:**
- Bid strategy: Target CPA (set at 150% of your acceptable CPL until 30+ conversions, then tighten)
- Ad formats: 15-second non-skippable with strong CTA + 30-second skippable with testimonial/proof
- Frequency cap: 10 impressions per user per week (decision-stage buyers need repetition to convert)
- Budget allocation: 25% of total YouTube budget

**Targeting Layer:**
- CRM retargeting: Upload SQL and late-stage opportunity contacts from Salesforce/HubSpot — video ads serve as sales acceleration during active evaluation
- High-intent site visitors: Pricing page, demo page, comparison page visitors in past 14 days
- Named account ABM (see Deliverable 4)
- Keyword-level video targeting: Serve ads before YouTube videos about [specific pain points], [competitor reviews], [category best practices]

**Success Metrics:**
- Demo conversion rate from video ad viewers: 2–5% of engaged viewers (30+ seconds) converting within 30 days
- Pipeline sourced: YouTube-touched opportunities in CRM within 14-day view window
- Cost per opportunity created: [Budget / opportunities created — target 20–35% of ACV]

## DELIVERABLE 3: VIDEO CREATIVE BRIEFS BY FORMAT

### Format A: 6-Second Bumper Ad (Brand Recall)

**Purpose:** Plant your brand name + category association in ICP minds before they search

**Script Structure (must be memorized in 6 seconds):**
- 0–1s: Visual hook (problem moment or surprising statistic — NO logo opener)
- 1–4s: Core value statement (7 words maximum)
- 5–6s: Brand name + category + logo

**Example Script for Revenue Intelligence SaaS:**
[0–1s]: VISUAL: Sales rep staring at blank CRM, frustrated
[1–4s]: VOICEOVER: "Know which deals are actually closing."
[5–6s]: LOGO APPEARS: "VelocityAI — Revenue Intelligence"

**Production Notes:**
- No audio required in first 2 seconds (YouTube auto-plays muted; viewer must choose to unmute)
- Text overlay must convey the message independent of audio
- End frame: Logo + tagline + URL (static, high contrast)

### Format B: 15-Second Non-Skippable (Problem Framing)

**Purpose:** Force ICP attention on a specific, painful problem they recognize immediately

**Script Structure:**
- 0–3s: The problem moment (specific, visceral, must cause recognition in ICP)
- 3–10s: Problem consequences (quantify the cost of inaction)
- 10–14s: Solution teaser (don't over-explain — create curiosity)
- 14–15s: CTA + URL

**Example Script:**
[0–3s]: VOICEOVER over footage of meeting: "Your CRO asks which deals will close this quarter. You pause. You're not sure."
[3–10s]: "Most sales teams lose 23% of pipeline to deals they thought were healthy. It's not your process — it's visibility."
[10–14s]: "VelocityAI gives your team deal intelligence before the quarter-end scramble."
[14–15s]: TEXT ON SCREEN: "VelocityAI.com/demo | See it free"

**Production Notes:**
- Captions mandatory (85% of YouTube is watched without audio)
- No skip button exists — first 3 seconds must earn attention, not ask for it
- Do not mention your company name until second 8+ (build problem credibility first)

### Format C: 30-Second Skippable In-Stream (Value Proposition)

**Purpose:** Convert interested viewers who didn't skip within 5 seconds into demo requests

**Script Structure:**
- 0–5s: THE HOOK — must prevent the skip (pattern interrupt, bold claim, or problem statement that makes the viewer say "wait, what?")
- 5–15s: Problem depth + empathy with the viewer's current situation
- 15–25s: Solution demonstration (show, don't just tell — screen recording, demo clip, data visual)
- 25–28s: Social proof (customer name + quantified result)
- 28–30s: CTA with urgency element

**Example Script:**
[0–5s]: "We analyzed 2 million sales deals. Reps who close 40%+ pipeline share exactly one habit their team doesn't know about." [PAUSE — viewer must want to know what the habit is]

[5–15s]: "Most revenue teams track activity metrics — calls made, emails sent. VelocityAI found that top performers watch for deal engagement signals that CRMs never capture: buying committee growth, champion job changes, multi-threading patterns."

[15–25s]: [SCREEN RECORDING: VelocityAI dashboard showing deal risk scores updating in real-time, red flag alerts surfacing stalled deals]

[25–28s]: "Pavilion increased qualified pipeline by 34% in 90 days. CFO signed off in week one."

[28–30s]: TEXT + VOICEOVER: "VelocityAI.com/demo — book a 20-minute overview"

**Production Notes:**
- First 5 seconds must be strong enough to stop a skip — test 3–4 different hook variations
- Captions mandatory + visual text reinforcement of key statistics
- End card (last 5 seconds): CTA button linking to landing page, subscribe button if you want channel growth

## DELIVERABLE 6: ATTRIBUTION & REVENUE MEASUREMENT

### View-Through Attribution Framework:

YouTube's attribution credit model for B2B SaaS:
- **Engaged view conversion (30+ seconds watched)**: 14-day window — if a viewer watches 30+ seconds of your ad and converts within 14 days, YouTube takes credit. This is your primary YouTube conversion signal.
- **View-through conversion (skipped or less than 30 seconds)**: 1-day window — lower confidence signal, use for directional insight only
- **Click-through conversion**: Standard — highest confidence, but B2B buyers rarely click ads directly

### CRM Pipeline Attribution Rules:

**YouTube-Sourced Opportunity:** Contact watched a YouTube ad → no prior CRM record → converted to demo request → created as YouTube-sourced opportunity in CRM

**YouTube-Influenced Opportunity:** Contact already in CRM → watched YouTube ad within 30 days of a pipeline stage progression → tag as YouTube-influenced (do NOT source attribute; this prevents double-counting with SDR or event attribution)

**CRM Integration Setup (Salesforce/HubSpot):**
- Add UTM parameters to all YouTube CTA landing pages: `utm_source=youtube&utm_medium=paid_video&utm_campaign=[campaign_name]&utm_content=[ad_creative_name]`
- Import Google Ads conversion data into CRM via native Google Ads integration or Zapier
- Create custom field: `YouTube_Engaged_View_Date` — populated when a CRM contact appears in a YouTube Customer Match audience AND has a recorded view in Google Ads data

### Monthly YouTube Revenue Report Template:

Track these metrics in your monthly paid media review:

**Reach & Awareness:**
- Unique ICP users reached (estimate from Google reach reports)
- Average frequency delivered to ICP
- Brand recall lift (from Brand Lift study, quarterly)

**Engagement & Consideration:**
- Total engaged views (30+ seconds) by campaign
- View rate by creative variant
- CTR to landing page

**Pipeline Contribution:**
- YouTube-sourced demo requests (direct click/view-through)
- YouTube-influenced open opportunities (first or last touch within 30 days)
- YouTube-influenced closed-won revenue (MoM trend)
- Pipeline influence ROI: [YouTube-attributed pipeline $ / YouTube ad spend]

**Efficiency:**
- CPV by campaign and creative
- CPA (cost per demo request) by campaign
- CPQL (cost per qualified lead): YouTube-sourced demos that progressed to SQL

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Customer Match lists aren't activating — Google says audience is "too small"**
Solution: Google requires a minimum of 1,000 matched users for a Customer Match audience to serve. If your upload of 5,000 emails only matches 800 users, try: (1) Upload additional contact fields — phone numbers and mailing addresses increase match rate by 15–25%. (2) Expand your TAL to include more contacts. (3) While waiting for match threshold, launch custom intent audiences and YouTube placement targeting to build engaged viewer retargeting lists organically.

**Problem: View rates are below 15% — ads are being skipped immediately**
Solution: The first 5 seconds of your skippable ad is failing. The three most common causes: (1) You open with a logo or company name — B2B buyers skip branded openers. Start with the problem or a bold, unexpected statement. (2) Your hook is too general — "Are you struggling with [broad category]?" won't stop a skip. Use specificity: a real number, a named competitor, a specific job title moment. (3) The visual is stock footage — viewers recognize and skip stock. Use real product demo screenshots, real customer footage, or data visualizations.

**Problem: YouTube is taking attribution credit for conversions that LinkedIn or SDR outreach drove**
Solution: Implement a strict attribution governance policy. Set YouTube view-through conversion window to 1 day in Google Ads settings (instead of the default 7-day). Use a separate UTM parameter for YouTube (`utm_source=youtube_vt`) so CRM can identify view-through vs. click-through conversions. In your pipeline attribution rules: only count YouTube-sourced when YouTube is the first AND last touch before conversion. For influenced pipeline, use the "any touch within 30 days" rule but exclude opportunities that had CRM-recorded SDR activity in the same 30-day window — those get SDR attribution.
