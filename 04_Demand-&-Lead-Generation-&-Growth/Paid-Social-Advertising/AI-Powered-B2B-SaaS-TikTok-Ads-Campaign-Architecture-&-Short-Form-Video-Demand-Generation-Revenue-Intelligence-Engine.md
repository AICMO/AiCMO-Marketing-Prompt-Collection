## DELIVERABLE 1: FULL-FUNNEL TIKTOK CAMPAIGN ARCHITECTURE

Design the TikTok campaign structure across three funnel stages using TikTok Ads Manager campaign objectives:

### Stage 1: Demand Creation (Top of Funnel — Awareness & Reach)

**Campaign Objective:** Reach or Video Views
**Goal:** Expose unreached ICP professionals to your brand and problem framing through edutainment content — before they actively evaluate software solutions

**Campaign Settings:**
- Bid strategy: CPM (Cost Per Mille) — target $6–$14 CPM for B2B professional interests (significantly lower than LinkedIn's $35–$80 CPM)
- Ad format: In-Feed Ads (native, autoplay in the For You page feed), 15–30 seconds optimal
- Frequency cap: 2–3 impressions per user per week (B2B buyers on TikTok are there for entertainment — over-frequency triggers unfollows and negative brand association)
- Budget allocation: 30% of total TikTok budget

**Targeting Layer:**
- Interest & Behavior: Software Development, Technology, Cybersecurity, SaaS, Startups, Entrepreneurship, Programming (select from TikTok's interest taxonomy under "Technology")
- Hashtag-based interest: Target followers of industry hashtags: #devops, #SRE, #softwaredevelopment, #cybersecurity, #vc, #startuptok, #techstartup (TikTok allows interest audiences built from hashtag engagement)
- Creator follower audiences: Build audiences from followers of the top 10–15 industry creators in your ICP's niche (see Deliverable 4 for creator identification)
- Geography and demographic: Target relevant tech hubs and major cities; age 25–44 (where B2B decision-makers skew heavily on TikTok); gender parity unless your ICP skews significantly one way
- Placement: TikTok feed only (exclude Pangle and external placements — these are content network placements that reach lower-intent audiences at lower CPMs but rarely convert for B2B)

**Success Metrics:**
- Unique reach: % of estimated ICP audience reached monthly
- Video completion rate: 40%+ on 15-second ads; 25%+ on 30-second ads (below 30% on 15s = hook failure)
- Profile visits generated from paid ads (leading indicator of brand curiosity)
- Lift to website traffic from TikTok (measured in GA4 — TikTok visitors who directly navigate back within 7 days)

### Stage 2: Demand Capture (Mid-Funnel — Consideration & Engagement)

**Campaign Objective:** Video Views (30-second view optimization) or Traffic (if driving to educational landing page)
**Goal:** Re-engage viewers who have shown buying signals — watched 50%+ of a previous TikTok ad, visited your website, or are in your CRM as leads not yet converted to demo/trial

**Campaign Settings:**
- Bid strategy: oCPM optimized for Video Views (2-second or 6-second views) OR Traffic (Clicks)
- Ad format: In-Feed Ads with stronger problem-solution narrative; Spark Ads amplifying creator content about your category
- Frequency cap: 4–6 impressions per user per month
- Budget allocation: 45% of total TikTok budget

**Targeting Layer (Priority Order):**

*Priority 1 — Pixel Retargeting (Highest Intent):*
- Website visitors from the past 30 days, segmented by page: Pricing page visitors (highest intent — serve demo-request CTA), Features page visitors (serve product demonstration creative), Homepage visitors who didn't reach a conversion page (serve problem-framing creative)
- TikTok Video Engagers: Custom audience of users who watched 50%+ of any of your previous TikTok ads or organic content in the past 60 days
- Lead Form Abandoners: Users who started but didn't complete a TikTok Lead Gen form (re-engage with social proof creative)

*Priority 2 — CRM Custom Audiences:*
- Upload email list of MQLs and known prospects not yet on trial or demo stage via TikTok Ads Manager Custom Audience (CSV upload with email, phone, mobile device ID where available)
- TikTok matches approximately 30–55% of B2B email uploads (lower than Meta, higher than LinkedIn for tech-savvy audiences)
- Serve: Customer case study creative showing ROI achieved by peers in their industry

*Priority 3 — Competitor Interest Audiences:*
- Build interest audiences targeting users who follow or engage with content tagged with your competitor names (use TikTok's "Creator Interactions" targeting to reach followers of competitor brand accounts)
- Hashtag: Users who engage with #[CompetitorName] content
- Serve: Differentiation narrative creative — your POV on how you solve problems competitors don't

**Success Metrics:**
- 6-second view rate: 65%+ (strong hook engagement threshold for B2B educational content)
- Full video completion rate: 35–55% for 30-second problem-solution ads
- Click-through rate to website: 0.8–2.5% for well-targeted B2B in-feed ads
- Cost per website click: $0.80–$3.00 for B2B tech audiences
- Trial/demo conversion rate from TikTok traffic: 1–4% (lower than LinkedIn due to lower purchase-intent context; compensate with volume)

### Stage 3: Pipeline Conversion (Bottom of Funnel — Decision)

**Campaign Objective:** Conversions (optimizing for demo request, trial signup, or lead form completion)
**Goal:** Convert high-intent accounts — website visitors who visited pricing or demo pages, CRM contacts in late-stage evaluation, or event attendees — into direct pipeline actions

**Campaign Settings:**
- Bid strategy: oCPM optimized for the specific conversion event (TikTok Pixel conversion optimization — requires minimum 50 conversion events/week for the algorithm to optimize effectively)
- Ad format: In-Feed Ads with direct CTA + TikTok Lead Gen forms (for faster mobile conversion — pre-populate with email/name from TikTok profile data)
- Frequency cap: 8–10 impressions per user per week (decision-stage buyers need repetition; TikTok's algorithm also naturally increases frequency for high-engagement content)
- Budget allocation: 25% of total TikTok budget

**Targeting Layer:**
- Pixel custom audience: Pricing page + demo page visitors in past 14 days (highest intent signal from website behavior)
- CRM SQL uploads: Contacts tagged as Sales Qualified Lead in CRM who haven't yet signed up for trial or booked demo
- Video Completion retargeting: Users who watched 75%+ of your conversion-stage creative in any prior campaign
- Lookalike from converters: Build a lookalike audience from your confirmed free trial signups or demo requesters (minimum 1,000 seed audience members for best quality)

**Success Metrics:**
- Cost per trial signup or demo request from TikTok: $40–$200 depending on ACV and conversion action (significantly lower than LinkedIn's $150–$600 but typically lower intent per lead)
- Lead quality rate: % of TikTok leads that reach SQL within 30 days (track separately from other channels to validate channel quality)
- TikTok-sourced pipeline: Opportunities in CRM where TikTok is first or last touch within 7-day click window

## DELIVERABLE 3: TIKTOK CREATIVE BRIEF TEMPLATES

### Format A: 15-Second Hook-Driven In-Feed Ad (Product Discovery)

**Purpose:** Interrupt the scroll in the first 3 seconds with a specific, surprising fact or problem statement that forces ICP professionals to stop and watch

**TikTok-Specific Creative Rules:**
- First 3 seconds must earn attention WITHOUT audio (TikTok autoplays with sound on, but many users scroll with device on silent — captions are mandatory)
- Native-feeling > polished corporate (A/B test: smartphone-shot UGC style vs. slick produced content — UGC typically wins 40–60% higher completion rates for B2B)
- Text overlay reinforces audio — never replace it
- End card: Logo + CTA text + URL — 2 seconds minimum

**Script Structure:**
- 0–3s: The hook (pattern interrupt — unexpected statistic, bold claim, or "POV: you work in [ICP role] and [specific painful moment]")
- 3–11s: Problem depth or surprising insight that makes the ICP feel understood
- 11–14s: Product name drop + core value statement (1 sentence maximum)
- 14–15s: CTA on screen ("link in bio to try free" or "tap to learn more")

**Example Script — Developer Tools SaaS:**

[0–3s]: TEXT OVERLAY: "This bug took our team 9 hours to find." | VOICEOVER: "This bug took our team 9 hours to find."

[3–11s]: "We had 4 engineers on a Zoom call, three dashboards open, and nobody knew which microservice was failing first. That's not a people problem. That's a tooling problem." [Show: frantic Slack messages, multiple dashboards, frustrated developer]

[11–14s]: "StackPulse maps your entire incident blast radius in seconds. Not hours."

[14–15s]: TEXT: "stackpulse.io — free 14-day trial | link in bio"

**Sound Strategy:** Native TikTok audio (trending sounds with low-key background beat) OR clear voiceover with no music competition — never corporate music beds

### Format B: 30-Second Edutainment Ad (Problem-Solution with Demo)

**Purpose:** Deliver enough value in 30 seconds that the viewer feels they learned something useful AND understands how your product solves it — this is the format that builds pipeline-intent brand associations at scale

**Script Structure:**
- 0–4s: Pattern interrupt hook (make the ICP say "wait, is this about me?")
- 4–14s: Educational insight that stands alone as valuable content (the "give before you ask" principle — if someone stopped watching here, they'd still walk away with something useful)
- 14–24s: Product demonstration (screen recording, not stock footage — showing actual software solving the exact problem you just described)
- 24–28s: Social proof anchor (a real company name + specific result — e.g., "Acme Corp cut mean time to resolution by 67% in month one")
- 28–30s: CTA with friction reduction ("No credit card, no sales call — 14-day free trial")

**Example Script — Incident Response SaaS:**

[0–4s]: VOICEOVER: "The three questions every SRE dreads in an incident: What's broken? What caused it? Who needs to know right now?" TEXT OVERLAY: "The 3 incident questions nobody can answer fast enough"

[4–14s]: "The average enterprise incident involves 7 different monitoring tools. Your engineers context-switch between PagerDuty, Datadog, Jira, Slack, and your runbooks — losing 20–40 minutes just correlating signals while the customer is down. Here's what a single incident timeline should look like instead:" [Show: clean, unified timeline]

[14–24s]: [SCREEN RECORDING: StackPulse dashboard — incident fires, system auto-correlates 14 signals into one root cause hypothesis in 8 seconds, auto-notifies stakeholders, creates incident channel in Slack, pulls relevant runbook] "That just happened automatically. No manual correlation. No bridge call confusion."

[24–28s]: "Netlify uses StackPulse. Their MTTR dropped from 4.2 hours to 38 minutes in 60 days."

[28–30s]: TEXT: "Free 14-day trial — no credit card | stackpulse.io"

**Sound:** Real ambient product audio (keyboard clicks, notification sounds) + calm, confident voiceover. Avoid dramatic music — B2B edutainment sounds authoritative, not alarming.

### Format C: Spark Ads Strategy (Amplifying Creator Content as Paid Ads)

**Purpose:** Leverage the authenticity of real B2B practitioners talking about your product or your category — and pay to distribute their content to a much larger targeted audience. Spark Ads outperform standard in-feed ads by 30–40% on completion rate and engagement because they look entirely native.

**Spark Ads Workflow:**

**Step 1 — Identify Creator Partners:**
Search TikTok for practitioners already using or reviewing tools in your category:
- Search: "best devops tools 2025", "incident management workflow", "SRE tools", "[your category] review"
- Filter for: 5,000–200,000 followers (sweet spot for B2B — large enough to seed lookalikes, small enough to be affordable and authentic)
- Engagement rate benchmark: 3%+ (likes + comments + shares / followers × 100)
- Confirm: Creator's audience matches your ICP (ask for media kit; TikTok Creator Marketplace shows audience demographics)

**Step 2 — Creator Brief for Paid Content:**
Send a brief that preserves their voice while hitting your goals:

*What to include in the brief:*
- Core message: The one thing you need the viewer to understand (not a feature list — one specific problem you solve)
- Hard requirements: Mention the product name, include a CTA (link in bio / tap the link), disclose as #ad or #sponsored
- Soft guidance: "Be honest — if you'd use it, say so. If there are tradeoffs, that's fine to mention. Authenticity is the point."
- What NOT to do: No corporate scripts, no jargon, no reading from a product page

*Budget ranges for B2B tech creators (2026):*
- 5K–25K followers (micro): $200–$800 per video
- 25K–100K followers (mid-tier): $800–$3,000 per video
- 100K–500K followers (macro): $3,000–$12,000 per video

**Step 3 — Spark Ad Activation:**
1. Creator publishes the organic post
2. Creator grants your TikTok Ads account Spark Ad authorization (done in TikTok Ads Manager under "Creator Authorization" — they receive an authorization code to share)
3. You run the creator's post as a paid Spark Ad to your targeted B2B audience
4. The ad appears as the creator's original post (showing their handle, follower count, organic comments) — completely native in appearance
5. Set a separate ad group with creator content vs. your brand content to compare performance

**Targeting for Spark Ads:**
- Layer 1: Creator's own audience (their existing followers — Spark Ads can reach beyond just followers)
- Layer 2: Your CRM retargeting audiences (serve the creator's authentic endorsement to your known prospects)
- Layer 3: Lookalike from your customer list (reach new ICP professionals with peer-validated social proof)

### Bidding Optimization Triggers:

**Increase budget when:**
- Conversion campaign reaches 50+ conversion events/week (algorithm optimizes much more effectively at scale — increase budget 30–50% to capitalize)
- Cost per trial signup stays below target for 5 consecutive days
- A creative variant achieves 50%+ 15-second video completion (scale spend on that specific creative immediately)

**Pause or reduce when:**
- 6-second view rate drops below 30% for 7 days (creative fatigue — rotate new hook variants)
- CPA exceeds 2× target for 14 days despite bid adjustments
- Conversion campaign hasn't reached 20 events in first 7 days (pixel may need recalibration — temporarily switch objective to Traffic to build pixel signal before re-enabling conversion optimization)

**Creative Rotation Rule:**
TikTok creative fatigues faster than any other B2B channel. Replace or rotate the top creative every 3–4 weeks even if performance looks acceptable — proactively testing new hooks prevents the cliff-edge performance drop.

## DELIVERABLE 5: ATTRIBUTION & REVENUE MEASUREMENT

### TikTok Attribution Windows for B2B:

TikTok's default attribution windows are more aggressive than B2B buying behavior warrants. Adjust in TikTok Ads Manager → Ad Account Settings → Attribution:

| Touchpoint Type | TikTok Default | B2B SaaS Recommendation | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|
| Click-through conversion | 7 days | 7 days | Standard; most B2B post-click conversions happen within 7 days |
| View-through conversion | 1 day | 1 day | Do NOT use 7-day view-through for B2B — over-credits impressions |
| Engaged view conversion | 6 seconds | 6 seconds | Minimum engagement signal before crediting view |

### CRM Pipeline Attribution Rules:

**TikTok-Sourced Opportunity:** Contact clicks a TikTok ad → no prior CRM record → fills demo form or starts trial → created as TikTok-Sourced opportunity in CRM (UTM: `utm_source=tiktok&utm_medium=paid_social&utm_campaign=[campaign]`)

**TikTok-Influenced Opportunity:** Contact already in CRM → interacts with TikTok ad within 30 days of a pipeline stage progression → tag as TikTok-Influenced in CRM (do NOT source-attribute; prevents double-counting)

**TikTok Dark Social Attribution:** TikTok drives significant brand discovery that converts days or weeks later via direct search, branded Google search, or word-of-mouth referral. To measure this "dark" influence:
- Add a self-reported attribution field to your demo/trial form: "How did you first hear about us?" — expect 8–20% of new pipeline to report TikTok when you're actively advertising
- Monitor branded search volume trends in Google Search Console — TikTok campaigns should lift branded queries by 15–35% within 30 days of launch
- Run a holdout test: pause TikTok for 30 days for a geo-matched control group to measure true incrementality

### Monthly TikTok Revenue Report Template:

**Reach & Awareness:**
- Unique users reached by ICP targeting layer
- Average frequency per user by campaign
- Brand search volume lift (GA4 + Google Search Console branded query trend)

**Engagement & Content Quality:**
- Video completion rates by creative variant and format
- Top 3 creative hooks by 6-second view rate
- Spark Ad performance vs. branded in-feed ad performance

**Pipeline Contribution:**
- TikTok-sourced trial signups and demo requests (click-through attribution)
- TikTok-influenced open pipeline (CRM tag count × average ACV)
- Self-reported TikTok attribution from form field data
- Cost per trial signup and cost per SQL by campaign

**Efficiency:**
- CPQL: Cost per SQL attributed to TikTok (TikTok-sourced CPL × SQL conversion rate)
- Blended CAC including TikTok spend
- Creator Spark Ads CPM vs. branded in-feed CPM comparison

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: TikTok conversion campaign isn't spending — impressions are near zero**
Solution: TikTok's conversion optimization requires a "learning phase" of at least 50 conversion events per week before the algorithm can effectively distribute spend. If you have fewer than 50 weekly conversions on TikTok, switch your campaign objective to Traffic (website clicks) or Video Views for 2–3 weeks to build pixel signal and audience data. Once you accumulate 50+ traffic-attributed conversions, re-enable conversion optimization. Alternatively, choose a higher-funnel conversion event (e.g., optimize for "ViewContent" page visits rather than "CompleteRegistration") to give the algorithm more signal volume to work with.

**Problem: TikTok leads are coming in but SQL conversion rate is very low — leads feel unqualified**
Solution: TikTok's audience skews younger and the platform context is entertainment-first, which means visitors arrive with lower immediate purchase intent than LinkedIn or paid search. Three fixes: (1) Add a qualifying question to your trial signup or lead form — even one question ("What's your company size?") filters out students and hobbyists while the algorithm learns from completions. (2) Shift budget toward retargeting audiences (CRM contacts, website visitors) who have already demonstrated interest — these convert at 3–5× the rate of cold interest targeting. (3) Implement a longer lead nurture sequence for TikTok-sourced leads: they may take 45–90 days vs. 20–30 days for LinkedIn — don't count TikTok as low-quality until you've tracked a full 90-day cohort.

**Problem: Creative is performing well in the first week but drops off sharply in week 2–3**
Solution: TikTok creative fatigues faster than any B2B channel — the same ICP scrolls TikTok multiple times per day, burning through your ad creative 3–5× faster than LinkedIn. Implement a 3-3-3 creative rotation system: maintain 3 active ad variations per ad group, launch 3 new creative tests per month, and retire the bottom-performing 3 after 21 days regardless of absolute performance. Keep a "hooks library" — a running document of 20–30 proven first-3-second openings you can repurpose with different body content to quickly produce new variants without rebuilding full videos.
