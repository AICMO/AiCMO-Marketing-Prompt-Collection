## Troubleshooting

**Problem 1: Sales reps reject marketing outreach to "their" accounts — protect deal relationships.**
Fix: Establish a clear Rules of Engagement document co-created with the Head of Sales: (1) Marketing NEVER sends direct email to contacts in active deals without AE opt-in; (2) LinkedIn ads and retargeting are always running and do not require AE approval — they are brand-level, not deal-level; (3) AEs receive all marketing intelligence on their deals but control which outreach sequences to activate; (4) Market the program internally as "marketing giving AEs superpowers" not "marketing taking over late-stage." Run a pilot with 5-10 willing AEs first and document win rate improvement before rolling out company-wide.

**Problem 2: Buying Committee Coverage Score (BCCS) is low because CRM data quality is poor — contacts not entered, roles not tagged.**
Fix: This is a process and incentive problem, not a technology problem. (1) Add BCCS to the weekly pipeline review — when AEs present deals, ask about BCCS; low BCCS deals get scrutinized for data quality; (2) Configure Salesforce validation rules that require minimum contact roles for Stage 3+ advancement; (3) Make it easy — AE enters deal contacts via a simple Salesforce Lightning component or Groove/Outreach sidebar; (4) Marketing helps fill gaps using LinkedIn Sales Navigator and 6sense — pre-populate contact records for AE to confirm, reducing their data entry burden.

**Problem 3: Economic buyer engagement remains zero even after running LinkedIn campaigns and sending executive content.**
Fix: Economic buyers at enterprises ($1B+ revenue companies) are extremely difficult to reach via marketing alone. Escalate to three approaches: (1) Champion-delivered introduction — coach the champion explicitly on HOW to introduce you to their CFO, including providing them a draft forwarding email and the exact reason the CFO should care (quantified ROI in the first sentence); (2) C-suite to C-suite — have your CFO or CEO send a personal note — not a sales note — to their CFO referencing a shared industry challenge and inviting a peer conversation; (3) Event-based activation — invite economic buyer to a highly exclusive executive dinner or roundtable (5-8 CFOs, no sales pitch, peer conversation format) where meeting them happens naturally. If none of these work after 60+ days, escalate to the CRO for a strategic account play.
