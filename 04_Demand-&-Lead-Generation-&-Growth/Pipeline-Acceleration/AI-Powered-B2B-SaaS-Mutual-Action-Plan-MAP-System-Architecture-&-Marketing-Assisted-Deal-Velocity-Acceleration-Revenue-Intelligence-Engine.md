Step 2 — AI-POWERED MAP GENERATION:

The system that produces a personalized MAP draft in under 5 minutes from Salesforce opportunity data:

INPUT FIELDS (Read from Salesforce at Stage 2 entry):
- Account.Industry → selects industry-specific milestone language and reference customer examples
- Opportunity.ACV__c → selects Commercial/Enterprise/Strategic MAP template tier
- Opportunity.Close_Date__c → back-calculates recommended milestone dates from target close date
- Opportunity.Competitor__c → adds competitor-specific milestones (e.g., "Competitive Bake-Off Completion") if competitor is in deal
- Opportunity.Decision_Maker_Titles__c → personalizes milestone owner names with actual buyer names from Salesforce contacts
- Account.Number_of_Employees__c → adjusts security review milestone timeline (enterprise procurement takes 2-4x longer than mid-market)
- Opportunity.Pilot_Required__c → adds Pilot Scoping and Pilot Review milestones if true
- Opportunity.Procurement_Complexity__c → adds Legal Negotiation milestone and adjusts timeline if "Complex" is selected

OUTPUT FORMAT (Delivered to AE via Highspot/Notion within 5 minutes):
- Personalized MAP document with buyer company name, economic buyer name, champion name
- All milestone dates pre-calculated from target close date, working backward
- Each milestone shows: What happens, Who owns it (buyer role + seller role), What success looks like, What marketing delivers to support it
- AE edits in <15 minutes: adjust dates based on buyer context, add any missing stakeholders, add company-specific notes

GENERATION MECHANISM:
- Trigger: Salesforce workflow fires when Opportunity Stage = "Stage 2 — Discovery" AND Close Date is populated AND Champion_Name__c is not null
- Process: Zapier or native Salesforce flow calls MAP generation API (built on GPT-4o or Claude with your MAP template as system prompt + Salesforce field values as context)
- Output: Highspot Workspace created for this opportunity with MAP document auto-populated | AE receives Slack notification with link to review + 1-click "Send to Buyer" option in Highspot Engage

Step 3 — MILESTONE-TRIGGERED CONTENT DELIVERY:

Marketing content delivery architecture tied to milestone dates, not arbitrary drip schedules:

TRIGGER LOGIC (Marketo/HubSpot):
- Content sequences are NOT time-based drips from enrollment date
- Content fires based on two signals: (1) Milestone date approaching (X days before milestone date in MAP) AND (2) Milestone not yet marked complete in Salesforce MAP tracking field
- This ensures content is always contextually relevant to where the buyer IS in the process, not where they were when the deal started

MILESTONE CONTENT DELIVERY RULES:
7 days before milestone: Pre-milestone enablement content → buyer stakeholder who owns the milestone
3 days before milestone: Reminder + supporting evidence → same contact
Milestone overdue by 3 days: Reactivation content → champion + AE alert
Milestone overdue by 7 days: Escalation content → senior buyer stakeholder + AE escalation Slack
Milestone marked COMPLETE: Momentum content → "You've completed X, here's what happens next" + next milestone preview

CONTENT LIBRARY REQUIREMENTS (12 core content assets to build for MAP system):
1. Champion Internal Selling Starter Kit: Stakeholder conversation guides + MAP introduction narrative + internal business case framework [delivered at Champion Alignment complete]
2. Security Pre-Package: SOC 2 Type II summary, penetration test executive brief, data residency FAQ, InfoSec FAQ (top 50 questions + answers) [delivered 48 hours before Technical Validation]
3. ROI Executive Summary Template: Pre-populated PDF with buyer company name, industry benchmark data, and placeholder fields for their specific metrics [delivered before Business Case Review]
4. Reference Preparation Guide: Industry-specific questions to ask, what outcomes to probe for, link to reference customer's G2 reviews [delivered before References Completed milestone]
5. Vendor Onboarding Acceleration Kit: Pre-completed security questionnaire (standard), insurance certificates, standard contract term explanations [delivered when Legal Initiated]
6. CFO Approval Package: Board-ready 1-page summary + industry peer benchmark + TCO comparison vs. status quo [delivered before Executive Approval milestone]
7. CISO/Security Decision-Maker Brief: Architecture overview, compliance certifications by framework (SOC 2, ISO 27001, GDPR, HIPAA where relevant), reference CISO contact [delivered 5 days before Security Review]
8. Pilot Success Playbook: Pre-pilot alignment template, success criteria framework, weekly check-in agenda, ROI measurement worksheet [delivered when Pilot Scoped]
9. Stakeholder Summit Brief: Role-specific 1-pagers for each buying committee member attending summit [delivered 5 days before Summit]
10. Contract Momentum Package: Congratulatory note from your CEO to buyer champion, customer community welcome, Day 1 onboarding preview [delivered when contract sent for signature]
11. Milestone Slip Recovery Content: Reactivation email sequences by milestone category (budget slip, security slip, legal slip, executive approval slip) — each with different messaging angle
12. Executive Sponsor Package: Buyer company research brief, deal summary, suggested agenda for exec-to-exec meeting [delivered to your exec 24 hours before Executive Sponsor milestone]

Step 4 — MAP SLIP DETECTION & REACTIVATION ARCHITECTURE:

The system that detects stalled milestones and fires precision reactivation:

SLIP DETECTION (Salesforce + Marketo):
- Daily Salesforce scheduled job: Query all open opportunities where MAP_Milestone_Due_Date__c < TODAY() AND MAP_Milestone_Status__c = "In Progress" (not "Complete" or "Rescheduled")
- Classify by milestone type: BUDGET_SLIP, SECURITY_SLIP, LEGAL_SLIP, EXEC_APPROVAL_SLIP, TECHNICAL_SLIP, CHAMPION_DARK
- Severity classification: Mild Slip (1-7 days overdue), Moderate Slip (8-14 days overdue), Critical Slip (15+ days overdue or second consecutive milestone slip)

REACTIVATION SEQUENCES BY SLIP TYPE:

BUDGET/EXECUTIVE APPROVAL SLIP:
Day +1: Champion email from AE — "Checking in on budget timeline — what's the best way to support you in the approval process?" + attach CFO Approval Package
Day +3: Marketing-triggered email to champion from marketing automation with peer company case study showing ROI realization timeline — "Other [Industry] companies that implemented in Q[X] saw results by [Date]"
Day +7: If no response — AE + marketing joint play: LinkedIn engagement from your CMO or CEO with buyer's CFO (if connected) + AE escalation email to champion
Day +14: CRO/CCO escalation play: Your CRO sends personal note to buyer's CFO inviting a 20-minute peer call to "understand how we can make this decision easier"

SECURITY REVIEW SLIP:
Day +1: SE sends direct email to IT/InfoSec contact: "I noticed your security review is past the planned date — do you have questions I haven't answered? Happy to jump on a call today."
Day +3: Marketing sends CISO/InfoSec contact a "Security Review Completion Shortcut" — pre-completed standard TPRM questionnaire, eliminating the #1 reason security reviews stall (waiting for vendor to complete their form)
Day +7: AE + SE joint escalation: Offer a Security Deep-Dive session (your CISO + their CISO) — frame as peer conversation, not sales call
Day +14: Champion reactivation: "What's blocking the security team? Here's what we've done for [Similar Reference Customer] who had similar InfoSec requirements."

LEGAL/CONTRACT SLIP:
Day +1: AE sends Procurement contact a Contract Acceleration Kit — pre-completed vendor questionnaire, summary of standard redlines you accept, offer for a "contract kickoff call" between your legal team and theirs
Day +3: Marketing sends Procurement contact a case study from a similar-size company: "How [Company] Completed Vendor Evaluation in 3 Weeks" — showing your vendor onboarding process is faster than alternatives
Day +7: AE escalation to champion: "Is there anything in the contract blocking the procurement team? Happy to jump on a call with our legal team and yours to clear redlines in one session."
Day +14: Executive escalation: Your CCO/CRO sends a note to the buyer's CPO offering to personally facilitate a resolution call

CHAMPION DARK (Champion Not Responding to AE Outreach for 14+ Days):
Day +1: Marketing sends champion a value-add touchpoint — not a follow-up, but a relevant piece of industry research or insight ("Saw this benchmark report and thought of your initiative")
Day +3: AE tries a different channel — LinkedIn connection or message if not previously connected
Day +7: Marketing runs LinkedIn ad campaign to champion's title at the account with relevant content — signal monitoring in Salesforce if champion clicks
Day +14: AE + marketing escalation: Identify secondary champion or executive sponsor in deal — marketing runs parallel outreach to secondary stakeholder to surface deal status
Day +21: AE trigger: Deal flagged as "Stuck — Champion Dark" in Salesforce, move to "Deal Rescue" program, activate full buying committee orchestration sequence

Step 5 — CHAMPION MAP ADOPTION ENABLEMENT:

How to get buyers to say yes to participating in a MAP:

THE MAP INTRODUCTION NARRATIVE (Champion's Internal Framing):
"I want to share something that's made our evaluation process easier. [Vendor] uses a Mutual Action Plan — basically a shared project timeline that keeps both our teams accountable and makes sure we hit our target implementation date without last-minute scrambles. It's a living document we both own. Do you have 15 minutes before our next call to walk through it together?"

Key framing principles:
- Never call it a "close plan" or "deal timeline" internally — it's an "implementation success plan" or "project alignment document"
- Every milestone is buyer-owned, not seller-owned — the MAP shows what the buyer needs to DO to achieve their outcome by their target date
- Frame each milestone as risk-mitigation for the buyer, not urgency for the seller
- The MAP protects the buyer's implementation timeline — frame any slip as "this puts your [business outcome] goal at risk," not "this affects our quarter"

CHAMPION ENABLEMENT ASSETS FOR MAP ADOPTION:
1. MAP Pitch Email Template: Ready-to-send email from champion to their stakeholders introducing the MAP as "our shared project plan" — written to be forwarded from champion's email, not from your company
2. MAP Onboarding Session Guide: 15-minute agenda for champion + AE to walk through the MAP together — creates co-ownership, surfaces missing milestones, confirms realistic dates
3. Internal MAP Sharing Template: How to present the MAP in an internal meeting — "Here's the timeline we've agreed to with the vendor that gets us live by [Date]"
4. MAP Success Case Study: Short customer story showing a peer company that used a MAP to hit their implementation deadline — makes MAP adoption feel normal and smart, not pressured

SHARED MAP WORKSPACE DESIGN:
- Platform: Highspot Engage (recommended for tracking) or Notion (easiest buyer adoption) or Google Doc (lowest friction, worst tracking)
- Structure: Tab 1 — MAP milestones with dates, owners, status; Tab 2 — Supporting documents (organized by milestone); Tab 3 — Meeting notes and next steps; Tab 4 — ROI model (buyer-editable)
- Buyer experience: Buyer accesses MAP via link (no login required in Highspot Engage), sees their milestones highlighted, can mark milestones complete — marketing tracks every interaction

Step 6 — FORECAST INTEGRATION & MAP ADHERENCE SCORING:

How MAP adherence connects to more accurate pipeline calls:

MAP ADHERENCE SCORE (Salesforce Custom Field: map_adherence_score__c):
- Calculate as: (Completed Milestones On Time) / (Total Milestones Due To Date) × 100
- Refresh: Daily Salesforce scheduled calculation
- Weighting in Opportunity Probability: 
  - MAP Adherence 85-100: Increase probability by +10 points (buyer is executing on plan)
  - MAP Adherence 65-84: No probability adjustment
  - MAP Adherence 40-64: Decrease probability by -15 points (deal is slipping consistently)
  - MAP Adherence <40: Decrease probability by -30 points + CRO alert + add to Deal Rescue queue

FORECAST MEETING INTEGRATION:
- Weekly pipeline review Salesforce dashboard: Sort opportunities by MAP Adherence Score — show AE forecast, MAP-adjusted forecast, and delta
- CRO call questions by MAP Adherence tier:
  - <65% adherence deals: "What milestone slipped? What's blocking it? What's the new close date based on remaining MAP milestones?"
  - 65-85% deals: "Which milestone are you most at risk on? What does marketing need to deliver to help?"
  - >85% deals: "What's left? Are you on track for a [date] signature?"
- Board forecast: Present "MAP-validated forecast" (opportunities with >75% MAP adherence and signed MAP document) separately from "AE-estimated forecast" — the delta is your quarterly risk exposure

SALESFORCE REPORTING:
- MAP Adoption Rate: % of Stage 2+ deals with a MAP initiated / total Stage 2+ deals
- MAP Adherence by Rep: Average MAP adherence score by AE — identifies coaching opportunities
- MAP Impact on Close Rate: Win rate for deals with MAP adoption >75% vs. <75% — this is your program ROI proof
- Forecast Accuracy by MAP Tier: Quarter-over-quarter forecast accuracy for MAP-adherent vs. non-MAP deals

## Troubleshooting

**Problem 1: Sales reps don't use the MAP system — they see it as administrative overhead and skip it.**
Fix: Three-lever approach: (1) Make MAP generation zero-effort — the MAP must auto-generate from Salesforce without the AE doing anything manually. AE's only job is 10 minutes of review and sharing with the buyer. If it takes longer, adoption will fail. (2) Show AEs the win rate data by MAP adherence — once reps see that MAP-adherent deals close at 2-3x higher win rates, self-interest drives adoption. Present this data at every SKO and weekly pipeline call. (3) Tie MAP adoption to deal advancement — configure Salesforce to require MAP initiation before an opportunity can advance to Stage 3. Make the MAP a gate, not a request.

**Problem 2: Buyers reject the MAP or ghost after it's shared — they see it as a sales manipulation tactic.**
Fix: This is a framing problem, not a MAP problem. (1) Never send the MAP via your company email — coach the champion to present it as "our shared project plan" in a meeting where they own the presentation. Buyer-introduced MAPs have 4x higher adoption than seller-introduced MAPs. (2) Change the milestone language from seller-centric ("Contract Signed") to buyer-centric ("Finance Team Completes Budget Approval — Securing Your Q3 Implementation Slot"). Every milestone should describe what the BUYER achieves, not what the SELLER needs. (3) Start with a 4-milestone "lightweight MAP" for commercial deals — not a 12-milestone enterprise plan. Reduce complexity until adoption is established, then add milestones as the program matures.

**Problem 3: MAP adherence score improves but forecast accuracy doesn't — deals still slip despite MAP adoption.**
Fix: MAP adherence measures process compliance, not deal health. If adherence is high but slippage continues, audit which specific milestones are completing on time (typically early-stage milestones) vs. which slip consistently (typically security, legal, and executive approval). These chronic slip milestones need deeper marketing support — pre-emptive content delivered 2-3 weeks before the milestone, not just at milestone date. Also investigate whether close dates in Salesforce are set by the AE (optimistic) or derived from MAP milestone math (realistic). If AEs input wishful close dates and the MAP back-calculates to the same date, you'll have a compliant MAP with an unrealistic timeline. Fix: CRO should require that close dates in Stage 3+ are MAP-derived, not AE-guessed.
