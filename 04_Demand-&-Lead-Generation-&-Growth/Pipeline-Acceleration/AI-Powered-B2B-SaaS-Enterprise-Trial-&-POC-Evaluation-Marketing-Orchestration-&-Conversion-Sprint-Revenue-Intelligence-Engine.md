# AI-Powered B2B SaaS Enterprise Trial & POC Evaluation Marketing Orchestration & Conversion Sprint Revenue Intelligence Engine - Systematically Win Every Evaluation You Earn With AI-Orchestrated Multi-Stakeholder Marketing That Converts Evaluating Accounts Into Paying Customers

**Difficulty:** Advanced | **Time:** 30-45 min | **Tags:** POC, free trial, evaluation marketing, pipeline acceleration, enterprise sales, buying committee, deal conversion, sales-marketing alignment, B2B SaaS, revenue marketing, demo conversion, evaluation sprint, proof of concept, trial-to-paid, deal velocity

## Overview
Designs a complete, AI-orchestrated marketing program that runs in parallel to an active enterprise proof-of-concept (POC) or free trial evaluation — systematically delivering value-reinforcing content, competitive proof, peer validation, and multi-stakeholder engagement to every evaluation participant from Day 1 through decision day. Use this when your sales team runs structured 30-to-90-day enterprise evaluations, your eval-to-close conversion rate is below 60%, or deals stall after a "successful" POC because economic buyers never got engaged and champions can't build internal consensus.

PHASE 3 — MILESTONE-TRIGGERED MARKETING INTERVENTIONS (Days 1-45):

AI-orchestrated campaigns triggered by evaluation events — not time-based, event-based:

TRIGGER 1 — FIRST CORE WORKFLOW COMPLETED (Product usage signal or CS/AE confirmation):
- What it signals: Evaluator has gotten hands-on and completed the most fundamental task the product is supposed to solve
- Marketing action: Auto-send "First Win Celebration" email to the evaluator — "You just [completed X] in [Product]. Here's what teams at [similar company] did after hitting this same milestone to maximize their results in weeks 2-3" — links to a curated "what's next" guide
- Champion notification: Slack or email to AE — "Evaluation milestone: [Evaluator name] just completed [milestone] at [Account]. Good moment to check in with champion on progress visibility — this is a strong positive signal to share with the EB"

TRIGGER 2 — EVALUATION STALL (No product activity or email engagement for 5+ consecutive days):
- What it signals: Evaluation momentum is lost — user got busy, hit a blocker, or lost confidence
- Marketing action: Email to champion from AE: "I noticed the team hasn't been in [Product] this week. Common at this stage — teams often hit [specific common blocker]. I've set up a 20-minute session with one of our implementation experts. Would [Day/Time] work?" — DO NOT send direct email to evaluators without champion approval
- AE alert: Slack to AE — "Evaluation engagement dark at [Account] for 5+ days. Marketing activating re-engagement sequence. Suggest you reach out to champion directly within 24 hours with a re-engagement offer (office hours, live session, blocker removal call)"

TRIGGER 3 — COMPETITIVE KEYWORD DETECTED (Gong/Chorus flags competitor mention in call OR 6sense shows competitor intent signal at account):
- What it signals: A competitive alternative is being seriously considered OR introduced during the evaluation
- Marketing action: Within 24 hours — deploy to champion via AE email: (1) Competitor-specific one-pager "Why [Customer at similar company] Switched From [Competitor] to Us — and What They Found After 3 Months"; (2) G2/Capterra comparison link showing head-to-head ratings in the most contested categories; (3) Offer for champion to speak directly with a customer who evaluated the same competitor and chose [Product]
- Economic buyer retargeting: Activate LinkedIn campaign targeting EB persona at account specifically with competitive trust-building content — not a sales pitch, a thought leadership piece positioning [Product] as the category leader with third-party validation
- AE alert: Slack to AE — "Competitive signal detected at [Account]: [competitor name] mentioned in [call/intent data]. Marketing has deployed competitive defense assets to champion. Consider raising directly in next call — here's the suggested talk track: [2-3 sentences]"

TRIGGER 4 — EVALUATION MID-POINT (Day 20-25 of a 45-day eval):
- What it signals: Half the evaluation is complete — this is the highest-risk dropout point as initial excitement fades and internal politics kick in
- Marketing action: "Your [Product] Evaluation Mid-Point Review" — champion email + optional group session invitation. Includes: (1) A personalized mid-point scorecard (auto-generated from product usage data or CS input) showing what's been accomplished vs. evaluation goals; (2) Invitation to a 30-minute "Mid-Point Success Call" with an implementation expert and a reference customer champion; (3) Updated business case template incorporating actual evaluation data gathered so far (not just projected ROI — actual results from the first 20 days)
- Economic buyer mid-point update: Send a brief "Evaluation Progress Brief" to the EB contact (if they're in the CRM) — from the AE, drafted by marketing — 1 page: what the team set out to prove, what they've demonstrated so far, what the final 3 weeks will focus on, and what the projected ROI looks like based on early data

TRIGGER 5 — POSITIVE EVALUATION OUTCOME CONFIRMED (Champion or CS reports POC goal achieved):
- What it signals: The product has technically passed the evaluation — but deals die here most often (champion can't close EB, no-decision, procurement delays)
- Marketing action: "Next Step Readiness Package" delivered to champion within 24 hours: (1) Final evaluation summary slide deck template — a 5-slide board-ready presentation showing: what was evaluated, what was proven, the ROI model with actual evaluation data, the risk of delay vs. moving forward now, and the recommended next step; (2) Reference call booking link — 3 champion references at similar companies who can speak to what happened AFTER go-live, not just the eval; (3) Urgency framing guide — helps champion articulate to their EB why delaying costs money (quantified delay cost calculator)

PHASE 4 — ECONOMIC BUYER ACTIVATION TRACK (Days 1-45, Independent Track):

A parallel marketing program specifically designed to engage the CFO/CPO/economic buyer who is NOT participating in the daily evaluation — running simultaneously with all other phases:

Week 1 — AWARENESS & CREDIBILITY:
- LinkedIn Thought Leadership Ad: Target EB title + company at account with a peer-validated content offer — "The Financial Controller's Guide to Evaluating [Category] ROI" or "What 47 CFOs Discovered When They Automated [Process]" — gated PDF, short form, routes new EB contact into Salesforce with tag "evaluation_economic_buyer_new"
- AE outreach offer to champion: Provide champion with a templated email to send to their EB from their own email — "Here's a 5-minute read I thought you'd find relevant as we get into this project" — links to the above content. Give champion the option to send or not; do not send directly without champion opt-in

Week 2 — PROOF & SOCIAL VALIDATION:
- Personalized case study email: AE sends personalized email to EB contact (if in CRM) or via champion — one-page PDF case study featuring CFO-level metrics from a company identical in size, industry, and use case. Focus ONLY on financial outcomes (ROI, payback period, cost savings, efficiency gains measured in dollar terms)
- LinkedIn retargeting: Serve EB at account (matched audience) a short customer quote video or 1-page insight — "CFO Perspective: What We Found After 90 Days With [Product]"

Week 3 — PEER VALIDATION & EXECUTIVE ENGAGEMENT:
- Executive event invitation: Invite EB to an exclusive executive-only virtual roundtable (5-8 CFOs from non-competitive companies, facilitated by your CFO or CEO, no sales pitch) — framed as industry peer conversation, not vendor event. If EB doesn't accept, pivot to recorded highlights sent via AE note
- Your CFO/CEO note: If deal is $150K+ ACV, your CMO or CFO sends a personal 1-paragraph note to EB — reference a shared industry challenge, include one relevant data point, and offer a 15-minute peer conversation, NOT a sales call. Written by marketing, sent from exec's email

Week 4-5 — DECISION SUPPORT:
- ROI Calculator link (personalized): Send EB a link to an interactive ROI calculator pre-populated with their company's estimated metrics (pulled from AE notes in CRM or publicly available data) — so they can see their own numbers, not generic benchmarks
- Board-ready deck template: Provide champion a template deck specifically designed for EB/board presentation — "How [Product] Pays for Itself in [Payback Period] and Why Delaying Costs [Quantified Monthly Cost of Inaction]"

PHASE 5 — EVALUATION CLOSE SPRINT (Days 30-45 for a 45-day eval):

Marketing-led urgency and decision-acceleration program for the final 10-15 days:

CLOSE SPRINT TRIGGER: Activated when evaluation end date is within 15 days AND deal has not moved to Closed Won stage.

WEEK 1 OF SPRINT (Days 30-37):
- Champion close readiness assessment: Email or Slack to AE — "15 days until [Account] evaluation ends. Quick check-in: Does the champion have the internal business case built? Have they presented to the EB? What's the plan for the final two weeks? Marketing can activate any of the following: [menu of 5 assets — reference call, ROI calculator, competitive comparison, executive note, urgency email template]. Reply with which to deploy."
- Reference call activation: Proactively book a reference call with 1-2 customer champions and/or economic buyers at similar companies — send the confirmed reference call invite directly to champion with suggested attendees to bring (including the EB if possible)
- Urgency email to champion: From AE — "Two weeks until we wrap the formal evaluation. Here's the 10-day plan to make the decision process smooth for your team and leadership: [list of specific actions and their owners]"

WEEK 2 OF SPRINT (Days 37-45):
- EB final value brief: AE sends EB a one-page "Evaluation Summary Brief" — written by marketing from champion's POC notes and AE update — 3 paragraphs: what was evaluated and proven, the financial case in their numbers, and the recommended next step with implementation timeline
- Peer urgency play: Champion receives a quote from a peer champion at a reference customer: "Here's what [Name, same title] said about waiting to move forward: [specific quote about the cost of delay or the outcome they achieved quickly post-go-live]"
- Pricing incentive (if approved by sales leadership): If deal includes a commercial incentive for signing before evaluation end, deploy a brief one-pager on the specific savings/value of acting within the evaluation window — NOT a discount announcement, a ROI-framing of why now is financially optimal
- Executive sponsor activation: For $100K+ ACV deals, your CRO or CEO makes a personal outreach to the EB in the final 7 days — a brief phone call or personal note offering to answer any remaining strategic questions peer-to-peer

EVALUATION DECISION DEADLINE MANAGEMENT:
- If evaluation end date passes without a decision: Trigger 30-day "decision pending" nurture sequence to champion with monthly value reinforcement content (new customer case study, product update relevant to their use case, ROI benchmark update)
- If evaluation closes lost: Trigger 6-month re-engagement sequence to champion (not EB) with quarterly value update — "Here's what other [industry] companies achieved with [Product] in Q[X]" — minimum cadence, maximum relevance, keeping the door open for the next evaluation trigger

## Success Metrics

**Evaluation Engagement Metrics (Input Quality):**
- Evaluation Health Score (EHS): Target average EHS > 70 across all active evaluations by Day 21 within 60 days of program launch
- Economic buyer contact rate: % of active evaluations where the EB persona has received at least 1 marketing touch by Day 14 (target: 80%+)
- Champion enablement asset adoption: % of champions who open and use at least 2 items from the champion toolkit (target: 75%+ — if lower, simplify delivery or change the assets)
- Daily evaluator engagement continuity: % of evaluations where evaluators log product activity at least once per week throughout the evaluation (target: >85%)

**Conversion Metrics (Revenue Impact):**
- Eval-to-close conversion rate: Overall conversion rate of POC/Trial stage deals to Closed Won — measure monthly, target +15-25 percentage points above pre-program baseline within 2 quarters
- Eval-to-close rate by EHS tier: Track conversion by EHS score at Day 21 — this is the leading indicator that validates whether your scoring model predicts real outcomes
- Average evaluation length: Days from POC stage entry to Closed Won decision — target 10-20% reduction on deals where close sprint is activated vs. control group
- No-decision/ghost rate: % of evaluations that end without any decision (neither closed-won nor closed-lost) — target reduction from baseline by 30%+ with 30-day decision-pending nurture activated

**Marketing Attribution Metrics (Proof of Contribution):**
- Marketing-sourced economic buyer engagement: % of won evaluations where EB had at least 1 attributed marketing touch before decision — report quarterly to CRO/CFO as "marketing-assisted close" metric
- Champion enablement asset influence: % of won evaluations where champion reported using the business case template or objection response guide (track via Highspot/Seismic view analytics and AE win report surveys)
- Competitive defense activation rate and outcome: When competitive defense protocol was activated, what % of deals still closed won vs. the overall average? — if higher, this is your proof that the protocol works

## Integration Tips

**Salesforce CRM:**
- Create a custom Salesforce field `evaluation_health_score__c` (Number) on the Opportunity object — update via weekly scheduled Marketo program or RevOps Flow, updated whenever any of the 6 EHS sub-components changes
- Create a `pov_end_date__c` field (Date) on Opportunity — used to trigger all date-relative marketing sequences in Marketo (milestone reminders, close sprint activation, decision-pending enrollment)
- Build a "POC Pipeline Health" Salesforce report showing all POC/Trial stage deals with their EHS score, EB contact status, champion asset usage count, and days remaining in evaluation — run this in your weekly pipeline review with sales leadership
- Create a Salesforce Campaign for each evaluation marketing program phase (POC Kickoff, EB Activation, Close Sprint) — use Salesforce Campaign Influence to attribute marketing touches to Closed Won revenue from evaluation-period marketing

## Troubleshooting

**Problem 1: AEs don't update the opportunity stage to POC/Trial consistently — some deals are in POC for weeks before marketing knows about it.**
Fix: This is a process enforcement problem. Three solutions in order of effectiveness: (1) Add `pov_start_date` as a required field when moving to POC/Trial stage in Salesforce — this forces AEs to complete it and gives marketing the correct date for sequences; (2) Have RevOps create a Salesforce report of all active evaluation opportunities updated more than 7 days ago and run it in weekly pipeline reviews — AEs who haven't updated lose visibility, which is a social incentive; (3) Configure a Salesforce validation rule that prevents Closed Won stage entry if the deal never moved through POC/Trial — this prevents the stage being skipped entirely.

**Problem 2: Champion toolkit assets are deployed but champion reports they don't have time to use them — they're managing the POC technically and can't do the internal selling too.**
Fix: The kit is too complex or being delivered all at once. Restructure to: (1) Deliver ONE asset at a time, at the moment it's most useful — not a bundle on Day 1; (2) Invest 30 minutes with the AE to co-present the top 2 assets to the champion on a live call — walking through what they are and when to use them dramatically increases adoption; (3) Offer to draft the internal business case FOR the champion using information they provide in a 20-minute call — marketing does the work, champion presents it. This "ghostwriting" approach has the highest conversion impact and lowest champion burden.

**Problem 3: Economic buyer never responds to LinkedIn ads, email, or executive outreach — the EB is completely invisible until they kill the deal at the end.**
Fix: This is common in organizations where the CFO/EB is deeply shielded. Escalate through three channels: (1) Champion-mediated delivery — provide champion with a 1-paragraph email + the EB content asset and ask them to forward it personally from their own inbox with a personalized note ("Sandra — I thought this was useful context for our project"); (2) Event-based meeting — invite the EB to a physical executive dinner or exclusive roundtable where the meeting happens naturally in a non-sales context; (3) Accept the constraint and invest all energy in giving the champion such a compelling, data-rich presentation that the EB approves without needing to be engaged personally — this means the champion's final presentation to the EB must be so turnkey that the champion can present it in 15 minutes and answer every likely CFO question with the data already in the deck. Design the champion toolkit around this reality.
