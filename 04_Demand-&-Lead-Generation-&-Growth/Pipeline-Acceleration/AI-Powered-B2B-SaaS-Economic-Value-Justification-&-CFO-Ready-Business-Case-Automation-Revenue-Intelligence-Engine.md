## Overview
Automates the creation of personalized, CFO-grade economic value justification packages — including ROI models, total cost of ownership analyses, and value realization roadmaps — for every active enterprise deal in your pipeline. Use this when deals stall at the economic buyer stage, when champions tell you "finance needs to approve this," or when your AEs are spending 10+ hours manually building business cases that are still losing on financial rigor.

**CFO Objection 2: "We won't actually reduce headcount — we'll just redeploy people."**

Response: "I've intentionally included every cost in this model including your internal implementation resource time, estimated at [N] hours × $[rate] = $[amount]. - Use Gong deal intelligence to auto-populate "CFO concerns stated on call" field in the Business Case object — so the CFO narrative is personalized to what they actually said

**HubSpot / Marketo Integration:**
- Map the Business Case delivery as a milestone in the deal stage marketing automation workflow
- Trigger a "Business Case Delivered" nurture sequence to the economic buyer: a 3-touch sequence of third-party validation content (analyst reports, customer results, peer benchmarks) timed 3/7/14 days post-delivery

- Publish business case templates as guided selling playbooks in your sales enablement platform — AEs configure inputs in a wizard interface that generates the formatted CFO package as a PDF

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Prospects refuse to share financial data needed to build the model.**
Fix: Lead with the benchmark-only version of the model — "Based on companies your size and industry, here's what we typically see." Then offer to refine it together: "If you can share your actual invoice volume and AP team size, we can make this much more accurate for your situation." The prospect's desire for accuracy typically overcomes their reluctance to share data when they see the draft model first.

**Problem: CFO dismisses the model as "marketing math" without engaging.**
Fix: This almost always means the model uses generic language instead of the CFO's specific words. Go back to your Gong transcripts — find the exact language the CFO or champion used to describe the problem. Rebuild the executive summary using their vocabulary. A CFO who said "we need to close the books 3 days faster" should see "3-day faster close" as the headline benefit, not "AP efficiency improvement."

**Problem: AEs aren't using the business case system — they're still building their own.**
Fix: This is a process design problem, not a quality problem. Identify the two or three AEs with the highest win rates and have them co-present a business case they used to close a deal. Use their peer credibility to drive adoption. Also audit whether the template actually saves time — if it still takes 4+ hours, redesign the data capture flow to pull from Salesforce automatically.
