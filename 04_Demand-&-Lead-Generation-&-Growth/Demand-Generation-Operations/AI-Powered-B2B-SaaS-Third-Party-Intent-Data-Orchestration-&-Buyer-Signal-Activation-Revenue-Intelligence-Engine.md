## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Intent signals are firing but SDRs aren't using them — the alert goes to Slack and gets ignored**
Solution: The channel is wrong, not the signal. SDRs live in their sales engagement platform (Outreach/Salesloft), not Slack. Push the intent context directly into the account record in Salesforce and into the sequence email as a pre-populated variable. Better yet, auto-enroll the contact in the appropriate intent sequence the moment the signal fires — SDRs shouldn't need to do anything except execute the call steps. Intent data that requires a human to "go look at the account" and "decide to do something" will always be deprioritized against the next demo to book. Make the system do the work.

**Problem: Bombora signals are firing on irrelevant accounts — high false positive rate inflating the "HOT" bucket**
Solution: Bombora Company Surge is topic-based, not buyer-based — it detects content consumption at the company level, which means a single employee reading a blog post can trigger a surge. Tighten your activation logic by requiring: (1) surge score ≥75 (not the default 60), (2) surge sustained for ≥5 days (not a single spike), AND (3) at least one additional corroborating signal (G2 view, job posting, or first-party visit). This "signal stacking" requirement reduces false positives by 40-60% while retaining the strongest true signals.

**Problem: Intent program launched but no measurable pipeline impact after 60 days**
Solution: The issue is usually one of three things: (1) activation speed — if it takes more than 48 hours for an SDR to contact a hot account, the signal window has closed and you're calling someone who already chose a competitor; (2) messaging mismatch — intent signals tell you *what topic* someone is researching, not the business problem driving it; your outreach needs to lead with the problem (e.g., "rapid headcount growth creating performance review chaos"), not the topic ("performance management software"); (3) contact coverage — if you only have 1 contact per account and that person isn't the buyer, the signal is wasted; use Clay or ZoomInfo to ensure you have 3-5 contacts across buying roles at every HOT account before activation.
