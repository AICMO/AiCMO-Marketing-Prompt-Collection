## Troubleshooting

**Problem: I don't have all the funnel metrics — large data gaps.**
Solution: Use the Quick Copy-Paste version with whatever data you have and explicitly mark unknown stages as "[Unknown — estimating]". The AI will use industry benchmarks to fill gaps and clearly flag which findings are benchmark-derived vs. data-derived. Start tracking missing metrics immediately using HubSpot's built-in funnel reports or Salesforce stage history.

**Problem: The work-backwards model shows I need 300% more MQLs — the gap seems impossibly large.**
Solution: This is the most common output for companies running below target. Instead of trying to close the entire gap via volume, ask the AI to run a Conversion Rate Sensitivity Analysis: "What if I improved each conversion rate by 5 percentage points — how much does that reduce the required MQL volume?" Often a combination of 10-15% volume increase + moderate conversion rate improvements closes a 50-60% pipeline gap. Reframe the sprint around conversion + moderate volume growth.

**Problem: Sales team disputes the funnel data or rejects the audit findings.**
Solution: Run the prompt again with an explicit RevOps alignment framing: add "AUDIENCE: This output will be reviewed jointly by the VP Marketing and VP Sales — frame all findings in shared revenue language, not marketing metrics language. For each stage conversion issue, present it as a joint marketing + sales problem with shared ownership." This generates language that reduces defensive reactions and positions the waterfall audit as a mutual tool, not a marketing blame assignment.
