- Use Clay to auto-enrich partner overlap accounts with: LinkedIn employee count changes, recent job postings (compliance/security hiring = buying signal), technology install data from BuiltWith

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Partners won't share their customer lists — no overlap data available**
Solution: Start without Crossbeam by building a manual "tech stack enrichment" table. Use Clay + BuiltWith to identify which ICP prospects have your partners' technologies installed. This gives you a proxy for "likely partner customer" without requiring partner consent. In parallel, pitch your top 3 partners on Crossbeam with the message: "I want to share our customer list with you so your AEs can see warm co-sell opportunities. All data is anonymized until both sides confirm the match." Focus on the value to them first—their AEs get warmer deals.

**Problem: AEs don't follow the ELG playbook — they revert to cold outreach templates**
Solution: Remove friction from adoption. Pre-build the outreach templates directly in Outreach/Salesloft with partner context pre-populated via CRM fields. Make the ELG template the *default* choice for partner-signal accounts by tagging those accounts in a filtered sequence view. Run a weekly "ELG Wins" spotlight in the sales team standup—share the AE who booked a meeting from partner signal + the conversion rate vs. their cold outbound. Social proof from peers converts faster than policy.

**Problem: Partner co-sell introductions feel transactional and prospects disengage**
Solution: Reframe the introduction from "let me connect you with a vendor" to "I want to show you how customers using both our tools are solving [specific problem]." The introduction email should lead with a specific customer outcome story, not a product pitch. Give the partner AM a pre-written introduction they can send in 30 seconds without needing to learn your product—the goal is to get one warm meeting, not to have partners close deals. Also: invest in co-created content (joint case studies, co-hosted events) so the relationship has established credibility before the co-sell ask.
