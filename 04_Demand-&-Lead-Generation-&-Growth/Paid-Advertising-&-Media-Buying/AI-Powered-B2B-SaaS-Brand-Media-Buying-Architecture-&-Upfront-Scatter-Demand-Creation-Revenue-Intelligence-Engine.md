Primary Measurement Framework: Continuous Brand Health Tracking

- Sample: 300-500 ICP-qualified respondents per wave
- Frequency: Quarterly (exposed vs. control group comparison)
- Control group: ICP respondents with verified non-exposure (geo exclusion or pixel suppression)

- Statistical significance threshold: 90% confidence minimum for reporting

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Brand lift studies showing no statistically significant awareness movement after 6 months**
Fix: First audit for effective frequency — if the average reached prospect has fewer than 6 exposures total, the campaign lacks minimum threshold for recall. Consolidate spend into fewer, higher-frequency placements rather than broad reach. Also check whether survey respondents are genuinely matching your ICP (low-quality panels can mask real lift among actual buyers).

**Problem: CTV CPMs are 40% above contracted rate at mid-campaign**
Fix: This signals audience overdelivery or inventory scarcity in your priority segments. First, invoke the contracted makegood clause with the platform. Then, shift 20-30% of remaining CTV budget to FAST channels (Pluto TV, Tubi) where CPMs are $20-35 vs. $60-80 for premium. Maintain frequency caps to protect brand experience while reducing unit cost.

**Problem: Scatter reserve being spent reactively without clear ROI signal**
Fix: Establish a "scatter trigger document" with pre-approved criteria before the campaign launches: specific competitor events, earned media thresholds, or awareness dip signals that justify each scatter deployment. Without triggers, scatter reserve devolves into unplanned spending with no measurement framework. Require each scatter buy to have a defined measurement hypothesis before execution.
