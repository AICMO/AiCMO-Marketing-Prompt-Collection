DELIVERABLE 1: WEBINAR TOPIC INTELLIGENCE & 90-DAY PROGRAM CALENDAR

Design a 90-day webinar calendar with a funnel-stage-balanced topic mix.

A) Topic Selection Framework

Use the following criteria to identify high-conversion webinar topics:

Awareness Stage (Month 1 — top-of-funnel, new audience acquisition):
Topic formula: "[Industry trend or alarming stat] + What [ICP title] need to do about it in [year]"
Audience target: Net new contacts not in CRM, early-stage researchers
Registration goal: High volume (200+ registrants); attendee-to-MQL conversion target 30-40%
Example structure: "Why 67% of [ICP companies] Are Losing Revenue to [Pain Point] — and the 3-Step Fix"

Consideration Stage (Month 2 — mid-funnel, active evaluators):
Topic formula: "How [peer company or role] [achieved specific outcome] using [methodology/product category]"
Audience target: Active database nurture, open pipeline deals (especially stalled at discovery stage)
Registration goal: Moderate volume (80-150), higher quality; attendee-to-opportunity conversion target 15-25%
Include a live product walkthrough segment (15 min max) with a customer speaker framing value before the demo

Decision Stage (Month 3 — bottom-of-funnel, late-stage acceleration):
Topic formula: "Live Q&A: Ask [Customer Name] How They [Specific Outcome] in [Timeframe]"
Audience target: Late-stage opportunities, buying committee members on active deals, champion nurture
Registration goal: Small, highly targeted (40-80 registrants); attendee-to-next-step conversion 40-60%
Use this format to enable peer selling without a sales pitch — customer speaks 70% of the time

B) 90-Day Webinar Calendar Output Format

For each of the 3 webinars, deliver:
- Exact webinar title (A/B test two options for registration page)
- Funnel stage + audience composition target (% new prospect / % nurture / % active pipeline)
- Speaker lineup: ideal internal speaker + ideal customer speaker + optional external thought leader
- 3 pre-webinar promotional hooks (LinkedIn posts, email teasers, partner amplification)
- Key poll questions (3 per webinar) mapped to qualification criteria
- CTA architecture: what you ask attendees to do during, immediately after, and 72 hours post-event
- Pipeline attribution expectation: sourced pipeline target and influenced pipeline target per webinar

C) Evergreen On-Demand Webinar Engine

Design one always-on webinar (pre-recorded, gated, SEO-optimized) that captures high-intent organic visitors 24/7.

Topic selection criteria: Target a keyword with >500 monthly searches that indicates active evaluation (e.g., "[Product category] comparison", "How to solve [pain point]", "[Competitor] alternative")
Gate configuration: Name + Work Email + Company + "What's your biggest challenge with [pain area]?" (this response auto-routes to the right sales sequence)
Replay nurture: Registrant who watches >70% of replay → scored as MQL → triggres 3-email "did you get what you needed?" sequence
Technical SEO: Title tag format = "[Pain Point] Webinar: [Outcome Statement] | [Brand]"; embed on a persistent /resources/webinars/ URL with updated replay embed

DELIVERABLE 2: REGISTRATION CONVERSION ARCHITECTURE

A) Landing Page Framework (target: >35% visitor-to-registrant conversion rate)

Headline formula (choose based on topic type):
- Stat-based: "X% of [ICP companies] Are [experiencing pain]. Here's How the Top Performers Fix It."
- Outcome-based: "How [Customer Company] [Achieved Outcome] in [Timeframe] — Live Walkthrough + Q&A"
- Urgency-based: "Join [X] [ICP titles] Learning [Topic] — [Date] Only, No Replay for 72 Hours"

Above-the-fold structure:
- Headline + 1-sentence description (what they'll learn + why now)
- Speaker credentials: Photo + Title + 1 credibility stat (e.g., "trained 10,000+ [ICP titles]" or "[X] years implementing [methodology]")
- 3-bullet "You'll walk away knowing:" — use specificity, not vague outcomes
- Registration form: First Name, Last Name, Work Email, Company, [1 optional qualification question]
- Social proof element: "[X] people registered" counter or "Attendees from [recognizable logos]"
- Time zone selector: Auto-detect timezone and display "Starts in X hours/days" based on visitor location

Below the fold:
- Detailed agenda with timestamps (builds confidence; reduces no-show rate)
- FAQ section addressing: "Will there be a recording?", "Can I submit questions in advance?", "Who else is attending?"
- Secondary CTA for visitors who won't register: "Download the [related asset] instead" (captures lead even if webinar timing doesn't work)

B) Promotional Email Sequence

Email 1 — 14 days before (Announcement):
Subject option A: "[First Name], [Customer Name] is joining us live on [Date] to share something most [ICP titles] get wrong"
Subject option B: "Seats are filling — [Webinar Title] | [Date]"
Body: Open with the counter-intuitive insight the webinar will reveal. Name the specific pain the webinar solves. Include speaker photo + credibility. Hard CTA: single button "Reserve My Seat."
Length: ≤120 words body, plus speaker bio block.

Email 2 — 7 days before (Value preview):
Subject: "What [Customer Name] said about [pain area] that surprised us"
Body: Give away one insight from the upcoming webinar. Frame it as a preview of what's coming. Add urgency: "[X] of [Y] seats claimed." CTA: "Join us live — [Date]."
Length: ≤100 words.

Email 3 — Day of (Urgency):
Subject: "It starts today at [Time] — here's your link, [First Name]"
Body: Single paragraph. Confirm the link, the time (with timezone), and what one thing they'll be able to do after. CTA: "Add to calendar / Join live."
Length: ≤60 words.

C) Show Rate Optimization Protocol (target: >55% show rate from registrants)

Industry average webinar show rate is 35-45%. Exceed it with:

1. Confirmation email immediately after registration:
   - Personalized subject: "You're registered — here's what to expect, [First Name]"
   - Include: calendar invite attachment (.ics), personal join link (never generic), one pre-work question ("What's the biggest challenge you're hoping we address? Reply to this email.")
   - Pre-work responses auto-log to CRM as a note on the contact and feed directly to the speaker for live Q&A personalization

2. Reminder Email 1 — 24 hours before:
   Subject: "Tomorrow: [Webinar Title] — your questions are shaping the agenda"
   Body: Reference that pre-submitted questions are influencing the live session. Include 1-2 anonymized examples of questions already submitted. This creates social proof and investment.

3. Reminder Email 2 — 1 hour before:
   Subject: "Starting in 60 minutes — your personal link inside"
   Body: 3 sentences max. Unique join link. One sentence on what they'll leave with.

4. SMS Reminder — 10 minutes before (for registrants who opted in to SMS):
   "[First Name], [Webinar Title] starts in 10 min. Join here: [short link]"

5. LinkedIn DM to named target accounts (for ABM-priority registrants):
   Send manually or via LinkedIn automation: "Hey [Name] — you registered for [webinar] that starts in 5 minutes. Here's your direct join link if you need it: [link]. Looking forward to seeing you there."

DELIVERABLE 3: LIVE WEBINAR EXECUTION FRAMEWORK

A) 60-Minute Agenda Architecture

00:00-05:00 — WELCOME & SOCIAL PROOF
Host opens with housekeeping (recording, Q&A, polls) + social proof announcement: "We have [X] people joining today from [number] companies across [industries]." This immediately validates the event and reduces early drop-off.

05:00-10:00 — PAIN AMPLIFICATION (no product yet)
Host or practitioner speaker opens with the industry problem. Use a specific stat, a customer quote (anonymized if needed), or a 60-second story about a company facing this exact challenge. Goal: make every attendee feel seen. Prompt first poll here.

POLL 1 — Pain confirmation + segmentation:
"Which of these is your biggest challenge with [pain area] right now?"
- Option A: [Challenge 1 — maps to product use case 1]
- Option B: [Challenge 2 — maps to product use case 2]  
- Option C: [Challenge 3 — maps to product use case 3]
- Option D: "We haven't started addressing this yet" (flags early-stage prospect)
Results auto-sync to CRM field "Webinar Pain Signal" on contact record.

10:00-25:00 — METHODOLOGY / FRAMEWORK (the "how," without the product)
Deliver the framework or methodology that solves the problem. This section should be actionable even if the attendee never buys — it builds trust and positions the company as a true expert. Use a named framework (e.g., "The 3-Stage Pipeline Velocity Model", "The Buyer Readiness Score Framework").

25:00-40:00 — CUSTOMER STORY & LIVE WALK-THROUGH
Customer speaker shares their before/after story. Structure: situation → specific problem → what they tried that didn't work → why they chose [product] → results (use specific numbers: "$1.2M additional pipeline in Q1", "43% reduction in onboarding time"). After customer story: 5-minute live product demonstration of the exact feature that delivered the result.

POLL 2 — Timeline and intent signal:
"How soon are you looking to solve [pain area]?"
- Actively evaluating solutions now
- Planning to address this in next 6 months
- On the roadmap but no timeline yet
- Just researching / no project
Results auto-update contact's lifecycle stage in CRM and trigger immediate sales notification if "actively evaluating."

40:00-50:00 — LIVE Q&A
Pre-load 3 questions from pre-registration submissions (these are softballs you can answer well). Prioritize live questions that show commercial intent ("Do you integrate with X?", "What's the implementation timeline?", "How does pricing work?"). For competitor questions: "Great question — we actually have a comparison page that walks through that in detail. I'll drop the link in chat and our team can walk you through it 1:1."

POLL 3 — CTA qualification:
"What would be most valuable as a next step for you?"
- See a personalized demo (routes to sales immediately)
- Get a copy of the framework/template we shared (routes to nurture + content fulfillment)
- Talk to a customer reference (routes to champion referral + CS team intro)
- Just exploring for now (routes to long-term nurture sequence)
This poll acts as a self-qualification CTA that dramatically improves follow-up relevance.

50:00-58:00 — OFFER & CTA
Speaker makes the offer: "For anyone who wants to see this applied to their specific situation, we're offering [specific offer: e.g., a free pipeline audit, a 30-minute strategy session, a complimentary template customization] for attendees this week only." Create genuine scarcity: "We have capacity to do [X] of these in the next 14 days."

58:00-60:00 — CLOSE
Thank speakers. Remind attendees replay will be available in 24 hours. "Reply to the email we send in the next hour with any questions. Our team reads every response personally."

B) Real-Time High-Intent Signal Detection

Configure webinar platform + marketing automation to flag these signals LIVE for immediate sales action:
- Attendee answered Poll 2 as "Actively evaluating solutions now" → Slack alert to rep within 60 seconds
- Attendee submitted a question in Q&A containing "pricing," "demo," "trial," "integrate," or "contract" → flag in platform + email rep immediately
- Attendee stayed for >45 of 60 minutes + clicked any CTA link during session → "Hot Attendee" tag applied in CRM
- C-suite or economic buyer title in registration joins live → immediate Slack alert to account owner

DELIVERABLE 4: POST-WEBINAR PIPELINE CONVERSION ENGINE

A) 72-Hour Follow-Up Sequences (Segment by behavior)

SEGMENT 1 — Live Attendees (attended ≥30 minutes):
Email 1 (within 60 minutes of session end):
Subject: "Thanks for joining — your [framework/resource] is inside"
Body: Thank them for attending. Deliver the promised resource (template, checklist, or framework). Reference their poll answer: "Based on your earlier response about [Poll 1 answer], I thought [specific resource] might also be relevant." CTA: Calendar link for 1:1 if Poll 3 showed interest in demo/reference.

Email 2 (48 hours post-event):
Subject: "Quick question, [First Name]"
Body: "Did [specific topic from webinar] resonate with your situation? Our team put together a [personalized next step based on their poll 3 answer]. Curious whether [specific use case] is something you're actively working on this quarter."
This email triggers a reply-based conversation, not a form fill.

Email 3 (72 hours post-event):
Subject: "The replay is live — and one thing we didn't have time to cover"
Body: Replay link. Bonus: share 1 additional insight not covered in the live session. "Given what we discussed, I also wanted to share [related insight]. Worth 15 minutes to talk through how this applies to [their company/industry]?"

SEGMENT 2 — Registered No-Shows:
Email 1 (within 2 hours of session end):
Subject: "[First Name], you missed a good one — replay inside"
Body: Acknowledge they couldn't make it (no guilt). Deliver replay link. Include a 90-second summary of the 3 key takeaways. CTA: "If you watch it and want to discuss, book time here."

Email 2 (3 days later, if no replay engagement):
Subject: "The one stat that surprised even our customer speaker"
Body: Share one compelling data point from the webinar. Use curiosity gap: "Full context is in the replay, but this one thing [specific insight] changed how [customer name] thought about [problem]." Replay link again.

SEGMENT 3 — Organic Replay Viewers (on-demand):
Triggered when any new or existing contact watches ≥70% of replay:
Email 1 (within 15 minutes of completion — automated):
Subject: "You just finished watching [Webinar Title] — what questions came up?"
Body: "Great — most people who watch [title] have a question about [most common question]. [Answer in 2 sentences]. Ready to go deeper? [Calendar link for 20-minute call]"

B) Sales Follow-Up Intelligence Package

Automatically delivered to the assigned rep within 1 hour of webinar end for every attendee in their territory or pipeline:

For each contact, provide:
- Attendance summary: attended live or no-show, minutes watched, questions submitted
- Poll answer digest: their answers to all 3 polls (their pain signal, their timeline, their desired next step)
- Chat transcript excerpts: any questions or comments they made in chat
- Recommended first outreach: email or call, specific angle based on their behavior
- Suggested talk track opener: "I saw you asked about [their question] during the webinar — here's the fuller answer..."

C) Replay Distribution & Demand Capture Strategy

On-demand page configuration:
- URL: /resources/webinar-[topic-slug]/ (permanent, indexed by search)
- Title tag: "[Webinar Title]: Watch the Replay | [Brand]"
- Embed replay video with new gate (captures registrants who discover via search)
- Add "Was this helpful?" CTA beneath player: thumbs up/down → yes triggers demo CTA, no triggers "What were you hoping to learn?" open text (fed back to content team)
- Automatic LinkedIn retargeting: anyone who visits replay page gets added to LinkedIn retargeting audience served "Ready to go deeper?" ads for 30 days

DELIVERABLE 5: PIPELINE ATTRIBUTION & ROI MEASUREMENT MODEL

A) CRM Tagging Protocol

Opportunity Source Classification:
- "Webinar-Sourced": Webinar was the first touchpoint → contact registered before any prior CRM activity
- "Webinar-Influenced": Contact had prior CRM activity → webinar engagement occurred before or during active opportunity

Campaign Influence Tagging (multi-touch):
Log webinar engagement as a Campaign Member on the associated Salesforce Campaign or HubSpot Deal:
- Status = "Attended" (live ≥30 min), "Registered — No Show", "Watched Replay" (≥50%), "Watched Replay Partial"

Custom Contact Fields to populate automatically post-webinar:
- Last Webinar Attended: [Webinar Name]
- Last Webinar Date: [Date]
- Webinar Pain Signal: [Poll 1 answer]
- Webinar Timeline: [Poll 2 answer]
- Webinar Requested Next Step: [Poll 3 answer]
- Webinar Hot Attendee Flag: [Yes/No]

B) 90-Day Attribution Window

Pipeline influence measurement rules:
- Any opportunity created within 90 days of webinar attendance counts as "Webinar Influenced"
- Any opportunity where webinar was the first CRM touchpoint counts as "Webinar Sourced"
- Report on both: influenced pipeline overstates impact; sourced pipeline understates it. Truth lives between the two.

C) Monthly Webinar Program ROI Report

Calculate and report monthly:

| Metric | Formula | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Cost per registrant | Total promo spend / total registrants | <$25 (top-of-funnel) / <$75 (bottom-of-funnel) |
| Show rate | Live attendees / registrants | >55% |
| Hot attendee rate | Hot attendees / live attendees | >20% |
| Attendee-to-MQL rate | MQLs from webinar / live attendees | >25% |
| Cost per MQL | Total program cost / MQLs generated | <$150 |
| Webinar-influenced pipeline | Sum of opportunities with webinar touch | Report monthly |
| Webinar-sourced pipeline | Sum of opportunities sourced by webinar | Report monthly |
| Pipeline ROI | Influenced pipeline / total program cost | Target >10x |
| Closed revenue influenced | Closed-won with webinar touch / total program cost | Report quarterly |

CONSTRAINTS:
- Every follow-up sequence must be fully automated — no manual email sends, no rep intervention required for Segments 1, 2, and 3
- All poll data must auto-sync to CRM within 5 minutes of session end
- No sequence should exceed 3 emails before pausing for rep review
- Replay must be available within 24 hours of the live session ending
- Zero "spray and pray" promotion — every promotional email must be suppressed for contacts who are already in active pipeline (sales owns those conversations)

## Integration Tips

- **HubSpot Workflows**: Create 3 enrollment triggers post-webinar: (1) Contact attended live + Poll 2 = "Actively evaluating" → enroll in Hot Attendee sequence + notify rep via Slack integration; (2) Contact registered + did not attend → enroll in No-Show sequence 2 hours after session end; (3) Contact watched replay ≥70% (via Wistia/Vidyard embed tracking) → enroll in Replay Viewer sequence. Use HubSpot's Custom Events to log poll answers as properties on the contact record — this enables segmentation and personalization at scale.

- **Salesforce Campaign Member Integration**: Sync every webinar registrant as a Campaign Member on the corresponding Salesforce Campaign with a status field reflecting their engagement level. Configure Campaign Influence (Salesforce) to credit the webinar Campaign for any opportunity created within 90 days of membership. Use Salesforce Reports to pull "Webinar Influenced Pipeline" as a standard weekly metric in your revenue review.

- **Goldcast / ON24 / Demio**: Use native CRM integrations to push engagement data (minutes watched, polls answered, questions asked, links clicked) directly to HubSpot or Salesforce within 5 minutes of session end. Configure Hot Attendee Alerts as native Slack notifications through the platform's webhook or Zapier integration — do not rely on manual CSV exports. Set up automated replay page creation with individual contact tracking links so every replay view ties back to a specific contact record.

- **LinkedIn Campaign Manager**: Upload your webinar registrant list as a Matched Audience to suppress them from the next webinar's promotion campaign (they're already registered). Simultaneously, upload attendees who haven't yet booked a demo as a retargeting audience for "Next step" creative: "You attended [Webinar Title] — here's the 1:1 walkthrough." Set up a Conversions event on the demo booking confirmation page to optimize LinkedIn campaigns toward high-intent post-webinar actions, not just registrations.

- **Zapier / Make.com**: Build a Zap that fires when a contact submits a webinar registration → checks if the contact has an open Opportunity in Salesforce → if yes, notifies the Account Owner immediately via Slack so they can send a personal invite reinforcement message. This prevents pipeline contacts from experiencing generic webinar marketing while a deal is active.

- **Vidyard / Wistia (Replay Hosting)**: Host the replay on a branded video platform (not YouTube) to capture individual viewer identity and track watch time at the contact level. Set a 50% and 70% completion webhook to trigger CRM activity logs and sequence enrollment. This turns your replay page into an always-on lead scoring engine.
