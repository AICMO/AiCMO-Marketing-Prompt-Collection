## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Community has low engagement (MAU < 15%) despite decent member count**
Fix: You likely have a "ghost town" dynamic caused by lack of value density. Audit the last 30 days of content — if fewer than 5% of posts received responses within 24 hours, you have a response problem, not a content problem. Implement a "seeded question" program where Community Manager posts 3 strategic questions per week targeting top-10 contributors personally. Expect MAU to increase 8-12 percentage points within 60 days.

**Problem: Unable to attribute pipeline to community because CRM and community platform don't share user IDs**
Fix: Add email address capture at community onboarding (required field) and implement a matching layer: export community emails weekly → match against CRM contacts → tag matched contacts with community membership date. Use HubSpot's deduplication API or a Clay table as the matching layer. Retroactively match last 12 months of community members to closed-won deals to establish baseline attribution.

**Problem: Champions burn out or go quiet after initial activation**
Fix: Champion programs fail when the value exchange becomes one-sided (company extracts, member doesn't receive). Audit benefit delivery — are champions actually getting: early product access, direct PM time, career-advancing exposure, peer network value? Implement a quarterly champion NPS survey (target >70). If score drops below 60, schedule 1:1 conversations with 5 churned champions to identify root cause. Most common issues: over-solicitation for references, no closed loop on feedback, peer community not active enough.
