## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Your brand appears in AI answers but always in a secondary or negative context (e.g., "Some users find [Brand] difficult to implement").**
Solution: This indicates AI engines are weighting negative review content or competitor-created comparison content over your owned assets. Audit which specific sources AI engines are citing for those negative framings — use Perplexity's citation links to identify the source.
