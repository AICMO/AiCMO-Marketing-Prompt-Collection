Definition: AI engines don't have sufficient confidence in your brand's authority in the category, so they default to citing established players.

Definition: A competitor has invested in content your brand hasn't — specifically, authoritative definitive guides, original research reports, or heavily-cited comparison pages.
Evidence: The same competitor's URL appears as a Perplexity source for 5+ different queries.
Remediation: Audit the competitor's highest-cited content assets → produce superior versions with more specificity, better structure, newer data, and your brand's unique proof points → execute a PR/syndication push to earn third-party links to your version.

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: AI answers vary significantly between sessions for the same query — monitoring results feel inconsistent**
Solution: This is expected. AI models produce stochastic outputs — the same query can return different answers. Standardize by submitting each query 3 times per platform per monitoring session and recording the modal result (most common answer). If answers differ substantially 2 out of 3 times, the query is "contested" — note it as high-priority because small GEO improvements can swing contested queries decisively.

**Problem: We improved SOM on Perplexity but ChatGPT still doesn't mention us — why?**
Solution: Perplexity is real-time web-grounded; ChatGPT's base model uses training data with a knowledge cutoff. Perplexity improvements can happen in weeks; ChatGPT base model changes reflect training cycles that happen over months. To accelerate ChatGPT improvement: (1) use ChatGPT with browsing enabled for monitoring — this uses Bing Search and responds to your content faster; (2) focus on earning citations in high-authority indexed publications (TechCrunch, G2, analyst reports) rather than only your own domain; (3) accept that ChatGPT base model is a 6-12 month GEO play, not a 30-day sprint.

**Problem: Leadership wants to know GEO ROI but we can't directly attribute pipeline to AI search**
Solution: Use a triangulation approach in your CMO report: (1) Self-reported AI leads (direct signal, undercounts actual), (2) Direct traffic growth to GEO content correlated with SOM improvements (indirect signal), (3) Market research — cite industry data that X% of your ICP uses AI tools for vendor research and apply that to your inbound mix as an AI search probability estimate. This three-signal approach builds credibility even before you have perfect attribution infrastructure.
