## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Sponsored answer CTR is below 1% across all query clusters.**
Solution: The sponsored answer is likely front-loading brand messaging instead of genuinely answering the buyer's question. Restructure so the first 60% of the answer is purely educational — the buyer clicked an AI answer for information, not an ad. Only introduce brand in the final 40% after trust is established. Test removing all explicit brand mentions from problem-aware queries entirely and relying on CTA destination for brand exposure.

**Problem: Can't attribute AI search traffic in CRM — leads show up as "direct" or "unknown."**
Solution: Implement UTM parameters on every sponsored answer CTA link (`utm_source=perplexity&utm_medium=sponsored-answer&utm_campaign=[query-cluster]&utm_term=[query-intent-stage]`). Additionally, add a hidden form field that captures the landing page URL including UTM parameters, ensuring attribution survives even if the browser strips referrer data. For Perplexity specifically, their click tracking may add a redirect — test UTM persistence in your analytics before launching.

**Problem: Platform policies reject sponsored answer creative as "promotional" or "misleading."**
Solution: AI answer engine advertising has stricter editorial standards than traditional display. Ensure every factual claim has a citation-ready source (even if not shown in the ad, have it available for review). Remove superlatives ("best," "leading," "#1") unless backed by third-party ranking. Reframe from product promotion to category expertise — your sponsored answer should read like content from a trusted analyst first, product mention second.
