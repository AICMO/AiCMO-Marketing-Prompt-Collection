## Overview

Designs a production-ready AI-orchestrated LinkedIn social selling system that monitors buyer intent signals — job changes, post engagement, company funding rounds, hiring surges, technology stack changes, and content interaction — and converts them into personalized, conversational LinkedIn outreach sequences that feel human, create genuine value, and book pipeline without cold-pitching. Use this when your outbound email response rates have plateaued and you need a scalable channel that reaches buyers where they already spend time and trust their network.

Design a complete buyer signal taxonomy. For each of the 8 signal categories, define the trigger, the intelligence it reveals, the personalization hook it unlocks, the optimal outreach timing window, and the priority tier.

SIGNAL TIER 1 — HIGHEST INTENT (Immediate outreach within 24–48 hours)

**Signal 1: Job Change into Buying Role**
Trigger: Target persona (e.g., VP Finance) joins an ICP account in a role with budget authority
Intelligence revealed: New executives typically make 2–3 technology decisions in their first 90 days; they're actively evaluating inherited tech stack and building their own vendor relationships
Personalization hook: Reference their new role, congratulate authentically, position yourself as a resource during their evaluation window — not a vendor
Timing: Outreach within 48 hours of the LinkedIn "started new position" notification
Priority: Tier 1 — highest conversion rate of all signals (new job = new evaluation = new budget)

Sales Navigator trigger: "Job change" alert on saved lead who has moved to ICP account
CRM action: Auto-create contact record in Salesforce/HubSpot, tag as "Job Change — High Priority," assign to territory AE

Connection Request Note (300 characters):
"Congrats on the new role at [Company], [First Name] — the move from [Previous Company] to leading [Function] at a [Company size] company is a big one. Happy to connect as you settle in. No agenda — just good to build the network."

Touch 2 — Value message (Day 3 after connection):
"[First Name] — one thing I've heard from other [Title]s coming into roles like yours is that the first 60 days involve a lot of inherited-system reviews. Happy to share a quick framework we put together on [relevant evaluation topic] if that's useful — no strings. Just DM me 'yes' if you want it."

Touch 3 — Soft ask (Day 8):
"[First Name] — a quick question. In your first few weeks at [Company], is [pain category your product solves] on your radar, or more of a Q3/Q4 problem? Asking because we just wrapped up a project with a similar company in [industry] and the parallels might be interesting. Happy to share context if timing makes sense."

**Signal 2: Prospect Publishes Pain-Point Content**
Trigger: ICP prospect publishes a LinkedIn post describing a problem your product solves — e.g., a CFO posts about the pain of month-end close taking 12 days
Intelligence revealed: Prospect is experiencing the problem now, has enough frustration to write publicly about it, and is likely evaluating solutions or at minimum receptive to validation
Personalization hook: Reference their specific post, add a perspective that extends their thinking — do not immediately say "we solve that"
Timing: Comment on the post within 4 hours, then send connection request same day, then follow up with DM within 48 hours

Comment Strategy (do this BEFORE connecting):
Write a substantive 3–5 sentence comment that validates their point, adds a data point or counterintuitive angle, and asks a follow-up question. This creates social proof before the DM arrives.

Example comment structure: "[Insightful first sentence that agrees with their premise] — [Add a specific data point, e.g., 'we saw this in a recent analysis of 47 finance teams where...'] — [Ask a question that shows you understand the nuance, e.g., 'Curious whether for your team the bottleneck is the reconciliation process or the reporting layer after close?']"

Connection Request Note (post-comment):
"[First Name] — loved your post on [topic]. My comment probably gave away that we've been thinking about this problem too. Would love to connect — no pitch coming, just genuinely interested in the conversation."

Touch 2 — Deeper value (Day 4):
"[First Name] — following up on your post. We actually ran a benchmark study on [their pain point] across [X] companies in [their industry] — the finding that surprised most people was [counterintuitive insight]. Happy to send the full report if useful. No form required — just say the word."

Touch 3 — Soft ask (Day 9):
"[First Name] — if you're still working through the [pain point] problem, I'd be happy to share how [peer company in same industry] approached it. 20-minute call, no deck. Would that be worth 20 minutes this week or next?"

**Signal 3: Company Funding Announcement**
Trigger: ICP target account announces Series B, C, or growth equity funding in LinkedIn news or Crunchbase
Intelligence revealed: New capital = immediate budget expansion + technology investment sprint + hiring acceleration. Decision-makers are in active build-out mode and evaluating vendors in priority categories.
Personalization hook: Congratulate on the milestone, reference what the funding will likely be used for (if press release mentions it), position as a category the funding investment should include
Timing: Outreach within 72 hours of announcement — this window closes fast as every vendor in your category will reach out

Connection Request Note:
"[First Name] — congrats on the [funding round] — big milestone for [Company]. The press release mentioned [specific use from release, e.g., 'scaling the finance team'] which is exactly where companies like yours typically run into [pain category]. Would love to connect as you build out."

Touch 2 — Strategic framing (Day 5):
"[First Name] — a quick observation from working with [2–3 companies] who went through similar growth stages after [Series B/C]: the [pain category] problem tends to compound faster than expected at [their revenue stage]. Happy to share a quick framework on where to prioritize if useful — no cost, just a good conversation."

Touch 3 — Social proof ask (Day 10):
"[First Name] — one of our customers, [Company in similar stage/industry], went through a [funding round equivalent] 18 months ago and the [pain area] was one of the first things they locked in post-raise. I could connect you directly if hearing it from them would be more useful than hearing it from me. Worth a quick conversation?"

SIGNAL TIER 2 — MEDIUM INTENT (Outreach within 3–5 days)

**Signal 4: Hiring Surge in Buyer's Function**
Trigger: ICP account posts 5+ open roles in the prospect's department within a 30-day window
Intelligence revealed: Rapid headcount growth creates operational complexity in exactly the department your product serves — hiring more people into a broken process amplifies the pain
Personalization hook: Reference the growth you noticed, frame it as a complexity challenge they're about to face, position your product as infrastructure that scales with growth

Sales Navigator filter: Account alert for "Senior Hires" or use LinkedIn company page job tab monitoring + Zapier → Slack notification

Connection Request Note:
"[First Name] — noticed [Company] is hiring [X] [roles] right now. Growing that fast usually means [pain your product solves] becomes a top priority fast. Would love to connect — a lot of context on how similar teams have handled this at scale."

**Signal 5: Prospect Engages with Competitor or Category Content**
Trigger: Target prospect likes, comments on, or shares a competitor's LinkedIn content, or engages with industry analyst content about your category
Intelligence revealed: Active research behavior — they're evaluating the category right now
Personalization hook: Do not reference that you saw them engage with a competitor (feels invasive). Instead, reach out with a category-level insight that positions you as a knowledgeable peer in the space they're researching.

Comment strategy: Engage with the same content thread with a substantive perspective, then connect

Connection Request Note:
"[First Name] — been following the conversation around [category topic] lately and your perspective in [community/group] stood out. Building connections with [titles] who are thinking seriously about this — would love to connect."

**Signal 6: Active in Relevant LinkedIn Group or Community**
Trigger: Prospect is active (posting, commenting) in a LinkedIn Group focused on your ICP's core pain — e.g., a CFO in the "Financial Close & Reporting Leaders" group
Intelligence revealed: Self-selected community member = high topic relevance; active participation = open to peer exchange; easier warm entry point than cold outreach
Personalization hook: Reference a specific thing they said in the group, engage there first, then connect as a peer member

Connection Request Note:
"[First Name] — fellow [Group Name] member. Your comment on [specific thread topic] last week resonated — [one sentence showing you read it]. Would love to connect."

DAILY EXECUTION SEQUENCE (Total: 55–60 minutes)

**Block 1: Signal Review (15 minutes)**
What to do:
- Open Sales Navigator → Saved Leads → Filter "Alerts" for job changes, profile views, mentions — review all new alerts from past 24 hours
- Open CRM → Intent data tab (6sense/Bombora) → Flag any accounts showing LinkedIn-correlated intent surges
- Check LinkedIn company page notifications — who commented, who followed, who engaged with recent company posts
- Quick-scan target accounts' LinkedIn pages for any company news published in last 48 hours
- Priority list: Flag top 10 prospects with active signals for outreach today

Output: 10 prioritized prospects ranked by signal strength; 3–5 prospects moving to Tier 1 (send today), 5–7 moving to Touch 2/3 sequences

**Block 2: Content Engagement Before Outreach (10 minutes)**
Why: Commenting on a prospect's post before connecting increases connection acceptance rate by 30–40% because you appear in their notifications as a non-stranger

What to do:
- Identify 3–5 prospects who posted content in the last 48 hours
- Write one substantive comment per post (3–5 sentences minimum, with a specific perspective or question — not "Great post!")
- Comment Rule: Never mention your company or product in a comment. Earn the relationship first.
- After commenting, send connection request same day with a note referencing the specific post

**Block 3: Message Drafting with AI (20 minutes)**
What to do:
- For each of today's 10 prioritized prospects, open their LinkedIn profile + paste context into AI tool (Claude, GPT-4, or Lavender)
- Use this prompt structure to generate message drafts:

AI DRAFTING PROMPT TEMPLATE:
"You are a B2B social selling expert writing LinkedIn messages for [Rep Name] at [Company]. We sell [product description] to [ICP description]. Today's prospect is [First Name Last Name], [Title] at [Company]. They triggered the following signal: [signal type and specific details]. Their most recent LinkedIn post was about: [topic and key point]. Write a Touch 1 connection request note (max 300 characters, no pitch, reference the specific signal) and a Touch 2 value message (2–3 short paragraphs, share a relevant insight or resource, soft question at the end, no call-to-action for a meeting yet). Tone: direct, confident, peer-to-peer. Do not use the words 'hope,' 'reach out,' 'touch base,' or 'synergy.' Do not compliment them generically."

- Review AI output: Edit first line to match your authentic voice; verify it references specific details correctly; cut any generic language
- Queue messages in LinkedIn automation tool (Expandi/Dux-Soup/Waalaxy) or schedule natively in Sales Navigator if using Advanced Plus

**Block 4: Conversation Management (10 minutes)**
What to do:
- Check LinkedIn message inbox for all replies received in past 24 hours
- Respond to every reply within 24 hours (LinkedIn rewards conversation velocity with greater reach)
- Response decision tree:
  - Positive reply / interest expressed → Move to meeting booking flow (see Module 4)
  - Request for more info → Send specific, relevant resource (1 link or PDF) + ask one clarifying question
  - "Not the right time" → "Understood — would it be OK to reconnect in Q[X]? Happy to put something on the calendar for a few months from now so it's not lost." Then set CRM reminder.
  - No reply after Touch 3 → Mark as "6-month nurture" in CRM; engage organically with their posts for 90 days; attempt reactivation via new signal if triggered

**Block 5: CRM Logging (5 minutes)**
- Log every connection request sent, every acceptance, every reply in Salesforce/HubSpot activity feed
- Create tasks for Touch 2 and Touch 3 timing based on acceptance date
- Update lead/contact record with LinkedIn conversation summary and current stage
- Flag any prospect who replied positively for AE review if this is SDR-run program

MODULE 4: PIPELINE CONVERSION PROTOCOL
═══════════════════════════════════════════════

Design the exact process for converting a LinkedIn conversation into a qualified pipeline opportunity.

CONVERSATION QUALIFICATION SIGNALS (when to move to meeting ask):

| Signal | What It Means | Your Move |
|--------|--------------|-----------|
| They describe a specific problem in detail | Active pain, not casual curiosity | Ask one qualifying question, then offer a 20-minute call framed around their specific situation |
| They ask about your product or company | Inbound interest — highest conversion moment | Respond warmly, give a 2-sentence answer, immediately offer a time to go deeper: "Would it be easier to show you live?" |
| They mention a timeline | Decision imminent | Move fast — offer 2 specific time slots in your message |
| They ask about pricing or case studies | Deep evaluation mode | "I'd rather walk you through the right numbers live so it's relevant to your situation — 20 minutes?" |
| They connect you with a colleague | Champion behavior | Thank them, immediately send connection request to colleague, restate the context |

THE HANDOFF MOMENT — Moving from DM to Calendar:

Do NOT send a Calendly link in a cold DM before they've shown interest. This signals "I want to pitch you" and kills trust.

Instead, use this protocol:
Step 1: First, confirm interest with a soft question: "Would it be worth a quick 20-minute call where I can [specific value proposition tailored to their signal]?"
Step 2: When they say yes (or express interest): Reply immediately with 2 specific time options — not a Calendly link yet. "I have Thursday at 10am or Friday at 2pm ET — which works better?" (This feels personal, not automated)
Step 3: When they pick a time: "Perfect — I'll send a calendar invite now. Quick question before we talk: [one pre-call qualifying question to determine if AE or SDR should own the call]."
Step 4: Send calendar invite via email (request their email in the DM) — this moves them out of LinkedIn and into CRM pipeline

COMMON DM OBJECTION SCRIPTS:

**"Not the right time right now"**
Response: "Totally understand — timing is everything with this. Can I ask what would need to change for this to become a priority? That way I know when to reconnect with something relevant rather than just checking in blind. And if it helps, I'm happy to share [specific resource] in the meantime — no agenda."

**"Send me some information"**
Response: "Happy to — I want to make sure I send the most relevant thing rather than a generic brochure. Two quick questions: [Question 1 that qualifies their use case] and [Question 2 that qualifies their timeline]. Based on your answers I'll send the one resource that's most on-point."
Note: Do NOT send a deck or pricing sheet to an unqualified prospect. Use this as a qualification checkpoint.

**"We already have a solution for this"**
Response: "Good to know — makes sense. Out of curiosity, what are you using? I ask because a lot of [their industry] teams start with [common incumbent] and find that [specific limitation] starts becoming a problem around [their likely growth stage]. Not saying that's your situation — but worth knowing if it's different."

**"Can you connect me with someone else?"**
Response: "Of course — who would be the best person to loop in? And in the meantime, should I share with you what I'd share with them so you have context? That way you can decide if it's worth their time."

PIPELINE ATTRIBUTION — CRM LOGGING PROTOCOL:

When a LinkedIn conversation converts to a booked meeting:
- Salesforce/HubSpot: Create Opportunity with Source = "LinkedIn Social Selling" + sub-source = [Signal Type that triggered outreach]
- Log conversation summary in Opportunity notes: Copy/paste key DM exchanges, note the signal, the prospect's pain in their words, any qualifying information gathered
- Tag deal with "LinkedIn Sourced" campaign for attribution
- AE pre-call brief: Share the key DM exchanges with the AE before the call — the prospect's exact words from LinkedIn are gold for discovery call personalization

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Connection acceptance rate is stuck below 30% even with personalized notes**
Solution: The most common cause is profile weakness, not message weakness. Run a profile audit before outreach: your headline should describe the value you create for buyers (not your title), your banner image should reinforce your company's ICP-relevant message, and your featured section should show social proof (customer case study, relevant content). Buyers accept connection requests from profiles that look credible and relevant. Also test removing the connection note entirely — counterintuitively, blank connection requests often achieve higher acceptance rates (34–38%) than notes that feel like sales outreach. Reserve personalized notes for Tier 1 signals where the relevance is unmistakable.

**Problem: Prospects accept the connection but never reply to Touch 2 or Touch 3**
Solution: The value in Touch 2 is likely too generic. If the "resource" you're offering feels like gated content they've seen before, they'll ignore it. Replace the resource offer with a specific insight about their company or situation: "I noticed [Company] has [specific observation from their LinkedIn or news] — most [titles] we talk to at that stage find that [pain] shows up differently than expected. Happy to share what we've seen." Specificity forces attention. Also examine your Touch 2 timing — if you're sending within 24 hours of connection, you feel like an automated bot. Wait 3 full days.

**Problem: LinkedIn automation tool is triggering account restrictions or message-sending limits**
Solution: LinkedIn detects automation through velocity patterns and browser fingerprinting. Immediately stop all automation, switch to manual outreach for 2 weeks, and lower daily volume to 15–20 connection requests per day. Use only cloud-based tools (Expandi, Waalaxy) rather than browser extensions — browser extensions (Dux-Soup in extension mode) are significantly more detectable. Set randomized sending windows (e.g., 8am–6pm with 5–15 minute delays between actions) and never run automated sequences on weekends. LinkedIn's limit is approximately 100 connection requests per week; exceeding this reliably triggers review.
