## Success Metrics

- **Conquesting CTR:** Target 3-6% CTR on Tier 1 competitor brand keywords (below this = ad copy isn't resonating with buyers' mindset at that search moment)
- **Conquesting Conversion Rate:** Target 3-8% form fill on comparison landing pages (below 3% = landing page isn't addressing the right objections; above 10% = you may be under-investing in this channel)
- **Conquesting CPA vs. Category CPA:** Healthy ratio is conquesting CPA at 1.5-3x your category CPA — if it's 5x+, the competition for those terms is too expensive relative to conversion quality
- **Win Rate on Conquesting Pipeline:** Track what % of demos/trials from competitor brand searches convert to closed-won — if significantly below your average, the conquesting traffic quality is wrong or your sales process isn't calibrated for competitive swaps
- **Brand Defense Impression Share:** Your brand keywords should maintain 85%+ impression share — any drop signals aggressive competitive bidding that needs a bid response
- **Comparison Page Bounce Rate:** Target <50% bounce on /vs/ pages — high bounce means the page isn't validating the buyer's comparison question fast enough

## Integration Tips

- **HubSpot:** Create a custom UTM parameter `utm_content=conq-[competitor-slug]` and map it to a HubSpot contact property "Competitor Conquesting Source" — this lets you report on conquesting pipeline separately from standard paid search and trigger competitor-specific sales sequences automatically when a conquesting contact books a demo

- **Google Ads Editor:** Use Google Ads Editor (desktop app) for bulk upload of competitor campaigns — you can build all competitor ad groups offline, apply shared negative keyword lists, and push to live in one batch rather than building in-UI competitor by competitor
- **Google Looker Studio:** Build a conquesting performance dashboard with separate data segments for each competitor — include impression share, CTR, CVR, cost per conversion, and pipeline value attributed; connect to your CRM via a connector like Funnel.io or Dreamdata for revenue attribution
- **Zapier + Slack:** Set up a Zapier workflow that fires a Slack alert to your demand gen channel whenever a competitor brand keyword campaign drops below 60% impression share — this is your early warning system for competitive bidding escalation
- **SEMrush / Auction Insights:** Run Google Ads Auction Insights report weekly and pipe results into a Google Sheet via SEMrush API or manual export — track which competitors are bidding on your brand terms and at what impression share, so you can respond proportionally

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Google disapproves my ads for trademark violation when I include a competitor name in ad copy**
Solution: Google's trademark policy allows you to use a competitor's trademark *as a keyword* but restricts using it *in ad text* if the trademark holder has filed a complaint with Google. If your ads are disapproved, remove the competitor name from headlines/descriptions and use category-level language instead ("Leading Revenue Intelligence Platform Alternative" vs. "[Competitor] Alternative"). You can appeal trademark restrictions if your use is for comparative advertising — file at g.co/adspoilcy with documentation of your comparison page.

**Problem: Competitor brand keywords convert at 10x my average CPA — the channel isn't profitable**
Solution: First, check if your comparison landing page is aligned to the specific intent of the keyword. A buyer searching "[Competitor] pricing" needs to immediately see a pricing comparison — not a generic comparison page. Create intent-matched landing page variants (pricing searchers → pricing comparison section; alternative searchers → broader feature comparison). If CPA remains high after landing page optimization, reduce budget on Tier 3-4 keywords and concentrate only on Tier 1 (pricing, demo, alternatives) where buyer intent is highest and conversion probability justifies the premium CPC.

**Problem: My competitors keep outbidding me on my own brand keywords — my brand impression share is dropping below 70%**
Solution: Google's Quality Score system favors the actual trademark owner on their own brand terms — your landing page experience and ad relevance score will naturally be higher than a competitor bidding on your brand, which means you pay significantly less per click for the same position. Raise your brand keyword bids by 20-30% to reclaim impression share (your effective CPC will stay low due to Quality Score advantage). Simultaneously, review your brand campaign ad copy to ensure it's maximally relevant (brand-specific headlines, brand-specific extensions) to widen the Quality Score gap vs. competitors.
