- Competitor Displacement → Dedicated comparison landing page per competitor: structured "X vs. Y" format with objective comparison table, win story from a customer who switched, and "Try [Your Brand] for free" CTA; DO NOT use generic homepage

B. Landing Page Quality Score Components (AI agent optimization checklist):
- Headline relevance: does the H1 contain the keyword or a direct synonym? (Required for QS ≥7)
- Above-fold CTA: is the primary conversion action visible without scrolling on both desktop and mobile?
- Page load speed: <3 seconds on mobile (PageSpeed Insights score 70+); each second of delay reduces conversion rate ~7%
- Form length: ≤5 fields for top-of-funnel; ≤7 fields for demo requests; never ask for fields you can enrich via Clearbit/ZoomInfo/Apollo
- Social proof density: minimum 3 trust signals above the fold (customer logo bar, G2 rating, named quote)
- Mobile responsiveness: form renders correctly, CTA button is thumb-sized, no horizontal scroll

C. Page Variant Testing Protocol:
- Primary test variables (in priority order): H1 headline, primary CTA copy, form placement (above vs. below fold), social proof type (logo wall vs. named quote vs. stat)
- Test duration: minimum 200 conversions or 4 weeks per variant before declaring winner
- AI agent role: monitor variant performance weekly; flag when statistical significance is reached (95% confidence); recommend winner promotion and next test hypothesis; do not auto-promote without human approval

**5. BIDDING STRATEGY & BUDGET PACING**

Design the Smart Bidding architecture:

A. Bidding Strategy by Campaign Type:

Brand Defense:
- Strategy: Target Impression Share (Target IS: 90%+, position: top of page)
- Rationale: brand terms should dominate SERP position regardless of CPA; budget is small relative to competitive risk of losing branded impressions
- Budget: set at ~110% of average daily spend to prevent brand term impression loss from budget exhaustion

Category Capture (established account with 50+ monthly conversions):
- Strategy: Target CPA — set initial tCPA at 15-20% above current average CPA to allow Smart Bidding flexibility during learning; tighten after 30 days of learning phase data
- Bidding inputs: import offline conversion events (MQL or SQL stage from CRM) — DO NOT optimize toward form fill alone; Smart Bidding will arbitrage low-quality form fills otherwise
- Portfolio bid strategy: if multiple category campaigns share similar conversion goals, use portfolio tCPA to allow budget reallocation across campaigns

Category Capture (new account or <30 conversions/month):
- Strategy: Maximize Clicks with manual CPC cap during data-gathering phase (first 60-90 days)
- Conversion volume threshold: transition to Smart Bidding only when ≥30 conversions/month per campaign with ≥50% of conversions being offline (pipeline-stage) events
- Manual CPC caps: set at estimated first-page CPC based on Keyword Planner data; adjust weekly based on impression share and average position data

Competitor Displacement:
- Strategy: Manual CPC or Enhanced CPC (eCPC) — avoid tCPA on competitor campaigns until 50+ monthly conversions because competitor traffic volume is typically too low for Smart Bidding learning; eCPC preserves control while allowing Google to adjust bids for high-conversion-probability signals
- CPC ceiling: set max CPC at no more than 2x average category CPC to prevent auction overbidding on competitor terms (which inflates CPCs for all players)

Performance Max (if deployed):
- Strategy: Target ROAS if account has revenue data; Target CPA if account tracks pipeline value; Maximize Conversions if new and in learning phase
- Asset group discipline: create separate asset groups per ICP segment and use case — never lump all audiences into one asset group
- Exclusion lists: upload your customer list as audience exclusion to prevent wasting budget on existing customers; add high-intent keyword themes as Search Themes to guide PMax search inventory

B. Budget Pacing Protocol (AI agent execution):
- Daily budget setting: set daily budgets at monthly budget ÷ 30, then add 10% buffer per Google's documented ability to overspend up to 2x daily budget on high-intent days
- Pacing alert thresholds: [AI agent should flag if any campaign has spent >60% of monthly budget by mid-month; trigger budget reallocation recommendation if high-performing campaigns are pacing to exhaust before end of month]
- Seasonal adjustment triggers: [identify fiscal quarter ends, industry event windows, and product launch periods that warrant temporary budget increases — provide exact percentage recommendations]

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Smart Bidding CPA is 40%+ above target after 30 days**
Solution: The most common cause is that Smart Bidding is optimizing toward low-intent form fills rather than pipeline-qualified events. Verify that your offline conversion import is working correctly (check the Google Ads conversion action for "Received" status on recent imports). If offline conversions are importing correctly, temporarily raise your tCPA target 25% to give the algorithm flexibility to exit the learning phase, then tighten it by 10% every 2 weeks until you reach your target.

**Problem: Competitor campaign impressions are low (below 500/day)**
Solution: Competitor brand keyword search volume is often lower than expected — many B2B buyers search the category term, not the competitor brand. First, check Google Keyword Planner for actual monthly search volume on your competitor terms. If volume is genuinely low, broaden competitor campaigns to include "[Competitor] alternative," "[Competitor] review," and "[Category] vs. [Competitor]" phrase match terms. Also verify you haven't accidentally added competitor brand terms as negatives.

**Problem: High CTR on keywords but very low conversion rate (<1%) on landing pages**
Solution: This is a keyword-to-landing-page relevance mismatch. Pull the search terms report to see the actual queries triggering clicks — often high-CTR keywords attract curiosity clicks from non-ICP searchers. Check the landing page headline for keyword match (the H1 must reflect the intent of the search, not just the product category). Also audit the landing page for form friction (remove any field that isn't absolutely required to route the lead), page load speed on mobile (use PageSpeed Insights), and above-the-fold social proof (add G2 rating or customer logo bar if missing).
