## Overview

Builds a full Google Performance Max (PMax) campaign architecture for B2B SaaS companies — one of the most misunderstood and misdeployed paid media channels for enterprise pipeline generation. Use this when you're running or planning PMax campaigns and need to structure asset groups, audience signals, conversion goals, and budget allocation to generate qualified pipeline rather than vanity traffic. This prompt eliminates the most common B2B PMax failure modes: brand cannibalization, bottom-funnel cannibalization, lead quality destruction, and wasted spend on non-ICP audiences.

#### Section 4: Conversion Goal Hierarchy & Offline Import Setup

**4.1 Conversion Action Weighting**

| Conversion Action | Type | Value Assigned | Optimization Priority |
|------------------|------|----------------|----------------------|
| Offline: Opportunity Won | Offline (OCI) | $[ACV × close rate] | Primary |
| Offline: SQL Created | Offline (OCI) | $[ACV × close rate × 0.4] | Primary |
| Offline: Demo Booked + Attended | Offline (OCI) | $[ACV × close rate × 0.2] | Primary |
| Demo Request (Form Fill) | Online | $[ACV × close rate × 0.08] | Secondary |
| ROI Calculator Completion | Online | $[ACV × close rate × 0.03] | Secondary |
| Research Report Download | Online | $[ACV × close rate × 0.01] | Micro — do NOT use as sole optimization goal |

**Why this matters:** If you optimize PMax solely on form fills, Google learns to generate form fill volume — which in B2B typically means driving low-quality leads, generic inquiries, SMB applicants, and competitors researching you. Offline conversion import forces Google to optimize for the pattern of users who eventually become revenue.

**4.2 Offline Conversion Import Setup (Salesforce → Google Ads)**

Step-by-step implementation:
1. Enable Google Ads conversion import in Salesforce (native connector or via Zapier/Make for non-Salesforce CRMs)
2. Map CRM stages to Google conversion actions: Demo Booked = "Demo Request Confirmed"; SAL = "Sales Accepted Lead"; SQL = "Sales Qualified Lead"; Opportunity Created = "Pipeline Created"; Closed Won = "Revenue Won"
3. Pass GCLID (Google Click ID) from landing page URL parameter → CRM lead record at point of first form fill. This is the critical link between ad click and eventual revenue.
4. Upload conversion data within 90 days of original click (Google attribution window)
5. Set conversion window to 120 days (matches your average sales cycle length)
6. Validate: In Google Ads → Tools → Conversions → Offline Conversion Imports — confirm data is flowing daily

**Days 1–30: Learning Phase (Do Not Optimize Aggressively)**

**Days 31–60: Signal Refinement**

Key actions:
- Download Placement report — identify and exclude low-quality placements (mobile games, irrelevant display domains)
- Refresh customer match lists (upload updated CRM export)
- Add new negative keywords based on Search Term report (if you have Search campaigns running alongside PMax)
- If offline conversions show SQLs and Opportunities: raise Target ROAS or lower Target CPA to push Google toward higher-quality traffic
- Review asset performance grades (Google rates assets Low/Good/Best) — replace "Low" assets within 30 days of launch
- Evaluate: Channel mix shifting? Should see more Search and YouTube and less Display as Google learns

**Days 61–90: Scale or Restructure**

Key actions:
- If CPL is tracking toward target and lead quality is validated (demo-to-SAL >30%): increase budget 20–30%
- If lead volume is high but quality is low (high CPL, low demo-to-SAL): add more restrictive audience signals, reduce budget, validate OCI is working
- Test new asset group: If one asset group is generating 70%+ of conversions, create a variant with different angle to find ceiling

- Quarterly reset: Archive underperforming asset groups. Rebuild with new customer match lists, updated copy, fresh offers.

## Integration Tips

- **HubSpot + Google Ads**: Enable native Google Ads integration in HubSpot. Map Deal Stages to Google Ads offline conversion actions. Use HubSpot's "Google Ad Clicks" contact property to capture GCLID at form fill — this enables GCLID matching for offline import.

- **Salesforce + Google Ads**: Use Salesforce's native Google Ads connector (AppExchange) or Zapier. Create a Google_Click_Id__c custom field on Lead and Contact objects. Use workflow rules to pass conversion data to Google Ads when Opportunity Stage changes.

- **Google Ads Editor**: Use Google Ads Editor (desktop app) to bulk-upload asset groups, negative keyword lists, and audience signals. Faster than the web interface for initial campaign builds.

- **Google Tag Manager**: Implement GTM to capture GCLID in a cookie (gclid cookie) and pass it as a hidden field value on all forms. This is the most reliable GCLID capture method and works even when Google auto-tagging is enabled.

- **Looker Studio / Google Data Studio**: Build a PMax performance dashboard pulling from Google Ads API — track by asset group, audience signal, and channel mix. Layer in offline conversion data from your CRM to see pipeline and revenue attribution by campaign.

- **Zapier/Make**: Automate customer match list refresh — trigger a CRM export and Google Ads list upload on a weekly schedule when new SQLs or customers are added to CRM segments.

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: PMax is generating high form fill volume but demo quality is terrible — sales team says leads are SMBs, non-ICP, or spam.**
Solution: Your conversion optimization signal is too shallow. Google is optimizing for form fills, not qualified pipeline. Immediately implement offline conversion import and reweight your conversion goals so that Demo Request carries low value and SQL/Opportunity carries high value. Add customer match lists of existing ICP customers as your highest-priority audience signal. Add negative demographic exclusions (under 25, remove broad age targeting). Review placement report and exclude mobile app placements. Expect 2–4 week re-learning period after changes.

**Problem: PMax campaign shows almost no Search impression share — nearly all spend is going to Display.**
Solution: This is common in the learning phase. Display is cheaper per impression so Google defaults to it when learning. Two fixes: (1) Make sure you have high-quality search assets (strong, specific headlines) — PMax uses these for Search inventory; (2) Add more specific custom intent keywords as audience signals — this helps Google identify Search inventory where your signals are present. If this persists beyond 45 days, consider splitting into a dedicated Search campaign for your highest-intent keywords and leaving PMax to handle broader inventory.

**Problem: GCLID match rate is below 50% — offline conversions aren't connecting to Google Ads clicks.**
Solution: GCLID capture is broken somewhere in the form submission flow. Check: (1) Is auto-tagging enabled in Google Ads? (Required for GCLID generation); (2) Is the GCLID cookie being written to your domain on landing? (Inspect browser cookies after clicking an ad); (3) Is the hidden GCLID form field actually capturing the cookie value? (Test by clicking an ad and checking the form HTML); (4) Is the CRM correctly storing the GCLID on the lead record? (Check a test submission). The most common failure point is cookie blocking from Safari ITP / Firefox — use GTM's first-party cookie solution to persist GCLIDs across sessions.
