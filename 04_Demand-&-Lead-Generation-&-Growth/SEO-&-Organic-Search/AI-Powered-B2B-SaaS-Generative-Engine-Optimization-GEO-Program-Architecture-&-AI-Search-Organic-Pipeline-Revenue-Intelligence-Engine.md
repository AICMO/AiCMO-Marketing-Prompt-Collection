## Troubleshooting

**Problem: We deployed LLMs.txt and optimized content 8 weeks ago but citation share hasn't improved.**
Solution: AI model knowledge cutoffs and crawl-to-citation lag can take 4–16 weeks depending on the platform. Prioritize Perplexity (fastest crawl-to-citation cycle, often 1–3 weeks). Verify your robots.txt is not blocking PerplexityBot and GPTBot — check server access logs for their user-agent strings. Also confirm your optimized content has actual external links pointing to it; AI models weight link authority similarly to traditional search engines.

**Problem: AI tools cite our G2 review page more than our own content — we don't control that narrative.**
Solution: This is common and actually a secondary GEO opportunity. Actively drive new G2 reviews from customers, specifically asking them to use outcome-specific language ("increased pipeline visibility by X%") rather than generic praise. Also create your own comparative content that directly addresses the same queries the G2 page wins for — with more depth, original data, and expert framing than any review aggregator can provide.

**Problem: Perplexity cites our blog post but names a competitor as the recommendation at the end of the AI answer.**
Solution: The citation and the recommendation are separate signals. You won the citation (structure and authority signals are working) but lost the recommendation (your product positioning language isn't compelling enough in context). Revisit the cited content: add a clear, benefits-first recommendation statement near the top and bottom of the page — AI models tend to extract the most direct, comparative language when formulating a recommendation. Add a section: "Who should use [Your Product]?" with an explicit ICP match description that outcompetes competitor descriptions on the same topic.
