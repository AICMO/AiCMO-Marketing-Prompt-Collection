## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Launch email open rate is below 20% (prospect list), indicating deliverability or subject line failure.**
Solution: Check spam score using tools like mail-tester.com before next campaign send. Test 2–3 subject line variants on 10% segments each before full deploy. Ensure sending domain has SPF, DKIM, and DMARC configured. Consider suppressing contacts who haven't opened in 180+ days from the launch list to protect sender score. For already-sent emails, activate LinkedIn ads to the same audience as a secondary awareness channel within 24 hours.

**Problem: Paid media CPL is 2x target in first week, burning through budget without sufficient pipeline.**
Solution: Pause broad targeting audiences first. Tighten LinkedIn to company size 1,000+ and overlay 6sense "Active" intent only. On Google Search, add negative keywords to block irrelevant queries (use Search Terms report immediately). Shift 25% of LinkedIn budget to Google Search if Search CPL is outperforming. If CPL remains high by Day 10, test switching from Lead Gen Form objective to Website Conversion objective (visitors who convert on-site often have higher intent than Lead Gen Form submitters).

**Problem: Demo pipeline is creating opportunities but they are stalling at late-stage — deals aren't closing.**
Solution: This is a sales enablement issue, not a demand problem. Audit the 10 most recent stalled deals: are they stuck on technical evaluation, pricing, legal, or stakeholder consensus? Create targeted content for each stall point: security review kit, ROI calculator for CFO, implementation guide for IT. Deploy "launch milestone" urgency: "Price locked for 90 days post-launch; annual commitment required to maintain launch pricing." Activate customer reference program — pair stalled deals with a reference customer who matches their use case and company profile.
