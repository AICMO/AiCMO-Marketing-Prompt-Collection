## Overview
Most B2B SaaS companies generate a burst of attention on launch day then watch pipeline stall within 2 weeks. This prompt engineers a complete 30-60-90 day post-launch revenue sustainment program that converts initial launch awareness into qualified sales opportunities through sequenced multi-channel activation, partner ecosystem mobilization, and AI-orchestrated buyer journey nurture — preventing launch decay before it kills your quarter.

## DELIVERABLE 1: LAUNCH MOMENTUM AUDIT

Apply the Launch Decay Framework — diagnose which phase you are in:
- Phase 1 (Days 0-7): "Launch Surge" — peak awareness, high volume, low intent quality
- Phase 2 (Days 8-21): "Momentum Risk Zone" — where 80% of programs stall; pipeline per day drops 60-80%
- Phase 3 (Days 22-60): "Pipeline Conversion Window" — self-identified evaluators are now in-market
- Phase 4 (Days 61-90): "Category Capture" — late majority entering awareness; competitive displacement opportunity

Based on metrics provided:
1. Calculate Launch Conversion Efficiency = (demo requests + trial activations) / total launch week visitors
   - Top quartile SaaS benchmark: 4-7%
   - Median: 1.5-2.5%
   - Below median: < 1%
2. Identify the primary leak point: Awareness → Interest → Intent → Demo Request → Opportunity
3. Assess social proof inventory: # case studies, # public customer logos, # G2 reviews available right now
4. Rate the program's "runway" — how many days of pre-built content assets remain before the team goes dark

Provide a 1-page launch health scorecard with Red/Yellow/Green ratings on: pipeline velocity, content inventory, social proof, competitive positioning, partner activation, and trial activation.

## DELIVERABLE 2: AUDIENCE SEGMENTATION FOR POST-LAUNCH ACTIVATION

Identify 5 distinct audiences requiring unique messaging and channel sequences:

SEGMENT A — "High-Intent Non-Converters"
Definition: Visited pricing or demo page during launch week, did not request demo or start trial
Size estimate: Typically 15-25% of launch week traffic
Urgency level: CRITICAL — these buyers are actively evaluating now
Recommended approach:
- Timeline: Activate within 48 hours of identifying this segment
- Message: Urgency + social proof + friction removal (e.g., "Skip the form — book directly")
- Channels: Retargeting display, LinkedIn Conversation Ads, SDR email + phone
- Primary CTA: Direct calendar link to AE or 15-minute demo
- Success metric: 15-25% conversion to demo scheduled within 10 days

SEGMENT B — "Trial Ghosts"
Definition: Created trial account, < 3 sessions, did not complete setup or reach activation milestone
Size: Varies; benchmark is 40-60% of trials go dark within 7 days
Urgency level: HIGH — highest intent cohort outside of direct demo request
Recommended approach:
- Timeline: Activate Day 3 post-trial-signup if no activation milestone reached
- Message: Jobs-to-be-Done framing ("You signed up because [JTBD]. Here's how to get there in 8 minutes.")
- Channels: In-app tooltips, behavioral email sequence, optional onboarding call offer
- Sequence length: 7 touchpoints over 14 days, then move to long-nurture
- Success metric: > 35% activation rate (reaching "aha moment")

SEGMENT C — "Research-Phase Evaluators"
Definition: Opened launch email, visited 3+ pages, no conversion; or returning visitor within 30 days
Size: Typically the largest segment
Urgency level: MEDIUM — buying decision 30-90 days out
Recommended approach:
- Message: Educational proof — case studies, ROI data, competitive comparison
- Channels: Email nurture, LinkedIn retargeting, content syndication
- Cadence: Bi-weekly content touch, triggered by engagement signals
- Success metric: 20-30% progress to demo request within 45 days

SEGMENT D — "ICP Dark Pool"
Definition: Firmographic ICP match, zero engagement with launch; your total addressable market not yet reached
Urgency level: LOW-MEDIUM — need awareness before intent
Recommended approach:
- Message: Category-level awareness ("There's a new way to [Job-to-be-Done]")
- Channels: LinkedIn Sponsored Content, programmatic ABM display, cold outbound email, partner co-marketing
- Sequence: 4-week awareness campaign → intent-signal triggered follow-up
- Success metric: 3-5% conversion to website visit from cold outreach

SEGMENT E — "Existing Customer Expansion"
Definition: Current customers who do not yet use this new product
Urgency level: HIGH — lowest CAC, highest close rate of any segment
Recommended approach:
- Message: Expansion narrative ("You're already [achieving X]. Now add [Y] with zero ramp-up.")
- Channels: CSM-led, in-app banner, targeted email from account team, executive sponsor outreach
- Offer: Launch-era pricing, dedicated onboarding support, early access benefits
- Success metric: 15-25% of existing base opts into expansion conversation within 60 days

For each segment, output: exact subject lines for first email, LinkedIn ad headline + body copy, SDR call opener script (2-3 sentences), and primary landing page CTA.

## DELIVERABLE 5: 90-DAY COMPETITIVE DISPLACEMENT PROGRAM (Days 68-90)

Apply the Switcher Pipeline Framework:

Step 1 — IDENTIFY COMPETITOR ACCOUNTS IN MARKET
- Use Bombora or G2 Buyer Intent to identify competitor customers showing evaluation signals
- Supplement with LinkedIn Sales Navigator: filter by competitor "skill" tag + ICP firmographics
- Target accounts where competitor contract renewal is likely within 6 months (estimated via average contract length)

Step 2 — DISPLACEMENT CAMPAIGN ARCHITECTURE
- Paid: LinkedIn and programmatic ABM campaign targeting identified competitor accounts
  - Ad creative: "[Competitor] users are switching. Here's why." + link to comparison page
- Outbound: SDR sequence personalized to competitor user pain points
  - Opener: "I know you're using [Competitor] for [use case]. Customers like [Reference] switched to [Product] and [achieved specific result]. Worth a 15-minute conversation?"
- Content: Case study featuring a switched customer (with permission) — "[Company] Switched from [Competitor] to [Product]: 90-Day Results"

Step 3 — MIGRATION OFFER
- Competitive pricing incentive: 20-30% first-year discount for documented competitor contract holders
- Migration support: Dedicated implementation resource for first 60 days
- Data migration tool or service: Remove technical switching friction
- ROI guarantee: Performance-based offer ("Achieve [metric] in 90 days or we refund Month 1")

Step 4 — CLOSE THE LOOP WITH SALES
- Weekly competitor displacement pipeline review in sales meeting
- Marketing supports: Reference calls, custom business case, executive sponsor involvement for larger deals

## Integration Tips

- **HubSpot**: Create a "Post-Launch Nurture" workflow with behavioral triggers (visited pricing page, started trial, opened launch email 2+ times). Sync contact properties to segment audiences automatically.
- **Salesforce + Pardot/Marketing Cloud**: Build campaign influence tracking tagged to "Launch: [Product Name] - Post-Launch Q[X] 202X" for 90-day attribution window. Use Campaign Hierarchy to roll up all post-launch touches.
- **6sense or Demandbase**: Create a "Post-Launch Intent" segment pulling accounts matching ICP firmographics + showing buying-stage intent signals. Feed directly to SDR prioritization queue and paid ABM campaigns.
- **LinkedIn Campaign Manager**: Build matched audiences from CRM lists (trial signups, pricing page visitors, demo no-shows) using LinkedIn Insight Tag or CSV upload. Run Conversation Ads to high-intent segment and Sponsored Content to awareness segments.
- **Amplitude or Mixpanel**: Set up a "Trial Ghost Alert" cohort — users who signed up > 72 hours ago with < 3 sessions and no activation event. Push to HubSpot/Marketo via webhook for immediate email sequence trigger.
- **Notion or Airtable**: Build a post-launch campaign tracker with columns for: Segment, Campaign, Channel, Launch Date, Budget, Leads Generated, Pipeline Created, Status. Review in weekly marketing/sales sync.
- **Slack**: Configure automated weekly digest via Zapier or native integration — pipeline created by source, trial activations, top-performing content — sent to #marketing-revenue channel every Monday morning.

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Trial activation rate stays below 20% despite re-engagement emails**
Solution: The bottleneck is usually onboarding complexity, not messaging. Run a 10-customer interview asking "Where did you get stuck?" before optimizing email copy. Most activation failures occur at integration setup or first-data-import step. Fix the product experience first, then re-run the sequence. If product fix is weeks away, offer concierge onboarding calls as a bridge.

**Problem: SDR outreach is getting low response rates on launch audience**
Solution: The launch email typically reaches the same persona who gets 50+ cold emails per week. Switch from a feature pitch to a signal-based opener: "I noticed [Company] is hiring [X role] — that usually means [related pain point]. Is [Product] on your radar as you scale?" Use LinkedIn voice messages or video for the top 20 highest-intent accounts instead of email.

**Problem: Partner activation is slow — partners are not promoting the new product**
Solution: Partners will not promote your product without a clear WIIFM (what's in it for me). Reframe the outreach: instead of "Please share our launch," offer: (1) a co-branded webinar that builds their audience too, (2) a revenue share on any deals they source, or (3) a "featured integration" spotlight placement on your marketplace. The joint webinar is typically the fastest activator — propose a specific date and handle all production.
