## Overview

Use it when launching new capabilities, add-on modules, or upgraded tiers where your existing customer base is the primary revenue opportunity.

## Troubleshooting

**Problem:** Email open rates are below 20% for existing customers (industry benchmark: 28-35%)
**Solution:** Check sender domain — existing customer emails should come from CSM or Customer Success email addresses, not a marketing domain. Also audit send time: Tuesday/Wednesday 9-11am local time consistently outperforms other windows for this audience. Personalize subject lines with company name or recent account event ("Congrats on your [milestone], [FIRST NAME]").

**Problem:** Tier 1 accounts aren't converting even after full CSM engagement
**Solution:** Run a rapid 5-account loss analysis: did they understand the value? Was price the issue? Was there a competing priority? Adjust your economic buyer messaging if ROI isn't landing — most common failure mode is CSMs selling to champions who can't get budget approval without a CFO-specific business case. Build the business case for the champion, not just the product pitch.

**Problem:** CSMs aren't following the playbook — outreach quality varies widely
**Solution:** Implement a "Launch Score" review in weekly team stand-up: review which Tier 1 accounts have no CSM activity after 2 weeks and assign them in the meeting. Record one strong CSM call using Gong and share it as the gold standard. Create a simple self-QA checklist: did I name the specific pain? Did I reference a similar customer? Did I get a next-step commitment?
