## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Launch day inbound volume is 10x+ expected and SDR team is overwhelmed — response times are slipping past SLA**
Solution: Activate your "overflow" protocol immediately — route Tier 2 leads directly to an AI SDR sequence (Clay/Smartlead) without human review. Upgrade your AI chat (Drift, Qualified) to proactively engage all website visitors with a "Did you see our announcement?" trigger. Temporarily relax Tier 1 criteria to only the highest-fit leads (VP/Director level at exact-ICP companies) and batch the rest into a "Day 2 priority" queue for Monday morning SDR blitz.

**Problem: Press coverage didn't land as expected — organic traffic is only 1.5x vs. projected 5x — pipeline is behind target by 3pm**
Solution: Don't panic and don't overspend on paid impulsively. First, activate your "earned media amplification" sequence: email your top 50 customers with a "We just launched — can you share this?" message with pre-written LinkedIn content they can post in one click. Second, contact any journalists or analysts who were briefed but haven't published — a same-day follow-up nudge is acceptable. Third, release 30% of your emergency paid budget toward branded + competitor keyword terms to capture intent that exists even without the press coverage.

**Problem: Conversion rate on the launch landing page is low (< 2%) despite strong traffic — people are reading but not converting**
Solution: Add a real-time exit-intent popup offering a "Watch the 3-minute product demo" with no form (embedded video). This captures buyers who aren't ready to commit to a full demo request. Add a "Talk to us live" chat trigger that fires after 45 seconds on the page. If conversion rate doesn't improve within 2 hours of these changes, test a stripped-down landing page variant with a single CTA (remove navigation, reduce text by 60%, lead with the ROI claim from your best case study).
