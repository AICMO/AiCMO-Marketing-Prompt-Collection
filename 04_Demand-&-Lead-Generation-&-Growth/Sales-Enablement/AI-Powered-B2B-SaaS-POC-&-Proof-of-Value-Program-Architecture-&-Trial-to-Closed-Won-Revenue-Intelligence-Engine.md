## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Champion won't get success criteria signed before POC begins**
Fix: Reframe the document as protection for the customer, not the vendor — "This ensures we're measuring what actually matters to you, not what's easiest for us to show." If they still resist, ask which criteria feel uncertain — that conversation often surfaces the real concern (misaligned expectations, internal politics, or a hidden technical risk).

**Problem: POC drags past 30 days with no clear outcome**
Fix: Invoke the success criteria document. Schedule a "Go/No-Go" conversation with the champion: "We agreed that if [Knockout Criterion] was achieved by [Date], we'd move to a commercial discussion. It was achieved. What would it take to schedule that conversation this week?" If they can't answer, escalate to the economic buyer directly.

**Problem: Results are good but deal stalls in procurement/legal**
Fix: This is no longer a POC problem — it's a procurement acceleration problem. Move to the Digital Deal Room prompt for Mutual Action Plan architecture. Key tactic: pre-build your security, legal, and compliance packages so procurement has nothing left to ask for. The POC success report becomes your "Exhibit A" in the legal agreement — reference it explicitly in the Master Services Agreement scope of work.
