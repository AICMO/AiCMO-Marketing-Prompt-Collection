   Week 1-2: [Foundational knowledge milestones]

   Day 31-60: [First pipeline milestone and coaching focus areas]
   Day 61-90: [Path to first closed deal]

Output all assets in structured, field-ready format. No filler. Every sentence should be something a rep can act on tomorrow.

## MODULE 1: TERRITORY ORIENTATION BRIEF

Purpose: A new rep reads this on Day 1 and immediately knows where to start, who to call, and how the territory is positioned competitively.

Generate:
1.1 Territory Snapshot

    - Why this territory matters to the company right now (strategic rationale)
    - 3 accounts to call in Week 1 and the specific reason each is prioritized (hiring signals, funding events, competitive displacement opportunity, etc.)

1.2 Buyer Environment

1.3 Competitive Terrain
    - Which competitor has the highest share in this territory and why
    - The 1 thing the dominant competitor does well that reps should never argue against

## MODULE 2: ICP PROFILE CARDS (one per persona)

Purpose: A rep reads this before every first call and knows exactly how to open, what to listen for, and how to advance.

For each persona, generate:

2.1 The Job-to-be-Done
    - What they were hired to accomplish (specific, not generic)
    - How their success is measured by their boss (the 1-3 metrics they're accountable for)
    - What "good" looks like in 12 months — the promotion-worthy outcome

2.2 The Fear Architecture
    - Biggest professional risk if they do nothing

    - Biggest internal objection they'll face from peers when championing us

2.3 Language Map
    - 5 phrases they actually use (pulled from customer call transcripts, G2 reviews, LinkedIn posts)
    - 3 phrases to avoid — vendor-speak that signals "this rep hasn't done their research"
    - The best opening question that proves you understand their world

2.4 Buying Behavior
    - How long they typically deliberate before recommending a purchase
    - Who they involve in the decision (and in what sequence)
    - What they need to see to feel "safe" recommending us internally
    - The 1 piece of proof that moves them faster than anything else

## MODULE 3: WIN STORY LIBRARY

Purpose: Give reps 3-5 stories they can tell fluently in discovery to build credibility with skeptical buyers.

Format each story with these elements:

3.1 Story Setup (30-second version)
    "I was talking to [persona title] at a [company type] company, [size], similar to yours. They were dealing with [specific painful situation — use their language]. Here's what happened."

3.2 The Trigger
    - What event forced them to act now (not "they had a pain" — what SPECIFICALLY changed)

3.3 Why They Chose Us
    - The specific thing that differentiated us (not "better product" — the exact moment they said "this is the one")
    - Which competitor they were evaluating and what made us win
    - Who was the internal champion and how they sold it internally

3.4 The Outcome (in their words)
    - Primary metric moved: [Specific number, timeframe]
    - Secondary benefit they didn't expect but valued
    - What they said when asked why they'd recommend us

3.5 How to Deploy This Story

    - How to transition: "Actually, that reminds me of a company we worked with..."
    - The follow-up: which case study or one-pager to send within 24 hours

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: The ICP profiles feel generic — reps aren't using them.**
Solution: The prompt requires real customer language as input. Run Module 2 again with verbatim quotes from 3-5 win call transcripts. If you don't have transcripts, source 10-15 quotes from G2 reviews written by your persona and paste them as input. Language specificity is what makes these profiles feel true.

**Problem: Win stories don't resonate — prospects say "that's not our situation."**
Solution: Stories fail when the Situation element is too general ("a SaaS company similar to yours"). Add 2 more specificity signals: company size, and the specific trigger event (e.g., "just closed their Series B" or "their VP Sales was 3 months into the role"). Relevance comes from situational matching, not industry matching.

**Problem: Ramp curriculum milestones aren't being hit — the 60-day timeline seems aggressive.**
Solution: Audit which module is causing the delay. The most common bottleneck is Module 3 (win story fluency) — reps know the story intellectually but can't deliver it conversationally. Add a 20-minute recorded story drill in Week 2: the rep tells all 3 win stories back-to-back to camera, then watches the playback and self-evaluates against the ICP persona they're targeting. Fluency comes from reps rehearsing out loud, not reading.
