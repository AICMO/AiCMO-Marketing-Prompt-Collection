Section 4: Proof of Concept (one customer story, same industry if possible, with specific metrics)
Section 5: Investment Summary (ACV + implementation cost + total first-year investment)
Section 6: ROI Model (simple 3-row table: Metric → Current State → With [Product] → Annual Value)
Section 7: Risk Mitigation (what happens if we delay vs. what we do to protect the timeline)

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Champion doesn't share the deal room with the economic buyer**
Solution: Don't rely on the champion alone. Use Module 7's Day 14 message to reach the economic buyer directly, framed as "I wanted to make sure you had visibility into where we are before your team's next planning cycle." Bypass, don't bulldoze.

**Problem: Prospect rejects the Mutual Action Plan — says "we don't do these"**
Solution: Rename it. Call it a "Partnership Launch Timeline" or "Shared Milestone Tracker." The name matters less than the function. Offer to co-build it live on a call rather than sending a pre-built version — co-creation increases buy-in by 3x in enterprise deals.

**Problem: The business case numbers don't match what finance will accept**
Solution: Ask the champion to share their internal ROI model template or to tell you what metric their CFO uses to evaluate investments (payback period vs. IRR vs. NPV). Then regenerate Module 3 with the CFO's preferred metric as the primary output. Never make the buyer translate your ROI model into their language — do it for them.
