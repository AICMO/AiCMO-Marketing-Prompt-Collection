## Troubleshooting

**Problem: 6+ weeks of posting with under 50 total views.**
Solution: This is almost always a distribution problem, not a content quality problem. Check: (1) Are you promoting each video to your existing email list within 24 hours of publish? If you have 500+ email subscribers and 50 views, your email promotion isn't working. (2) On LinkedIn, are you posting natively (uploading video directly) or sharing a YouTube link? LinkedIn suppresses external links — native video gets 5-10x more reach. (3) Are you engaging with every comment within 2 hours of posting? LinkedIn's algorithm rewards early engagement velocity. Fix these three before assuming content quality is the issue.

**Problem: Views are strong but zero leads or demo requests.**
Solution: Your CTA architecture is broken. Audit: (1) Is your lead magnet specifically relevant to that video's topic? A generic "download our eBook" on a video about churn prediction fails — it must be "Download the Churn Risk Scorecard I mentioned." (2) Is your landing page load time under 2 seconds on mobile? 60%+ of YouTube video clicks happen on mobile. (3) Is the CTA happening too late? Move it to 50% of video length instead of end. (4) Check your pinned comment — is the link working and is the copy creating urgency?

**Problem: Video analytics show great engagement but CRM shows zero video-attributed pipeline.**
Solution: Attribution setup is broken, not the video program. Verify: (1) Are UTM parameters passing through from video description links to your landing pages and then to your forms? Use UTM.io or a UTM spreadsheet to test every link. (2) Is your MAP (Marketo/HubSpot) set up to capture UTM parameters on form submission and write them to contact fields? This requires explicit field mapping setup — it's not automatic. (3) Are your SDRs asking about video consumption in discovery calls and logging it in Salesforce? Self-reported attribution often closes the UTM gap. Run a 30-day attribution audit: manually search your CRM for contacts who submitted video lead magnet forms, then check if any became opportunities — if yes, your attribution model isn't capturing it and needs fixing.
