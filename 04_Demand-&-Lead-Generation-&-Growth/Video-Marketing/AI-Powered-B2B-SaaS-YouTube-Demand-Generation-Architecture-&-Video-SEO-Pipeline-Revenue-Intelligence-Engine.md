## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Videos are getting views but not converting to demo requests.**
Fix: Audit your CTAs — most B2B YouTube channels bury the CTA at the end where 70% of viewers have already left. Add a mid-video CTA at the 40-50% watch-time mark that offers a high-value asset (template, calculator, framework) rather than asking for a demo. Capture the email first; nurture to demo second. Also ensure your video landing page matches the specific promise of the video, not a generic homepage.

**Problem: High click-through rate on thumbnails but low avg view duration (under 35%).**
Fix: Your hook is failing. The thumbnail and title made a promise your opening 90 seconds didn't deliver. Audit your first 30 seconds — are you immediately addressing the viewer's pain, or are you doing a brand intro, "welcome back to the channel," or slowly building to the point? Rewrite your hook formula: *State the exact problem in one sentence → Promise the specific outcome → Give a credibility signal → Tell them what's coming.* Cut everything before that.

**Problem: Sales team isn't using videos in outreach — adoption is zero.**
Fix: SDRs won't search YouTube for relevant videos to share. Build a 1-page "Video Sales Toolkit" that maps each video to a specific outreach scenario (e.g., "Send Video 3 when prospect mentions manual spreadsheet tracking"). Load the video URLs into your sales engagement tool (Outreach, Salesloft) as saved resources. Run a 15-minute enablement session showing one specific email template with a video embedded. Track which reps send videos — public leaderboard creates competitive adoption pressure.
