## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Reps record videos but prospects aren't watching them (view rate <20%)**
*Cause:* Email delivery or subject line is burying the video, OR thumbnail isn't compelling enough to click.
*Fix:* Test subject lines with explicit "video" reference (e.g., "Quick video for you, Marcus" or "[VIDEO] Your RevOps forecast question from last week"). Ensure animated thumbnail (GIF of first 3 seconds) is visible in email preview. Check that video is embedded as linked thumbnail image, not a naked URL. A/B test subject lines with and without "video" — typically lifts open rate by 19-25%.

**Problem: Reps aren't creating videos consistently (adoption rate stalls at <40%)**
*Cause:* Script templates are too complex OR production setup feels intimidating OR reps don't believe video works.
*Fix:* Reduce the Tier 3 template to a single 60-second script with only 3 fill-in blanks. Run a live "video day" where the entire team blocks 2 hours to record 10 videos together with a facilitator — peer pressure and shared experience dramatically increases adoption. Share weekly rep leaderboard showing video creation volume and correlated reply rates — when reps see peers closing deals with video, adoption follows within 2-4 weeks.

**Problem: Video deal rooms aren't being opened by all stakeholders (champion shares but others don't engage)**
*Cause:* Champion is forwarding the link but not contextualizing it for each stakeholder, OR the deal room is too long/complex for busy executives.
*Fix:* Record a separate 30-second "deal room intro video" for each stakeholder type (CFO version, IT version, end-user version) that the champion can forward with a single sentence ("I thought you'd specifically find the CFO section helpful"). Add a visible "Start Here" button at the top of the deal room that links to the executive summary video — reduce friction for busy stakeholders who won't scroll. Consider sending stakeholder-specific mini-videos directly from the AE (not through champion) for enterprise deals over $75K ACV.
