## Troubleshooting

**Issue 1: Founder feels uncomfortable on camera / content sounds scripted**
- Solution: Never provide a full script — only 3-bullet talking points with key phrases. Have founder record a test video answering "What's the one thing your ICP gets wrong about [topic]?" with no preparation. Use this as the reference for authentic delivery tone. Schedule recording immediately after an energizing customer call when ideas are fresh. Authenticity performs 40–60% better than polished delivery for B2B founder content.

**Issue 2: High view counts but zero ICP engagement or DMs**
- Solution: Audience is too broad. Audit follower composition — if <15% match ICP, implement ICP enrichment strategy: comment on 5 ICP target account employees' posts daily for 2 weeks (builds algorithmic affinity). Add ICP-specific signals to content ("If you're a CRO running a $10M+ quota, this is specifically for you"). Run a 30-day experiment where every video opens with an ICP-specific qualifier.

**Issue 3: Content calendar runs dry; founder runs out of ideas after 4 weeks**
- Solution: Implement the "Sales Call Mining" system — after every customer/prospect call, have the founder or AE note the top 2 objections or questions raised. These become next week's video topics. Also mine: 1-star reviews of competitors on G2/Capterra, ICP community questions in Slack/Reddit/LinkedIn groups, responses to "What's your #1 challenge with [category]?" posts. Aim for 3x more ideas than videos planned to maintain buffer.
