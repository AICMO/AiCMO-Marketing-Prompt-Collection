## Troubleshooting

**Problem: LinkedIn Live RSVP-to-attendance rate is below 20%**
Solution: RSVPs on LinkedIn are low-commitment clicks — treat them as email opt-ins, not commitments. Increase Day-1 and Day-0 reminder touchpoints; add a personal DM to top 20 RSVPs from the host's personal account 2 hours before broadcast. Test different broadcast days/times (Tuesday-Thursday, 11am-1pm ET typically outperform Monday/Friday and early morning). Add a specific "exclusive insight" promise in Day-0 reminder to create urgency.

**Problem: LinkedIn's algorithm is not showing the event to non-followers**
Solution: LinkedIn's event amplification requires early engagement velocity. In the first 2 hours after creating the LinkedIn Event, have 5+ team members and the host mark "Interested" and share the event to their personal feeds. Use LinkedIn's paid Event Ads (minimum $10/day) targeting ICP job titles to seed algorithmic amplification. Ensure the host's personal post about the event (not the company page post) goes out first — personal content receives 5-8x more organic reach than company page content.

**Problem: Post-event SDR outreach feels cold and gets ignored**
Solution: The SDR's value proposition must be hyper-specific to what happened in the broadcast. Brief SDRs on 3-5 specific talking points from each episode that signal buying intent (e.g., "If they asked about [specific Q&A question], they're in evaluation mode — use this talk track"). Give SDRs access to attendance lists with the specific questions each attendee asked in Q&A — this context turns cold outreach into warm follow-up. Never let more than 4 hours pass between live attendance and the first SDR touchpoint for Segment A attendees.
