## Integration Tips

- **Salesforce**: Create a custom "Referral" Lead Source picklist value, a custom Referral_Source__c lookup field on Contact, and a Referral_Payout__c custom object. Build a Process Builder flow that auto-creates a payout record when an Opportunity with Referral source closes as Won. This gives you full referral pipeline visibility in Salesforce without any external referral tool required.
- **HubSpot**: Use HubSpot's contact properties to store Referral Code (unique per customer) and Referral Source. Build a workflow: When Contact property "Referral Code" is known + Deal stage moves to "Closed Won" → Create task for referral payout + send internal notification. Use HubSpot's Revenue Attribution reports to compare referral vs. non-referral deal velocity.
- **PartnerStack**: Best choice if you want a fully managed referral portal with automatic link generation, a customer-facing dashboard showing referral status and rewards, and native Salesforce/HubSpot integration. Setup time: 2-3 weeks. Cost: ~$500-$2,000/month depending on tier. Best for programs expecting 50+ active referrers.
- **Referral Rock**: More affordable alternative to PartnerStack ($200-800/month). Lacks the enterprise polish but works well for Series A/B companies. Native integrations with HubSpot, Salesforce, and Stripe for payout automation.
- **Gainsight / ChurnZero**: Configure your CS platform to create a Referral Invitation CTA on customer accounts that meet eligibility criteria. When CSMs see the CTA during their QBR prep, they can trigger the referral invitation directly from the CS platform — dramatically increasing activation rate because it's human-initiated, not just automated email.
- **Stripe**: For cash payouts to self-serve or SMB customers, Stripe's Instant Payout feature enables same-day referral reward delivery — eliminating the reward delay that kills referral program momentum. Set up a simple Zapier zap: Salesforce Opportunity Closed Won (referral source) → Stripe Payout → Referrer email confirmation.
- **Notion / Confluence**: Build a "Referral Program Runbook" documenting: eligibility criteria, incentive tiers, attribution rules, dispute resolution process, and monthly review agenda. Share with Customer Success, Sales, and Finance so all teams operate from the same playbook.

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Only 5-8% of eligible customers are making referrals despite good NPS scores.**
Solution: Low participation almost always traces to one of three causes: (1) The invitation moment is wrong — check whether you're sending the invitation during a high-satisfaction moment or a generic batch email. Referral invitations sent within 48 hours of an NPS 9-10 response convert at 3-4x the rate of batch emails. (2) The incentive isn't valued — $500 account credit means nothing to a customer paying $2K/month; the reward needs to be ~3-5% of their annual contract value to feel meaningful. (3) The process feels complex — customers won't refer if they don't clearly understand what counts as a qualified referral and exactly when/how they'll be paid. Simplify the program explanation to 3 sentences maximum.

**Problem: Referral leads are coming in but converting at the same rate as cold leads.**
Solution: The quality issue means referrers are sharing too broadly rather than targeting qualified prospects. Add a "Referral Qualification Brief" to the enablement kit: a one-page explainer showing the exact profile of your ideal customer (company size, tech stack, active pain points) with 3 example companies that ARE a fit and 3 that are NOT. Train CSMs to review this brief with each referrer during their first referral program conversation. Qualified referral leads should close at 25-35% higher rates than outbound — if they're not, the ICP communication is the gap.

**Problem: The finance team is pushing back on referral payouts because attribution is disputed.**
Solution: Establish your attribution rules in writing before the first payout dispute occurs — not after. The three most common disputes in B2B SaaS referral programs: (1) "The prospect was already in our CRM" — rule should be: referral attribution wins if referrer engagement occurred before any active pipeline stage; (2) "Two customers referred the same prospect" — rule should be: first referral link click wins (PartnerStack handles this automatically; custom builds need a timestamp-based CRM rule); (3) "The deal was inbound, the customer just happened to mention knowing [referrer]" — rule should be: require verified referral link usage for payout eligibility (verbal referrals are goodwill, not tracked). Document all three rules in your referral agreement, get finance sign-off before launch, and you'll eliminate 90% of disputes.
