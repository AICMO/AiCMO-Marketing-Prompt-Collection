## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Community joins but nobody posts or engages (ghost town effect)**
Solution: This is the "cold start problem." Fix it by seeding discussions before members arrive. Before launch, get 5 founding members to post 3-4 discussion threads covering the Three Camps. When new members join, they see an active community, not an empty room. Also, DM every new member within 24 hours with a specific, personalized question based on their profile — the first reply almost always comes from a direct ask.

**Problem: Community grows but pipeline doesn't — can't attribute revenue**
Solution: The attribution gap usually means CQL scoring isn't triggering outreach, or outreach is too salesy and members disengage. Audit your CQL threshold — if nobody is hitting 100 points, lower the engagement point values or reduce the threshold. If they're hitting it but not converting, listen to the first 3 community SDR outreach calls. The frame should be educational — "I noticed your discussion about X, here's data we have" — not "ready for a demo?"

**Problem: Founding members stop engaging after the first month**
Solution: Founding members joined for exclusivity and co-creation. If they feel like regular members after 30 days, they'll disengage. Run a quarterly "founding member council" call where you genuinely share strategy and ask for input. Give them research data first before anyone else. Feature them in content. The moment the community feels like a funnel to them, you lose them.
