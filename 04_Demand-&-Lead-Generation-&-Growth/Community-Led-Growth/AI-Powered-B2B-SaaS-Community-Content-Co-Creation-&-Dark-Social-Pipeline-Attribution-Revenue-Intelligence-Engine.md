## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Ambassadors agree to participate but don't publish content consistently.**
Fix: The friction is in the creation workflow, not motivation. Build a Notion template library with 5-6 pre-written LinkedIn post frameworks they only need to personalize. Add a weekly "ambassador Slack nudge" sent automatically every Monday with content idea + template. If dropout persists after 30 days, it's a fit issue — move on and recruit replacements.

**Problem: Dark social UTM clicks are generating traffic but demos aren't converting at the same rate as other channels.**
Fix: Dark social visitors arrive with high awareness but low urgency. The landing page experience needs to acknowledge peer-referred context ("Heard about us from someone in your network?"), lead with social proof from the community, and offer a lower-friction first step (free report download, community membership invite) before pushing demo. AB test community-specific landing pages vs. standard pages.

**Problem: Self-reported attribution shows high community % but finance doesn't accept it as a hard metric.**
Fix: Build a triangulation proof: show that weeks with high seeding activity correlate with direct traffic lifts and demo volume increases. Pull the last 12 months of seeding events and overlay with direct traffic and demo request data. Present the correlation (even without perfect causation) alongside self-reported data. Add CRM engagement data ("X% of Closed Won contacts had community engagement logged before opportunity creation") as a third data point.
