## Troubleshooting

**Issue 1: Community feels like a "trap" for competitor customers — they disengage after joining.**

Solution: Audit your onboarding sequence for over-sales language. The first 30 days should deliver zero commercial calls-to-action. Focus entirely on peer value: connections, content, and community belonging. Use the 80/20 rule: 80% of community programming serves members regardless of what tool they use; 20% naturally highlights your product's advantages.

**Issue 2: Competitor customers join community but don't self-identify, making SCQL scoring impossible.**

Solution: Add a softer profile enrichment mechanism — instead of a direct "What tool do you use?" question, ask "What's your biggest [category] challenge right now?" and use AI to classify responses into intent signals. Combine with LinkedIn profile enrichment (Clay, Clearbit) to identify tool usage from profile data, and use intent data platforms to cross-reference account-level signals.

**Issue 3: Sales team doesn't trust community-sourced leads and deprioritizes SCQLs.**

Solution: Run a 90-day A/B test showing win rate and sales cycle data for community-sourced leads vs. other inbound. Community-influenced deals should close 20-30% faster with higher win rates — present this data in a monthly sales-marketing alignment meeting. Create a dedicated "Community Pipeline" view in CRM so sales has visibility into the community engagement context before their first conversation.
