## Troubleshooting

**Problem: High CPM (>$35) and low reach on cold audiences**
Solution: Meta's B2B targeting is inherently less precise than LinkedIn, so CPMs can spike when audiences are too narrow. Broaden cold audiences to 200K+ by reducing interest stacking layers. Test Advantage+ Audience (letting Meta's algorithm find your ICP) with a $2,000/month test budget — for many B2B SaaS companies this outperforms manual targeting at lower CPM once the pixel has 500+ Lead events. Also check: are you accidentally targeting a highly competitive audience that finance and insurance brands are also targeting? Those industries drive up CPMs industry-wide.

**Problem: Lots of Meta leads but low SQL rate (<15%)**
Solution: This is almost always an audience quality or offer mismatch problem, not a creative problem. First, run an analysis of the job titles and company sizes of the last 50 Meta leads in your CRM. If they don't match ICP, tighten the audience OR add a more qualifying question to your lead form (e.g., "How many sales reps are on your team?" with a gated answer that auto-disqualifies < 5). Second, if ICP looks correct but qualification is low, the offer is attracting researchers not buyers — replace the content download with a "15-minute Revenue Operations Benchmarking Call" offer that filters for active evaluation intent.

**Problem: BOFU campaign shows great results in Meta Ads Manager but CRM shows no pipeline impact**
Solution: This is the B2B Meta attribution paradox — view-through conversions are often being credited by Meta that your CRM doesn't see. First, audit your UTM parameters: all Meta BOFU ads should use utm_source=meta, utm_medium=paid-social, utm_campaign=bofu-[campaign-name]. Check HubSpot/Salesforce to confirm these UTMs are being captured on contact records. Second, switch from 7-day click + 1-day view attribution to 7-day click only in Meta Ads Manager to see the "apples-to-apples" view. Finally, implement a 90-day pipeline influence report: any closed-won deal where a contact in the account saw a Meta ad in the 90 days before close counts as Meta-influenced, even if not last-touch.
