## Overview
This prompt engineers a complete pricing page conversion system that diagnoses friction, restructures value communication, and designs psychologically-optimized page architectures to maximize trial starts and trial-to-paid conversion rates. Use it when launch metrics are below industry benchmarks (trial start rate < 3%, trial-to-paid < 20%) or when preparing for a pricing page redesign or packaging refresh.

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Visit-to-trial rate improved but trial-to-paid rate dropped.**
This usually means the pricing page changes attracted lower-intent visitors or removed friction that was filtering out non-ICP leads. Fix: Add one qualifying question in the trial signup flow ("What's your team size?") to re-add lightweight intent filtering, and ensure the Day 1 activation email is persona-specific (different emails for different ICP segments).

**Problem: The A/B tests aren't reaching statistical significance.**
At low traffic volumes (< 1,000 pricing page visitors/month), most A/B tests will take 3-6 months to reach significance. Fix: Use a "lean testing" approach — run heuristic-based changes as direct implementations with before/after cohort comparison rather than true A/B tests. Focus on qualitative signals (session recordings, trial interview feedback) until traffic scales.

**Problem: Annual plan adoption stays low despite pricing page changes.**
Annual pricing requires trust that the vendor will be around and worth paying for. Fix: Add a "cancel anytime" annual plan guarantee (prorated refund), surface customer tenure stats ("Average customer has been with us 2.8 years"), and deploy a dedicated "Annual vs. Monthly" FAQ entry that addresses the lock-in fear directly. Consider an introductory annual offer (2 months free) surfaced as a post-trial-start in-app modal.
