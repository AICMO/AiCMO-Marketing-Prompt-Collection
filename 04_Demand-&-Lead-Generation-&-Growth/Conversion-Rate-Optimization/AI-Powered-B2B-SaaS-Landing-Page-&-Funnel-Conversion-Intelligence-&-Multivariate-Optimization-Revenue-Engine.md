## Troubleshooting

**Problem: A/B test shows a winner but lift disappears or reverses after full rollout**
*Root cause:* Novelty effect (new visitors engage with "new" variant out of curiosity), or the test ran during an anomalous traffic period (seasonal spike, PR coverage, competitor outage driving unqualified traffic).
*Fix:* Always run tests for minimum 2 complete business cycles (typically 2-4 weeks minimum), never fewer than 7 days regardless of apparent significance. Check traffic source consistency between control and variant cohorts — if a PR spike hit during variant exposure, exclude that period. Use sequential testing / always-valid inference methods (Optimizely Stats Engine, Evan Miller's SPRT calculator) that don't require fixed sample sizes and adjust for peeking. Post-rollout: monitor CVR daily for 2 weeks and compare to pre-test baseline.

**Problem: The AI-generated recommendations are too generic and don't account for our specific niche or technical product**
*Root cause:* The prompt received insufficient qualitative input — without verbatim customer language, sales call transcripts, and competitive specifics, the output defaults to best-practice generalities.
*Fix:* Enrich the prompt with: (1) 5+ verbatim customer interview quotes about the problem they hired your product to solve, (2) 3 actual sales call objection transcripts (copy from Gong/Chorus), (3) 5 specific G2 or Capterra review excerpts (both positive and negative), (4) exact competitor landing page headlines and CTAs. Also specify your ICP with extreme precision: not "VP of Sales" but "VP of Sales at a Series B-D vertical SaaS company with a 10+ person outbound SDR team using Outreach or Salesloft." The model cannot infer specificity you haven't provided.

**Problem: Traffic volume is too low to run statistically meaningful A/B tests (fewer than 2,000 visitors/month)**
*Root cause:* A/B testing requires sufficient sample size to reach statistical significance within a reasonable timeframe. At <2,000 visitors/month, most tests would take 6-12 months to call, making the data stale before results arrive.
*Fix:* Shift strategy to: (1) implement "best practice + qualitative validation" changes without A/B testing — use 5-second tests via UsabilityHub or UserTesting.com to validate copy variants with your ICP, (2) use Hotjar or Typeform pop-up polls directly on the landing page to gather objection data, (3) run Bayesian A/B tests using tools like AB Tasty or Convert which require 30-40% less traffic than frequentist methods, (4) prioritize paid traffic growth to accelerate test velocity before investing heavily in multivariate experimentation. Document all changes with before/after screenshots and conversion data for trend analysis even without formal significance.
