## Troubleshooting

**Issue 1: LinkedIn audience match rates are below 20%, leaving most of the buying committee unreachable.**
- Solution: LinkedIn matches on email and name — work email match rates average 30-55%. Improve match rates by: (1) Uploading full name + work email + company domain (not personal emails), (2) Adding "first party opt-in" data flag to comply with LinkedIn's terms, (3) Using LinkedIn Company Targeting as a fallback for all target accounts — target by company name + job function + seniority to reach personas even when emails don't match, (4) Use LinkedIn Sales Navigator Team edition to build persona-specific prospect lists within target accounts and export those for more precise targeting

**Issue 2: Sales team isn't using the committee engagement intelligence in their outreach — adoption is low.**
- Solution: (1) Reduce friction — don't ask AEs to log into ad platforms; surface all data IN their CRM deal view and as a Slack notification, (2) Run weekly "deal reviews" using committee coverage data — make it a standard sales meeting agenda item so AEs see value of the data, (3) Provide templated outreach messages pre-written for each engagement signal ("I noticed someone from your finance team was researching our ROI model this week — happy to walk your CFO through it"), (4) Tie committee coverage score to forecast accuracy — show AEs that deals with 60%+ coverage close at higher rates

**Issue 3: Committee retargeting is consuming budget but not generating measurable pipeline velocity improvement.**
- Solution: (1) Verify your "control group" — compare accounts with active committee retargeting vs. similar accounts without it (difficult but essential), (2) Check if sales is responding to committee intelligence alerts — if coverage is high but AEs aren't following up, the pipeline acceleration benefit is lost, (3) Audit creative relevance — if CFO engagement rate is <0.5%, the message isn't resonating; test financial-first vs. risk-reduction vs. peer-comparison angles, (4) Ensure landing page alignment — if a CFO clicks a "CFO ROI" ad and lands on a generic product page, bounce rate kills the program; each persona-targeted ad MUST have a persona-matched landing page or gated asset
