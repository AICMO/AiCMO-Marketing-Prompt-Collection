## Overview
Designs and automates an end-to-end partner lifecycle marketing system that moves newly recruited channel, technology, and referral partners from onboarding through first-deal closed and into a sustained pipeline-generating state. Use this when your partner program is growing in headcount but not in partner-sourced revenue, when more than 50% of recruited partners never generate a single deal, or when you have no systematic process for re-engaging dormant partners.

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Partners complete onboarding but still don't register deals after 90 days.**
Solution: The issue is usually not enablement — it's opportunity identification. Add a "deal hunting session" call at Day 45 where the partner manager reviews the partner's existing client list and co-identifies the top 3 accounts who should be introduced to your product. Partners who receive a specific account target to pursue are 4× more likely to register a deal than those who receive generic training only.

**Problem: Partner health score is improving (emails being opened, portal logins increasing) but pipeline isn't materializing.**
Solution: High engagement + zero pipeline usually indicates the partner is learning but not actively selling — they're "interested but not committed." Trigger the co-sell play immediately: offer to join one of their existing client calls as a guest to demonstrate the product in context. The first live co-sell call converts partners to pipeline contributors at a dramatically higher rate than any amount of self-directed enablement.

**Problem: Dormant partner reactivation emails are being ignored.**
Solution: Email-only reactivation rarely works for dormant partners — they've mentally categorized your program as low-priority. Upgrade the channel: send a physical direct mail piece (a handwritten note with a $50 gift card to their local coffee shop and a 30-day pipeline incentive offer) to Tier 1 and Tier 2 dormant partners. Direct mail response rates for partner reactivation are 5–8× higher than email for cold partners. Budget $25–75 per dormant high-potential partner for physical outreach.
