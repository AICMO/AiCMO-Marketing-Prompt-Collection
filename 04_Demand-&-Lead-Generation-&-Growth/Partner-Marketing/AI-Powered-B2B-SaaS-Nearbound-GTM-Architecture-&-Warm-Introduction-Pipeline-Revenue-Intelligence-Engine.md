## Overview
Designs a complete Nearbound go-to-market system that converts your entire ecosystem — technology partners, customer champions, investors, employee networks, and community relationships — into a systematic warm introduction engine generating 2–5x higher win rates than cold outbound. Use this when cold outreach response rates fall below 3%, when you have 50+ active customers with strong business networks, or when you want to build a repeatable program where partner and customer relationships reliably produce pipeline instead of sitting dormant.

## Integration Tips

- **HubSpot/Salesforce**: Create a custom picklist value "Nearbound" under Lead Source. Add a sub-field "Nearbound Type" with values: Customer Intro, Partner Intro, Investor Intro, Employee Network, Community. Add a Contact Role type "Nearbound Champion" to Opportunities — this creates a complete attribution trail.
- **Crossbeam**: Connect Crossbeam → Salesforce via native integration. Auto-populate a custom Account field "Partner Overlap" with partner names when overlap is detected. This surfaces near-real-time warm intro opportunities directly in the CRM record without requiring SDRs to log into Crossbeam separately.
- **LinkedIn Sales Navigator**: Use the "Save to CRM" feature to log TeamLink connections directly to the Account record. Set a recurring Sales Navigator Alert for job changes at all Tier 1 target accounts — a job change is the single best nearbound trigger because the new hire is actively building relationships.
- **Slack**: Build a /nearbound slash command that queries Sales Navigator via API and returns "Your company has X people connected to [Target Company] at the VP+ level" — makes the program ambient and part of the daily SDR workflow rather than a separate tool to check.
- **Outreach/Salesloft**: Create a "Nearbound" sequence type — 3 steps over 10 days, not 8 steps over 28 days. Warm intros convert in 1–2 touches. A long sequence signals you don't trust the introduction.

## Troubleshooting

**Problem:** Champions accept the introduction request but the intro never gets sent — weeks pass with no action.
**Solution:** Never rely on the champion to draft the message. Always deliver a pre-written forwarding message they can send in 30 seconds. Follow up exactly 3 business days after the request with: "Hi [Name] — just wanted to check if the note to [Contact] landed in your sent folder OK. Happy to resend the draft if it got buried." This is the single highest-leverage action in the entire program.

**Problem:** Technology partner AEs agree to make introductions but nothing happens — they're too busy.
**Solution:** The partner AE has no incentive if the co-sell doesn't affect their quota. Before making any co-sell request, find out exactly how the partner AE's compensation works and position your product as something that helps their renewal or upsell. If the partner has an official co-sell program (like Salesforce's ISV co-sell tier), enroll in it so intros are trackable and the AE gets credit. If no formal program exists, offer to write a public case study featuring the partner's role in the deal — AEs value visibility to their own leadership.

**Problem:** The CRO dismisses nearbound pipeline because "we don't really know if the intro caused the win."
**Solution:** Build strict CRM discipline from Day 1. Every nearbound opportunity must have: (1) Lead Source = Nearbound set at creation, (2) a Contact Role record showing the champion/partner who made the intro with Role = "Nearbound Champion," (3) a logged activity record documenting the introduction chain. After 90 days, run a cohort analysis: "Opportunities with a logged Nearbound Champion closed at X% vs. Y% for opportunities without — here is the ARR impact." Present this data in the monthly pipeline review before the CRO asks for it.
