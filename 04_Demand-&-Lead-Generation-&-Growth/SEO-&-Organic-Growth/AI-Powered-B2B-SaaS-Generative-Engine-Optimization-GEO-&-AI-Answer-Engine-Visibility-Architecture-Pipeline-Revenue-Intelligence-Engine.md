## Integration Tips

**Perplexity & ChatGPT Monitoring Automation:**
- Use Make.com or Zapier to run a weekly API-powered monitoring workflow: send 30 tracked queries to ChatGPT API and Perplexity API, log responses to Google Sheets, flag any queries where your product is/isn't mentioned
- Tools like Profound.co, Share of AI (shareof.ai), and Semrush's AI Overviews tracker automate this at scale

**Content Production with AI:**
- Use Claude or GPT-4 to draft category guides, then layer in proprietary data/frameworks before publishing
- Use Perplexity to research what sources current AI responses are citing for your target queries — those are your link-building and coverage targets

**CRM Attribution:**
- Add "AI Search" as a lead source option in HubSpot/Salesforce and train your sales team to ask "How did you first hear about us?" to capture self-reported AI attribution
- Create UTM parameters for Perplexity referral traffic: utm_source=perplexity, utm_medium=ai-search

**G2 Review Velocity:**
- Build an automated post-onboarding review request sequence in HubSpot: trigger at Day 45 (after value is delivered), send personalized email with G2 link, follow up at Day 52 if no review. 100+ reviews is the threshold where AI models consistently cite you in category lists.

**llms.txt Implementation:**
- Create a /llms.txt file at your domain root (similar to robots.txt) with structured product description, key capabilities, ICP, customer outcomes, and links to key content — emerging standard that AI crawlers are beginning to use for structured brand context

## Troubleshooting

**Problem:** AI tools consistently recommend competitors but never our product despite good content.
**Solution:** Check robots.txt immediately — GPTBot, PerplexityBot, ClaudeBot may be blocked. Also audit if your primary content sits behind login gates or JavaScript rendering that AI crawlers can't parse. Create a "GEO-friendly" version of key pages with clean HTML and explicit structured data.

**Problem:** We appear in AI responses but only as a passing mention, not a recommendation.
**Solution:** AI models recommend products they can confidently describe. Build an entity page (like a detailed Wikipedia-style page about your product on your own domain), get a Wikipedia article if your company qualifies, and increase third-party coverage on high-authority sites (Forbes, Inc, TechCrunch, G2 editorial). Citation confidence correlates with the volume and authority of third-party mentions.

**Problem:** Can't attribute pipeline to AI search — leadership doesn't believe in the channel.
**Solution:** Implement a 30-day "AI search attribution sprint": add an AI Search option to your demo request form's "How did you hear about us?" field, brief sales team to capture it in discovery, and pull Perplexity/ChatGPT referral sessions from GA4. Even 5-10 self-reported AI-search deals in a quarter builds the internal business case for continued GEO investment.
