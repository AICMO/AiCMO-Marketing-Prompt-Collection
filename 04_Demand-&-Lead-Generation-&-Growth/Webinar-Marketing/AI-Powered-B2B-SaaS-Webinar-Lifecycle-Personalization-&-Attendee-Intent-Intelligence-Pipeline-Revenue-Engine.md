## Overview

This prompt engineers a fully automated, AI-driven webinar personalization system that orchestrates hyper-relevant experiences across every lifecycle stage — from account-targeted invitation to behavior-triggered post-event revenue conversion — using attendee intent signals to accelerate pipeline velocity. Use it when you want your webinar program to function as a precision demand-generation machine, not just a broadcast channel.

Design a complete AI-powered webinar lifecycle personalization system that includes:

   - Define a 0-100 attendee intent score based on: attendance duration, poll responses, chat activity, Q&A submissions, resource downloads during the session

   - Low engagement attendees: Content-led nurture sequence, no direct sales contact for 14 days

5. ATTRIBUTION & PIPELINE TRACKING:
   - Define the UTM/tracking structure for all webinar-sourced touchpoints
   - Specify which CRM fields to update post-webinar per attendee behavior

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Show rate is below 35% despite strong registration numbers.**
- Root cause: Invitation-to-confirmation gap (weak confirmation experience) or topic-audience misalignment. Run an A/B test on confirmation email CTA clarity and add a "what's in it for you" sentence specific to the registrant's role (pull from registration form data). Also audit whether registered attendees are genuinely ICP — if you're over-indexing on broad outreach, filter registration to ICP accounts only.

**Problem: Hot-tier attendees aren't converting to pipeline after AE outreach.**
- Root cause: AE outreach is too generic (not leveraging engagement signal) or the ask is too big (jumping to demo before value is established). Require AEs to reference the attendee's specific webinar Q&A question or poll response in their outreach. Offer a micro-commitment next step (5-min call, send a relevant 1-pager) rather than a full demo request. If the conversion rate is below 20%, schedule a weekly debrief between marketing and AE team to review hot-tier outreach quality.

**Problem: Can't track webinar engagement data in CRM accurately.**
- Root cause: Integration between webinar platform and CRM isn't syncing granular engagement data (just attendance Y/N). Most enterprise webinar platforms (Goldcast, ON24, Demio) offer native CRM integrations that push detailed engagement fields. If your platform doesn't, export the engagement CSV post-event and use a Make/Zapier flow to map columns to CRM fields. Alternatively, use a Google Sheet as an interim database and sync via HubSpot Data Sync.
