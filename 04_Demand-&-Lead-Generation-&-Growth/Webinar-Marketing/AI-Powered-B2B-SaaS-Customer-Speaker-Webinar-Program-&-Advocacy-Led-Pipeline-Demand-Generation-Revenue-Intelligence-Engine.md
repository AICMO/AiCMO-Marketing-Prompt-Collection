## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Customer speakers agree but cancel at the last minute**
Solution: Build a buffer system — always have 1 backup speaker scheduled for each webinar date. Create a "Speaker Reserve" list of 3-5 pre-briefed, pre-approved customers who can step in with 2 weeks notice. Offer a "Micro-Webinar" format (20 minutes instead of 45) as a lower-commitment entry point for reluctant speakers.

**Problem: Webinar attendance is high but pipeline conversion is low**
Solution: The audience isn't ICP-qualified. Audit your registration list — if >40% are from companies outside your ICP, tighten promotion targeting. Add a qualification question to the registration form ("What is your company's annual revenue?" or "How many reps are on your sales team?"). Use LinkedIn Matched Audiences to suppress existing customers and target only ICP-fit prospects in paid promotion.

**Problem: Customer speakers go off-message and oversell or undersell your product**
Solution: The briefing wasn't strong enough. Schedule a 30-minute speaker rehearsal (not optional), and provide a one-page "Things to Say / Things Not to Say" cheat sheet. Have a moderator (your marketer) co-host who can redirect with prepared bridging questions: "Sarah, can you tell us more about the specific workflow that changed?" — this naturally pulls the conversation back on track without making the speaker feel controlled.
