## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Registration is pacing 40% below target at the 8-week mark.**
Fix: Immediately launch a "Last Chance Early Bird" extension (cap it at 72 hours for urgency), activate speaker social amplification — send each confirmed speaker a Twitter/LinkedIn kit with 3 pre-written posts and ask them to share that week. Also pull your top 200 open opportunities and have AEs personally invite their champions with a "my company is hosting this and I want you there" message. These 3 levers typically recover 60–80% of a registration gap within 2 weeks.

**Problem: Attendees are engaged at sessions but no post-event meetings are converting.**
Fix: The problem is usually timing, not interest. If your follow-up goes out more than 48 hours post-event, conference energy dissipates. Restructure: have AEs send a personal Loom video (60 seconds) within 24 hours for Tier 1 accounts — "here's what I thought when I saw you in the [session name] session." Video follow-up generates 3–4x higher reply rates than text email in post-event contexts. Also review whether your CTA is too big — "book a full demo" is too heavy post-event; "15-minute debrief on your conference takeaways" converts significantly better.

**Problem: Sponsors feel they didn't get ROI and are threatening not to renew.**
Fix: The mistake was measuring sponsor ROI in badge scans instead of in pipeline attribution. Within 7 days post-event, deliver each sponsor a custom report showing: total impressions from their session + branded touchpoints, raw lead list of attendees who engaged with their booth or session, and — most powerfully — a pipeline attribution analysis showing any deals that touched their content. Sponsors who see one closed deal traced to their session renew at 90%+ rates. Build this report into the sponsor contract as a deliverable, and lock in renewal conversations at the event itself (Day 2 sponsor breakfast works well for this).
