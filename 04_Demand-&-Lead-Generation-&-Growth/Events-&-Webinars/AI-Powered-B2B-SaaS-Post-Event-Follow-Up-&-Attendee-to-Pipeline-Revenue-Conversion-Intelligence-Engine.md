## Integration Tips

- **Salesforce + Marketo:** Use Marketo program member statuses to auto-segment by tier. Map Marketo engagement score to Salesforce Lead Score field. Create SFDC Campaign Members for the event with tier tags for ROI reporting.
- **HubSpot:** Use workflow branching on form submission + webinar attendance data. Set up deal-creation workflows for Tier 1 leads with auto-assigned owners based on territory.
- **Outreach / Salesloft:** Use Marketo or HubSpot triggers to auto-enroll Tier 1 leads into a dedicated post-event sales sequence within minutes of session end. Use dynamic fields to pull in poll responses and Q&A data as personalization tokens.
- **Zoom Webinars:** Export Q&A and poll data immediately post-event. Use Zapier to push Q&A questions submitted per attendee into their CRM contact record as an activity note — this becomes the personalization anchor for Tier 1 outreach.
- **ON24:** Leverage ON24's engagement scoring natively and sync to Marketo/Salesforce via their native integration. Set engagement score thresholds to trigger Marketo smart campaigns.
- **Slack:** Configure Salesforce or HubSpot to push a Slack alert to the #sales-hot-leads channel the moment a Tier 1 lead is identified. Include all engagement context in the alert.
- **6sense / Bombora:** Layer intent data on top of event engagement. An attendee in a high buying-intent account (6sense Stage 4–6) who is also Tier 2 engagement should be auto-upgraded to Tier 1 routing.
- **Gong / Chorus:** After demo calls are booked, tag all event-sourced calls in Gong for call review. Analyze win rates and deal velocity vs. non-event sources to calibrate future event investment.

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Tier 1 email response rate is below 8% despite personalization.**
Solution: Audit the personalization tokens — generic "we noticed you attended" language performs no better than blast emails. The email must reference the attendee's specific engagement: their exact question, the poll answer they chose, or the specific session moment they reacted to. If engagement data is unavailable, fall back to account-level personalization (industry news, recent company trigger event, competitor the account likely evaluates). Also check send timing — emails sent within 90 minutes of event end consistently outperform those sent 24 hours later.

**Problem: Sales team isn't following up on event leads within the SLA window.**
Solution: Remove human discretion from the routing step. Use Outreach or Salesloft to auto-enroll Tier 1 leads into a sales-owned sequence automatically — the rep doesn't need to manually start it. Simultaneously, send a Slack alert with all context AND automatically create an overdue task in CRM. Escalation logic should auto-notify the SDR manager if a Tier 1 lead is not contacted within 3 hours. Run weekly SLA compliance reporting and review in the Monday pipeline standup.

**Problem: No-show replay email generates high clicks but zero pipeline.**
Solution: The replay itself is not a pipeline signal — it's a re-engagement signal. Don't route replay clickers to sales immediately. Instead, score them and treat high engagement (watched 60%+ of replay) as equivalent to a Tier 3 live attendee. Add a second email in the replay sequence that presents a low-friction next step: a diagnostic assessment, a template download, or a peer benchmark report — something that identifies buying intent beyond content consumption. Only route to sales after a second behavioral signal confirms interest.
