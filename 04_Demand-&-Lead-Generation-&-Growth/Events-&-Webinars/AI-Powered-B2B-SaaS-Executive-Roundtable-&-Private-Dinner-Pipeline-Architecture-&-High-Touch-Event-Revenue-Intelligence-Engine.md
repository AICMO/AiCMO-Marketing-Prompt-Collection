## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Invitation acceptance rate below 25% despite personalized outreach**
*Solution:* Acceptance rate is primarily driven by perceived peer caliber, not your product's desirability. Audit your invitation copy — if your company name appears in the subject line or first sentence, you've signaled a sales event, not a peer exchange. Reposition as "[Industry] Executive Roundtable, curated by [your company name as convener]." More importantly, name-drop 2-3 confirmed attendees in every invitation — acceptance rate increases 40-60% when executives see specific peers they respect attending. If your TAL is cold, use mutual LinkedIn connections to make introductions before the formal invitation, reducing cold outreach to warm referral.

**Problem: Strong attendance but no post-event pipeline — Track A emails get no replies**
*Solution:* Buying signals were either not present or not captured. Post-event, hold a mandatory 15-minute AE debrief — if AEs cannot name 2-3 specific things each Target A attendee said that indicated urgency or pain, the signal capture system failed. Fix this with structured in-event tools: provide AEs a simple 5-question "signal capture card" to fill out during or immediately after the event. Additionally, audit your Track A email copy — if it references a generic point from the event rather than something the specific person said, it reads as mass outreach. The email must prove you were listening to them specifically.

**Problem: Difficulty attributing closed revenue to the event program — sales disagrees on credit**
*Solution:* Define attribution rules in advance and get CRO sign-off before running the first event. The cleanest framework: (1) Sourced pipeline — event is source if opportunity was created within 60 days of attendance AND no prior opportunity existed at that account. (2) Accelerated pipeline — event is credited with acceleration if the deal closed 30+ days faster than segment average; calculate this by comparing velocity for event-attendee deals vs. similar-stage deals from the same quarter. (3) Never claim both source and acceleration credit for the same deal. Document these rules in a RevOps governance doc and present them at the next QBR before the event program launches.
