## Troubleshooting

**Problem:** Low meeting show rate (below 50%) despite confirmed bookings.
**Solution:** Implement a 2-step confirmation system: calendar invite immediately upon booking + day-before text/WhatsApp from the AE (not the SDR). Offer a clear agenda so they know the 30 minutes is worth protecting. Add a "what would make you cancel?" question at booking to surface objections early.

**Problem:** HOT leads go cold within 2 weeks post-event.
**Solution:** The first 48-hour follow-up is almost always the failure point. Check whether emails are personalized (specific conversation reference) vs. generic. Add a pattern-interrupt: send a Loom video follow-up referencing a specific thing they said at the event. Attach the ROI calculation you did on the spot at the booth.

**Problem:** CRM data is incomplete or inconsistent across team members after the event.
**Solution:** Run a 30-minute CRM data blitz the evening of the last event day — team together, reviewing each lead, filling MEDDIC fields while memory is fresh. Make this a team ritual, not optional. Use a shared lead log (Google Sheet works fine) during the event and batch import on the final evening.
