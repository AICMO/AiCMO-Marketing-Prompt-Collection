Builds a complete Substack publication strategy that converts your company's expertise into a compounding, subscriber-driven demand generation engine — one where Substack's recommendation algorithm distributes your content to new ICP readers autonomously, and subscriber emails flow directly into your CRM as high-intent pipeline signals. ## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Growth stalls after initial warm audience — open rates drop, subscriber growth slows**
*Solution:* This happens when you've exhausted your warm network and Substack's algorithm hasn't started amplifying yet. Fix: aggressively pursue 3 mutual "Recommend" exchanges with publications that have 2,000+ subscribers in adjacent niches. One successful cross-promotion can add 200–500 new subscribers in a week. Also audit your last 6 issue subject lines — if they're descriptive rather than curiosity-driving, rewrite your next 4 to lead with a counterintuitive claim or a number.

**Problem: Lots of subscribers but zero pipeline attribution — sales team says "we never hear people mention it"**
*Solution:* The conversion architecture isn't active. Audit: (1) Are you importing subscribers to CRM with enrichment? (2) Is lead scoring configured and are thresholds calibrated? (3) Is the SDR team trained on Substack subscriber outreach? Most common failure: subscriber emails live in Substack but are never synced to the CRM. Fix the sync first, then verify the SDR sequence is actually enrolling high-score contacts.

**Problem: Writers are inconsistent — issues ship late, quality varies, some weeks nothing publishes**
*Solution:* Treat it like a product, not a project. Assign one Editor-in-Chief with weekly editorial authority (not the CEO). Build a 6-week issue backlog before launch. Create a brief template that any writer can fill in 20 minutes, enabling a ghostwriter or AI draft to be produced from the brief. Consider hiring a fractional newsletter editor (8-10 hrs/month) to manage production workflow, edit for voice consistency, and keep the calendar on schedule.
