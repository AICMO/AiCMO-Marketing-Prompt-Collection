## Overview
This prompt architects a fully automated Digital Deal Room (DDR) and self-serve buyer evaluation experience that lets B2B buyers progress through complex evaluations on their own terms — without waiting for sales responses. Use it when your buyers are self-directed (spending 70%+ of their journey in dark social, review sites, and peer networks before talking to sales) and your sales cycle stalls because the evaluation experience is fragmented, slow, or entirely dependent on rep bandwidth.

## Integration Tips

- **Aligned.io / DealHub / Recapped:** Dedicated deal room platforms with built-in analytics, stakeholder tracking, and engagement alerts — use these for the deal room shell; connect to CRM via native Salesforce/HubSpot integrations
- **Notion + Tally:** Low-cost alternative for early-stage companies — build deal room in Notion, use Tally for the ROI calculator, track engagement via Notion analytics; export to HubSpot via Zapier
- **Salesforce / HubSpot CRM:** Map every deal room engagement signal to a custom field or activity log — build a Deal Room Engagement Score as a calculated field to surface in pipeline reviews
- **Highspot / Seismic:** Use as the content layer inside the deal room — both platforms track individual asset engagement at the stakeholder level and feed signals back to CRM automatically
- **Loom / Vidyard:** Record personalized 90-second video welcomes for each stakeholder and embed in their deal room section — personalized video increases deal room engagement by 40%+ vs. text-only welcome
- **DocuSign / PandaDoc:** Automate NDA execution and security document release — trigger document delivery when buyer reaches the trust center section; track completion as a high-intent signal
- **Slack (Sales Rep Notifications):** Configure deal room platforms to push Slack alerts when high-intent signals fire — e.g., "🔥 [CFO Name] at [Company] just completed the ROI calculator and viewed pricing for the 2nd time"
- **Zapier / Make (formerly Integromat):** Orchestrate cross-tool automations — deal room engagement → CRM update → sales rep Slack alert → automated follow-up email from rep's address

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Champions share the deal room link but colleagues never access it.**
Solution: The champion needs a "warm handoff script" — write a 3-sentence Slack/Teams message template the champion sends when sharing the link. Frame it around the colleague's specific concern ("I shared this with you specifically because of the security section — it addresses all the questions Legal usually raises"). Add a stakeholder-specific landing page so each person sees content relevant to their role first, not the full room.

**Problem: Deal room analytics show visits but no deep engagement (everyone bounces after 1-2 pages).**
Solution: The room has a navigation problem or the first content visible is the wrong content for that buyer's stage. Audit the first three sections: are they demanding too much cognitive effort upfront? Apply the "Rule of One" — each section should have one primary CTA, not six assets competing for attention. A/B test leading with the ROI calculator vs. customer story vs. interactive demo to find what creates the first "pull" into deeper exploration.

**Problem: Dormancy re-activation sequences aren't working — emails get ignored.**
Solution: Dormancy is rarely about the deal room content — it's usually an internal political or budget issue the champion is navigating. Switch from content-push to intelligence-pull: instead of "Here's a new resource," ask "What's standing in the way for you right now?" A direct, human, low-pressure question from the rep (not an automated sequence) reactivates more stalled deals than any content asset. Use the deal room's last-viewed section as context for the question.
