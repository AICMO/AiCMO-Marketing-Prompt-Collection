## Troubleshooting

**Issue: Security questionnaire responses feel too generic and CISOs push back saying they don't answer their specific questions.**
*Fix*: The most common cause is that company context variables (cloud provider, subprocessors, encryption standards, pen test dates) are left as generic placeholders rather than replaced with real company data. Security professionals immediately identify AI-generated generic responses. Every response in Module 2a must reference your actual certification audit dates, your actual cloud infrastructure (e.g., "AWS us-east-1, with SOC 3 report available at aws.amazon.com/compliance"), and your actual pen test vendor and date. Run your completed questionnaire responses through a "specificity check" — every response should contain at least one number, one date, or one named third-party that can be independently verified.

**Issue: The security review package is being sent but the CISO isn't engaging with it — still asking for a formal questionnaire process.**
*Fix*: CISOs are conditioned to run their own process, not yours. The package is not a replacement for their questionnaire — it's a supplement that pre-answers most questions so the questionnaire takes hours instead of weeks. Reframe the package in the CISO welcome letter: "I've sent this so that when your team sends us your standard questionnaire, 80% of it is already answered. We'll respond to the remaining questions within 48 hours." Also, ensure you're publishing to Whistic or a similar security network — many enterprise procurement teams now pull vendor security profiles directly instead of sending custom questionnaires.

**Issue: Sales reps aren't engaging the CISO early enough — they introduce security review only after the CFO approves budget, adding months to the cycle.**
*Fix*: Add a mandatory CRM field that captures "CISO / Security Team Identified" as a required deal field at the Evaluation stage (not the Negotiation stage). Build a deal health score that flags deals without CISO contact as "at risk" after 30 days in evaluation. Marketing should also create a "CISO early engagement" email template that sales reps can send within the first week of evaluation — positioning it as proactive transparency rather than vendor pressure: "We know security review will be part of your process — here's our complete security posture now so you can loop in your team whenever you're ready."
