**Difficulty:** Advanced | **Time:** 25-30 min | **Tags:** customer-marketing, onboarding-automation, first-90-days, time-to-value, churn-prevention, expansion-revenue, lifecycle-marketing, b2b-saas, hubspot, salesforce, marketing-automation, customer-success

## Overview

Most B2B SaaS companies hand off new customers to Customer Success and let marketing go silent — then wonder why 30–45% of early-stage churn happens before the 90-day mark. This prompt architects a fully automated marketing-driven onboarding program that runs parallel to CS, accelerates feature adoption through behavioral triggers, identifies champions in the first 30 days, builds executive relationships before QBRs, and sequences the first expansion conversation at precisely the right moment. Use it immediately after a new customer contract is signed to compress time-to-value, reduce early churn, and plant the seeds for expansion revenue before the first renewal cycle.

Build me a complete 90-day new customer marketing program:

1. **ONBOARDING WELCOME SEQUENCE (Days 1–7)** — Role-segmented email sequences for Champion, Economic Buyer, and End Users. The Champion gets a "you made the right call" validation email within 2 hours of contract signing with a 90-day success roadmap. The Economic Buyer gets a 1-sentence email from the VP of Customer Success (automated, looks 1:1) confirming their investment is in good hands. End Users get a "you've been invited" activation sequence that makes first login feel like an exclusive access event, not IT onboarding.

2. **ADOPTION TRIGGER CAMPAIGNS (Days 8–45)** — Behavioral automation triggered by product usage events: If a user hasn't logged in within 5 days of provisioning → send feature-value email showing what peers in their role accomplish in their first week. If a user completes first core action → send a "you just did something most teams take 3 weeks to figure out" celebration email. If adoption stalls at <30% of licensed seats → alert CSM and trigger a parallel champion email with an internal adoption toolkit to share with their team.

3. **CHAMPION DEVELOPMENT SEQUENCE (Days 15–45)** — A 5-touch sequence that transforms the economic buyer who signed the contract into an internal advocate: Day 15 — personalized "early wins" summary using actual product data from their account; Day 25 — introduction to your Customer Advisory Board or power user community; Day 35 — LinkedIn connection request from your VP of CS (templated, sent via LinkedIn automation); Day 40 — invitation to co-present their early results at an upcoming webinar or customer panel; Day 45 — first "expansion readiness" signal: if they're using 80%+ of licensed features, flag to AE for an upsell conversation.

4. **EXECUTIVE RELATIONSHIP PROGRAM (Days 30–60)** — Automated executive-to-executive touchpoints: Day 30 — CEO or VP sends a "one month in" personal note (automated, but personalized with actual account data); Day 45 — invitation to exclusive executive roundtable or EBR prep call; Day 60 — "innovation preview" email: early access to upcoming features relevant to their use case, making the economic buyer feel like an insider before the formal QBR.

5. **END USER MOMENTUM PROGRAM (Days 1–60)** — Gamified adoption campaigns for the end users doing the daily work: Weekly "team leaderboard" email showing usage leaders across their team (triggered only if 3+ users active); "Achievement unlocked" emails when users hit specific milestones (first integration, first export, first collaborative forecast); A 30-day check-in survey with an AI-generated insights summary delivered back to each respondent, making them feel heard.

6. **EXPANSION READINESS SEQUENCE (Days 60–90)** — A 3-touch pipeline for identifying and activating expansion conversations: Day 60 — Usage intelligence email to Champion: "Here's what your team has accomplished + the 2 capabilities your fastest-growing peers add at this stage"; Day 75 — ROI milestone email: "Based on your usage, here's what we estimate your team has saved/earned" with hard numbers pulled from product data; Day 90 — Expansion CTA: a specific, non-pushy invitation to a "strategic growth session" with the AE to review Year 2.

Output: Complete email copy for each sequence, behavioral trigger specifications, CSM alert templates, adoption milestone definitions, and CRM/MAP workflow logic. Every step must run autonomously — no "schedule a call to discuss this with your team" cop-outs.

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Welcome emails are arriving 4–6 hours after contract signing, making them feel automated rather than personal.**
Solution: The delay is typically in the DocuSign → Salesforce webhook → HubSpot sync chain. Map the trigger to fire directly from DocuSign's webhook (or the CRM contract object update) rather than waiting for a daily CRM sync. For Economic Buyer emails specifically, pre-draft the message before contract signing and use a 30-minute auto-send delay after the trigger fires — this feels intentional rather than instant, without the awkward 4-hour gap.

**Problem: End-user adoption emails are going to spam, especially for enterprise accounts with strict email filtering.**
Solution: Enterprise IT departments often block marketing automation domains. Solve this with three tactics: (1) Route end-user onboarding emails from a company subdomain (onboarding@yourcompany.com) rather than a marketing automation sending domain; (2) Include a plain-text version of all onboarding emails — spam filters penalize HTML-heavy templates; (3) For accounts > 250 employees, have the CSM send the first end-user welcome email manually (or semi-manually via a 1-click HubSpot template) before automating subsequent touches.

**Problem: The Day 75 ROI email is generating replies but the AE isn't following up in time, killing the expansion conversation.**
Solution: Set a Salesforce Task auto-created for the AE when the Champion replies to the Day 75 email, with a 24-hour due date and a "HIGH PRIORITY — EXPANSION SIGNAL" label. Simultaneously send an internal Slack notification to the AE and their manager: "🟢 [Company Name] Champion replied to ROI email — expansion signal confirmed. Task created in Salesforce." The 24-hour follow-up window is critical — response rates drop 68% if the AE waits more than 48 hours.
