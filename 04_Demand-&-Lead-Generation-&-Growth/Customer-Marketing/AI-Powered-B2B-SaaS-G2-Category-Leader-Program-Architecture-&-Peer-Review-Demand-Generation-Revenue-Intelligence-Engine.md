## Integration Tips

**G2 + Salesforce/HubSpot:**
- G2 Buyer Intent integrates natively with Salesforce and HubSpot via G2's integrations marketplace
- Map G2 intent account data to Account object in CRM; trigger workflow when G2 Intent score exceeds threshold
- Create custom field: `G2_Intent_Score`, `G2_Last_Profile_Visit`, `G2_Competitor_Pages_Viewed` to enable segmentation

**Review Request Automation:**
- HubSpot: Use Workflow trigger on NPS response property change → delay 2 hours → send personalized email from enrolled contact's owner
- Salesforce + Marketo: Trigger on Customer Health Score milestone or renewal date approach → Marketo smart campaign with CSM token merge
- ChurnZero / Gainsight: Configure in-app NPS trigger → automated G2 review request with deep link

**G2 Buyer Intent → Sales Sequence:**
- Outreach/Salesloft: G2 fires webhook → creates Prospect + adds to G2-Intent sequence automatically
- Set up Slack alert to SDR/AE channel when Tier 1 G2 intent account fires: include account context, contact suggestions from LinkedIn Sales Navigator, and personalized email draft

**G2 Analytics:**
- Pull G2 profile view data monthly → load into Google Sheets or Looker for trend analysis

**Incentive Compliance:**
- G2 gift card program: available through G2's official program only — always disclose in review request

- TrustRadius: No paid incentives allowed — focus on authentic timing (post-onboarding, post-renewal)

## Troubleshooting

**Issue: Review request emails getting low open rates / reviews not coming in**
- Solution: Move review ask from marketing email to direct CSM email (1:1 personal send from CSM's own inbox vs. marketing automation platform) — personal send typically 40-60% higher open rates. Use plain text email, not HTML. Shorten the ask to 2-3 sentences. A/B test subject lines: question format ("Can I ask a quick favor?") consistently outperforms statement format.

**Issue: G2 Buyer Intent firing for too many non-ICP accounts, overwhelming SDR capacity**
- Solution: Layer G2 intent with ICP firmographic filtering before triggering SDR alert — require match on company size + industry + account in CRM (known account) to trigger Tier 1 action. Route unmatched intent accounts to marketing nurture sequence only. Set SDR daily cap on G2 intent activations to preserve high-quality pursuit.

**Issue: Reviews are coming in, but average rating is declining / mixed reviews hurting badge status**
- Solution: Root cause analysis — run NPS segmentation to identify which customer cohort is leaving mixed reviews (by product tier, use case, onboarding path, or CSM). Implement "pre-review health check" in CSM workflow: only send review request to customers with last NPS ≥ 7 and no open critical support tickets. Prioritize addressing root cause product/service issues before re-engaging disappointed customers for reviews.
