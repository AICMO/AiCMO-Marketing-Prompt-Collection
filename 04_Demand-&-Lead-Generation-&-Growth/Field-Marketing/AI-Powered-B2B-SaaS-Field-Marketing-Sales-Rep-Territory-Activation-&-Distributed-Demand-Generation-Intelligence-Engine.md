## Integration Tips

- **Salesforce / HubSpot CRM**: Create a custom "Territory Activation Source" picklist field on Opportunity with values: Self-Sourced Outbound, Field Event, Local Digital, Partner-Assisted. Use automation to alert rep when intent scores update for territory accounts. Build a rep-specific dashboard showing self-sourced pipeline by week.
- **Outreach.io / Salesloft**: Import all signal-triggered email sequences as locked templates with rep-customizable fields (account name, signal detail). Use the "Account-Based Sequence" feature to group all contacts at target accounts so the rep works one account at a time rather than contact-by-contact.
- **LinkedIn Sales Navigator**: Save custom lead lists per territory event invite pool. Activate job-change, mention, and news alerts for all Tier 1 account contacts to surface trigger-based outreach automatically. Use InMail credit strategically for Tier 1 contacts who don't accept connection requests.
- **6sense / Demandbase / Bombora**: Configure territory-specific intent dashboards. Push weekly top-10 signal accounts automatically to rep's CRM task queue every Monday morning. Integrate intent scores into opportunity routing rules so highest-intent accounts get immediate rep notification.
- **Eventbrite / Google Forms**: Use for event registration — collect name, title, company, and one qualifying question ("What's your biggest [pain point] challenge right now?"). Feed responses into CRM before the event so rep knows each attendee's context walking in.
- **Calendly**: Embed in every email template as the CTA. Set up a specific event type for "Territory Intro Call" (20 minutes) with custom confirmation and reminder emails referencing the specific signal that prompted the outreach.

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Reps say they don't have time for field marketing activities on top of their quota.**
Solution: Frame the program as pipeline replacement, not addition — "This is how you generate the pipeline that replaces cold calling." Pre-load all sequences in Outreach/Salesloft so rep actions are 1-click execution, not writing from scratch. Cap rep time commitment at 2 hours/week max; document the time required per activity so it's transparent. Track self-sourced pipeline separately in CRM so reps see direct credit for their marketing activity.

**Problem: Event attendance is poor — 40% show rate on confirmed RSVPs.**
Solution: Shift from RSVP to calendar-hold confirmation — require attendees to accept a calendar invite (not just reply "yes") to create commitment. Add a co-host (peer executive, investor, or analyst) to the invite to remove the vendor perception and increase show rate. Send a text reminder 24 hours before for anyone where you have a mobile number. Limit events to 15 people max — smaller room size creates FOMO and increases show rate vs. large events.

**Problem: Pipeline is being created but not attributed to field activities — credit going to centralized marketing.**
Solution: Establish a mandatory "Event Touch" logging rule: within 24 hours of any event, rep logs an activity on every attendee account in CRM with tag "[Event Name] — Attended." Set up a Salesforce/HubSpot workflow to flag any new opportunity created within 90 days of that touch as "Influenced — [Event Name]." Run a monthly attribution audit comparing event attendee lists to new opportunities; use the Campaign Influence object to capture multi-touch attribution without overwriting the primary source field.
