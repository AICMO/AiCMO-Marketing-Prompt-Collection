## Overview
This prompt architects a complete territory-based field marketing program for B2B SaaS companies — designing regional demand generation campaigns, trade show strategy, executive engagement sequences, and field marketing ROI attribution by geo. Use it when you need to translate a national pipeline target into a city-by-city, rep-by-rep execution plan that field marketers and regional sales managers can run in lockstep.

**DELIVERABLE 2: TERRITORY PROGRAM ARCHITECTURE**

For each priority territory, design three signature program types:

**Program Type 1: Demand Creation Campaign (90-day pipeline build)**

*Format options (choose 1-2 per territory per quarter):*
- Practitioner Workshop (20-40 attendees): hands-on skill-building session led by customer practitioner or internal expert, non-salesy, provides immediate take-home value. Best for: early-stage category awareness, practitioner ICP who needs education before purchase consideration.
- Executive Breakfast/Roundtable (8-15 CXO-level attendees): invitation-only peer discussion on a burning strategic issue, facilitated by external moderator or respected voice, no product pitch. Best for: high-ACV deals, economic buyer access, top-of-funnel executive relationship building.
- Industry Meetup Sponsorship (100-300 attendees): sponsor or co-produce an existing practitioner community event that draws your ICP. Lower cost, higher reach. Best for: brand awareness in dense ICP metro, filling top of funnel, SDR prospecting lists.

*Campaign architecture for each program:*
- Invitation campaign: [T-6 weeks] targeted LinkedIn outreach + personalized email from AE + field marketer to named ICP target list; [T-3 weeks] second invitation wave with agenda and speaker confirmed; [T-1 week] final confirmation sequence with logistics and pre-read content
- Pre-event ABM support: 4-week geo-targeted display campaign in territory cities featuring pain point content (not product) to warm ICP accounts before event; retarget event registrants with mid-funnel content in the 2 weeks prior
- Day-of experience design: physical environment, session flow, networking architecture, conversation guide for AEs, and exit survey instrument
- Post-event follow-up architecture: [T+24 hrs] personalized email from AE with session recap and relevant case study; [T+3 days] marketing sends curated insights document and related content; [T+7 days] SDR cadence begins for non-AE-owned contacts; [T+14 days] field marketer sends NPS survey and "did you find this valuable?" check-in; [T+30 days] AE reviews all event contacts in pipeline review with field marketer

**Program Type 2: Pipeline Acceleration Campaign (for existing open opportunities)**

*Format options:*
- Executive Sponsor Dinner (6-10 attendees): invite 3-4 prospects currently in active deals alongside 2-3 customer references at similar stage/industry. The customer-to-prospect ratio is the secret — buyers trust buyers more than vendors. Field marketer facilitates introductions. AEs attend but do not pitch.
- Deal-Specific Microsite + Direct Mail Sequence: for deals $100K+ ACV stuck in mid-funnel, produce a hyper-personalized "strategic plan for [Company Name]" that includes industry benchmarks, ROI model pre-populated with their data, and 2-3 reference customer stories from their exact industry. Deliver via FedEx with a handwritten note from the regional VP.
- Customer Site Visit Program: arrange for prospect to visit a customer in same city or industry who can serve as a live reference. Field marketer coordinates logistics, prepares the customer host, and creates a follow-up brief.

*Acceleration metrics:*
- Stage velocity: measure average days-in-stage for deals that received field marketing acceleration touch vs. control group
- Win rate lift: track close rate for pipeline that received ≥1 field marketing interaction vs. no-touch pipeline
- Multithreading: measure buying committee contacts added after field event engagement

**Program Type 3: Customer Expansion Campaign (existing customers, NRR growth)**

*Format options:*
- Regional User Group (30-80 customers): quarterly or bi-annual customer gathering in top-density metros. Mix product roadmap previews, practitioner skill-shares, and peer networking. Designed to drive feature adoption, multi-product expansion, and reference generation.
- Customer Advisory Board (CAB) Chapter Session: bring the national CAB on the road to regional markets, giving local customers access to executive leadership and peer strategy conversations. Signals investment in regional customers.
- Co-hosted Industry Event with Anchor Customer: partner with a marquee customer in the territory to co-produce an industry education event. Customer gets visibility, you get their network.

*Expansion revenue linkage:*
- Map each customer attendee to open expansion opportunities in CRM
- Track product usage changes 60 days post-event (leading indicator for upsell readiness)
- Generate reference candidates from engaged attendees within 30 days of event

**DELIVERABLE 3: TRADE SHOW & CONFERENCE SELECTION FRAMEWORK**

*Evaluation criteria for each event under consideration:*

**Tier 1: Must-Attend Flagship Events** (highest ROI, full sponsorship investment)
Criteria: ICP density >500 attendees matching your buyer persona; vendor floor includes ≥3 direct competitors; opportunity for speaking slot or workshop session; location overlaps with top 2-3 pipeline-priority territories; prior year demonstrated sourced pipeline >3x sponsorship cost.

Budget model: [Sponsorship + booth + travel + staff + pre/post show campaign] = $[amount]; Required pipeline yield to justify: [3x spend = $Y]; Required meetings to generate pipeline yield: [number, using historical meeting-to-opportunity conversion rate]

**Tier 2: High-Value Regional Events** (selective investment, field marketing execution)
Criteria: 100-500 ICP attendees in a high-priority territory; opportunity for speaking, hosting a private dinner, or breakout session co-production; cost <$25K all-in; AE from local territory can attend and own follow-up.

**Tier 3: Monitoring Only** (analyst/intelligence gathering, no investment)
Criteria: ICP attendance unclear; competitor-heavy; no clear pipeline return path. Field marketer sends 1 rep to gather competitive intelligence and build analyst relationships, no booth or sponsorship.

**Tier 4: Pass** (decline)
Criteria: ICP concentration <50 qualified attendees; location disconnected from pipeline-priority territory; event organizer unwilling to share attendee list or post-event lead data; previous year demonstrated <1x pipeline return.

*Pre-show campaign architecture (for Tier 1 and 2 events):*
- [T-8 weeks] Identify full target list of ICP attendees using event registration data, LinkedIn Sales Navigator, and ABM platform; build named account list with AE assignment
- [T-6 weeks] AE-personalized LinkedIn InMail + email to top 50 target accounts requesting pre-scheduled meeting at the event
- [T-4 weeks] Geo-targeted digital campaign in event city targeting ICP job titles with "Will you be at [Event Name]?" messaging and CTA to book meeting
- [T-2 weeks] Follow up with all registered contacts who haven't responded; confirm all scheduled meetings with agenda
- [T-0, at event] Field marketer manages meeting logistics, tracks real-time scans and conversations, escalates high-intent contacts to AE within 4 hours

*Post-show pipeline conversion system:*
- [T+24 hrs] Field marketer sends personalized follow-up email to all leads scanned with segmented message by conversation type (hot/warm/cold)
- [T+48 hrs] AE personal outreach to all hot leads; SDR sequence begins for warm leads
- [T+2 weeks] Field marketer audits CRM for all event contacts — ensures 100% have been logged with next step
- [T+30 days] Field marketer presents post-event pipeline report with MQL count, opportunity count, pipeline ARR, and estimated influenced revenue at next sales-marketing alignment meeting

**DELIVERABLE 4: EXECUTIVE ENGAGEMENT PROGRAM ARCHITECTURE**

*Executive Dinner Playbook:*

Design the signature executive engagement format for high-ACV deals ($75K+ ACV):

**Selection criteria for executive dinner invitees:**
- Prospects: Economic buyer (VP+ or C-suite) at accounts in active Stage 2+ opportunity; OR economic buyer at high-priority target accounts with no prior contact; mix 60% prospects / 40% customers
- Customers: Reference customers at same company stage, industry, and deal size as prospect invitees; customers who are active users and can speak to business outcomes; customers who have approved reference participation
- Internal attendees: 1 AE (attends, does not pitch), 1 field marketer (facilitates logistics), 1 executive sponsor (VP of Sales, CMO, or CEO depending on prospect seniority). Rule: internal attendees should never outnumber prospect+customer attendees.

**Dinner execution model:**
- Guest list: 6-10 invitees maximum (larger is a cocktail party, not a relationship dinner)
- Venue selection: Private dining room at a restaurant known in local market; avoid hotel ballrooms; invest $150-$300 per person food/beverage
- Conversation design: Pre-agreed discussion topic (not product — a shared industry challenge); prepared discussion guide with 5-7 conversation starters; designated facilitator role
- No slides, no pitches: the entire format depends on its reputation as a peer conversation, not a vendor event
- Post-dinner follow-up: AE sends personal note within 24 hours referencing a specific conversation moment; field marketer sends "thank you + resource" to customers who attended within 48 hours; non-salesy pipeline progression tracked in CRM

**Executive Dinner Program KPIs:**
- % of dinner prospects advancing to next pipeline stage within 30 days: target ≥40%
- % of dinners resulting in ≥1 new qualified opportunity: target ≥60%
- Customer reference re-engagement rate post-dinner: target ≥50% willing to do future reference call
- Cost-per-qualified-opportunity from executive dinner program: benchmark vs. other pipeline sources

**DELIVERABLE 5: SALES-FIELD MARKETING ALIGNMENT ARCHITECTURE**

*Joint Territory Planning Process:*

**Quarterly Territory Planning Meeting (QTPM) — 90 minutes per territory:**
Agenda:
1. Pipeline coverage review: current open pipeline vs. 3x quota coverage target by territory (15 min)
2. ICP target account prioritization: top 20 target accounts this quarter, ranked by intent signal and ICP fit (20 min)
3. Program calendar agreement: which field programs will run in territory this quarter, with AE commitments for invitee sourcing and post-event follow-up (20 min)
4. Event sponsorship review: which trade shows AE wants to attend, pipeline generation plan for each (15 min)
5. Marketing asks from AEs: content gaps, competitive assets, customer references needed (15 min)
6. Field marketing asks from marketing: AE participation in event follow-up, introduction sourcing for target accounts, feedback on program quality (5 min)

**Weekly Territory Sync (15-minute standup, optional for low-pipeline-coverage territories):**
- AE reports on post-event follow-up completion (% of contacts logged in CRM with next step)
- Field marketer reports on upcoming program registrations and any at-risk events (low registration)
- Shared dashboard reviewed: pipeline generated this week from field marketing programs

**SLA Structure (Marketing-to-Sales):**
- Field marketer delivers: Qualified MQLs from events within 24 hours of event end; pre-event target account list 4 weeks before each program; post-event pipeline report within 5 business days
- AE delivers: Invitee list for each field program ≥3 weeks in advance; post-event follow-up completion within 48 hours; CRM logging of all event conversations within 24 hours of event

**Conflict Resolution Protocol:**
When AE does not follow up on event leads: Field marketer flags at weekly sync → if no action by T+5 days, SDR team takes over follow-up → AE receives weekly report showing "leads still open without next step" → quarterly territory review includes follow-up compliance rate as a metric

**DELIVERABLE 6: FIELD CONTENT & ASSET FACTORY**

*Content system designed around the field marketing calendar:*

**Pre-event content (customized for each territory program):**
- Invitation letter: AE-personalized, references a specific account trigger (job posting, funding announcement, product launch, news event) and connects it to the event topic. Not a mass email — designed to feel like a personal note.
- Territory-specific landing page: features local customer logos, local speaker (if applicable), and testimonial from a customer in same geo. Not the generic national event page.
- Pre-read content package: 1-2 page briefing on the event discussion topic with 3-5 data points relevant to the invitee's industry and role. Sent 72 hours before event.

**At-event content:**
- Facilitator guide: discussion questions, timing, how to handle dominant voices, how to pivot if conversation stalls
- Leave-behind package: physical or digital, includes: 1-pager summary of event discussion themes (printed night-of or morning-after for physical delivery), customer ROI case study in invitee's industry, personalized QR code linking to a dedicated post-event content hub
- Survey instrument: 3-question paper card or digital (via QR): "What was most valuable?" / "What topic should we discuss next?" / "Would you attend again and bring a peer?" (captures referral intent)

**Post-event content (delivered within 72 hours):**
- Event recap document: 400-500 word synthesis of key discussion themes, 3-5 "insights from the room," and 2-3 actionable takeaways. Authored in AE's voice. Positions the company as the convener of smart thinking in the space.
- LinkedIn thought leadership post: AE or company page post highlighting discussion themes (not product features). Tags participants who have agreed to be tagged. Drives organic reach and surfaces the event to non-attendees.
- Sales follow-up sequence: 3-touch sequence customized to the event theme, starting with the recap, progressing to a relevant case study, ending with a soft CTA to a discovery call

**Content governance:**
- Field marketer owns template library; creates master versions quarterly
- AE personalizes 20% of each asset (account-specific details, personal relationship reference)
- Marketing ops ensures all field content assets are tagged in CRM/MAP for attribution tracking

**DELIVERABLE 7: FIELD MARKETING ATTRIBUTION & ROI SYSTEM**

*Attribution model:*

**Event-sourced pipeline:** Opportunity was created in CRM within 90 days of an event, the contact who became the primary buyer attended the event, and no prior CRM activity exists for that contact at that account. Attributed 100% to field marketing.

**Event-influenced pipeline:** Opportunity existed prior to event OR the attended contact is not the primary buyer, but the attending contact is part of the buying committee and event interaction advanced the relationship. Attributed a weighted fraction (typically 25-40%) to field marketing per multi-touch attribution model.

**Event-accelerated pipeline:** Opportunity was stuck in the same pipeline stage for >30 days; after field marketing interaction, it advanced to next stage within 14 days. Attributed acceleration credit to field marketing.

*Reporting cadence and format:*

**Weekly field marketing report (for internal field team):**
- Events completed this week: attendance, MQL count, CRM logging completion %
- Events coming next week: registration count vs. target, at-risk events
- Pipeline movement: opportunities that advanced stage this week with field marketing attribution

**Monthly field marketing report (for CMO and VPS):**
- Pipeline sourced MTD and QTD vs. target
- Pipeline influenced MTD and QTD vs. target
- Program ROI by event type (cost-per-sourced-opportunity by program format)
- Territory performance: which territories are above/below pipeline target
- Top 5 deals with field marketing influence currently in active pipeline

**Quarterly business review (for board/exec team):**
- Field marketing pipeline contribution as % of total company pipeline
- Sourced pipeline 3x ROI on spend: [field marketing budget $X generated $Y in pipeline, $Z in closed won]
- Benchmark vs. prior quarter and prior year same quarter
- Territory heat map: visual representation of pipeline density by city
- Top programs by ROI: rank event types by pipeline generated per dollar invested

*Attribution tooling requirements:*
- CRM (Salesforce / HubSpot): custom field for "Field Marketing Program" on opportunity record; event campaign tagged on contact record; campaign membership driving pipeline attribution reports
- MAP (Marketo / HubSpot): all event registrations imported as campaign members; behavior scoring applied for event attendance (attendance = +25 behavioral points in lead scoring model)
- ABM platform (6sense / Demandbase): territory-level intent surge monitoring; event registrant enrichment; post-event retargeting audience activation

## Success Metrics

**Leading Indicators (measured weekly):**
- Event registrations vs. target (target: hit 120% of target seat count 1 week before each event)
- AE invitee sourcing compliance: % of AEs who submitted target account lists 3 weeks before event (target: ≥90%)
- Post-event CRM logging rate: % of event contacts entered in CRM with next step within 24 hours (target: ≥85%)
- Pre-event digital campaign performance: CTR on geo-targeted campaigns ≥0.4%, CPL ≤$85

**Lagging Indicators (measured monthly/quarterly):**
- Marketing-sourced pipeline from field programs: target ≥2x quarterly field marketing budget
- Pipeline influenced by field programs: target ≥5x quarterly field marketing budget
- Cost-per-sourced-opportunity by program type: executive dinner ≤$8,500, practitioner event ≤$4,200, trade show ≤$12,000
- Stage advancement rate: ≥35% of pipeline with field marketing touch advances at least 1 stage within 30 days of event
- Win rate on field-influenced pipeline vs. non-field pipeline: target ≥10 percentage point lift

**Program Health Indicators:**
- NPS from event attendees: target ≥55
- AE satisfaction with field marketing partnership (quarterly survey): target ≥8/10
- Event repeat attendance rate: ≥40% of attendees attend ≥2 programs in a rolling 12-month period
- Customer reference participation rate in field programs: ≥30% of eligible customer references participate in at least 1 event per year

## Integration Tips

**Salesforce / HubSpot CRM:**
- Create a custom "Field Marketing Program" picklist field on the Campaign object; map all field events to this field for program-type reporting
- Build a "Field Marketing Pipeline" report view filtered by campaign type = Field Marketing; create quarterly dashboard visible to CMO and VP Sales
- Set up automated task creation when a contact is added to a field marketing campaign: "Field Marketing Event Follow-Up Due In 24 Hours" assigned to owning AE

**Marketo / HubSpot Marketing Automation:**
- Build event-specific program templates with pre-event invitation sequence, day-of reminder, and 5-touch post-event nurture sequence; clone and localize for each event
- Create smart lists by territory + ICP fit score for targeted event invitations; suppress list includes any prospect who attended ≥1 event in last 30 days (avoid over-inviting)
- Set behavioral scoring: event registration +15 points, event attendance +25 points, post-event content engagement +10 points per asset; pass to sales at 100+ cumulative score

**6sense / Demandbase (ABM Platform):**
- Use intent keyword clusters to identify accounts in territory-level surge (keywords: [your category] + "evaluation" + "alternatives" + "pricing"); prioritize these accounts for next field program invitation list
- Build geo-filtered audience segments by territory; activate 4-week pre-event display campaign targeting ICP job titles within 50-mile radius of event venue
- Post-event retargeting: build audience of all event attendees; run 2-week "follow the conversation" campaign with event recap content + next CTA

**Splash / Eventbrite / Bizzabo (Event Management):**
- Integrate with CRM so all registrations auto-create campaign members in Salesforce; auto-append UTM source = "field-marketing" for attribution
- Automate reminder email sequences triggered by registration date: T-7 days, T-1 day, day-of logistics
- Post-event: auto-pull attendance list and sync to CRM within 2 hours of event end; trigger post-event email sequence immediately

**Postal.io / Sendoso (Gifting Platform):**
- Set trigger: when AE tags an executive dinner prospect as "confirm attending," auto-queue a day-before gift delivery (curated local artisan item, $35-50); Sendoso or Postal.io handles fulfillment
- Build a "thank you" automation for all event attendees: 48 hours post-event, field marketer approves dispatch of personalized thank-you note with a relevant book or resource

**Gong / Chorus (Conversation Intelligence):**
- Build a field marketing mention tracker: alert field marketer when AE mentions a field marketing event in a follow-up discovery call (keyword: event name); confirms attribution and post-event engagement quality
- Pull call snippets where prospects reference the field event positively; use as program testimonials for internal field marketing case studies shared with sales team

## Troubleshooting

**Problem:** AEs submit invitee lists 2 days before the event, too late to run a real invitation campaign.
**Solution:** Make invitee submission a hard dependency in the event planning process — if list isn't received 3 weeks before the event, the event is cancelled or budget is reallocated to a territory whose AE is engaged. Communicate this policy in the quarterly planning meeting, not when it happens. Frame it as protecting AE's pipeline opportunity, not punishing non-compliance.

**Problem:** Event attendance is consistently low (less than 50% of registered seats fill).
**Solution:** Diagnose root cause — (1) invitation list quality: are you inviting real ICPs or generic contacts the AE added to "hit a number"? Audit list against ICP criteria before finalizing. (2) Venue/format mismatch: is the event format genuinely valuable or does it feel like a sales pitch in disguise? Have a senior customer serve as co-host to signal peer credibility. (3) Invitation timing: are invitations going out 4+ weeks ahead with a specific agenda? Last-minute invitations produce last-minute declines.

**Problem:** Field marketing pipeline attribution is disputed by sales leadership — "those deals were already in motion before the event."
**Solution:** Shift the conversation from "sourced vs. not sourced" to "influenced pipeline velocity." Track how quickly deals advance through stages when they have ≥1 field marketing touch vs. no-touch pipeline. Present stage velocity and win rate lift data rather than arguing about attribution credit. When you can show field-influenced deals close 18 days faster at a 12-point higher win rate, budget debates become pipeline conversations.
