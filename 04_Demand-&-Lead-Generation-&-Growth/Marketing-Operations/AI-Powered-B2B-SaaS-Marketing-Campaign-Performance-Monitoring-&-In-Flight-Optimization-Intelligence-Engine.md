## Overview
Diagnoses every active marketing campaign in real time, identifies which programs are over-performing or underperforming against plan, and generates a prioritized optimization action list — budget reallocation decisions, creative pivots, audience adjustments, and pause/scale triggers — so demand gen and marketing ops teams can maximize pipeline output without waiting for the end-of-month report. Use this weekly (or daily during high-spend periods) to catch problems early, capitalize on winning programs, and defend your budget with data before the quarterly board meeting.

## Integration Tips

**HubSpot Marketing Hub:**
- Pull campaign performance data from HubSpot's Campaign Analytics report (Marketing > Campaigns > Analytics) — export to CSV and paste into the Advanced Version SECTION 2
- Use HubSpot's "Compare to Goal" feature in campaign settings to auto-calculate pacing percentages before pasting
- Budget reallocation recommendations can be executed directly in LinkedIn Campaign Manager and Google Ads Manager — HubSpot doesn't control budgets but tracks conversions

**Salesforce + Pardot / Marketing Cloud Account Engagement:**
- Use Pardot's Campaign Influence Report (Type 1: First Touch, Type 2: Last Touch, Type 3: Even Distribution) to populate the Pipeline Contribution column
- Salesforce Campaign Hierarchy report shows pipeline influenced across parent/child campaign structure
- Run optimization prompt output through Salesforce Campaign Update fields to keep historical record

**Google Looker Studio / Tableau:**
- Connect Google Ads, LinkedIn Ads, and CRM data into a single Looker Studio dashboard (use Supermetrics or Funnel.io as connectors)
- Set up automated weekly export of this dashboard data to feed the prompt — eliminates manual data gathering
- Build a "Campaign Health Monitor" dashboard with RAG (Red/Amber/Green) status calculated automatically using conditional formatting

**Notion / Google Sheets Workflow:**
- Copy the Campaign Health Scorecard output into a shared Google Sheet with your demand gen team
- Add a "Decision Made" and "Action Owner" column — use this as your weekly campaign optimization tracker
- Archive previous weeks' scorecards to track improvement over time and identify recurring underperformance patterns

**Slack Integration:**
- Use Zapier to trigger a weekly Slack message to your #demand-gen channel with the latest campaign pacing data, prompting the team to run the prompt and share results

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: The AI generates generic recommendations like "improve your ad creative" without specific actions.**
Solution: In the Quick Version, make sure you paste actual CPL numbers, click-through rates, and conversion rates — not just budget and MQL counts. The more specific your input, the more specific the output. If you can add "Variant A: 0.68% CTR, Variant B: 0.22% CTR" to your creative details, you'll get specific variant recommendations instead of general advice.

**Problem: The pipeline impact estimates in optimization recommendations seem too high or unrealistic.**
Solution: Anchor the AI by providing your own internal CPL-to-pipeline conversion math in the context: "Our average pipeline-per-MQL is $4,200 based on $18K ACV × 23% MQL→SQL rate." This forces the AI to use your actual unit economics rather than generic benchmarks, which may not match your company's sales efficiency.

**Problem: Campaign data takes too long to gather manually — this analysis becomes a half-day project.**
Solution: Build a master "Campaign Performance Paste Sheet" — a Google Sheet template pre-formatted with all the fields the Advanced Version requires (budget, spend, KPIs, CPL, etc.). Update it weekly in 20 minutes from your HubSpot/Salesforce reports and Google Ads exports. Once built, running this optimization cycle should take under 30 minutes total including AI analysis time.
