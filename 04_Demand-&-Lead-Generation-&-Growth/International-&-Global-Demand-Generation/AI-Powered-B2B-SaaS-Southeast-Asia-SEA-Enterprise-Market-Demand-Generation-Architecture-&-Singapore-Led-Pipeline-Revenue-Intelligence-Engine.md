## Troubleshooting

**Issue 1: AWS ISV Accelerate co-sell opportunities are being registered but no AWS Enterprise Sales team is engaging on joint pursuit — pipeline is not moving.**
- Root cause: AWS co-sell engagement requires the ISV (you) to actively manage the relationship with the specific AWS Enterprise Account Manager (EAM) covering each target account; co-sell is not automatic — AWS EAMs have 50-100 accounts and need a compelling reason to prioritize your ISV opportunity in their account.
- Solution: (1) Request a co-sell alignment meeting with your AWS ISV BD contact and the specific EAMs covering your top 10 target Singapore accounts; in this meeting, explain why your product creates a larger AWS footprint (more EC2, RDS, or Lambda usage) — AWS EAMs respond to deals that increase AWS consumption; (2) Register at least 5 co-sell opportunities in ACE (AWS Customer Engagement) portal immediately — AWS ISV Accelerate requires minimum engagement activity to maintain active partner status; (3) For Singapore financial services accounts, ask AWS if these are EDP (Enterprise Discount Program) customers — if yes, your software sold through Marketplace applies toward their EDP commitment, which makes the EAM highly motivated to co-sell; (4) Offer to co-host an AWS-branded executive briefing for 8-10 Singapore enterprise accounts — AWS's Marketing Development Funds (MDF) may cover part of the cost, and joint AWS + Nexelerate branding dramatically increases executive attendance and engagement.

**Issue 2: Singapore enterprise deals are stalling in the MAS TRM vendor review phase — IT risk teams are requesting documentation you don't have, and deals that seemed close are now silent for 8+ weeks.**
- Root cause: MAS TRM vendor review is a formal process that requires specific documentation categories (cloud security architecture, BCP/DR documentation, incident response procedures, subprocessor disclosures, penetration testing results, audit report) — missing even one category causes IT risk teams to pause evaluation until documentation is provided.
- Solution: (1) Build a MAS TRM Vendor Assessment Package proactively — don't wait for a prospect to request it; use the MAS TRM Guidelines (November 2021, publicly available on MAS.gov.sg) to identify the 8-10 documentation categories financial institutions will request; (2) Engage a Singapore-based cybersecurity consultant (Bishop Fox Singapore, Mandiant Singapore, or Trustwave Singapore) to conduct an external penetration test and issue a findings report — this is the most commonly missing document that stalls Singapore FinServ deals; (3) Develop a pre-filled MAS TRM self-assessment questionnaire template that your sales engineers can complete for each financial institution prospect in under 2 hours (rather than custom-building each time); (4) For deals stalled in TRM review, request a technical call between your CISO (or Head of Security) and the bank's IT risk team directly — peer-level security conversations resolve documentation questions faster than sales-team intermediation.

**Issue 3: Indonesia partner introductions are happening but deals aren't progressing — local SI partners are presenting your product to enterprise accounts but no POC agreements are being signed.**
- Root cause: Indonesia SI partners are typically presenting your product as part of a broader solutions catalog without the deep product conviction required to win a competitive evaluation; they may also be positioning at the wrong buyer level (presenting to IT managers rather than business function decision-makers who feel the pain your product solves).
- Solution: (1) Run a joint account strategy session with your Indonesia SI partner for each stalled account — understand who they've spoken to, what objections arose, and what the prospect's specific pain is; in 80% of cases, you'll discover the SI presented to IT rather than the VP Sales / Head of Revenue Operations who feels the problem and has budget authority; (2) Offer to co-present at the next stalled account meeting — attend in person if possible (Jakarta quarterly trip); your direct presence as the software vendor alongside the SI partner signals commitment to the Indonesia market and resolves the "this vendor doesn't take Indonesia seriously" perception; (3) Create a 10-page Bahasa Indonesia executive presentation specifically for Indonesia SI partner sales calls — the SI partner should use this, not your English-language global deck; the localized presentation signals that you understand Indonesian enterprise priorities; (4) Implement a co-selling incentive for Indonesia SI deals: for any deal the SI partner brings to a signed POC agreement within 90 days, increase their margin by 5% — this creates urgency and prioritization of your product within the partner's deal pipeline.
