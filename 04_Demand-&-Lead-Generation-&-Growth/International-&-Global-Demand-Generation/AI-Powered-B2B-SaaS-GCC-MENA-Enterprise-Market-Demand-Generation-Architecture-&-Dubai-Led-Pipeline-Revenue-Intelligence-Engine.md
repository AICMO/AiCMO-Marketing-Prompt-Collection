## Troubleshooting

**Problem: GITEX generated 200+ business cards but zero qualified pipeline after 3 months**
Solution: GITEX networking is quantity-oriented; pipeline requires deliberate post-show qualification. Within 48 hours of show close: segment all contacts into Hot (had a substantive conversation about a real business problem), Warm (expressed interest but vague), Cold (collected card opportunistically). Send personalized follow-up to Hot contacts within 5 business days — not a generic "great meeting you" email but a message referencing your specific conversation with a relevant insight or case study. Warm contacts enter a 6-touch nurture sequence over 90 days. Cold contacts enter email newsletter only. GITEX pipeline conversion requires human-led follow-up, not automated marketing sequences.

**Problem: SI partner committed to collaborate but has sent zero introductions after 3 months**
Solution: SI partnership activation in GCC is relationship-dependent and requires ongoing executive investment — the business card exchange and commercial agreement signing is just the beginning. Schedule monthly lunch meetings (not calls) with your SI alliance contact in Dubai; bring a concrete account list of 5-10 named target accounts and ask which ones they have active relationships in; offer to co-fund a joint workshop or GITEX dinner for the SI's clients; the first SI-introduced opportunity typically requires 6-9 months of consistent relationship investment. If the SI contact is not generating introductions after 4 months, escalate to their Partner level rather than continuing at the business development level.

**Problem: KSA government prospect is interested but says "we need to finalize our annual IT budget" — deal has stalled 6+ months**
Solution: KSA government budgeting is cyclical and tied to the Saudi fiscal year (January-December) with budget approval typically finalizing in November-December for the following year. If you entered the pipeline in Q2, expect no decision until Q4 at earliest. Maintain the relationship during the stall: monthly WhatsApp message (preferred communication channel for Saudi business contacts over email) with a relevant industry insight or Vision 2030 program update; arrange a site visit to your team's Riyadh office (even a temporary co-working visit signals commitment); connect your SI partner to the prospect to maintain momentum through the stall period.
