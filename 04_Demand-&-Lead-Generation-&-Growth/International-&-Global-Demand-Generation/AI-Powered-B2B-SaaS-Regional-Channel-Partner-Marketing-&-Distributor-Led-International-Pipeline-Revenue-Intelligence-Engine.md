## Overview
This prompt architects a channel partner marketing program that generates international pipeline through local resellers, value-added resellers (VARs), system integrators (SIs), and distributors — enabling B2B SaaS companies to scale demand generation in EMEA, APAC, and LatAm without building large local direct marketing teams. Use it when entering new geographies, scaling beyond your initial market entry footprint, or when direct demand gen is cost-prohibitive in a region.

## Integration Tips

- **Salesforce PRM / Partner Portal:** Use Salesforce's Channel Management or Alliances module to automate deal registration workflows; set up partner-specific campaign records with auto-populated UTM source fields so pipeline attribution is automatic — eliminates spreadsheet-based MDF tracking that falls apart at scale
- **HubSpot:** Create a "Partner" contact property and company record to segment partner contacts from direct prospects; build a partner-specific pipeline stage ("Partner Registered") before SAL handoff so partner-sourced deals are visible separately from direct deals throughout the funnel
- **PartnerStack / Impartner:** Connect partner portal to CRM via native integration; configure automatic MDF request workflows triggered by completed campaign proof-of-execution uploads (screenshot of email blast, event attendance list, webinar recording link)
- **LinkedIn Campaign Manager:** Use partner company page as a "boosted" audience signal for your paid social campaigns — target followers of your partner's company page who match your ICP title filters; budget $500–$1,000/month per active partner for co-branded LinkedIn thought leadership ads amplifying partner content
- **Canva for Teams / Figma:** Create a partner co-branding template library in Canva Teams or Figma with locked brand elements and editable partner logo zones; share template link with partners so they can self-serve co-branded social graphics without submitting a brand request to HQ
- **Zoom Webinar / Hopin:** Standardize on a single webinar platform across all partner co-marketing events; pre-build a "partner webinar package" (registration page template, reminder emails, post-event follow-up sequence) that can be cloned for each new partner event in under 30 minutes

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Partner signed the agreement but hasn't run any marketing activities after 60 days**
Solution: This is the most common failure mode in channel marketing. The fix is almost always a lack of a specific next action, not lack of interest. Schedule a 30-minute "First Campaign Planning Session" within 2 weeks of onboarding — come with a pre-built campaign brief for one specific co-marketing activity, a draft email for the partner to send to their list, and a concrete date for the event. Partners don't plan campaigns from scratch; they execute campaigns HQ designs for them. If a partner still won't act after 2 structured nudges, reclassify them to Tier 3 and reallocate MDF to more active partners.

**Problem: Partner is generating MQLs but they're converting to pipeline at less than half the rate of direct-sourced leads**
Solution: This signals an ICP mismatch — the partner's customer base doesn't match your ICP, or the campaign messaging attracted the wrong contacts. Audit the last 20 partner-sourced MQLs: What company size, industry, and title are converting vs. not? If the partner's audience is systematically outside your ICP (e.g., they serve SMBs but your sweet spot is mid-market), restructure to co-market at the right company size tier or deprioritize this partner. Also check whether partner deal registration is capturing post-event follow-up — sometimes partners log leads who expressed interest but haven't been through a proper qualification conversation.

**Problem: Partner is requesting MDF for activities that don't generate pipeline (branded merchandise, office renovations, general advertising)**
Solution: This is common when MDF guidelines are vague. Fix it upstream by publishing a clear Eligible MDF Activities List before any MDF is allocated — e.g., "Eligible: customer-facing events, webinars, co-branded digital ads, lead generation content. Ineligible: partner internal training, non-co-branded advertising, merchandise without lead capture component." For existing inappropriate requests, decline politely with a redirect — "We can't fund general advertising, but we can fund a joint webinar to your customer list on [topic] — here's a campaign brief we already built for exactly this." Partners respond better to alternatives than hard no's.
