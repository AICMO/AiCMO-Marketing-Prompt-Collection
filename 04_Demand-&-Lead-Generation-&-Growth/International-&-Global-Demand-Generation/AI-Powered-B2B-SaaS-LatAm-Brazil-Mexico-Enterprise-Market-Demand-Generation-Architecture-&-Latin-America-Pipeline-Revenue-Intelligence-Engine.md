## Troubleshooting

**Problem**: WhatsApp outreach getting blocked or low response rates in Brazil.
**Solution**: Brazilian buyers have WhatsApp fatigue from spam; your first message must be hyper-relevant and use the buyer's name + company + specific pain point. Never send the same template message twice to the same contact. Use voice messages (áudio) in Step 4 — they cut through text noise and are culturally natural in Brazil. Ensure your WhatsApp Business Profile shows a professional company name, verified status, and response time under 1 hour (set auto-reply for off-hours).

**Problem**: Mexico pipeline stalling at Director level, never reaching C-suite approval.
**Solution**: Mexican enterprise buying requires explicit C-suite endorsement but C-suite rarely takes cold outreach. Build a "Padrino Strategy" — identify which Consulting/Advisory firm the CFO or CTO of your target company trusts (often a Big 4 or niche CDMX boutique), engage that firm first, and let them introduce you upward. Also build an ROI model specifically for Mexican context (peso-denominated savings, regulatory risk reduction, peer company references) that the Director champion can use in internal presentations to their VP.

**Problem**: Brazil deals dying at contract stage due to FX or payment terms disputes.
**Solution**: Proactively offer a BRL-denominated contract option with an FX adjustment clause tied to PTAX (Banco Central do Brasil's official rate). Alternatively, offer a USD contract with 12x monthly installments (parcelamento) — this is psychologically easier for Brazilian finance teams than a lump annual payment. Engage dLocal or Stripe LatAm early in deal stages so payment processing is never the reason a deal dies.
