## Overview
This prompt builds a UK and Northern Europe demand generation architecture for B2B SaaS companies entering or scaling across the UK, Ireland, Nordics (Sweden, Norway, Denmark, Finland), and Benelux (Netherlands, Belgium, Luxembourg). **UK Adaptation Guidelines**:
- Replace American superlatives ("world-class," "#1 platform," "10x faster") with specific, substantiated claims ("reduces onboarding time by 40% on average, based on 50 customer deployments")

Best sending windows: Tuesday–Thursday, 7:30–8:30am GMT (catches UK commuters on mobile) or 12:15–1:00pm GMT (lunch scroll). Avoid Mondays (high email volume day) and Friday afternoons entirely.
