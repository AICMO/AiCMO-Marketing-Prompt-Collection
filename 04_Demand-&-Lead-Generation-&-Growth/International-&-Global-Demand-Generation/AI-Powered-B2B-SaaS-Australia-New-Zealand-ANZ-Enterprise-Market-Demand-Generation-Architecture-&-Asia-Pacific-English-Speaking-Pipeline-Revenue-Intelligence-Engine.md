## Troubleshooting

**Problem**: US case studies and references are not resonating with ANZ enterprise buyers.
**Solution**: Australian buyers need to see Australian proof. Prioritize capturing even one ANZ customer case study — a one-page PDF with a recognizable Australian brand name outperforms ten US case studies. If you have no ANZ customers yet, create a regulatory-landscape guide for your category in Australia (e.g., "APRA's Operational Risk Guidance and what it means for [your category] teams") — this demonstrates local knowledge without requiring a local customer logo.

**Problem**: Outbound response rates from Australian contacts are very low.
**Solution**: Australian senior executives have high skepticism toward US-style cold outreach. Three fixes: (1) Lead with something specifically Australian — reference a local regulation, a local industry event, or a local challenge they face that US companies ignore; (2) Shorten your sequence — Australians respond poorly to 7+ touch aggressive cadences; a 3-touch sequence spaced 10–14 days apart performs better; (3) Warm-up via LinkedIn engagement before emailing — comment on 2–3 of their posts, connect with a brief relevant note, then email 1–2 weeks later as a "known" contact.

**Problem**: Government procurement opportunities are taking too long to materialize.
**Solution**: Australian government sales cycles are long (12–24 months for enterprise contracts) but are predictable and transparent via AusTender. Short-term fix: target state government agencies which have faster procurement than federal; identify Small Business exemptions (under AUD $80,000 for some agencies, under $200,000 for some state jurisdictions) that bypass formal tender processes; engage the Digital Marketplace for federal government if your product qualifies. Medium-term: register on the relevant whole-of-government panel in your category — once registered, you'll be invited to quote on opportunities without a full tender process.
