## Troubleshooting

**"Our reply rates are good but prospects seem creeped out by how specific we are"**
Audit your signal reference language. Avoid phrases like "I noticed you visited our website" or "I can see you're researching [category]" — these feel surveillance-like. Instead, reference public, congratulatory, or easily discoverable signals ("Saw the funding announcement on TechCrunch" / "Noticed the job posting for RevOps Analyst"). If using intent data, translate it into a reasonable inference rather than a data disclosure ("Given the scaling I'm seeing in your market segment..." vs. "Our data shows your team has high intent for contract management tools").

**"We're getting high reply rates on Tier 1 but our SDRs can't keep up with the research volume"**
Build a Clay.com research automation that does 80% of Tier 1 research automatically: pulls LinkedIn recent posts, company news from last 30 days, funding details from Crunchbase, and tech stack from BuiltWith. Give SDR a 5-minute review step rather than 30-minute research step. Reserve true 30-minute deep research for accounts with ACV potential above $X threshold only.

**"Our signals are firing but by the time SDRs reach out, the window has passed"**
Set up real-time Slack alerts for Tier 1 signals (under 2-hour response target) vs. daily digest for Tier 2-3. Build an auto-enrollment rule in your sequencer so Tier 1 accounts get enrolled in sequence immediately on signal detection, with SDR notified to customize Touch 1 before it sends (use a 4-hour delay buffer). Measure signal-to-first-touch latency weekly and make it a team metric.
