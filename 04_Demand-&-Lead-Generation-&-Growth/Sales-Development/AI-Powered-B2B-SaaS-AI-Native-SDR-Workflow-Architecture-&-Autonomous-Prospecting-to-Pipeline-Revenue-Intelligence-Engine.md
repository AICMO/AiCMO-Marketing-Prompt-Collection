## Troubleshooting

**Problem: SDRs are copy-pasting AI output without review, leading to generic messages that feel robotic.**
Solution: Implement a mandatory "SDR Edit Gate" — the workflow requires the SDR to modify at least one sentence in each AI-generated first touch before launching. Make the AI output editable (not locked), and track edit rates as a quality metric. Recognize SDRs who add the most distinctive personalization, not just those who send the most emails.

**Problem: AI account briefs are pulling outdated information (company context is 6+ months old).**
Solution: Set data freshness rules in Clay and your AI agent configuration — only surface news and signals from the last 90 days for trigger events, last 12 months for funding/financials. Build a "data freshness score" into the account brief header so SDRs know when to manually verify. Add a quick-check step for LinkedIn to confirm contact is still at the company before sending.

**Problem: AEs are not using the handoff packages and are asking prospects the same discovery questions SDRs already answered.**
Solution: Run a 30-minute AE training session on using the package (15-minute timebox for reading before every first call). Create an AE accountability metric: first-call advancement rate should improve 20%+ after SDR handoff packages. Add AE feedback loop (thumbs up/down) to each handoff package so AI learns what format AEs find most useful. Escalate persistent non-use to sales leadership as a revenue operations problem, not an SDR problem.
