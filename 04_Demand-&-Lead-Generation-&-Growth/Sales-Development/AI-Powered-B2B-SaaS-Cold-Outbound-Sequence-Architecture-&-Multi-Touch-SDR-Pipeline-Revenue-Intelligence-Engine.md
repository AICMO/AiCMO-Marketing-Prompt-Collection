## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Emails landing in spam or Gmail promotions folder**
Root cause is almost always one of three issues: domain reputation, email structure, or sending volume. Check domain health in Google Postmaster Tools — aim for "High" sender reputation. Remove all links and images from touches 1–2 (links trigger spam scoring). Keep plain-text formatting throughout. Reduce sending volume to under 50 emails/day per mailbox during the first 30 days of a new domain. Verify SPF, DKIM, and DMARC DNS records are correctly configured. If deliverability issues persist after these fixes, use a separate sending subdomain (mail.company.com vs. company.com) and warm it with human-like sending patterns over 4–6 weeks before scaling.

**Problem: High open rates (45%+) but near-zero reply rates (under 1%)**
Your subject line is working but the body copy isn't earning a response. The most common culprits: CTA friction is too high (asking for a 30-minute demo vs. a 15-minute call), the value proposition reads like marketing copy rather than a peer-to-peer conversation, or the email is too long (over 150 words). Fix in this order: (1) reduce CTA to a yes/no question or a 15-minute time ask, (2) strip every sentence that doesn't directly earn a reply — aim for 75–100 words, (3) add a P.S. with a micro-question ("P.S. Is forecast accuracy even on your radar right now?"), (4) switch psychological driver from authority to curiosity or social proof.

**Problem: Replies are overwhelmingly negative, angry, or unsubscribe-heavy (over 2%)**
You are reaching the wrong ICP, wrong persona, or the pain statement doesn't match their reality. Before rewriting copy, diagnose the list: are 30%+ of accounts the firmographic profile of your actual customers? If list quality is poor, fix the data layer first — copy cannot rescue a fundamentally wrong audience. If list quality is validated, rewrite the pain statement using verbatim language from your last 5 customer win interviews — not internal assumptions. Replace abstract pain claims ("improve forecasting efficiency") with concrete, quantified pain ("8 hours/week reconciling spreadsheets"). Run a 30-contact test with a completely different opening angle before rebuilding the full sequence.
