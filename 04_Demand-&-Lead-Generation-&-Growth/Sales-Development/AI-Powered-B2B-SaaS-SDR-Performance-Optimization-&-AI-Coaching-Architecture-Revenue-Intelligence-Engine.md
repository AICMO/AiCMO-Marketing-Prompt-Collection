## Integration Tips

- **Outreach / Salesloft**: Export sequence-level performance reports weekly and feed into the A/B testing framework. Use Outreach AI or Salesloft Rhythm to trigger coaching sequences directly in the platform based on performance conditions
- **Gong / Chorus**: Tag top-performing discovery calls with a "coaching gold" label. Use Gong Deals to correlate SDR outreach message quality to downstream pipeline conversion rates
- **Salesforce**: Create a custom "SDR Coaching Score" field that tracks each SDR's adoption of AI coaching recommendations — this becomes a leading indicator for pipeline in the following month
- **Clay**: Build a "coaching signal" column that flags accounts where the SDR has been attempting contact for 30+ days with no reply, triggering an automatic message rewrite recommendation
- **6sense / Bombora**: Feed intent signal data into the sequence prioritization module so high-intent accounts always rise to the top of each SDR's daily work queue, reducing wasted outreach on cold accounts
- **Slack**: Build a Zapier or native integration that delivers weekly coaching briefs to each SDR's Slack DM every Monday morning at 8:00 AM — removes friction of logging into a separate coaching tool

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: SDRs ignore or don't act on AI coaching recommendations**
Solution: Don't deliver coaching as a report — deliver it as a specific 1-action task. Instead of "Your reply rate is low, here's advice," say: "Your one task for Tuesday: Rewrite your Step 1 email opener using this BASHO template, send to these 10 accounts, and reply to this message with what you wrote." Track completion, not just delivery. Gamify adoption with a leaderboard showing which SDRs have completed the most coaching experiments.

**Problem: A/B tests take too long to reach statistical significance**
Solution: Reduce the number of variables you test simultaneously. Many B2B SDR programs have too small a sample size to test more than 2-3 hypotheses per quarter at 95% confidence. Drop to 80% confidence for directional learning, increase outreach volume per variant, or aggregate results across the entire team rather than individual SDRs. Use tools like Evan Miller's A/B test calculator to pre-calculate minimum sample sizes before launching any test.

**Problem: Top-performer replication creates cultural resentment on the team**
Solution: Never present the replication framework as "everyone needs to do what [top performer] does." Instead, position it as: "Our team found these patterns work best for our ICP, and we want everyone to have access to them." Anonymize individual data in team-wide coaching materials. Give top performers formal recognition and a small bonus for contributing their best templates to the shared library — this makes them feel appreciated rather than exposed.
