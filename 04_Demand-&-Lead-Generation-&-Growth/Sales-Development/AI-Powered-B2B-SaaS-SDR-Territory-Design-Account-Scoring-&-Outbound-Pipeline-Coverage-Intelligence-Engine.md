## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Account scores feel static — territory doesn't reflect real-time buyer behavior**
*Solution:* Ensure behavioral/intent signals have "decay" built in — an email open from 90 days ago shouldn't carry the same weight as one from last week. Add a time-weighting multiplier: signals from last 14 days = 1.0x weight; 15–30 days = 0.7x; 31–60 days = 0.4x; 60+ days = 0.1x. This requires updating the scoring formula to pull signal timestamps, not just binary "yes/no" presence.

**Problem: SDRs ignore scoring and revert to gut-feel account selection**
*Solution:* The scoring system won't stick unless it's embedded in the daily workflow, not just a report SDRs can ignore. Fix: (1) Make the score the default sort in every SDR's CRM view — they see Tier 1 accounts first every time they log in. (2) Manager uses territory health score in weekly 1:1 as the primary conversation anchor — not activities, but coverage. (3) First 2 weeks: manager approves the SDR's weekly "hit list" against score — this forces the habit.

**Problem: Tier 1 list is too large — 80+ accounts per SDR makes prioritization meaningless**
*Solution:* You've set the Tier 1 threshold too low. Raise the scoring bar: move the Tier 1 minimum from 70 to 78. Alternatively, add a hard cap: SDR can only have 25 Tier 1 accounts in active sequence at any time — the rest queue into a "Tier 1 Pending" list sorted by score. When a Tier 1 account exits sequence (replied, disqualified, or completed), the next highest-scored account from Pending auto-populates. Build this as a CRM workflow.
