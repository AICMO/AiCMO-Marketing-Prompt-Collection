## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Review generation machine produces reviews but G2 category rank does not improve.**
Solution: G2 weighting favors recency within the last 12 months — if your reviews are old (>12 months), new reviews add less than expected to your Satisfaction Score. Audit your review date distribution. Also check profile completeness: G2 penalizes profiles below 80% completion in their algorithm. Finally, verify you are in the correct G2 categories — reviews in a secondary category do not count toward your primary category Satisfaction Score.

**Problem: Comparison page traffic is high but conversion rate is below 3%.**
Solution: Comparison page visitors are in "research mode" not "ready to buy" mode — they need a lower-commitment CTA. Replace "Request Demo" with "Download the [Competitor] Migration Checklist" or "Talk to a Switch Specialist" (not a generic sales rep — specialist framing reduces resistance). Also audit the page for social proof: if your case studies feature companies that do not match the visitor's ICP, conversion drops significantly. Add industry and company-size filters to your case study section.

**Problem: G2 Buyer Intent identifies accounts but SDR conversion to meeting is below 12%.**
Solution: G2 Intent signals show *category research*, not *active vendor evaluation* — some accounts are early-stage researchers, not ready to talk. Segment Intent signals by recency and frequency (accounts that researched your category AND your competitor's profile in the same 7-day window are 3-4x more likely to take a meeting vs. monthly researchers). Adjust the SDR outreach trigger to require both signals before prioritizing. Also review the SDR email copy: if it references G2 explicitly ("we noticed you were researching alternatives on G2"), it tends to feel invasive. Better framing: "Working with [ICP-similar company] who recently switched from [Competitor]—wanted to share what they learned" — adds value rather than revealing surveillance.
