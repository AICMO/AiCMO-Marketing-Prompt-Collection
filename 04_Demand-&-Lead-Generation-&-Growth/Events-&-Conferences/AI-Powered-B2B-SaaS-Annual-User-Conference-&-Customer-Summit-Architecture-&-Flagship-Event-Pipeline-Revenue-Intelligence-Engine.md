## Troubleshooting

**Issue: Low customer attendance despite invitations — registration rate below 30%**
Solution: Identify the 3 most common objections (budget, travel time, can't get away from work) and address each directly. For budget: introduce "team scholarship" program where customers who submit a session proposal get free registration. For travel: arrange group hotel block at discounted rate and share a "build your business case for attending" email template that customers can forward to their manager. For availability: offer a fully-produced virtual attendance option with live Q&A access — even partial engagement converts to expansion conversations.

**Issue: Sessions feel like vendor demos — attendees disengage or give low ratings**
Solution: Institute a session quality gate: all sessions must be submitted with an abstract that includes (a) practitioner speaker credential, (b) specific quantified outcome the audience will achieve, and (c) a peer review score from 3 internal stakeholders who score on "Would I attend this if I were a customer?" before it's accepted. Brief every speaker 30 days before event with your anti-vendor-speak guidelines. Post-event: any session rated below 3.8/5.0 has its speaker automatically removed from future conference consideration.

**Issue: Post-event pipeline conversion falls below 5% — conference momentum doesn't translate to closed deals**
Solution: Root cause is almost always one of three things: (1) SDR/AE follow-up is too slow — implement a Day 1 same-day follow-up rule for all AE conversations at the event, (2) post-event content is generic — use AI to generate personalized follow-up emails that reference specific sessions attended and conversations held, or (3) the prospect attendee quality was wrong — too many practitioners and not enough budget holders. For next year, add ICP scoring to registration form and route non-ICP registrants to virtual attendance rather than in-person seats.
