## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Gifted creators aren't posting after receiving product**
Fix: Audit your seeding brief — over-scripted briefs kill creator motivation because they feel like unpaid ad labor rather than a genuine gift. Remove all "must include" language and replace with "here are some angles that have resonated with our community." Add a second follow-up at day 14 (most first-time gifted creators need a gentle nudge — not a demand). For persistent non-posters after 2 seedings, remove from roster and redirect budget to paid UGC tier where posting is contractually guaranteed.

**Problem: Creator content performs well organically but underperforms as paid ads**
Fix: Organic and paid audiences are different contexts. Content optimized for "community validation" (comments, saves, shares) doesn't always lead with a strong enough commercial hook for cold paid audiences. Commission separate "paid-first UGC" from top creators — same authentic voice, but brief them to lead with the product benefit in the first 2 seconds and include a clear purchase signal by second 15. Test this paid-specific creative alongside the boosted organic posts to isolate the format effect.

**Problem: Program is generating lots of content but influencing very few conversions**
Fix: Three likely root causes: (1) Creator audience doesn't match buyer ICP — audit top 10 creators' audience demographics vs. your actual customer data and remove mismatches; (2) Seeding wrong products — ensure hero product is the $45-85 price point sweet spot for cold social conversion, not $120+ items that require longer consideration; (3) Missing paid amplification — organic creator content alone rarely moves the needle on revenue, the program becomes a revenue driver when top-performing posts are whitelisted and scaled with paid budget behind them.
