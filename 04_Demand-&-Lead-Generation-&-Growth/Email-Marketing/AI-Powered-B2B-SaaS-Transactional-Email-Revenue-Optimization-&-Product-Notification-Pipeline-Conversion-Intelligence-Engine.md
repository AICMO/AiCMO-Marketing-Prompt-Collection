## Integration Tips

**Customer.io / Braze / Intercom:**
- Create event-triggered campaigns using product events as entry conditions
- Use Liquid templating to inject behavioral data: `{{ user.trial_days_remaining }}`, `{{ user.top_feature_used }}`
- Build suppression lists: exclude users who already upgraded from receiving upgrade CTAs in transactional emails

**HubSpot:**
- Use Smart Content rules to swap CTA blocks by lifecycle stage (Lead vs. MQL vs. Customer)
- Connect to Deals to suppress upgrade CTAs from accounts with active sales opportunities (avoid undercutting sales)
- Build workflows: transactional email CTA click → create task for CS rep if Enterprise-size company

**Salesforce + Pardot:**
- Pass transactional email engagement data as activity records on Contacts
- Use Engagement Studio to branch nurture paths based on transactional email clicks
- Score upgrade CTA clicks as high-intent signals (25–50 pts) in behavioral lead scoring model

**Stripe / Billing Integration:**
- Pull `plan_name`, `trial_end_date`, `usage_percent`, `payment_method_last4` via API for deep personalization
- Trigger payment failure sequence automatically from `invoice.payment_failed` webhook
- A/B test annual plan upsell in billing confirmation emails (high receptivity moment)

**Segment / CDP:**
- Stream product events into email platform to power milestone-triggered emails
- Create computed traits: `days_since_last_login`, `feature_depth_score`, `trial_conversion_probability`
- Build audiences: "Trial users with >5 logins AND invited teammate AND viewed pricing page" → priority upgrade sequence

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Transactional emails with marketing CTAs are getting classified as spam or triggering unsubscribes**
*Solution:* Ensure the primary purpose (≥70% of copy) is the transactional message. Move commercial CTAs to the bottom 30% of the email or into the P.S. line. Remove "Unsubscribe from marketing" links — use instead "Update notification preferences." Have legal review the four highest-volume emails before launching. Check inbox placement using tools like GlockApps or Litmus.

**Problem: CTAs in transactional emails are being clicked but not converting to upgrades**
*Solution:* The landing page experience is breaking conversion, not the email. Map the click destination — most teams send transactional email CTA clicks to the homepage, not a pre-filled upgrade flow. Build dedicated landing pages with session continuity (auto-log-in, pre-selected plan, pre-populated billing info). For trial expiry emails, link directly to the upgrade checkout with `?plan=starter&source=trial_expiry_d5` UTM tracking.

**Problem: Transactional open rates are dropping after adding marketing CTAs**
*Solution:* You're likely seeing domain reputation bleed — your sending infrastructure sees higher complaint rates from the CTA emails, and mailbox providers are adjusting placement. Separate your transactional email sends to a dedicated subdomain (e.g., `notifications.yourdomain.com`) with its own IP reputation, distinct from your promotional email domain. Also audit your suppression logic — are unsubscribed users still receiving transactional emails with marketing content?
