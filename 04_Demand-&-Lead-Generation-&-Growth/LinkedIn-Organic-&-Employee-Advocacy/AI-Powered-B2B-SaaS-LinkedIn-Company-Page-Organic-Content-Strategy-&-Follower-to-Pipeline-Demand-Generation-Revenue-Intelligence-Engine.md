## Integration Tips

- **HubSpot:** Create a custom "LinkedIn Organic" original source in HubSpot. Add UTM parameters (`utm_source=linkedin&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=[pillar]`) to all links in posts to track MQL sourcing automatically.
- **LinkedIn Campaign Manager:** Export your company page visitor list monthly and upload to Campaign Manager as a Matched Audience for retargeting campaigns. This turns organic reach into paid pipeline acceleration for <$1,000/month.
- **Shield Analytics or Aware:** Use these LinkedIn analytics tools (both integrate with Slack/Notion) to automate weekly performance reporting without logging into LinkedIn manually.
- **Buffer or Hypefury:** Schedule posts with these tools but ensure you interact natively on LinkedIn (not through the scheduler tool) to avoid engagement rate penalties.
- **Zapier + ManyChat or MobileMonkey:** Automate DM follow-up sequences for keyword-triggered comments. Connect to HubSpot to auto-create contacts when someone sends the trigger keyword.
- **Salesforce:** Add a "LinkedIn Organic Touch" activity field to Opportunity objects. Train SDRs/AEs to log LinkedIn engagement before first meetings to measure organic-influenced pipeline.
- **Notion or Airtable:** Build your content calendar here and use AI writing tools (Claude, GPT-4, Jasper) directly in your workflow to produce first drafts at 10x speed before human editing.

## Troubleshooting

**Problem:** Posts are getting good impressions (1,000+) but zero engagement and no DMs
- Solution: Your hooks are working but your content isn't delivering the promised value. Audit the body of your top-impression posts: are you giving specific, actionable insight or generic advice? Replace one "educational" post per week with a specific customer story or data point. Test "DM me [keyword]" CTAs — these convert 3-5x better than link CTAs for cold audiences.

**Problem:** Content is engaging but followers aren't converting to pipeline
- Solution: Your content is likely top-of-funnel only. Introduce Pillar 4 (Product Storytelling) posts once per week — framed as "how a [customer type] solved [specific problem] using [outcome-based product description]." Also check your page CTA button: it should link to a high-intent landing page (demo, trial, or specific use case page), not your homepage.

**Problem:** Engagement is dropping after consistent posting
- Solution: Audience fatigue from repetitive formats or topics. Conduct a content audit of your last 20 posts — if more than 40% are the same format or pillar, diversify immediately. Introduce a poll to reset engagement signals, and try one piece of genuinely contrarian content that challenges a core assumption your ICP holds. The algorithm rewards content that drives comments from people who disagree.
