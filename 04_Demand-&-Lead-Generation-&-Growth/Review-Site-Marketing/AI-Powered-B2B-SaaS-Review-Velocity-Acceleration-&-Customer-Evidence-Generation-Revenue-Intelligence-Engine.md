## Troubleshooting

**Problem:** CSMs resist asking customers for reviews, saying "I don't want to bother my accounts."
**Solution:** Reframe the ask for CSMs — position it as "helping your customer become a more visible expert in their space" rather than "doing marketing a favor." Train CSMs to say: "Leaving a review on G2 gets your name and perspective seen by 5,000+ finance leaders researching AP automation every month — it's good for your career, not just for us." Also, reduce the ask to one sentence with a direct link — 90% of review completion friction is navigation, not willingness. Remove all friction from the CSM's job by providing pre-written, personalized templates they send in one click.

**Problem:** Review completion rates below 15% despite outreach.
**Solution:** Audit three variables: (1) Timing — are you asking too early (before the customer has experienced real value) or too late (after the relationship has cooled)? Move trigger 1 from Day 30 to Day 60 and test completion rate change. (2) Channel — email alone typically drives 12–15% completion; adding a CSM personal Slack message after the email increases completion to 25–32%. (3) Friction — test a "one-click direct link to G2 review form" (bypassing G2 login page) vs. asking customers to find the review themselves. Direct links increase completion by 40–60%.

**Problem:** A cluster of negative reviews appearing on G2 following a product issue or support failure.
**Solution:** Immediately activate the Reputation Management protocol: (1) Alert the CS and product leadership team within 4 hours of detecting 2+ negative reviews in 7 days. (2) Every negative review gets a public response within 24 hours acknowledging the issue and inviting the customer to connect directly. (3) The CS team personally calls each negative reviewer within 48 hours to understand the issue and offer resolution. (4) Once the issue is resolved, re-contact each reviewer with a direct link to edit or update their review — most customers will improve their rating if their problem was genuinely solved. Do NOT offer incentives to update negative reviews — this violates G2's terms of service and can result in profile suspension.
