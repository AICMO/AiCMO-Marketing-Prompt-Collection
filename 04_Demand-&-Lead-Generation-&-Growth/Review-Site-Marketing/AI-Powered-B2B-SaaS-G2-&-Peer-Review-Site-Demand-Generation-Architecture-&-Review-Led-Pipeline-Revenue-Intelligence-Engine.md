- **ICP Fit Overlay Scoring (add to intent score):**

  - 80–100: Immediate SDR outreach within 2 hours + Tier 1 ABM activation + LinkedIn ad suppression (they're already in active pipeline)

## Troubleshooting

**Problem:** G2 Buyer Intent signals are low volume (fewer than 20 accounts/week) and quality is poor — most accounts are too small or wrong industry to be ICP.
**Solution:** G2 intent signal volume is directly correlated to your G2 category ranking. If you're ranked #4–#7 in your category, your profile gets significantly fewer views than #1–#3, which means fewer intent signals. Prioritize the review velocity program (add 40+ reviews in the next 90 days) to improve rank to top 3, which typically increases profile views by 2–4× and corresponding intent signal volume. Also verify you're subscribed to the correct G2 categories — add the 2 secondary categories where your ICP researches (Expense Management, Invoice Management) to expand the intent signal universe. Separately, review your ICP filter criteria — if you're filtering too aggressively by employee count, try widening the range by 20% and see if qualified accounts are being excluded.

**Problem:** SDRs are ignoring the G2 intent queue, continuing to work unstructured lead lists instead of prioritized intent accounts.
**Solution:** The root cause is almost always one of three things: (a) SDRs don't understand the value of intent data — run a 30-minute training showing the conversion rate comparison between intent-sourced and non-intent leads; (b) the intent queue is too complex or lives in a system SDRs don't already use daily — simplify to a single Salesforce view or Slack channel they see every morning with no more than 5–10 accounts per day; (c) SDRs aren't rewarded for working the intent queue — work with RevOps to track intent-source meetings separately and show SDR managers the comparison data. Consider a brief "G2 intent first" policy where SDRs must work the intent queue before moving to other outreach each day.

**Problem:** G2 Ads cost-per-click is increasing and ROI is declining — spend is rising but pipeline generation is flat.
**Solution:** Rising G2 Ad CPCs indicate category competition is increasing (more vendors bidding). Shift strategy in two ways: (1) Reduce spend on broad category page ads (where you're competing against all vendors) and shift budget to competitor comparison page ads (where you're specifically targeting buyers comparing you to Tipalti or BILL — these visitors are 2× more likely to be in buying mode), and (2) Improve your G2 profile conversion rate so every click converts better — a 2× improvement in profile-to-demo conversion rate = 50% reduction in effective cost-per-lead even if CPC stays flat. Audit: add more customer testimonials, add product tour video, improve CTA button copy, add pricing transparency.
