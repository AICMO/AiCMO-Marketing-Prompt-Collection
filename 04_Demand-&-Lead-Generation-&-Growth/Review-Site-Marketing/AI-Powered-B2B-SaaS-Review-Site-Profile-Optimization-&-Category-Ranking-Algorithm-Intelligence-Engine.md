## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Review request emails are being ignored (< 10% completion rate)**
Solution: The biggest conversion killer is email length and friction. Shorten your initial ask to < 100 words, include the exact time estimate ("takes 4 minutes"), and add a direct link that pre-fills the reviewer's company name and role — reducing the effort from "fill out a form" to "just answer these 5 questions." Also, the highest completion rates come from CSM personal emails on mobile — coach CSMs to send the ask immediately after a positive QBR call while the emotion is fresh.

**Problem: We're generating reviews but our Grid position isn't moving**
Solution: G2's algorithm weights recency heavily — a burst of 20 reviews that all come in the same 2-week period gets discounted by the algorithm versus 20 reviews distributed across 6 weeks. Pace your outreach: aim for 4–5 reviews per week rather than 20 in one campaign. Also check whether you're getting reviews from diverse company sizes (SMB, mid-market, enterprise) and industries — algorithm rewards demographic diversity of reviewers.

**Problem: Negative reviews are appearing and dragging down our star rating**
Solution: Do not respond defensively. Respond publicly within 24 hours with a 3-sentence format: (1) Thank them for honest feedback, (2) Acknowledge the specific issue they raised, (3) Offer to continue the conversation privately (share your email). Then internally, use the negative review as a CSM alert — route the account to your churn prevention playbook immediately. Simultaneously, accelerate review requests to your Tier 1 Promoters to increase review volume so individual negative reviews are numerically diluted without suppressing them.
