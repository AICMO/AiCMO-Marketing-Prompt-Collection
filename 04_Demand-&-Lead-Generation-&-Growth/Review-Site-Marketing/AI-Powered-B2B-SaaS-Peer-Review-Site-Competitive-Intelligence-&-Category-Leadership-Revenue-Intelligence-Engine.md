## Overview
This engine transforms peer review platforms (G2, Capterra, Gartner Peer Insights, TrustRadius, GetApp) from passive badge collectors into active competitive intelligence systems and pipeline generators. Deploy it when you need to identify competitor vulnerabilities through negative reviews, capture category grid leadership, convert review site comparison traffic to pipeline, or arm sales with real-time social proof at the exact moment a competitor's customer is expressing dissatisfaction.

## Troubleshooting

**Problem**: Review velocity campaign triggers G2 review fraud detection and reviews get removed
**Solution**: Never batch-request reviews — space requests over a 60-90 day rolling cadence. Avoid any language that implies reviews need to be positive ("we'd love a 5-star review" violates G2 terms). Use G2's approved incentive program (gift cards through G2 Rewards are compliant; offering them outside G2's system is not). Target customers who've already expressed high satisfaction in CSAT/NPS surveys — their reviews are more likely to be detailed, specific, and pass G2's quality filters. Reviews from customers with longer account tenure (12+ months) also score better in G2's algorithm.

**Problem**: Competitor displacement cold outreach gets low reply rates despite pain-point targeting
**Solution**: Generic references to "we saw G2 reviews" feel like a template. The copy must reference a specific, verifiable pain point pattern: "We've spoken to 40+ [Competitor] customers in the last 6 months who all flagged the same issue — [specific pain]. Are you seeing the same thing in your team?" Specificity creates credibility. Test first lines that open with a direct number or stat from competitor review data. Also check: are you targeting the right title? The person leaving negative reviews on G2 may not be the economic buyer — you may need to ladder up one level.

**Problem**: Can't access enough competitor review data to build campaigns without a G2 subscription
**Solution**: G2's public profiles show the full distribution of reviews by star rating and the most helpful reviews without a paid subscription. For bulk extraction, use Clay.com to scrape public G2 profiles and run through Claude or GPT to categorize pain points by theme. Alternatively, Crayon, Klue, and Kompyte offer automated competitor review monitoring as part of their competitive intelligence platforms. For Gartner Peer Insights, reviews are publicly searchable by product. Prioritize depth over breadth: 50 deeply analyzed competitor reviews yield more actionable intelligence than 500 surface-level ones.
