## Overview
Builds a fully managed content syndication program that distributes gated B2B content (whitepapers, research reports, benchmark studies) through paid publisher networks to reach in-market buyers at ICP accounts who have never visited your website. Use this when you've maxed out your owned audience channels and need to access net-new buying audiences at scale — particularly for enterprise deals requiring 6-12 month nurture cycles.

NURTURE SEQUENCE ARCHITECTURE:

Syndicated leads must receive follow-up based on what they downloaded, not a generic brand nurture. Structure:

EMAIL 1 (Within 60 minutes of lead capture):
- Subject: "[First Name], here's what we found in [specific data point from their downloaded report]"
- Content: 2-3 specific insights from the report they downloaded + one unanswered question the report raises
- CTA: "Read the full finding" → link to a related blog post or landing page (NOT a demo request)
- Send from: Marketing alias (not a rep), to set expectations for content relationship

EMAIL 2 (Day 3):
- Subject: "Most [their job title]s are surprised by this finding"
- Content: Peer benchmark data from the report — how do they compare?
- CTA: "Take the 2-minute assessment" → link to an interactive maturity assessment or self-scoring tool

EMAIL 3 (Day 7):
- Subject: "What [company similar to theirs] did differently"
- Content: A specific, detailed customer story (NOT a case study PDF) — 250 words, first person, real outcome numbers

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Leads are coming in but SDRs say they're terrible quality — wrong titles, wrong company sizes, no budget**
- Root cause: Publisher targeting parameters were set too broadly, or the publisher's "title normalization" is mapping non-ICP titles to your target titles
- Solution: Request a raw data export from the publisher showing the actual email domains and LinkedIn URLs for the last 100 leads; spot-check against your ICP definition; demand credit or replacement leads for non-ICP contacts; reduce content syndication to only the publisher's "verified registered users" segment (more expensive per lead, dramatically better quality); add company headcount as a hard filter if not already set

**Problem: Email open rates on the nurture sequence are below 20% and no one is engaging**
- Root cause: Either the "From" name isn't trusted (using company name vs. a human name), the subject lines are too generic, or the send timing is wrong (arriving during weekend or holiday)
- Solution: Switch from a company alias to a named human sender (e.g., "Sarah Chen from Veloxa") for all nurture emails; A/B test subject lines with the downloaded report title in the subject (specificity beats cleverness); ensure Email 1 sends within 30 minutes of lead capture — drop in open rate after 2 hours is significant; check email deliverability with a tool like GlockApps; confirm your CRM isn't batching syndicated lead imports instead of processing in real time

**Problem: 90-day ROI report shows leads-to-opportunity rate below 3% despite reasonable lead quality scores**
- Root cause: The nurture sequence is converting to MQLs but SDRs aren't following up, or SDRs are following up but the content downloaded doesn't align with what the SDR team is saying in their outreach
- Solution: Audit SDR activity — what % of MQLs received a call + email within 48 hours? If below 75%, it's a follow-up process issue, not a lead quality issue; verify the SDR's opening line references the specific content downloaded (a generic opener from a content syndication lead is a conversion killer); check if leads scoring 60-74 are being included in SDR routing — many programs benefit from a brief SDR "warm touch" even for below-threshold leads from high-intent accounts; if SDR follow-up is strong and conversion is still low, the content is likely attracting top-of-funnel researchers who aren't actively in a buying cycle — shift budget toward bottom-funnel assets (ROI calculators, competitive guides) which attract leads closer to decision
