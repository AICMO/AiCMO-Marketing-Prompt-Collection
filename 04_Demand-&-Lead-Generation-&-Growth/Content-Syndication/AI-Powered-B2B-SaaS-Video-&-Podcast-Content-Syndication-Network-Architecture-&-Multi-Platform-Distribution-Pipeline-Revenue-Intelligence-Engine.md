## Troubleshooting

**Problem:** Clips perform poorly on LinkedIn despite high-quality source content.
**Solution:** LinkedIn native video algorithm rewards early engagement velocity in the first 60 minutes. To seed it: (1) post during ICP work hours (7:30-9:00 AM or 11:30 AM-1:00 PM in the target timezone), (2) immediately comment on your own post with a text summary so the algorithm registers comment activity, (3) ask 3-5 internal employees to engage (like + comment — not just like) within the first 30 minutes, (4) check that the video starts with a pattern interrupt in the first 3 seconds — LinkedIn auto-plays silently, so open with bold on-screen text or an immediate visual hook, not a talking head introduction.

**Problem:** Can't attribute pipeline to podcast — listeners convert months later with no memory of the podcast.
**Solution:** Three-layer approach: (1) Include a unique "listener offer" in your podcast outro (e.g., "Stackline listeners get a free revenue model audit — use code REVOPS at stackline.com/audit") — this gives you direct, trackable podcast-sourced conversions; (2) always include a UTM-tagged link in the show notes description on every platform and train your host to reference it verbally; (3) add "Podcast" as an explicit option in your demo request form's "How did you hear about us?" dropdown — self-reported attribution from podcast listeners is typically 3-5% of your total form fills but represents disproportionately high-intent buyers.

**Problem:** Content repurposing takes 6-8 hours per piece instead of the target 2-3 hours.
**Solution:** The bottleneck is almost always clip selection (deciding which moments to cut) not production itself. Solve it by: (1) using OpusClip or Vidyo.ai for automated AI clip selection — these tools identify speaker energy peaks, applause, quotable moments, and topic density; accept their top 8 suggestions and reject/modify rather than starting from scratch; (2) create a "clip selection brief" that you give to the AI tool: your ICP's top 5 pain points, your company's key differentiation points, and 3-5 phrases your ICP uses — the AI selects clips that match these rather than generic "engagement" signals; (3) batch repurposing for 3-4 pieces on the same day to amortize the context-switching cost and get your coordinator into a flow state.
