## Overview
Designs a fully operational account-based content syndication program that delivers targeted content exclusively to named accounts on your Tier-1 and Tier-2 ABM target lists — reaching buying committee members inside priority accounts through publisher networks, not just any ICP-fit leads at random companies. Use this when you have a defined target account list of 200-2,000 companies and need to generate multi-stakeholder engagement inside those specific accounts before your sales team arrives — compressing awareness-to-meeting timelines from 12+ months to 60-90 days.

PUBLISHER PLATFORM SELECTION FOR ACCOUNT-BASED SYNDICATION:

Unlike standard content syndication where you pay per lead from any ICP-fit contact, account-based syndication requires publishers with true named account matching capabilities. Evaluate each platform:

TIER-1 ABM-NATIVE PUBLISHERS:

MADISON LOGIC:
- Account matching: Upload named account list; ML matches against registered user base; typical B2B SaaS match rate: 65-80% of uploaded accounts
- Persona targeting: Target by job function and seniority level within matched accounts; can target multiple titles simultaneously (e.g., "VP Engineering" AND "CTO" AND "Director of Product" at the same account)
- Content delivery: Display ads + sponsored content + email distribution to registered users at matched accounts
- Reporting: Account-level engagement reports showing which named accounts had 1+ content interactions this week
- Best for: Enterprise accounts ($1B+ revenue target companies) where ML has high coverage; mid-market match rates can be lower
- CPM-based pricing: $25-55 CPM for named account targeting; budget around $3,000-8,000/month for 100 Tier-1 accounts
- Minimum commitment: Quarterly program ($30,000+)

TECHTARGET PRIORITY ENGINE:
- Account matching: Intent data first, content delivery second — identifies accounts in your target list actively researching relevant topics, then serves your content to those contacts
- Persona targeting: Strong in IT/DevOps/Security buyer personas; title-level targeting within named accounts
- Unique advantage: "Intent + match" combination — only spend impressions on target accounts showing active research intent, not all matched accounts
- Reporting: Account-level content engagement + intent signal changes week over week
- Best for: Technology buyer personas (VP Engineering, CISO, DevOps Director, Cloud Architect) at technology companies
- Pricing: Intent data subscription + content delivery fees; budget $8,000-15,000/month for 200+ named accounts
- Warning: Lower match rates for non-technology company target lists; healthcare, legal, and financial services coverage is thinner

DEMANDBASE ENGAGEMENTS:
- Account matching: Deep integration with 6sense, Rollworks, Demandbase's own data — if you're already a Demandbase customer, account lists sync directly without CSV upload
- Persona targeting: Functional + seniority targeting within matched accounts; integrates with LinkedIn for cross-platform retargeting
- Unique advantage: If you use Demandbase ABM, your account engagement scores from content syndication feed directly into your ABM platform's account health dashboard — no data reconciliation required
- Reporting: Unified account engagement across paid media, content syndication, and organic visits
- Best for: Companies already on Demandbase platform looking to add content syndication as a channel; native platform integration is the key differentiator
- Pricing: Typically bundled with Demandbase platform subscription; additive cost $5,000-12,000/month for named account content program

BOMBORA AUDIENCE SOLUTIONS (Account-Based):
- Account matching: B2B intent data + content syndication; filter your named account list to only accounts showing active Bombora topic intent before running content to them
- Persona targeting: Functional targeting within intent-qualified named accounts
- Unique advantage: Intent signal pre-filtering means you only spend syndication budget on accounts actively researching — reduces waste vs. running to all named accounts regardless of current activity
- Reporting: Intent trend reports by account + content engagement signals
- Best for: Programs where you want to prioritize syndication spend toward the subset of target accounts showing buying signals NOW, rather than equally across all named accounts

LINKEDIN MATCHED AUDIENCES (Document Ads + Thought Leader Ads):
- Account matching: Upload named account list; LinkedIn matches by company name and domain; typical match rate: 70-85% for accounts with 50+ employees and active LinkedIn presence
- Persona targeting: Most precise persona targeting available — exact job title, function, seniority, AND named account simultaneously; can target "VP of Engineering at [your specific 200 named accounts]" not just "VPs of Engineering generally"
- Content delivery: Document Ads (gated PDF delivery through LinkedIn, native lead gen form captures contact info); Thought Leader Ads (executive content promoted to target accounts)
- Unique advantage: LinkedIn is the only platform where you can reach every buying committee persona at a named account with different content in the same week — create 4 separate campaigns for 4 different personas at the same target account list
- Reporting: Account-level and contact-level engagement; no anonymous traffic — every engagement is a known LinkedIn profile
- Best for: Any B2B SaaS company where buying committee includes business buyers (not just IT); especially effective for HR tech, Finance tech, Marketing tech where LinkedIn targeting precision outweighs TechTarget's IT-buyer coverage
- Pricing: LinkedIn CPCs for Document Ads: $8-22 per click in B2B SaaS; budget $5,000-15,000/month for named account campaigns; no minimum commitment but performance improves with $3,000+ per campaign per month

ACCOUNT ENGAGEMENT SCORING MODEL:

Standard content syndication tracks individual lead scores. Account-based syndication requires account-level aggregation — summing engagement signals across all contacts from a named account to produce a single "account engagement score" that drives sales activation.

ENGAGEMENT SIGNAL WEIGHTS (0-100 point account score):

CONTENT ENGAGEMENT SIGNALS (40 points maximum):
- Decision-stage asset downloaded (ROI calculator, competitive comparison, vendor evaluation guide): 20 points per contact, maximum 20 points per asset per account
- Consideration-stage asset downloaded (evaluation framework, architecture guide): 12 points per contact, maximum 12 points per asset per account
- Awareness-stage asset downloaded (benchmark report, trend analysis): 6 points per contact, maximum 6 points per asset per account
- Multiple assets downloaded by same contact (progressive engagement): +4 bonus points per additional asset beyond first
- Multiple personas engaged (different buying committee seats touched): +8 points per additional unique persona tier represented

BEHAVIORAL SIGNALS (30 points maximum):
- Account showing rising intent signals in ABM platform (6sense intent grade A or B, Demandbase "Hot" account, Bombora surge score >70): 20 points
- Website visits from account's IP range (corporate proxy or individual visits): 8 points per unique page category visited (product, pricing, case studies)
- LinkedIn Company Page follows or ad engagement from account employees: 4 points
- G2 or Capterra profile visit from account domain: 6 points
- Attended webinar or virtual event (account employee registered or attended): 12 points

ORGANIZATIONAL SIGNALS (30 points maximum):
- Buying committee coverage: 2+ personas from account engaged: 15 points
- Buying committee coverage: 3+ personas from account engaged: 25 points
- Buying committee coverage: 4+ personas from account engaged: 30 points (full buying committee engagement — maximum signal)
- Executive-level engagement (C-suite or VP+ from account interacted with any content): +5 bonus points
- Economic buyer specifically engaged: +5 bonus points (finance function or C-level content consumption)

ACCOUNT SCORE THRESHOLDS AND ACTIONS:

Score 80-100 — HOT ACCOUNT: Immediate sales activation
- AE notification within 24 hours with full account intelligence brief
- SDR assigned if no AE coverage; outbound sequence starts within 48 hours
- Recommend: AE + marketing review call within 72 hours to agree on approach

Score 0-39 — COLD ACCOUNT: Awareness building

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Publisher account match rates are below 50% — we uploaded 300 accounts and only 150 are reachable**
- Root cause: Publisher databases match on company name and domain; companies with multiple legal entities, recent acquisitions, or name variations in the publisher's database won't match; also, smaller companies ($50M-100M revenue) are often underrepresented in publisher databases vs. enterprise accounts
- Solution: Before final publisher commitment, request a match rate estimate by uploading a sample of 50 accounts from your list; if the estimate is below 60%, clean your account list (add alternate company name variations, use parent company names for subsidiaries, add the primary domain without subdomains or www); also consider that publisher match rates vary significantly by company size — if your Tier-2 list is mid-market companies under 500 employees, LinkedIn Matched Audiences will consistently outperform Madison Logic or TechTarget for account coverage; for unmatched accounts, shift budget to LinkedIn where 70-80%+ account match rates are typical for B2B companies with active employee presence

**Problem: Buying committee coverage isn't improving — we keep reaching the same L&D contacts but can't get the CHRO or CFO to engage**
- Root cause: Most content syndication publishers serve content to registered users who opt in to receive content — CHROs and CFOs register for content syndication networks at much lower rates than practitioners and functional directors; also, awareness-stage content (benchmark reports, trend analysis) doesn't typically compel economic buyers to download because it doesn't solve their specific decision-making problem
- Solution: For economic buyer personas specifically, LinkedIn Thought Leader Ads and Executive Document Ads consistently outperform traditional content syndication — LinkedIn allows targeting "C-level AND named accounts" simultaneously, reaching executives who would never register for a content syndication network; create a dedicated economic buyer content asset that addresses the "cost of inaction" and financial risk framing that CFOs/CHROs respond to (not a feature overview, but a board-ready ROI analysis); also consider direct mail or executive gifting to the 15-20 CFOs on your Tier-1 list as a complement to digital content syndication — physical touches have dramatically higher response rates for this persona tier when combined with digital warm-up

**Problem: AEs are ignoring the account intelligence briefs — hot accounts are being created but no one is acting on them**
- Root cause: AEs receive too many notifications and the content syndication brief is competing with pipeline review, sales forecasting, and customer meetings; also, AEs frequently don't believe in the quality of "marketing-sourced account signals" because they've received too many false positives from marketing in the past
- Solution: Reduce notification noise by only triggering AE alerts when accounts reach 80+ points AND have a specific economic buyer (VP+ level) engagement signal — not just any contact from the account; add a "why now" narrative to every brief (one paragraph written in plain language explaining exactly what the account has done in the last two weeks and why this is a meaningful buying signal, not marketing metrics); create a 90-day retrospective showing AEs which content syndication hot accounts converted to pipeline vs. which didn't — when AEs see real historical conversion data, they become believers; finally, run a monthly "ABM hot account review" meeting where marketing and sales review the top 10 hottest accounts together, agree on an outreach strategy, and hold each other accountable — this joint ownership dramatically improves AE follow-through rates
