## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Publishers push back on replacement lead requests, claiming all leads meet contractual specs**
Solution: Document your lead quality scoring model in writing before signing publisher contracts. Include a clause that defines "qualified lead" as scoring ≥40 on your ICP criteria (job title seniority, company size, industry), not just "valid business email and company name." Review existing contracts — most publishers only guarantee data validity, not ICP fit. For renewals, negotiate an ICP fit rate guarantee (e.g., "minimum 60% of leads must score 40+ on buyer's ICP scoring model") with a volume credit for shortfalls.

**Problem: CRM attribution is broken — can't tell which syndication leads converted to pipeline**
Solution: This is the #1 reason syndication ROI analysis fails. Fix it in two steps: First, ensure every publisher delivers leads via API or webhook with a unique publisher UTM parameter that persists to the CRM Contact record (not just the first-touch cookie). Second, create a "Syndication Source" field on Opportunity in Salesforce that auto-populates from the associated Contact's publisher source field when an Opportunity is created. Without these two pieces of data hygiene, your publisher scorecard will always have attribution gaps. If you're starting fresh, implement this before scaling syndication spend.

**Problem: Lead quality scores improve but opportunity creation still low — sales team not working the leads**
Solution: Syndication lead quality scores mean nothing if SDRs aren't prioritizing them correctly. Audit SDR activity: Are leads with scores 60+ being contacted within 1 hour? Are SDRs using the persona-matched sequence or defaulting to their generic template? The fix has three components: (1) Set up Salesforce view/queue filter so SDRs only see syndication leads scored 60+ in their primary view, (2) build persona-matched email sequences (not generic) that reference the specific content downloaded ("I saw you downloaded our [Report Title] — the section on [specific challenge] tends to resonate with [job title] teams who are..."), (3) create a "Syndication Lead" SLA dashboard visible to SDR managers showing response time compliance by rep.
