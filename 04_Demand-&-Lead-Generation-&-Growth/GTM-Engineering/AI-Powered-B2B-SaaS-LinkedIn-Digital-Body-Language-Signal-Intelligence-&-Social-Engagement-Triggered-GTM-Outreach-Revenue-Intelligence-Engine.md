## Overview
When a prospect from a target account likes your CEO's post, comments on a competitor's product announcement, or engages with industry pain point content, they're broadcasting buying intent in real time — before they fill out a single form. This engine decodes LinkedIn social engagement signals into a tiered priority system, builds an automated monitoring infrastructure across company pages, thought leader profiles, and competitor content, and orchestrates hyper-personalized outreach sequences that arrive within hours of the engagement — while the context is still warm and the prospect's mind is on the problem your product solves.

Signal D: Multiple engagement touchpoints from the same person within 7 days (engagement clustering)

2.2 TOOL STACK AND DATA PIPELINE
For each monitoring tool, define: signal type captured, data format output, update frequency, cost tier, Clay integration method

PRIMARY TOOLS:

Trigify (trigify.io):
- What it monitors: LinkedIn post engagement (likes, comments, shares) on specified profiles and company pages
- Data output: Structured webhook with engager name, profile URL, company, role, engagement type, post content snippet
- Update frequency: Near real-time (1-4 hour delay from engagement)
- Clay integration: Direct webhook to Clay table → new row created per engagement event
- Best for: Monitoring your own content and specified competitor profiles for ICP engager detection
- Cost tier: $99-$299/month depending on profile volume

PhantomBuster:
- What it monitors: LinkedIn profile scraping, company page post scraping, hashtag post engagement lists
- Data output: CSV export or API — engager profiles, post URLs, engagement counts
- Update frequency: Scheduled (daily or twice-daily recommended)
- Clay integration: PhantomBuster webhook → Clay HTTP request → new row creation
- Best for: Hashtag monitoring, bulk post engagement extraction, supplementary coverage
- Cost tier: $56-$128/month

Taplio:
- What it monitors: Engagement on connected LinkedIn profiles (your team's personal accounts), follower analytics, post performance
- Data output: Taplio API or CSV — engager data, follower growth, connection network analytics
- Clay integration: Zapier/Make → Clay via webhook
- Best for: Monitoring thought leader profiles when Trigify has coverage gaps
- Cost tier: $49-$149/month per seat

Shield Analytics:
- What it monitors: Deep analytics on your own LinkedIn company page and personal profiles — follower demographics, engagement rates by content type, top performing posts
- Data output: Dashboard analytics, CSV export — not real-time engager lists but content performance intelligence
- Best for: Understanding which of your content generates the highest-quality ICP engagement, informing what to publish more of
- Cost tier: $6-$49/month

LinkedIn Sales Navigator:
- What it monitors: Profile view alerts (who viewed your profile), InMail open data, lead list engagement alerts
- Data output: Native Sales Navigator interface — limited API access without enterprise contract
- Best for: Profile view signals from named accounts, champion tracking via saved lead alerts
- Cost tier: $100-$150/month per seat

## Troubleshooting

**Problem:** Signal volume is low — fewer than 5 Tier A signals per week despite monitoring active content publishing.
**Solution:** The bottleneck is almost always content reach, not monitoring infrastructure. If your CEO's posts are reaching 500 impressions per post, you will generate minimal signal volume. Prioritize growing content reach before optimizing the signal system: post 4-5x per week on pain points (not thought leadership), use the first comment strategy (CEO comments on their own post within the first 30 minutes — increases algorithmic reach by 3-5x), and tag relevant customers or industry figures in posts where contextually appropriate. Target: each pain point post reaching 3,000-10,000 impressions from relevant ICP audiences before your signal volume becomes operationally significant.

**Problem:** High volume of signals detected but low reply rate on outreach — personalization is not resonating.
**Solution:** Run a message audit on the last 30 outreach messages sent from signal triggers. The most common failure mode is messaging that implicitly references the LinkedIn activity ("I noticed you engaged with our content" or "I saw you liked our CEO's post") — this triggers an "I'm being monitored" reaction and tanks reply rates. Rewrite all frameworks to lead with the *insight* the engagement revealed, not the act of engagement itself. Test Framework 1 (extend the insight) vs. Framework 2 (peer observation) across the same signal type — typically one outperforms by 40-80% depending on your category and buyer archetype.

**Problem:** Clay Trigify integration is missing signals — some engagements from ICP accounts are not being captured.
**Solution:** Trigify monitors profiles you configure — it cannot monitor every post on LinkedIn. Expand your monitoring list: add your top 5 SDRs' personal LinkedIn profiles (if they publish regularly), your most active content contributors, and the top 3 competitor executives who post most frequently (not just company pages). Additionally, implement a weekly manual review process: SDR or marketing ops reviews LinkedIn analytics for each monitored profile and checks for high-engagement posts that may have been missed by Trigify's monitoring window. Target: <20% signal miss rate for Tier A engagement types (comments and shares).
