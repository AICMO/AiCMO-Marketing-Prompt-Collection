REGULATORY EVENT CLASSIFICATION SYSTEM:
For each detected regulatory event, auto-classify by impact on your GTM program:

- **Tier 1 — Immediate Activation (launch in 72 hours):** Regulation is now FINALIZED with hard enforcement date, directly names your ICP companies, creates clear software purchase obligation. Examples: DORA enforcement date passing, SEC Cybersecurity Rule disclosure requirements taking effect.
- **Tier 2 — 30-Day Launch (campaign in 30 days):** Regulation passed/finalized but enforcement 6-24 months out. Window to be the first vendor with authoritative compliance content before competitors. Examples: EU AI Act implementation periods, new NIST framework publication.

## Troubleshooting

**Problem:** SDR team doesn't know the regulation well enough to send credible outbound — buyers see through the compliance language immediately.
**Solution:** Before launching sequences, require every SDR to read the actual regulation (executive summary + penalty provisions, not just the blog post) and pass a 10-question quiz on the specific regulatory obligations your ICP contacts own. Build a "regulatory briefing deck" with exact language from the regulation, real examiner questions from early enforcement, and the 3-5 specific compliance requirements your product addresses. Compliance buyers will ignore any vendor who can't speak the actual regulatory language — and reward the ones who can.

**Problem:** Regulatory campaign launches 6+ months after competitors who already own the "authoritative content" position.
**Solution:** Implement the regulatory monitoring system in Part 1 immediately — don't wait for a regulation to become urgent before starting. The 6-12 month window between regulation finalization and enforcement date is your content authority window. If you arrive late, pivot to the "audit remediation" angle (post-enforcement buyers) rather than trying to out-content established incumbents. Consider partnering with a law firm or industry association to co-publish regulatory guidance — instant credibility in 4-6 weeks without years of content-building.

**Problem:** Regulatory campaigns generate interest but deals stall because budget wasn't planned — compliance buyers say "we agree we need this but it wasn't in the budget."
**Solution:** This is why the penalty risk calculator and "cost of non-compliance" framing are essential — the alternative to buying your product is a regulatory fine 5-20x your contract value. Build a dedicated "Regulatory Budget Release" one-pager for champions to use in internal budget conversations: "Here is the regulatory obligation we are required to meet, here is our current gap, here is the cost of the solution, here is the cost of non-compliance under [penalty provision]." Compliance spend routinely gets emergency budget approval when framed as fine avoidance — help your champion make that internal case.
