## Troubleshooting

**Problem: GitHub community is large but zero commercial conversion**
Fix: The OSS-to-commercial gap is almost always a visibility problem, not a product problem. Audit: (1) Is your commercial product easy to find from the GitHub README? Add a prominent "DataFlo Cloud" section with a link. (2) Does your docs site capture emails? Add a free account CTA to every docs page. (3) Are you capturing company-email signups? Gate the hosted/cloud product behind work email. Start there before assuming the product is the issue.

**Problem: Community backlash when mentioning commercial product**
Fix: Backlash is a signal of trust, not hostility — your community cares enough to react. Audit your ratio: for every commercial mention in the community, are you delivering 10x value in OSS improvements, helpful issue responses, and non-commercial content? If not, earn the right first. When you do mention commercial, frame it as "this solves enterprise problems the OSS version intentionally doesn't" — acknowledge the distinction, don't blur it.

**Problem: Can't identify which OSS users are enterprise-ready (no telemetry, no email capture)**
Fix: Retroactive identity is possible. Launch: (1) A docs newsletter ("DataFlo Weekly — tips from the community") that captures work emails. (2) A free cloud tier that requires signup. (3) A community Slack/Discord with optional LinkedIn profile linking. These create first-party identity without requiring telemetry. Start here before shipping a telemetry system.
